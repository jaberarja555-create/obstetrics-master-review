6. Management: methods
MethodHow · key points
Membrane sweepFinger through the os, circled to free membranes from the lower segment → local prostaglandins. At 40–41 wk; cuts the need for formal IOL
Dinoprostone (PGE₂, Prostin)3 mg tablet in posterior fornix, repeat after 6 h if not in labour, max 2 doses. For the live fetus. CTG before and after
Misoprostol (PGE₁)Cheap, stable, strong. IUFD: dose by weeks (see 3.6). Live term fetus: 25 µg PV 6-hourly only, never in a scarred uterus
Foley balloonCatheter past the internal os, balloon 30–50 mL saline, on traction 12–24 h. No hyperstimulation: choice in previous CS
LaminariaDry seaweed tent swells with cervical fluid and opens the canal; works like the balloon
ARMAmnihook between contractions, head fixed in the pelvis. Note liquor colour, feel for cord. Releases prostaglandins; add oxytocin
OxytocinIV infusion by titration, best after ARM. Start ≥ 6 h after the last prostaglandin
