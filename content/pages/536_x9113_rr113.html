7.6Partogram & CTG stations
Data stations: plot or read a labour partogram, and read a CTG aloud in a fixed order, then give the diagnosis and the plan.
1The partogram
  • Opened in the active phase at 4 cm: the first X on the alert line
  • Alert line rises 1 cm/h
  • Action line 4 h to the right
  • The WHO Labour Care Guide (2020) starts the active phase at 5 cm with no lines; local units and exams still use the partograph
2Symbols and recording
  • Cervix X every 4 h
  • Descent O in fifths palpable
  • FHR, pulse, contractions every 30 min
  • Liquor I/C/M/B/A
  • Moulding 0 to +3
  • Contractions as boxes per 10 min: dotted < 20 s, hatched 20–40 s, solid > 40 s
  • Oxytocin and drugs
  • BP 4-hourly, temperature 2-hourly, urine
3Reading the partogram
  • Who
  • Fetus
  • Progress (X against the lines, O, contractions)
  • Mother
  • Verdict and plan
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Crossing the alert line → Assess the cause; ARM ± oxytocin
  • Good contractions, no descent, moulding +3 → Obstructed labour: CS
