10. Advice

Early walking (VTE, bladder, bowel) · pelvic floor exercises · fibre and fluids · perineal hygiene, hand washing before and after pad change · rest and good diet · intercourse when lochia stops and the perineum heals · space the next pregnancy ≥ 24 months.

11. The rules (WHO)

Start within 1 hour of birth, skin to skin · exclusive for 6 months (no water, no sugar water) · on demand, 8–12 feeds a day · continue with family foods to 2 years. Caesarean section is no reason to delay.

Good attachment: mouth wide open · chin touches the breast · lower lip turned out · more areola seen above the mouth than below · slow deep sucks, no pain.

13. Contraindications
ConditionAdvice
Galactosaemia (baby)Absolute: lactose-free formula
Active TB, untreatedStart treatment; mask, express milk; baby gets isoniazid + BCG. Breastfeed once smear-negative (≈ 2 wk of treatment)
HIVIn our hospitals: breastfeed exclusively for 6 months on ART; no mixed feeding (see 2.12)
DrugsCytotoxics, radioactive iodine, lithium, amiodarone, ergots, tetracycline, chloramphenicol
OthersHTLV-1 · herpes lesion on the breast (that side) · severe maternal illness (express if possible)

Not contraindications: hepatitis B (baby vaccinated + HBIG) · mastitis · fever · caesarean · most antibiotics, heparin, warfarin, insulin, labetalol, nifedipine, PTU.
