3.1Bleeding in Early Pregnancy

1. Definition

Early pregnancy bleeding = bleeding before 24 wk (viability). Causes: miscarriage (commonest) · ectopic (most dangerous) · molar pregnancy · incidental: ectropion, polyp, cervicitis, Ca cervix.

Miscarriage = spontaneous loss of a pregnancy before 24 wk; abortion = induced loss. About 15–20% of known pregnancies miscarry.
