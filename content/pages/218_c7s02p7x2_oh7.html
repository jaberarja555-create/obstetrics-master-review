Station: Antepartum Haemorrhage

The station

"Mona, 32, G4 P3 at 33 weeks, had vaginal bleeding 2 hours ago. Take a focused history." Then: the two main causes, and what you must not do.

Presenting the case

"Mona, 32, G4 P3 at 33 weeks, two previous caesareans, had painless bright red bleeding while asleep, with a smaller bleed at 30 weeks. No pain, baby moving. Impression: placenta praevia, with accreta risk. I would resuscitate, avoid PV, cross-match and scan for placental site."
