8. Examiner marking points
  • Starts at 4 cm on the alert line
  • Draws alert and action lines right
  • X and O in the correct cells
  • Contraction boxes: number + shading
  • Reads fetus → progress → mother
  • Ends with diagnosis and plan
  • Names her, parity, GA, time
  • Says "left of the alert line"
  • Gives the rate in cm/h
  • Notes SROM with clear liquor
  • Comments on contractions and mother
  • Calls it normal labour
  • Says "crossed the alert line"
  • Finds the cause: weak contractions
  • Excludes obstruction and fetal distress
  • ARM + oxytocin, VE after 2 h
  • Knows oxytocin dilution and start
  • "Crossed the action line"
  • Good contractions + no descent
  • Moulding +3 = obstruction
  • Reads fetal and maternal distress
  • Says no oxytocin; CS now
  • Catheter for fistula prevention
