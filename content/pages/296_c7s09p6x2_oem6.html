  • Names it, notes time, calls help
  • Stops maternal pushing

  • No fundal pressure, no strong pull
  • Documents and debriefs
  • Correct hand on correct shoulder
  • Suprapubic: heel of hand, not fist on fundus
  • Records times and anterior shoulder
  • Checks baby's arm, mother's perineum
  • Honest, simple talk to the parents

Cord Prolapse Drill

18. The station

Task (6 min): "Para 3, 38 wk, transverse lie corrected to cephalic, membranes ruptured spontaneously at 5 cm. The midwife sees a loop of cord at the introitus; FHR 80. Manage." A pelvic model with a cord, a couch, a Foley set. You must show the hand and the position.

19. The drill
Step · whyماذا تقول
1Call for help, note the time; tell theatre: category 1 caesarean; stop oxytocinيا حاجة، الحبل السري نزل قدّام الجنا، ولازم نولّدك بسرعة. نحن كلنا معاكي.
2Hands off the cord: handling and cold cause spasm; outside the vulva → tuck it in gently, warm wet swab
3Elevate the presenting part: two fingers push the head up and stay there until the baby is outإيدي حتفضل جوّه لحدّي العملية عشان تحمي الجنا. ممكن تتضايقي شوية.
4Position: knee-chest, or exaggerated left Sims (head down, pillow under the hip) for transferاتلفّتي على ركبك وصدرك، وخلّي صلبك لفوق.
5Long wait or transfer: fill the bladder with 500 mL warm saline via Foley, clamp; empty it before the uterine incisionحنركّب قسطرة ونملأ المثانة بمحلول، ده بيرفع الجنا من الحبل.
6Contracting: terbutaline 0.25 mg SC · O₂ to mother · FHR by Doppler
7Deliver by the fastest route: CS ≤ 30 min; fully dilated with head low → instrumental · neonatal team presentحنمشي العمليات هسي، ودكتور الأطفال حيكون موجود.
