4. Investigations: confirm the leak
Fern testDried liquor on a slide: fern pattern of salt crystals
UltrasoundLow AFI supports · gestation, growth, presentation
Exam trap

No digital VE unless she is in labour: each VE carries bacteria up and shortens the time to infection. Sterile speculum is safe. Baseline FBC, CRP.

Distinguishing features
Wet pants on coughing, no poolStress incontinence
Nitrazine blue, bleeding tooMay be false positive
Clear pool, ferns on slideRuptured membranes
Fishy grey discharge, pH 5Bacterial vaginosis
Clinical scenario
32 wk, gush of clear fluid, no pains. First examination?
Sterile speculum: look for pooling. No digital VE.
5. Differential diagnosis

Urine (stress or overflow incontinence: acidic, smells of urine) · vaginal discharge (BV, candida) · show · liquor = alkaline, clear, ferns.

6. Complications
Maternal
  • Chorioamnionitis → sepsis, death
  • Abruption
  • Operative delivery · endometritis
Fetal, neonatal
  • Prematurity (leading cause of death) · sepsis
  • Cord prolapse, compression
  • Lung hypoplasia (ROM < 24 wk)
