Classical CS today: no lower segment (very preterm, < 28 wk) · transverse lie with back down and ruptured membranes · large fibroid or dense adhesions in the lower segment · anterior praevia with large vessels · perimortem CS.

Viva questions
When is the prophylactic antibiotic given?
Before the skin incision, not after the cord is clamped.
After a classical CS, next delivery?
Elective caesarean: no trial of labour.

Station: "Write the post-operative orders for this woman after an emergency LSCS for failure to progress" or "she is 6 h after CS: review her". Write clear, dated, signed orders: drug, dose, route, frequency, duration.
