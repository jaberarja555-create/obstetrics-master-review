8Prognosis
  • Maternal death 13–80%
  • Up to 85% of survivors have hypoxic brain damage
  • High perinatal death
  • Recurrence rare; next delivery in a hospital with ICU
  • Report for maternal death review
5.4Shoulder dystocia
The head is delivered but the shoulders do not follow with gentle downward traction, so extra manoeuvres are needed (or a head-to-body interval > 60 s). About 1 in 200 vaginal births; mostly unpredictable: about half occur in babies under 4 kg.
1Pathophysiology
  • Shoulders stay antero-posterior: the anterior shoulder sticks behind the symphysis pubis
  • Rarely the posterior shoulder on the sacral promontory
  • A bony problem: episiotomy alone does not free it
  • Diabetes: large trunk, normal head
  • Each minute the fetal pH falls about 0.04; injury risk rises after 5 min
2Risk factors
  • Antenatal: previous SD
  • Macrosomia (EFW > 4 kg)
  • Diabetes
  • BMI > 30
  • Post-term
  • Intrapartum: prolonged late 1st or 2nd stage
  • Mid-cavity instrumental delivery
  • Oxytocin augmentation, induction
