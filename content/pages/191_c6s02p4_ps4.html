8. Investigations
AimTests
Find the organismBlood cultures before antibiotics · high vaginal + cervical swabs (lochia) · MSU · wound swab · throat swab (GAS) · sputum · milk culture if mastitis fails
SeverityFBC (WBC, platelets) · lactate · CRP · urea, creatinine, electrolytes · LFT · clotting, fibrinogen (DIC) · glucose · blood gas
In SudanMalaria film or RDT in every fever · Widal is unreliable: culture for typhoid
ImagingPelvic ultrasound: retained products, collection · CXR · leg Doppler · CT pelvis if fever persists (abscess, pelvic vein thrombosis)
Clinical scenarios
Day 4: T 35.4 °C, pulse 128, RR 26, confused. Sepsis?
Yes: hypothermia + qSOFA ≥ 2. Sepsis Six now.
Fever persists 5 days on antibiotics, scan shows no collection. Likely cause?
Septic pelvic thrombophlebitis: CT, add heparin.
9. Differential diagnosis

Malaria · typhoid · pyelonephritis · appendicitis or cholecystitis · PE (fever, fast breathing, low SpO₂) · transfusion reaction · drug fever · thyroid storm · meningitis after spinal.

Exam trap

Do not wait for culture results or a "second fever" to start antibiotics in a sick woman. And a normal temperature does not exclude sepsis: hypothermia and tachycardia are worse signs.

Distinguishing features · Fever is not sepsis
Fever, rigors, ring forms on filmMalaria
Fever, pleuritic pain, SpO₂ 91%PE: CTPA
Fever hours after a transfusionTransfusion reaction
Headache, neck stiffness after spinalMeningitis
