7.5Speculum & vaginal examination
Two procedure stations: sterile speculum examination in pregnancy (swabs, ruptured membranes) and digital vaginal examination in labour (cervix, station, position, Bishop score).
1When to pass a speculum
  • Suspected ruptured membranes
  • Bleeding with a known non-praevia placenta
  • Discharge, swabs
  • No digital VE in suspected PPROM or unknown placental site
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Suspected ruptured membranes → Sterile speculum only, no digital VE
2The instruments
  • Cusco: bivalve, self-retaining, views the cervix
  • Sims: single blade, needs an assistant, views the anterior wall (VVF, prolapse)
3The steps
  • Warm it, wet with sterile water (no gel when testing for ROM)
  • Part the labia
  • Insert closed, sideways, pointing back
  • Rotate 90°, open, lock
  • Look at the cervix and fornices
  • Loosen before removing
4Taking swabs and confirming ROM
  • High vaginal swab from the posterior fornix
  • Endocervical swab inside the os (1–2 cm) for chlamydia and gonorrhoea
  • ROM: pool of liquor, flow on coughing, nitrazine turns blue, fern on a slide
