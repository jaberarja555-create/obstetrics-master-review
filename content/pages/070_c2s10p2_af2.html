Exam trap

Hepatitis E is mild in non-pregnant adults but causes fulminant liver failure in the 3rd trimester (death up to 20%). Consider it in jaundice outbreaks (unsafe water).

8. Management
  1. HDU / ICU with obstetric, medical and anaesthetic team. Inform a liver unit early.
  2. Glucose: 10–50% dextrose infusion; check blood glucose every 1–2 h.
  3. Clotting: IV vitamin K 10 mg, FFP, cryoprecipitate, platelets before delivery.
  4. BP and fluids: strict balance, catheter, central line if needed; treat hypertension.
  5. Deliver once stable, whatever the gestation: usually caesarean. No spinal / epidural if platelets < 80 or clotting deranged.
  6. After birth: supportive care; watch for PPH, hypoglycaemia, kidney failure. Most recover within days, with no lasting liver damage. Rarely: liver transplant.
Recurrence

Can recur in a later pregnancy, mainly if the couple carry an LCHAD gene. Test parents and baby.

Best next step

Ill, jaundiced, vomiting at 36 wk: check glucose, INR, platelets, BP first.
