18. Position: sutures and fontanelles

Run a finger along the sagittal suture to a fontanelle. The posterior fontanelle (small, Y-shaped, 3 sutures) marks the occiput: where it points is the position. Feeling the anterior fontanelle (large, diamond, 4 sutures) easily means the head is deflexed (OF 11.5 cm presents, against SOB 9.5 cm when flexed).

POSITIONS · Green = favourable (OA) · blue-green = transverse, rotation still to come · orange = posterior: longer labour, more instruments.

Rotation still needed to reach OA: LOA 1/8 · LOT 2/8 · LOP 3/8 (long rotation). Most OT and OP heads turn to OA as labour goes on.
