10Drugs in pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Sertraline: SSRI of choice after birth
  • Paroxetine: fine in breastfeeding, avoid in early pregnancy (heart defects)
  • Fluoxetine: long half-life, not first choice
  • Lithium: Ebstein anomaly; high in milk → bottle-feed
  • Valproate: avoid in women who may conceive (neural tube defects, low IQ)
11Prevention and recurrence
  • Ask every woman at booking about past depression, psychosis, bipolar, family history
  • Bipolar: switch valproate before pregnancy, shared plan, prophylaxis right after birth
  • Recurrence: PND about 25% next birth
  • Psychosis about 50%
  • Bipolar with past or family puerperal psychosis about 60%
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Postnatal depression last time → About 1 in 4 recurrence
6.4Maternal mortality
Maternal death = death of a woman while pregnant or within 42 days of the end of pregnancy, from any cause related to or aggravated by the pregnancy or its management, but not from accidental or incidental causes.
1Classification
  • Direct: obstetric complications or their treatment (PPH, eclampsia, sepsis, VTE, ectopic, AFE, anaesthesia)
  • Indirect: disease made worse by pregnancy (heart disease, anaemia, malaria, HIV)
  • Late: 42 days to 1 year
  • Coincidental: accident, violence; not counted
  • Pregnancy-related death: any cause to day 42
