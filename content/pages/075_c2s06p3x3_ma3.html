11Prevention
IPTp with SP · treated bed net

SP 3 tabs 500/25 mg observed at ANC: first dose as early as possible in the 2nd trimester (from 13 wk), then at every scheduled visit ≥ 4 wk apart, at least 3 doses (WHO); high-transmission areas · treated net nightly from the first visit · indoor spraying · iron, folate.

Local practice

Many local clinics still give two doses: the first after quickening (16–20 weeks), the second at least 4 weeks later.

Exam trap

No SP in the 1st trimester or with cotrimoxazole; folic acid 0.4 mg, not 5 mg. No primaquine (fetal G6PD haemolysis).

Clinical scenario
Confused, sweating, on IV quinine. First test?
RBG: quinine releases insulin.
