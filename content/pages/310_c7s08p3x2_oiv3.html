10. Examiner marking points
  • Gives a clear indication
  • Lists all FORCEPS checks
  • Head ≤ 1/5, station ≥ spines
  • Knows the exact position
  • Consent: risks, alternative = CS
  • Theatre back-up for mid-cavity
  • Cup centre on the flexion point
  • Checks no maternal tissue trapped
  • Pulls only with contractions
  • Direction: along the pelvic axis
  • Knows the stop rules
  • Names the 3 forceps and their use
  • Left blade first, left hand
  • Locks without force
  • Three checks of application
  • Traction in the J-curve
  • Compares with ventouse
