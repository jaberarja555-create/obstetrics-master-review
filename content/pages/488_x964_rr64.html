⚠️ Exam trap
  • Number of boxes = frequency, shading = duration; fewer than 3 in 10 min or under 40 s is inefficient
2Reading a CTG (DR C BRAVADO)
  • Contractions 3–5 in 10 min; over 5 = tachysystole
  • Baseline 110–160 bpm
  • Variability 5–25 bpm
  • Accelerations (≥ 15 bpm for 15 s) = healthy fetus
  • Decelerations: early (head compression), late (placental hypoxia), variable (cord), prolonged over 3 min
  • CTG is sensitive, not specific: in low-risk women it only raises caesareans
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Dip after the peak of every contraction, pre-eclampsia → Late decelerations: placental insufficiency
  • Flat trace 30 min, then accelerations return → Fetal sleep: normal
3CTG classification
  • Suspicious: baseline 100–109 or 161–180, variability under 5 for 30–50 min, uncomplicated variable decelerations
  • Pathological: baseline under 100 or over 180, variability under 5 for over 50 min, sinusoidal, repeated late decelerations, one over 3 min
  • Normal = all normal; suspicious = one suspicious; pathological = one pathological or two suspicious
  • FHR over 160: fever, chorioamnionitis, hypoxia
  • Under 100: cord prolapse, abruption, rupture, epidural hypotension
  • Sinusoidal: severe fetal anaemia
🩺 Exam scenario
  • FHR 175, mother 38.6 °C, ROM 30 h → Chorioamnionitis
