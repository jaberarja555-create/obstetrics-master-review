9Management of severe malaria & complications
  1. Call for help, HDU. Left lateral, airway, O₂, IV access.
  2. RBG at once (treat low first), film, Hb, creatinine · IV artesunate now.
  3. Fits: diazepam, or MgSO₄ if eclampsia is possible.
  4. Fluids with care: catheter, hourly urine, chest crepitations.

10Intrapartum & postpartum
  • Paracetamol, cooling · continuous CTG · CS for obstetric reasons only
  • Shorten 2nd stage if very sick · active 3rd stage (PPH) · check Hb, RBG
  • Placenta to histology · blood film on the newborn
11Prevention
IPTp with SP · treated bed net

SP 3 tabs 500/25 mg observed at ANC: first dose as early as possible in the 2nd trimester (from 13 wk), then at every scheduled visit ≥ 4 wk apart, at least 3 doses (WHO); high-transmission areas · treated net nightly from the first visit · indoor spraying · iron, folate.

Local practice

Many local clinics still give two doses: the first after quickening (16–20 weeks), the second at least 4 weeks later.
