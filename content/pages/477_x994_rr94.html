7.2Focused history stations
Headache, flashing lights and BP 150/100 at 34 weeks → Severe pre-eclampsia
Shoulder-tip pain and fainting at 7 weeks → Ruptured ectopic
Painless bleeding with a previous CS → Placenta praevia
Frame
Personal details · presenting complaint · history of pregnancy · past obstetric · gynaecological · medical, surgical · drugs, allergy · family · social
Dating
LMP, cycle, EDD (Naegele: +7 d −3 mo +1 yr) · early scan (CRL)
Obstetric terms
Gravida (pregnancies) · para (births ≥ 24 wk) · twins count as one · grand multipara ≥ 5
Past pregnancies
Mode, previous CS indication and type · PPH, PE, preterm birth, stillbirth, macrosomia (check glucose)
Clinical pictures
Vesicles + severe vomiting: mole · pain, hard uterus, dark blood: abruption · bleed at ROM, bradycardia: vasa praevia · pica: iron deficiency
Local pictures
Haematuria, canal water: schistosomiasis · fever, rigors, no bed net: malaria
Exam traps
High BP at 10 weeks = chronic hypertension · wet on coughing without pooling = stress incontinence · present a long history in 2–3 min
7.3Obstetric abdominal examination
Fundus at the umbilicus → About 20 weeks
Hard round ballotable mass in the fundus → Breech
Fundus and pelvis empty → Transverse lie
Preparation
Empty bladder (adds ≈ 3 cm) · lying with left tilt 15° after 20 wk · expose from xiphisternum to symphysis · general: BP, pulse, oedema, pallor
Inspection
Size, shape · scars (Pfannenstiel, midline) · striae, linea nigra · fetal movements
Fundal height
12 wk symphysis · 20 wk umbilicus · 36 wk xiphisternum · SFH = weeks ± 2 cm from 24 wk
Leopold grips
Fundal · lateral (back) · Pawlik (one hand above the pubis) · pelvic (facing her feet)
Engagement
Head in fifths: ≤ 2/5 palpable = engaged · fingers converge = not engaged
Auscultation
Pinard/Doppler over the anterior shoulder · 110–160/min, count 1 min · FH above umbilicus: breech
Exam traps
Tense uterus with a fluid thrill = polyhydramnios · SFH > 2 cm off dates → scan · thank and cover the patient
