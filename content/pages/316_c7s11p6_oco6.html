Exam trap

Phenytoin and phenobarbitone are not the drugs to start in pregnancy (hydantoin syndrome, neonatal drowsiness). Preferred: lamotrigine or levetiracetam; avoid valproate. Enzyme inducers make the pill, POP and implant fail: copper IUD, LNG-IUS or DMPA.

What to say · if she fears the drug
"I understand you worry about the medicine. A bad fit is more dangerous for you and the baby than the safer medicine we will choose together."
«أنا فاهم إنك خايفة من الدواء. لكن النوبة الشديدة أخطر عليك وعلى الجنا من الدواء الأسلم البنختاره سوا.»

Viva questions
Why give the baby vitamin K?
Enzyme inducers lower the baby's vitamin K: bleeding risk.
Which opioid to avoid in labour?
Pethidine: its metabolite causes fits.
