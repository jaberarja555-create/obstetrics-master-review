14. Explaining a 24-hour urine collection
What to say
“Tomorrow at 7 in the morning pass urine into the toilet and throw it away. From then, collect every drop in this bottle, day and night, and keep it in a cool place. At 7 the next morning pass urine one last time into the bottle and bring it to us.”
«بكرة الساعة سبعة الصباح، أول بول نزّليه في الحمام وما تجمعيه. من بعده اجمعي كل البول في القزازة دي، بالنهار وبالليل، وخليها في مكان بارد. تاني يوم الساعة سبعة الصباح بولي آخر مرة في القزازة وجيبيها لينا.»
  1. Discard the first morning sample, keep the last one: exactly 24 h of urine.
  2. A missed sample spoils the test: start again. Write the start and end times on the bottle.
  3. Use the lab bottle (it may hold a preservative: do not pour it out); the lab records the total volume and protein in grams.
Viva questions
Why discard the first sample in a 24 h collection?
It was made before the start time; it would add extra hours.
Which is faster than 24 h urine to quantify protein?
Spot protein:creatinine ratio (PCR).
15. Common errors
Marks commonly lost

No gloves · strip read at one time for all pads · touching the pads · bottle left open · not checking expiry · reporting only protein · calling 1+ "significant proteinuria" without PCR · forgetting to link the result to the BP.
