3.5Fetal growth restriction
Fetal growth restriction is a fetus that fails to reach its growth potential from a pathological cause. SGA (weight below the 10th centile) is a size, not a disease: about two thirds of SGA babies are constitutionally small. FGR raises perinatal death 6–10 times.
1Definitions
  • SGA: below the 10th centile for gestation
  • FGR: pathological failure to grow
  • Low birth weight: under 2.5 kg at any gestation (two thirds preterm, one third SGA)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • EFW 8th centile, normal Doppler, small parents → Constitutional SGA
