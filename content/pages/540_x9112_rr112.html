5Vaginal examination in labour
  • Every 4 h in the 1st stage (2 h on oxytocin)
  • Not before the placenta is located in APH
  • Two fingers along the back wall
  • Dilatation, effacement, consistency, position, application
  • Membranes and liquor (clear, meconium, blood)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • APH, placental site unknown → No VE: scan first
⚠️ Exam trap
  • No cervix felt around the head = fully dilated
6Bishop score
  • Dilatation, length, station, consistency, position
  • ≥ 8 favourable
  • ≤ 6: ripen the cervix first
  • Maximum 13
7Station, position and moulding
  • Station 0 = level of the ischial spines
  • Posterior fontanelle: Y-shaped, 3 sutures
  • Anterior: diamond, 4 sutures; easily felt = deflexed
  • Moulding +3 (not reducible) = obstruction
  • Caput is soft swelling
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Y-shaped fontanelle felt anteriorly → Occipito-anterior
  • Diamond fontanelle felt anteriorly → Occipito-posterior, deflexed
