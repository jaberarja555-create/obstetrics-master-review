Exam trap

Ergometrine clamps veins: blood rushes to the heart → pulmonary oedema. A fast oxytocin bolus drops BP. Carboprost: bronchospasm, pulmonary hypertension. For PPH: oxytocin slowly, misoprostol.

Presenting the case

"I explained to Zainab that we aim for spontaneous labour and a vaginal birth in hospital, with early epidural, a short second stage and slow oxytocin. LMWH stops when labour starts and restarts after birth, warfarin from day 5. She will stay in HDU for at least 3 days, can breastfeed, and will use a progestogen-only method."

Viva questions
Why is the heart most at risk immediately after birth?
Autotransfusion from the emptied uterus: sudden volume load.
Can she breastfeed on warfarin?
Yes: warfarin is safe in breastfeeding.
