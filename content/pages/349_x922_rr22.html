7.5Speculum & vaginal examination
Suspected ruptured membranes → Sterile speculum only, no digital VE
APH and the placental site unknown → No VE: scan first
Y-shaped fontanelle felt anteriorly in labour → Occipito-posterior position
Speculum
Cusco (bivalve, self-retaining) · Sims (VVF, prolapse) · sterile water, no gel in ROM · rotate 90° and open
ROM tests
Pool in posterior fornix · cough · nitrazine blue (pH 7–7.5) · fern on slide
Swabs
High vaginal (fornix) · endocervical for chlamydia/gonorrhoea
Digital VE
Dilatation, effacement, consistency, position · station (0 = spines) · presenting part, position (fontanelles, sutures) · moulding, caput · liquor
Head position
Posterior fontanelle Y-shaped, 3 sutures · anterior diamond (4 sutures) easily felt = deflexed · moulding +3 not reducible
Bishop
≥ 8 favourable · ≤ 6 ripen · max 13
Exam traps
Every 4 h in the 1st stage (2 h on oxytocin) · no cervix felt around the head = fully dilated · always use a chaperone
7.6Partogram & CTG stations
Cervical dilatation crosses the alert line → Assess cause, ARM + oxytocin if needed
Good contractions, no descent, moulding +3 → Obstructed labour: CS
FHR below 100 for more than 3 minutes → Pathological CTG: act now
Partogram
Opened at 4 cm · alert line 1 cm/h · action line 4 h later · cervix X, descent O (fifths)
Recording
FHR, pulse, contractions every 30 min · VE every 4 h (2 h on oxytocin) · liquor C/M/B · moulding · BP, urine
Contractions
Boxes per 10 min · dotted < 20 s, hatched 20–40 s, solid > 40 s · target 3–4/10 min
CTG reading (DR C BRAVADO)
Risk · contractions · baseline 110–160 · variability 5–25 · accelerations (15 bpm × 15 s) · decelerations · overall
Decelerations
Early: head · late: placenta · variable: cord · prolonged > 3 min
Augmentation
Oxytocin 5 IU in 500 mL, start 2 mU/min (4 drops/min), increase every 30 min
Exam traps
Head ≥ 3/5 palpable at full dilatation = CPD · reduced variability > 50 min is pathological · catheter 14 days after obstructed labour
