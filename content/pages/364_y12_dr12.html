Rx Obstetric DrugsObstetric Drugs364
Blood pressure, heart
Safe
  • Labetalol, nifedipine
  • Enalapril (after birth)
  • Methyldopa (may cause depression)
Avoid or use with caution
  • Atenolol (slow heart rate in the baby)
  • High-dose diuretics (reduce milk)
Clot prevention
Safe
  • Low-molecular-weight heparin, heparin
  • Warfarin
Avoid or use with caution
  • Direct oral anticoagulants (rivaroxaban, apixaban)
Diabetes, thyroid
Safe
  • Insulin, metformin
  • Levothyroxine
  • Propylthiouracil up to 150 mg/day, carbimazole up to 15 mg/day
Avoid or use with caution
  • Radioactive iodine
Mental health, epilepsy
Safe
  • Sertraline, olanzapine
  • Lamotrigine, levetiracetam, carbamazepine
Avoid or use with caution
  • Lithium (check the baby’s levels)
  • High-dose benzodiazepines
Contraception
Safe
  • Progestogen-only methods: pill, implant, injection (DMPA), hormonal coil
Avoid or use with caution
  • Combined pill in the first 6 months (reduces milk)
  • Cabergoline, bromocriptine (stop milk)
Other drugs
Safe
  • Antimalarials: artemether-lumefantrine, quinine, artesunate
  • Antiretroviral therapy for HIV
  • Iron, folic acid
Avoid or use with caution
  • Methotrexate and anticancer drugs
  • Amiodarone
