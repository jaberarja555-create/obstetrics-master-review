Chapter 3 · 3.1 Bleeding in Early PregnancyMolar Pregnancy (GTD)111
11Clinical features

Amenorrhoea 6–8 wk + lower abdominal pain + dark spotting · shoulder-tip pain, fainting (blood in the abdomen) · cervical excitation (pain when the cervix is moved), adnexal mass. Fate: tubal abortion (expelled from the tube end) or rupture.

12Investigations

TVS: empty uterus, adnexal mass, free fluid (an intrauterine sac should be seen once hCG is above 2000 IU/L) · β-hCG twice, 48 h apart: in a normal intrauterine pregnancy it rises by ≥ 35% (older teaching: 63%, which misses some normal pregnancies); in an ectopic it usually rises less than this, stays flat (plateau) or falls slowly · laparoscopy if still unclear. DDx: miscarriage, ovarian cyst accident, PID, appendicitis.
