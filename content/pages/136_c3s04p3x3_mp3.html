Locked twins (rare)

Twin 1 breech, twin 2 cephalic: chins lock, twin 1's head is trapped. Prevent: caesarean when twin 1 is breech.

Third stage: PPH

Active management + oxytocin infusion for 2–4 h after birth. Uterus overstretched: watch closely.

Distinguishing features
Twin 1 cephalic, twin 2 breechVaginal birth
Twin 1 breechElective caesarean
Twin 2 transverse, sac intactExternal cephalic version
Twin 2 transverse, sac rupturedInternal podalic version
Clinical scenario
Twin 1 delivered. Twin 2 longitudinal, cephalic, membranes intact, contractions weak. Next?
Oxytocin, then ARM once the head enters the pelvis.
