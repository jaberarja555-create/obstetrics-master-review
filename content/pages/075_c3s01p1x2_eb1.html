4. Investigations

Speculum: os open or closed, local cause · CBC · blood group + Rh · urine · malaria film · TORCH in 1st-trimester loss only. Missed on TVS: CRL ≥ 7 mm, no heart; or mean sac ≥ 25 mm, no embryo.

5. Management by type
ThreatenedReassure · no admission if she lives near a hospital · bed rest and progesterone are unproven · no intercourse until the bleeding stops · later in pregnancy she has a higher risk of APH and preterm labour
CompleteConfirm the uterus is empty on scan · pregnancy test should be negative after 2–3 wk
Inevitable, incomplete, missed: three options
ExpectantWait 2–4 wk for the products to pass · works best < 8 wk · missed: wait no longer than 4 wk (a dead fetus kept longer can cause DIC) · she may bleed or get infected while waiting
MedicalMisoprostol (table below) · preferred from 12 wk · may fail; side effects: cramps, diarrhoea, fever
SurgicalMVA up to 12–14 wk, or D&E (dilatation and evacuation) later · definitive · the choice for heavy bleeding, shock or sepsis

6. Misoprostol doses
UseDose
Incomplete, < 13 wk600 µg oral or 400 µg SL once
Missed, < 13 wk800 µg PV or 600 µg SL · repeat after 3 h if needed
13–24 wk (missed, IUFD)400 µg SL/PV every 3 h until passed
Priming before evacuation400 µg PV 3 h before: softens and opens the cervix, so dilatation is easier and the cervix tears less
