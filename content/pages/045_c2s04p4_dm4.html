14. Intrapartum care

Keep glucose 4–7 mmol/L. CTG · glucose hourly · CS first on the list. Type 1: insulin + dextrose from established labour. Infusion: soluble insulin 50 U in 50 mL saline by pump, 0.5–2 U/h by glucose, + 5–10% dextrose in a second line. Never insulin without dextrose.

Hospital protocol

Vials + syringes: teach drawing up and storage (fridge or clay-pot cooler; open vial ~4 wk). No pump in labour: add insulin to the dextrose bag, about 1 U per 5 g dextrose, glucose hourly.

15. Postnatal care
  • Baby: paediatrician at birth. Feed within 30–60 min, then 2–3-hourly. Heel-prick glucose from 2 h (aim > 2.6).
  • GDM: stop all drugs. Fasting glucose or OGTT at 6 wk, then yearly for life.
  • Type 1: pre-pregnancy dose or lower · breastfeed · progestogen-only or IUCD.
Exam trap · DKA

Occurs at lower glucose in pregnancy. Ketones, pH < 7.3, HCO₃⁻ < 15. Saline 1 L in 1st hour · insulin 0.1 U/kg/h (dextrose once glucose < 14) · K⁺ 20–40 mmol/L once < 5.5 · treat cause. Fetal distress: stabilise mother first, then decide on delivery.
