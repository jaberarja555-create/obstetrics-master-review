4Thyroid storm
  • Poor control + trigger (labour, CS, infection)
  • Fever, confusion, tachycardia or AF, heart failure
  • HDU: fluids, cooling, propranolol, PTU, iodine 1 h after PTU, IV hydrocortisone; treat the trigger
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Fever, AF and confusion after CS → Thyroid storm
5Hypothyroidism
  • About 1%; Hashimoto's commonest; also thyroidectomy, radioiodine, iodine deficiency
  • High TSH, low free T4
  • Risks: miscarriage, pre-eclampsia, low birth weight, stillbirth, lower IQ in the child
  • Raise levothyroxine by 25–30% as soon as pregnancy is confirmed
  • TSH each trimester and 6 wk after any change; target under 2.5 in the first trimester, under 3 later
  • Back to the pre-pregnancy dose after birth
🩺 Exam scenario
  • On thyroxine, TSH rising, takes it with ferrous sulphate → Take iron 4 h apart
6Postpartum thyroiditis
  • Autoimmune; onset usually 3–4 months after birth
  • Hyper phase 1–4 months, hypo phase 4–8 months, most recover by 1 year
  • Hyper phase: beta-blocker, not antithyroid drugs
  • Hypo phase: thyroxine, stop at 6 months to test recovery
  • Recurs in 70%; yearly TFT
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Palpitations 3 months after birth, painless goitre → Thyroiditis: propranolol
