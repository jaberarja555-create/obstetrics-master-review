12. Magnesium sulphate
Prevents and treats eclamptic fits

Loading 4 g IV over 5–15 min → maintenance 1 g/h for 24 h after delivery or the last fit.

Toxicity in order: loss of knee jerk → respiratory depression (RR < 12) → cardiac arrest. Kidneys clear it: keep urine ≥ 30 mL/h.

Antidote: 10 mL of 10% calcium gluconate IV slowly. Fit on MgSO₄: extra 2 g IV; still fitting: phenytoin, then general anaesthesia.

Exam trap

Restrict fluids to 80 mL/h. Leaky capillaries flood the lungs: drips cause pulmonary oedema.
