Presenting the case

"Amal, 34, G4 P3 at 26 weeks, fasting 6.0 and 2 h 8.4: both above the cut-off (fasting ≥ 5.6, 2 h ≥ 7.8 mmol/L). No diabetes before. Her last baby weighed 4.6 kg and her mother is diabetic; BMI 33. No thirst or weight loss. Impression: gestational diabetes. Plan: diet and glucose profile, metformin if targets are missed, growth scans every 4 weeks."

Common errors

Not asking if diabetes was known before · forgetting the previous baby's weight · calling it "high sugar" and leaving her scared.

Viva questions
Why do anomalies occur with pre-existing DM but not GDM?
Organs form at 3–8 wk; GDM starts after 20 wk.
Commonest neonatal problem in a diabetic's baby?
Hypoglycaemia.
