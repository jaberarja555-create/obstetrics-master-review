7.3Obstetric abdominal examination
Fundus at the umbilicus → About 20 weeks
Hard round ballotable mass in the fundus → Breech
Fundus and pelvis empty → Transverse lie
Preparation
Empty bladder (adds ≈ 3 cm) · lying with left tilt 15° after 20 wk · expose from xiphisternum to symphysis · general: BP, pulse, oedema, pallor
Inspection
Size, shape · scars (Pfannenstiel, midline) · striae, linea nigra · fetal movements
Fundal height
12 wk symphysis · 20 wk umbilicus · 36 wk xiphisternum · SFH = weeks ± 2 cm from 24 wk
Leopold grips
Fundal · lateral (back) · Pawlik (one hand above the pubis) · pelvic (facing her feet)
Engagement
Head in fifths: ≤ 2/5 palpable = engaged · fingers converge = not engaged
Auscultation
Pinard/Doppler over the anterior shoulder · 110–160/min, count 1 min · FH above umbilicus: breech
Exam traps
Tense uterus with a fluid thrill = polyhydramnios · SFH > 2 cm off dates → scan · thank and cover the patient
7.4Blood pressure & urine testing
Small cuff on a large arm → Falsely high reading
Readings 145/95 twice, 4 hours apart at 30 weeks → Hypertension: test urine for protein
Urine: nitrites and leucocytes positive → UTI: send MSU for culture
BP technique
Rest 5 min, sitting · cuff bladder covers ≥ 80% of arm (large cuff if arm > 33 cm) · arm at heart level
Korotkoff
Inflate 30 mmHg above palpated systolic · deflate 2 mmHg/s · systolic K1 · diastolic K5 (K4 if sounds go to zero)
Thresholds
Hypertension ≥ 140/90 twice · severe ≥ 160/110 (treat now, re-check in 15 min)
Urine dipstick
Midstream, dip ≈ 1 s · protein 1+ ≈ 0.3 g/L · glucose 2+ → OGTT · ketones → hyperemesis
Proteinuria
Significant: PCR ≥ 30 mg/mmol or ≥ 0.3 g/24 h · severe > 5 g/24 h
Bacteriuria
2–10% of pregnant women · always treat
Exam traps
Record to the nearest 2 mmHg · never round to 0 or 5 · read each pad at its own time
