7.11Counselling stations
Counselling stations: a simulated patient needs information and a decision. Use one frame for all of them and check her understanding.
1The counselling frame
  • Introduce
  • Establish what she knows
  • Facts in 2–3 chunks without jargon
  • Options
  • ICE
  • Check understanding (teach-back)
  • Plan
  • Leaflets and follow-up
2Intrauterine fetal death
  • Break the news (SPIKES)
  • Labour starts within 3 wk in 85%
  • DIC in about 30% after 3–4 wk
  • Induction preferred: mifepristone 200 mg, then misoprostol 36–48 h later
  • CS only for a usual obstetric reason
  • Seeing and holding the baby, her choice
  • Cabergoline 1 mg
  • Anti-D if Rh-negative
  • Investigations
  • Review at 6 wk
🩺 Exam scenario
  • IUFD at 34 wk, well → Induction with mifepristone then misoprostol
3Heart disease
  • Cardiac output rises about 40%
  • Joint clinic
  • Fetal echo at 20–22 wk
  • Warfarin: LMWH in weeks 6–12 (and from 36 wk)
