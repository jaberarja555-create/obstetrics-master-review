4.3Preterm labour
Contractions at 30 weeks, cervix 2 cm → Preterm labour: steroids, nifedipine, MgSO₄
Symptomatic woman, cervical length 12 mm → High risk of birth within 7 days
Previous preterm birth, cervix 20 mm at 20 weeks → Cerclage or vaginal progesterone
Definition
Labour from 24 to < 37 wk · threatened: contractions without cervical change
Causes
Infection (commonest proven) · previous preterm birth (strongest risk) · twins, polyhydramnios · cervical weakness · APH
Prediction
Fetal fibronectin negative → birth unlikely in 7–14 d · cervix ≤ 15 mm symptomatic
Steroids
24–34 wk: dexamethasone 6 mg × 4 (12-hourly) or betamethasone 12 mg × 2 (24 h)
Tocolysis
Nifedipine first line, only 48 h (time for steroids) · indomethacin < 32 wk · MgSO₄ < 32 wk for neuroprotection
Delivery
Below 34 wk no ventouse (forceps if needed) · history-indicated cerclage 12–14 wk, removed at 37 wk
Exam traps
Tocolysis gives 48 hours, not term · indomethacin after 32 wk closes the ductus · RDS is the commonest neonatal problem
4.4Prelabour rupture of membranes
Gush of fluid at 32 weeks, pool in posterior fornix → PPROM: erythromycin, steroids, no VE
Term PROM, no labour after 24 hours → Induce
Fever, tender uterus, offensive liquor, FHR 170 → Chorioamnionitis: antibiotics and deliver
Definitions
PROM: rupture before labour ≥ 37 wk · PPROM < 37 wk · prolonged > 24 h
Diagnosis
Sterile speculum: pooling · nitrazine (blue, pH 7–7.5) · ferning · no digital VE
PPROM treatment
Erythromycin 250 mg 6-hourly for 10 days · steroids < 34 wk · no tocolysis · deliver at 37 wk
Monitoring
Temperature and pulse 4-hourly · CBC, CRP 2–3 times a week · CTG
Chorioamnionitis
Fever > 37.8 °C, tachycardia, tender uterus, fetal tachycardia · ampicillin + gentamicin · deliver at any gestation
Complications
Preterm birth, cord prolapse, infection, pulmonary hypoplasia (< 24 wk)
Exam traps
Co-amoxiclav in PPROM causes NEC · digital VE raises infection risk · term PROM: 75% labour within 24 h
