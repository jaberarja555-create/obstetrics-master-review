6.3Postpartum mood disorders
Day 4, tearful, copes with the baby → Baby blues: reassure
Low mood for 4 weeks, EPDS 15 → Postnatal depression: sertraline, psychology
Day 5, confused, delusions about the baby → Puerperal psychosis: admit today
Baby blues
50–80% · peak day 4–5 · settles within 2 wk · still present at 2 wk → think depression
Postnatal depression
10–15% · within the first year · EPDS ≥ 13 (question 10 = self-harm) · sertraline 50 mg (safe in breastfeeding)
Puerperal psychosis
1–2 per 1000 · onset in 2 wk · risk: bipolar disorder, previous psychosis (50% recurrence)
Psychosis treatment
Mother and baby unit / psychiatric admission · antipsychotics · ECT if severe
Risks
Suicide, infanticide · PND recurrence ≈ 25%
Drugs
Avoid fluoxetine in newborn breastfeeding · lithium: bottle-feed
Exam traps
Psychosis is a psychiatric emergency · ask EPDS question 10 the same day · screen at 6 wk
6.4Maternal mortality
Death from PPH 2 days after birth → Direct maternal death
Death from malaria at 30 weeks → Indirect maternal death
Woman who survived eclampsia with ICU care → Maternal near miss
Definitions
Death in pregnancy or within 42 days of its end · late: 42 d to 1 year · coincidental: accident
Direct vs indirect
Direct: obstetric complications (PPH, PE, sepsis, ectopic) · indirect: disease worsened by pregnancy (malaria, heart disease, anaemia)
Measures
MMR per 100 000 live births · maternal mortality rate per women 15–49 · lifetime risk
Figures (2023)
Sudan MMR 256 · world 197 · sub-Saharan Africa 448 · SDG 3.1: < 70 by 2030
Causes
World: haemorrhage · Sudan: puerperal sepsis, PPH, PE · UK: VTE
Three delays
1: deciding to seek care · 2: reaching care · 3: receiving care · comprehensive EmONC adds CS and transfusion
Exam traps
A road accident is coincidental, not counted · MMR denominator is live births, not women · skilled attendance is the key intervention
