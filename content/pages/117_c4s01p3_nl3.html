10. Mechanism of labour: cardinal movements
Mnemonic · The cardinal movements of labour, in order
EngagementDescentFlexionInternal rotationExtensionRestitutionExternal rotationExpulsion

Each movement follows the one before it: the head engages and descends, flexes, rotates to bring the occiput anterior, extends to be born, then restitutes and rotates externally so that the shoulders can deliver.

Distinguishing features
Head 5/5 palpable, primi, 39 wkNot engaged: CPD? praevia?
Swelling crosses sutures, gone in 2 daysCaput succedaneum
Head untwists after it is bornRestitution
Occiput turns under symphysisInternal rotation
