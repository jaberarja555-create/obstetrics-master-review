8. What a past finding changes now
Previous CSScan placenta site (praevia, accreta); plan VBAC or repeat CS; deliver in hospital
Previous PPHA major PPH (≥ 1000 mL) tends to recur:Hb before birth, IV access and group-and-save in labour, active third stage, oxytocin infusion
Previous pre-eclampsiaAspirin 75 mg 12 → 36 wk, calcium; BP and urine each visit
Previous GDM or macrosomiaOGTT (16 wk if past GDM, then 24–28 wk)
Previous stillbirth, FGRGrowth scans, early delivery plan, look for the cause (diabetes, APS, syphilis)
Previous preterm birthTreat infections, cervical assessment, steroids if preterm labour starts
