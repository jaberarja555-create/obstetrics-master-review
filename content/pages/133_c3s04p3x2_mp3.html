Uncomplicated DCDA twins are often delivered at 38 weeks and MCDA twins at 37 weeks. Timing above follows NICE NG137.

Twin 1 cephalic

Aim for vaginal birth, whatever twin 2's presentation.

Caesarean if

Twin 1 non-cephalic · MCMA · triplets · usual obstetric reasons · retained twin 2.

Locked twins (rare)

Twin 1 breech, twin 2 cephalic: chins lock, twin 1's head is trapped. Prevent: caesarean when twin 1 is breech.

Third stage: PPH

Active management + oxytocin infusion for 2–4 h after birth. Uterus overstretched: watch closely.
