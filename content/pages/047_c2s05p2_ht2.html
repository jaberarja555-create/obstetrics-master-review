6. Investigations
  • Urine: dipstick → 24 h protein or PCR
  • FBC: platelets, haematocrit
  • RFT: creatinine, uric acid
  • LFT, LDH, film · clotting if platelets low
  • Fetus: growth scan, liquor, umbilical artery Doppler, CTG
UMBILICAL ARTERY DOPPLER · As the placenta fails, diastolic flow falls, disappears, then reverses. Absent or reversed EDF = deliver soon.
Clinical scenario
PE at 32 wk, umbilical Doppler shows reversed EDF. Plan?
Steroids, MgSO₄, deliver (usually caesarean).
7. Differential diagnosis of fits in pregnancy

Eclampsia (HTN + proteinuria, > 20 wk or ≤ 48 h after birth) · epilepsy · hypoglycaemia · meningitis · cerebral venous thrombosis · cerebral malaria.

Hospital protocol

Fit + fever + normal BP = cerebral malaria until proven otherwise. Test glucose and malaria in every fit; unsure? Give MgSO₄ + IV artesunate.

8. Complications
MaternalEclampsia · intracranial haemorrhage (leading cause of death) · HELLP · DIC · acute kidney injury · pulmonary oedema · abruption · PPH
FetalGrowth restriction · oligohydramnios · prematurity (mostly iatrogenic) · fetal distress · stillbirth
