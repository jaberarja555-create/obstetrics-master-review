2.4Diabetes in pregnancy
OGTT at 26 weeks: fasting 5.8, 2 h 8.4 mmol/L → Gestational diabetes
Fasting 7.4 mmol/L at booking → Overt diabetes: insulin
Baby of a diabetic mother is jittery at 2 hours → Neonatal hypoglycaemia
Diagnosis
75 g OGTT at 24–28 wk (16 wk if previous GDM) · GDM: fasting ≥ 5.6, 2 h ≥ 7.8 · overt: fasting ≥ 7.0, 2 h ≥ 11.1
Fetal risks
Cardiac anomaly commonest · caudal regression most specific · macrosomia, shoulder dystocia · stillbirth after 36 wk · neonatal hypoglycaemia, RDS
Treatment
Diet → metformin → insulin · fasting ≥ 7 at diagnosis → insulin first · targets: fasting < 5.3, 1 h < 7.8
Monitoring
Anomaly scan + fetal echo 18–20 wk · growth scans every 4 wk · glucose in labour 4–7 mmol/L (insulin 0.5–2 U/h)
Delivery
Pre-existing: 38–39⁺⁶ wk · EFW > 4.5 kg: offer CS
After birth
GDM: stop drugs, OGTT at 6 wk · type 2 in 50% within 10 yr · pre-existing: HbA1c < 6.5% before conception
Exam traps
Insulin needs fall suddenly after the placenta is delivered · HbA1c > 10% → delay pregnancy · DKA in pregnancy: insulin 0.1 U/kg/h
2.5Hypertensive disorders of pregnancy
BP 150/100 at 34 weeks, proteinuria 2+ → Pre-eclampsia
BP 170/115, epigastric pain, platelets 60 → HELLP: stabilise and deliver
Tonic-clonic fit at 36 weeks with hypertension → Eclampsia: MgSO₄
Classification
Chronic (before 20 wk or > 12 wk after birth) · gestational (≥ 140/90 after 20 wk) · pre-eclampsia (+ proteinuria ≥ 300 mg/24 h or PCR ≥ 30) · eclampsia · HELLP
Severe features
BP ≥ 160/110 · headache, visual flashes · epigastric pain · platelets < 100 · oliguria < 30 mL/h · rising urate
Antihypertensives
Oral labetalol (also nifedipine, methyldopa) · severe: IV hydralazine 5 mg or labetalol · never ACE inhibitor or ARB
MgSO₄
4 g IV loading → 1 g/h for 24 h after birth or last fit · toxicity: lost knee jerk first → RR < 12 → 10 mL 10% calcium gluconate
Delivery
Delivery is the cure · mild PE 37–38 wk · steroids < 34 wk · fluids ≤ 80 mL/h · oxytocin, no ergometrine
Prevention
Aspirin 75 mg 12 → 36 wk if high risk · postpartum BP peaks day 3–5
Exam traps
Molar pregnancy causes PE before 20 wk · leading cause of death is brain haemorrhage: control BP · MgSO₄ prevents fits, it does not lower BP
