7.3Obstetric abdominal examination
Obstetric abdominal examination on a patient or model: prepare, general look, inspection, fundal height, Leopold grips, engagement, liquor and the fetal heart, then present eight points.
1Equipment and preparation
  • Doppler or Pinard, gel, tape, gloves
  • Empty bladder (a full one adds about 3 cm)
  • Lying with a pillow under her right hip (left tilt 15°) after 20 wk
  • Expose from xiphisternum to symphysis
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Dizzy, pale and sweaty flat on her back → Supine hypotension: tilt her left
2General look and examination
  • Well or distressed
  • Pallor (eyelid, palms)
  • BP, pulse, temperature, urine
  • Pitting oedema, varicose veins
