6. Hypothyroidism

1 % of pregnancies. Causes: Hashimoto's (commonest), thyroidectomy, radioiodine, drugs (amiodarone, lithium); iodine deficiency. TFTs: high TSH + low free T4.

Risks: infertility, miscarriage, pre-eclampsia, low birth weight, stillbirth, and lower IQ in the child (early brain needs maternal T4).

Levothyroxine plan

Raise the dose by 25–30 % as soon as pregnancy is confirmed (e.g. 2 extra tablets a week). TSH before conception and each trimester; recheck 6 wk after any change. Target TSH < 2.5 in the 1st trimester, < 3 later. After birth: back to the pre-pregnancy dose. Safe in breastfeeding.

7. Postpartum thyroiditis
1–4 monthsHyper phase: preformed T4 leaks
4–8 monthsHypo phase: store used up
By 1 yearMost recover

Autoimmune, as immunity returns after pregnancy. Treat symptoms, not numbers: hyper phase → β-blocker (not antithyroid drugs); hypo phase → thyroxine, stop at 6 months to test recovery. Recurrence 70 %; annual TFT. DDx: Graves', Hashimoto's, Sheehan's.
