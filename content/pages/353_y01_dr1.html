Doses as used in this book (Sudan practice). OD once daily · BD twice · TDS three times · QDS four times · stat once now · PO by mouth · SL under tongue · PV vaginal · BOX = boxed (black-box) warning.
1Uterotonics: third stage, postpartum haemorrhage, induction
OxytocinOxytocin receptor → rhythmic contractions
Main use
AMTSL (all births) · PPH · induction / augmentation
Dose · route · how long
AMTSL 10 IU IM within 1 min of birth · PPH 10 IU IM/slow IV, then 40 IU in 500 mL over 4 h · IOL 5 IU in 500 mL saline, start 4 drops/min, double every 30 min to 3–4 contractions/10 min · cardiac disease 5 IU slow IV
Side effects · monitor
Hyperstimulation, fetal distress, rupture · hypotension if fast IV bolus · water intoxication (large doses in dextrose)
When · cautions
Labour, 3rd stage, postpartum · BOX not for elective induction without indication
ErgometrineErgot alkaloid → sustained tonic spasm + vasoconstriction
Main use
PPH (atony), 2nd line
Dose · route · how long
0.5 mg IM (or slow IV) · may repeat once after 15 min
Side effects · monitor
Vomiting, sharp BP rise, coronary spasm
When · cautions
After delivery only · Never: hypertension, pre-eclampsia, heart disease, undelivered twin
Misoprostol (PGE₁)Prostaglandin E₁ → contractions + cervical ripening
Main use
PPH prevention / treatment · IOL · miscarriage, IUFD · cervical priming
Dose · route · how long
PPH prevention 600 µg PO stat (no oxytocin) · PPH treatment 800 µg SL or rectal · IOL live term 25 µg PV 6-hourly or 25 µg PO 2-hourly · IUFD < 27 wk 200 µg 4–6-hourly, 27–28 wk 100 µg 4-hourly, > 28 wk 25 µg 6-hourly · missed miscarriage < 13 wk 800 µg PV · priming 400 µg PV
Side effects · monitor
Fever, shivering, diarrhoea · hyperstimulation, scar rupture
When · cautions
BOX abortifacient, teratogenic (Möbius) outside obstetric use · lower doses with previous CS · no fridge needed
Carboprost (15-methyl PGF₂α)PGF₂α → strong contraction
Main use
Refractory PPH (atony)
Dose · route · how long
250 µg deep IM every 15 min · max 8 doses (2 mg)
Side effects · monitor
Bronchospasm, diarrhoea, vomiting, fever
When · cautions
Never in asthma · BOX hospital use, exact dosing · keep in fridge
Tranexamic acidAntifibrinolytic: blocks plasminogen
Main use
PPH (any cause) · trauma
Dose · route · how long
1 g IV over 10 min within 3 h of birth · second 1 g after 30 min if still bleeding
Side effects · monitor
Nausea · no benefit after 3 h
When · cautions
Postpartum · start early with uterotonics
Dinoprostone (PGE₂)Cervical ripening
Main use
Induction of labour, live fetus
Dose · route · how long
3 mg tablet in posterior fornix · repeat after 6 h · max 2 doses
Side effects · monitor
Hyperstimulation, nausea
When · cautions
Term IOL · CTG before and after · start oxytocin ≥ 6 h later
