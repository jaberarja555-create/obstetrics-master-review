6Episiotomy
  • Only for a reason (not routine)
  • Mediolateral, 60° from the midline at crowning
  • Lidocaine 1% spread along the line (max 3 mg/kg)
  • Two fingers protect the head
  • Equals a 2nd-degree tear
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Head crowning, perineum about to tear badly → Mediolateral episiotomy
7Degrees of perineal tear
  • 1st: skin
  • 2nd: perineal muscle
  • 3a: < 50% of external sphincter
  • 3b: > 50%
  • 3c: internal sphincter
  • 4th: rectal mucosa
  • 3rd and 4th repaired in theatre
🩺 Exam scenario
  • More than 50% of the external sphincter torn → 3b: repair in theatre
8Repair
  • First stitch 1 cm above the apex
  • Vaginal lining continuous to the hymen
  • Muscle
  • Skin subcuticular
  • Rapid polyglactin 2-0
  • PR and swab count after
