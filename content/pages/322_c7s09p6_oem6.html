TimesHead delivered · help arrived · body delivered → head-to-body interval
Which shoulderAnterior shoulder left or right (tells the paediatrician which arm to check)
ManoeuvresEach one in order, its time, and who did it · traction used: routine axial only
BabyWeight, Apgar 1 and 5 min, cord gases, arm movement, clavicles
MotherBlood loss, perineum and cervix checked, tear degree, repair
TalkExplained to the parents · team debrief
What to say · to the parents afterwards
“After the head came out, the baby's shoulder got stuck behind the bone. We changed your position and pressed above the pubic bone, and the baby came out at 2 minutes. The children's doctor is checking him now, especially his arm.”
«بعد ما راس الجنا مرق، كتفه اتحجز ورا عضم العانة. غيّرنا وضعك وضغطنا فوق العانة، والجنا اتولد بعد دقيقتين. دكتور الأطفال بيكشف عليه هسي، خصوصاً على إيده.»
Presenting the case

"Diabetic para 2, 4.3 kg baby. Shoulder dystocia, right shoulder anterior. Help called at 10:02; McRoberts and suprapubic pressure; delivered at 10:04, interval 2 min. Apgar 6 and 9; right arm moving. EBL 600 mL, 2nd-degree tear repaired. Parents informed."
