5. Differential diagnosis

Urine (stress or overflow incontinence: acidic, smells of urine) · vaginal discharge (BV, candida) · show · liquor = alkaline (pH 7–7.5; vagina 4.5), clear, ferns.

6. Complications
Maternal
  • Chorioamnionitis → sepsis, death
  • Abruption
  • Operative delivery · endometritis
Fetal, neonatal
  • Prematurity (leading cause of death) · sepsis
  • Cord prolapse, compression
  • Lung hypoplasia (ROM < 24 wk)
7. Management by gestation
Clinical picture
