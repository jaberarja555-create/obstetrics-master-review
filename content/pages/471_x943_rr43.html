10Ectopic pregnancy
  • Implantation outside the uterine cavity; the ampulla of the tube is the commonest site
  • Positive hCG + empty uterus = ectopic until proven otherwise
  • Risk factors: PID, previous ectopic, tubal surgery, IUCD or progestogen-only pill, IVF, endometriosis
  • Features: amenorrhoea 6–8 wk, lower abdominal pain, dark spotting
  • Shoulder-tip pain, fainting: blood in the abdomen
  • Cervical excitation, adnexal mass
11Ectopic: investigations
  • Transvaginal scan: empty uterus, adnexal mass, free fluid
  • An intrauterine sac should be seen once hCG is above about 2000 IU/L
  • Serial hCG 48 h apart: a viable pregnancy rises ≥ 35%; ectopic usually rises less, plateaus or falls slowly
  • Laparoscopy if still unclear
  • Differential: miscarriage, ovarian cyst accident, PID, appendicitis
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Never send home a woman with pain and a positive hCG before ectopic is excluded
12Ectopic: treatment
  • Expectant: stable, little pain, hCG under 1000 and falling
  • Methotrexate 50 mg/m² IM: stable, hCG under 5000, mass under 3.5 cm, no heartbeat
  • Check hCG day 4 and day 7: success = day-7 level ≥ 15% lower than day 4; then weekly to negative
  • Surgery (laparoscopy): unstable, hCG over 5000, mass over 3.5 cm or live embryo
  • Salpingectomy if the other tube is healthy; salpingotomy if it is damaged (follow hCG)
  • Ruptured with shock: resuscitate and laparotomy now
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Empty uterus, hCG 3000, stable → Methotrexate
  • Pain, shock, fluid in the pelvis → Laparotomy now
