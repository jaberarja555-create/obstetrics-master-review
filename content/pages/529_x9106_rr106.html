4Risk factors and systemic review
  • Age < 18 or > 35
  • Consanguinity
  • Occupation
  • Past disease
  • Always a short systemic review
5Hypertension and pre-eclampsia
  • Headache, visual disturbance, epigastric pain, swelling
  • High BP at 10 wk = chronic hypertension
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Headache, flashing lights, BP 150/100 at 34 wk → Severe pre-eclampsia
6Bleeding in early pregnancy
  • Amount, clots, tissue passed, pain, fainting
  • Shoulder-tip pain = blood in the abdomen
  • Vesicles + severe vomiting = mole
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Shoulder-tip pain and fainting at 7 wk → Ruptured ectopic
7Antepartum haemorrhage
  • Painless bright red (praevia) vs pain, hard uterus, dark blood (abruption)
  • Bleeding at ROM with fetal bradycardia = vasa praevia
  • No digital VE until the placenta is located
