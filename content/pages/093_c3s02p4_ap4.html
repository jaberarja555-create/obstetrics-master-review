23. Definition

Deficient decidua: villi attach too deep and the placenta will not separate.

24. Classification
TypeDepth of villiShare of PAS
AccretaAttached on the myometrium (no decidua)≈ 75%
IncretaInvade into the myometrium≈ 15–20%
PercretaGo through the serosa, into bladder or bowel≈ 5%
25. Risk factors
  • Praevia + previous caesarean
  • Myomectomy, D&C, Asherman
  • IVF · age > 35 · multiparity
26. Pathophysiology
  • Scar: no decidua (Nitabuch layer)
  • Villi anchor in muscle
  • No cleavage plane → torrential bleed
27. Clinical features

Usually silent antenatally; found on scan. Percreta may cause haematuria. At delivery: placenta will not separate, then massive PPH if forced.

28. Investigations

Colour Doppler scan in every praevia with a scar · MRI if unclear or posterior.

Scan signs of PAS
  • Lost retroplacental clear zone
  • Many vascular lacunae
  • Thin myometrium (< 1 mm)
  • Broken bladder line
  • Bridging vessels to bladder
