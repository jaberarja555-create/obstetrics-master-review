7Drugs
  • GDM ladder: diet → metformin (500 mg/day up to 2 g/day; first drug) → insulin
  • Glibenclamide 2.5–15 mg if no insulin (neonatal hypoglycaemia)
  • Soluble insulin before meals; isophane at bedtime for high fasting glucose
  • Type 1: about 0.7 U/kg/day early, 1 U/kg/day or more late; basal-bolus is best
  • Steroids and β-agonists raise insulin needs
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • GDM, 2 wk of diet, fasting 5.9 → Add metformin
  • New GDM, fasting 7.4 at diagnosis → Insulin straight away
8Timing and mode of birth
  • Pre-existing: 37⁺⁰–38⁺⁶ wk by induction (NICE); local units often 38–39 wk
  • GDM, controlled: 38 wk to 40⁺⁶ wk
  • EFW over 4.5 kg: offer elective caesarean (shoulder dystocia)
9Labour and after birth
  • Glucose 4–7 mmol/L in labour; hourly checks; CTG; CS first on the list
  • Type 1: insulin + dextrose from established labour; never insulin without dextrose
  • Baby: feed within 30–60 min, then 2–3-hourly; heel-prick glucose from 2 h (aim over 2.6)
  • GDM: stop all drugs; fasting glucose or OGTT at 6 wk, then yearly
  • Type 1: return to the pre-pregnancy dose or lower (placenta gone)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Type 1, glucose 3.1 just after delivery → Cut insulin: placenta gone
10Diabetic ketoacidosis
  • Occurs at lower glucose in pregnancy
  • Ketones, pH below 7.3, HCO₃⁻ below 15
  • Saline 1 L in the first hour, insulin 0.1 U/kg/h, dextrose once glucose below 14, K⁺ once below 5.5
  • Fetal distress: treat the mother first, not an emergency CS
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Vomiting, Kussmaul breathing, glucose only 11 → DKA
