8Drugs considered safe in every trimester
Pain, feverParacetamol
AntibioticsPenicillins, cephalosporins, erythromycin, azithromycin, metronidazole
Blood pressureLabetalol, nifedipine, methyldopa
DiabetesInsulin, metformin
Clot preventionLow-molecular-weight heparin, unfractionated heparin
ThyroidLevothyroxine
SupplementsIron, folic acid, calcium
VomitingPromethazine, cyclizine
MalariaArtemether-lumefantrine, artesunate
EpilepsyLamotrigine, levetiracetam
DepressionSertraline
9Drugs in breastfeeding
Pain
Safe
  • Paracetamol
  • Ibuprofen
Avoid or use with caution
  • Codeine and tramadol (sedation and breathing depression in the baby)
Antibiotics
Safe
  • Penicillins, cephalosporins
  • Erythromycin
  • Metronidazole (pause feeding 12 hours after a single 2 g dose)
Avoid or use with caution
  • Tetracycline for long courses
  • Chloramphenicol
  • Ciprofloxacin when another antibiotic will do
