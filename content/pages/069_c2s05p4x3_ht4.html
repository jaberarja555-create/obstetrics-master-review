14HELLP syndrome

Severe variant of PE: Haemolysis (LDH ↑) · Elevated Liver enzymes · Low Platelets (< 100). BP may be only mildly raised. Treat as severe PE and deliver. Risks: DIC, liver rupture (shock). Bleeding: FFP, cryo, platelets.

Exam trap

Epigastric pain + vomiting at 34 wk = HELLP until proven otherwise, not gastritis: check BP, platelets and LFTs first.

Clinical picture
HELLP

15Delivery
WhenPIH / mild PE: 37 wk (NICE). Severe PE, eclampsia, HELLP: once stable, whatever the gestation. Steroids if < 34 wk
Local practice

Many local units deliver gestational hypertension and pre-eclampsia without severe features at 38 weeks.

HowInduction and vaginal birth preferred. Caesarean if Bishop < 6 and birth not expected soon, or fetal distress
