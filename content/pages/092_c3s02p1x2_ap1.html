6. Complications

Maternal: shock · DIC · PPH · AKI · Sheehan syndrome · hysterectomy · death. Fetal: prematurity · hypoxia, stillbirth · growth restriction · anaemia.

7. Initial management: the first hour

Anti-D 500 IU IM within 72 h if RhD-negative + Kleihauer (none: 1500 IU) · O₂ 10–15 L/min · O-negative blood if collapsed.

Hospital protocol

Peripheral unit: cannula, fluids, O₂, left tilt → transfer with a midwife, blood group note + sample. Do not wait for tests; ask relatives to donate early.
