2. Occipito-posterior position

Definition: longitudinal lie, vertex presentation, occiput in a posterior quadrant of the pelvis (over a sacro-iliac joint or the sacrum). A malposition, not a malpresentation. Incidence: ≈ 25% in early labour; ROP is commoner. Aetiology: android or anthropoid pelvis · deflexed head · anterior placenta · weak contractions.

Clinical features

  • Backache in labour
  • Prolonged 1st and 2nd stage
  • Early urge to push before full dilatation
  • Early ROM · swollen anterior lip

Examination

  • Flat, saucer-shaped lower abdomen
  • Limbs in front · FHS in the flank · head high
  • PV: anterior fontanelle easily felt in front
Clinical picture
Occipito-posterior
Backache+early urge to push+anterior fontanelle in front=OP position
