3. Risk factors (DZ)
  • IVF (2 embryos) 20–30% · IUI · clomiphene
  • Previous twins · family history
  • Age ↑ · parity ↑ · African race
4. Clinical features
  • Severe hyperemesis (high hCG)
  • Uterus large for dates
  • ≥ 3 fetal poles after 24 wk
  • Two fetal hearts (rates differ)
5. Investigations

Ultrasound: number, viability, dating. Chorionicity at 11–13+6 wk; if late and unclear, treat as MC.

Scan signs of chorionicity
  • Lambda (twin-peak): chorion wedge → DC
  • T sign: thin, no wedge → MC
  • 2 placentas, 2 sexes → DC
  • No membrane → MCMA
Clinical scenarios
Scan at 12 wk: one placenta, thick membrane with a triangular wedge. Chorionicity?
Dichorionic (lambda sign): fused placentas.
IVF woman, 20 wk, fundus at 28 wk, severe vomiting early on. Most likely cause?
Multiple pregnancy. Confirm by ultrasound.
6. Differential diagnosis: large for dates

Wrong dates · polyhydramnios · macrosomia (diabetes) · fibroids · ovarian mass · molar pregnancy.

Clinical picture
Twins on examination
Large for dates+many fetal parts+2 hearts=Twins: scan
7. Complications
Maternal
  • Hyperemesis · anaemia
  • Pre-eclampsia (≈ 5×) · GDM
  • Preterm labour · polyhydramnios
  • Praevia · APH · malpresentation
  • PPH · operative delivery
Fetal
  • Miscarriage · vanishing twin
  • Anomalies (2× in MC) · Down
  • Prematurity: main cause of death
  • FGR (25%) · discordance
  • Stillbirth · cerebral palsy

Intrapartum: malpresentation · cord prolapse · hypoxia of twin 2 after twin 1 · cord entanglement (MCMA) · locked twins · vasa praevia · PPH.
