40Clinical features

Impending rupture (obstructed labour): Bandl's ring rising, tender thin lower segment, maternal tachycardia, haematuria. A catheter in labour shows early haematuria.

Clinical picture
Uterine rupture
scar or obstruction+pain+contractions stop+head rises=Rupture
Clinical scenarios
VBAC in labour: sudden pain, contractions stop, FHR 70. Next step?
Resuscitate, immediate laparotomy.
Cardinal sign of uterine rupture on vaginal examination?
Loss of station: presenting part rises.

42Complications

Maternal: haemorrhage, shock, bladder injury, hysterectomy, infection, death · fetal: death in 50–75%, hypoxic brain injury.
