4. Pathophysiology

Poor placental flow → fetal hypoxia → blood redirected to brain, heart, adrenals (brain sparing) and away from gut, kidneys, liver. So: liver glycogen ↓ → AC falls first; renal flow ↓ → oligohydramnios.

5. Clinical features and screening

Early dating scan (before 13 wk) comes first: no dates, no diagnosis.

  • SFH lag > 3 cm → growth scan
  • Fewer movements (< 10 in 12 h)
  • Small baby, little liquor
  • 1st trimester: low PAPP-A
  • 2nd: ↑ AFP, hCG, inhibin · ↓ estriol
  • Uterine artery Doppler 20–24 wk
