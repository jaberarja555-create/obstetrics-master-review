3.2Antepartum Haemorrhage

1. Definition

APH = genital tract bleeding after 28 wk and before the end of the 2nd stage. It complicates about 3–5% of pregnancies.

Clinical picture
Definition of APH
Genital bleeding+≥ 28 wk+before 2nd stage ends=APH
2. Causes
SourceCauses
PlacentalPlacenta praevia · abruptio placentae · marginal sinus bleed
Non-placentalVasa praevia · uterine rupture · show · trauma · cervix: polyp, Ca
UnexplainedNone found; still ↑ preterm birth, FGR, stillbirth
3. Clinical assessment

History: amount, colour, pain, trigger, fetal movements, previous CS. Examination: pulse, BP, pallor; uterine tone, tenderness, lie, presenting part, FHR. No digital PV until scan excludes praevia; then speculum is safe.

4. Investigations
  • FBC, cross-match 4 units
  • PT, aPTT, fibrinogen, FDP · clot test (no clot in 7–10 min = low fibrinogen)
  • Creatinine · Kleihauer · malaria
  • CTG (continuous in abruption)
  • Ultrasound: edge to os (TV safe)
  • Presentation, growth, liquor
5. Differential diagnosis: praevia vs abruption
