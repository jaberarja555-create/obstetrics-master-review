8. Management: the drill
FIRST MINUTES · Save the mother first; that saves the baby. Never wait to prove the diagnosis.
Phase 1 · keep her alive
  • O₂ 15 L/min, intubate early; SpO₂ ≈ 100% protects the brain
  • Left tilt or push the uterus left, off the IVC
  • Fluids with care: right heart failing
  • Low BP after fluids: vasopressor (phenylephrine, noradrenaline) ± inotrope
  • Fits: protect the airway
Phase 2 · stop the bleeding
  • Blood + FFP 15 mL/kg
  • Cryo if fibrinogen < 2 g/L · platelets if < 50
  • TXA 1 g IV
  • Atony: oxytocin 40 IU/500 mL over 4 h, carboprost 250 µg IM
  • Still bleeding: hysterectomy
Distinguishing features
Arrest at 16 wkCPR only, no PMCS
Arrest at 34 wk, 4 min no pulsePMCS where she lies
Survived, BP 70/40 after fluidsVasopressor
Blood oozes, fibrinogen 1.2 g/LCryoprecipitate
9. Management: perimortem caesarean
WhenCardiac arrest at ≥ 20 wk (fundus at or above the umbilicus), no output after 4 min of good CPR
TimingStart the knife at 4 min · baby out by 5 min of arrest
WhereWhere she lies: no transfer to theatre, no consent, CPR continues during the operation
WhyUterus off the IVC and aorta → venous return ↑ → CPR works: mainly for the mother

Not arrested, undelivered: CTG; deliver quickly once she is stable. ICU after: ventilation, monitoring, obstetrics + anaesthesia + haematology.
