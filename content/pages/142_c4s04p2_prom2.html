8. Antenatal management of PPROM
AdmitAt least 48–72 h, in a unit with a nursery
AntibioticErythromycin 250 mg oral 6-hourly × 10 days (or until labour): delays labour, ↓ infection
Steroids24–34 wk: dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12-hourly × 4 or betamethasone 12 mg IM × 2, 24 h apart
MgSO₄< 32 wk if birth is expected within 24 h
TocolysisNo role: it may hide infection
MotherTemperature, pulse, tenderness 4-hourly · FBC, CRP 2–3×/wk
FetusDaily CTG (FHR ↑ = early sign) · weekly scan
DeliverAt 37 wk if well · at once if infection, fetal distress, abruption
Exam trap

Do not use co-amoxiclav in PPROM: it raises necrotising enterocolitis in the baby. Erythromycin is the drug.

Hospital protocol

Sepsis is a leading cause of death of mothers and newborns in Sudan: MEOWS chart, no VEs, act on the first fever or fetal tachycardia.

Distinguishing features
PPROM 30 wk, mother and fetus wellErythromycin + steroids, wait
PPROM 30 wk + FHR 175, feverAntibiotics IV + deliver
PPROM 30 wk + contractionsNo tocolysis; MgSO₄, GBS cover
PPROM, cord at the introitusCord prolapse: CS now
Clinical scenario
Antibiotic of choice in PPROM, with dose?
