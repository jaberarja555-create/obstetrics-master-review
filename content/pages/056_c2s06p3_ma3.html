8. Management: drug treatment
Sudan protocol 2023

AL 20/120 mg in every trimester, with fatty food or milk: 4 tablets twice daily for 3 days; repeat a dose that is vomited within 30 min · no ACT: oral quinine × 7 days · no vein: IM artesunate · IV quinine over 4 h in dextrose.
