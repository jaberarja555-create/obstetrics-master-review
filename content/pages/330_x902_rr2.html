1.3Antenatal care
Booking visit at 10 weeks → Hb, group and Rh, HIV, HBV, syphilis, urine, scan
Previous pre-eclampsia, now 12 weeks → Aspirin 75 mg nightly from 12 to 36 wk
Rh-negative woman with bleeding at 30 weeks → Anti-D within 72 h
Contacts
Book before 12 wk · WHO: at least 8 contacts · anomaly scan 18–22 wk · repeat Hb at 28 wk · presentation at 36 wk
Supplements
Folic acid 0.4 mg (5 mg if high risk) to 12 wk · iron 30–60 mg daily · calcium 1.5–2 g from 20 wk if intake low
Td vaccine
2 doses 4 wk apart in the first pregnancy · 5 doses give lifelong protection
Malaria IPTp
SP (3 tablets) after quickening (16–20 wk), doses ≥ 4 wk apart · not with cotrimoxazole
Screening
Asymptomatic bacteriuria: treat · OGTT at 24–28 wk if at risk · anaemia Hb < 11 g/dL
High-risk features
Age ≥ 35 · height < 150 cm · ≥ 3 miscarriages · previous CS, PPH, stillbirth · medical disease
Exam traps
SP is not given with cotrimoxazole (HIV) · teach the danger signs at every visit · move near the hospital 2 wk before the EDD if high risk
Chapter2
Medical Disorders in Pregnancy
2.1Hyperemesis gravidarum
Vomiting all day at 9 weeks, ketonuria, weight loss > 5% → Hyperemesis gravidarum
Hyperemesis with confusion, ataxia and nystagmus → Wernicke encephalopathy: thiamine
Vomiting that starts after 12 weeks → Look for another cause
Cause
High hCG · worse in twins and molar pregnancy · peaks 9–12 wk
Investigations
Urine ketones (severity) · U&E (low K⁺, Na⁺) · ultrasound (twins, mole) · TFT (gestational thyrotoxicosis) · PUQE ≥ 12 = admit
Biochemistry
Hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis · hypokalaemia · hyponatraemia
Treatment
0.9% saline + KCl · thiamine 100 mg IV before dextrose · antiemetic: promethazine 25 mg or cyclizine 50 mg → metoclopramide 10 mg → hydrocortisone · LMWH
Complications
Wernicke · central pontine myelinolysis (Na⁺ corrected > 10 mmol/L in 24 h) · VTE · Mallory–Weiss tear · weight loss
Last resort
Termination when all treatment fails
Exam traps
No dextrose before thiamine · low TSH with high T4 and no goitre = gestational thyrotoxicosis (no antithyroid drugs) · correct sodium slowly
