Exam trap

The MMR denominator is live births, not total births or women; and it is a ratio, per 100 000, not per 1000 (perinatal mortality is per 1000).

6. Causes
Direct
  • Genital tract sepsis (often group A strep)
  • Haemorrhage: PPH, APH, rupture
  • Hypertensive disorders: stroke, liver, multi-organ failure
  • Unsafe abortion, ectopic
  • Obstructed labour → rupture, sepsis
  • VTE: PE, cerebral vein thrombosis
  • AFE · anaesthesia · acute fatty liver
Indirect
  • Cardiac disease: rheumatic, cardiomyopathy, MI, aortic dissection
  • Anaemia · malaria · HIV, TB, hepatitis E
  • Epilepsy, subarachnoid haemorrhage
  • Asthma · diabetes · sickle cell
  • Pancreatitis · malignancy
