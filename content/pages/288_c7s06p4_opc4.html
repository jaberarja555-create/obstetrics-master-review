Presenting the findings
"Fatima, 34, para 5 at 41 weeks. Her cervix stayed at 7 cm for 4 hours, past the action line, and the head stayed 3/5 despite strong contractions, moulding +3. Fetal tachycardia with meconium; she is febrile, ketotic, with bloody urine. This is obstructed labour with fetal distress; I will resuscitate her and do an emergency caesarean."
