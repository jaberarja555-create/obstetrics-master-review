4Failure to progress
  • First stage: under 2 cm in 4 h in the active phase, or crossing the action line
  • Second stage: over 2 h primi (3 h epidural), over 1 h multi (2 h epidural)
  • Prolonged labour: over 18 h
  • Causes: power (commonest, mainly primi), passenger (OP, malpresentation, macrosomia), passage (CPD)
  • Weak contractions, no CPD, fetus well: ARM + oxytocin, VE in 2 h
  • Tired and dry: fluids and analgesia
  • Slow progress in a multipara = obstruction until proved otherwise
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Primi, 6 cm for 4 h, 2 weak contractions → ARM + oxytocin
5Obstructed labour
  • No descent despite good contractions from a mechanical block: CPD, brow, mento-posterior face, shoulder, hydrocephalus, locked twins
  • Exhausted, dry, ketotic, febrile
  • Bandl's ring, head 3/5 or more at full dilatation, moulding +3, caput, swollen cervix, haematuria
  • Complications: uterine rupture (multi), VVF/RVF (primi), sepsis, PPH, foot drop, fetal death
  • Resuscitate, antibiotics, catheter → caesarean even if the fetus is dead
  • Catheter 14 days after birth to prevent fistula
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Grand multipara, obstructed labour, given oxytocin → Uterine rupture
  • Leaking urine 10 days after a long labour → Vesicovaginal fistula
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Never give oxytocin in obstructed labour
6Fetal distress
  • Pathological CTG; thick meconium; FHR under 100 or over 160; late decelerations
  • Fetal scalp pH: 7.25 or more normal; 7.21–7.24 repeat in 30 min; 7.20 or less: deliver
  • Intrauterine resuscitation: left lateral, stop oxytocin, IV fluids, treat hypotension, terbutaline 0.25 mg SC for hyperstimulation, VE for cord prolapse
  • Deliver: first stage → emergency CS; second stage → instrumental
