8. Danger signs every woman should know
  • Vaginal bleeding in pregnancy or after birth
  • Fits, severe headache, blurred vision
  • Fever, foul discharge
  • Breathlessness, chest pain
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Labour > 12 h · baby moves less
9. Prevention
Before pregnancyFamily planning (fewer high-risk and unwanted pregnancies) · treat anaemia · girls' education · delay marriage
Antenatal careAt least 4–8 visits: BP and urine, Hb and iron-folate, malaria IPT and nets, HIV test, danger signs, birth plan
BirthSkilled birth attendant for every birth · partograph · AMTSL (oxytocin 10 IU IM) · clean delivery
EmONCBasic: parenteral antibiotics, oxytocics, MgSO₄, manual placenta, MVA, assisted birth, newborn resuscitation · Comprehensive: + caesarean and blood transfusion
Postnatal careChecks in the first 24 h, day 3, week 1–2 and 6 wk: bleeding, BP, temperature, breasts, mood; most deaths happen in the first days after birth
Abortion carePost-abortion care: MVA or misoprostol, antibiotics, contraception
AuditMDSR: notify every death, review it with no blame, act on the findings · near-miss reviews
Hospital protocol

Every maternal death is notified and discussed in the hospital review meeting (MDSR), with no blame: the aim is to fix the delay, not to punish.
