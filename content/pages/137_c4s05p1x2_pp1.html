5. Clinical features

Often none · uterus small (low liquor) or large (macrosomia) for dates · fewer movements.

Postmaturity syndrome · ≈ 20% of post-term babies

Long, thin, scaphoid abdomen, little fat · peeling skin of palms and soles · long nails · no vernix or lanugo · meconium-stained skin and cord · alert, anxious look. Also seen earlier in FGR.

6. Investigations

Confirm dates · scan: deepest pool (< 2 cm = oligohydramnios), weight, lie · CTG · Doppler · Bishop score.

8. Management: antenatal
THE WEEKS · Sweep, then offer induction at 41+3: nobody reaches 42 wk without a plan.
