Mastitis and breast abscess (usually in the second week) must also be checked in every febrile postnatal woman.

7. Recognising sepsis early

Every postnatal woman has her observations on a MEOWS chart (modified early obstetric warning score). One red or two yellow triggers → urgent review by a doctor; suspect sepsis first if there is fever or a source.

AimTests

SeverityFBC (WBC, platelets) · lactate · CRP · urea, creatinine, electrolytes · LFT · clotting, fibrinogen (DIC) · glucose · blood gas
In SudanMalaria film or RDT in every fever · Widal is unreliable: culture for typhoid
