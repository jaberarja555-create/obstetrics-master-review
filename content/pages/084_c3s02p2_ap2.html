10. Risk factors
  • Previous caesarean
  • Previous praevia · D&C, myomectomy
  • Multiparity · age > 35 · smoking
  • Twins (large placenta) · IVF
11. Pathophysiology
  • Placenta sits on the lower segment
  • 3rd trimester: segment stretches
  • Placenta shears off its bed
  • Thin muscle: no living ligature
12. Clinical features

History: painless, bright red, recurrent, "causeless" bleeding; small warning bleed first. Examination: shock matches the visible loss · uterus soft, non-tender · presenting part high or malpresentation · fetal heart usually normal.

Clinical picture
Placenta praevia
Painless bright red+soft uterus+high head=Praevia
13. Investigations
  • TV scan: the standard, safe
  • Rescan a low placenta at 32 wk
  • Previous CS: Doppler ± MRI (accreta)
  • Bloods as for any APH
Exam trap

A transvaginal scan is safe in praevia: the probe stays in the fornix. The danger is a finger in the cervix.

14. Management
Heavy bleed, unstableResuscitate → caesarean at any gestation
Bleed settles, pretermAdmit · steroids < 34 wk · 6–8 units ready · aim 37 wk
Major, no bleedingElective caesarean at 37–38 wk
Edge > 2 cm from osVaginal birth can be tried

Bed bleeds at CS: oxytocin → underrun sutures → balloon → artery ligation → hysterectomy.
