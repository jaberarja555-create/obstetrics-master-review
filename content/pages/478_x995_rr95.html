7.4Blood pressure & urine testing
Small cuff on a large arm → Falsely high reading
Readings 145/95 twice, 4 hours apart at 30 weeks → Hypertension: test urine for protein
Urine: nitrites and leucocytes positive → UTI: send MSU for culture
BP technique
Rest 5 min, sitting · cuff bladder covers ≥ 80% of arm (large cuff if arm > 33 cm) · arm at heart level
Korotkoff
Inflate 30 mmHg above palpated systolic · deflate 2 mmHg/s · systolic K1 · diastolic K5 (K4 if sounds go to zero)
Thresholds
Hypertension ≥ 140/90 twice · severe ≥ 160/110 (treat now, re-check in 15 min)
Urine dipstick
Midstream, dip ≈ 1 s · protein 1+ ≈ 0.3 g/L · glucose 2+ → OGTT · ketones → hyperemesis
Proteinuria
Significant: PCR ≥ 30 mg/mmol or ≥ 0.3 g/24 h · severe > 5 g/24 h
Bacteriuria
2–10% of pregnant women · always treat
Exam traps
Record to the nearest 2 mmHg · never round to 0 or 5 · read each pad at its own time
7.5Speculum & vaginal examination
Suspected ruptured membranes → Sterile speculum only, no digital VE
APH and the placental site unknown → No VE: scan first
Y-shaped fontanelle felt anteriorly in labour → Occipito-posterior position
Speculum
Cusco (bivalve, self-retaining) · Sims (VVF, prolapse) · sterile water, no gel in ROM · rotate 90° and open
ROM tests
Pool in posterior fornix · cough · nitrazine blue (pH 7–7.5) · fern on slide
Swabs
High vaginal (fornix) · endocervical for chlamydia/gonorrhoea
Digital VE
Dilatation, effacement, consistency, position · station (0 = spines) · presenting part, position (fontanelles, sutures) · moulding, caput · liquor
Head position
Posterior fontanelle Y-shaped, 3 sutures · anterior diamond (4 sutures) easily felt = deflexed · moulding +3 not reducible
Bishop
≥ 8 favourable · ≤ 6 ripen · max 13
Exam traps
Every 4 h in the 1st stage (2 h on oxytocin) · no cervix felt around the head = fully dilated · always use a chaperone
