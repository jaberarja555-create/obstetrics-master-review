7.6Partogram & CTG stations
Cervical dilatation crosses the alert line → Assess cause, ARM + oxytocin if needed
Good contractions, no descent, moulding +3 → Obstructed labour: CS
FHR below 100 for more than 3 minutes → Pathological CTG: act now
Partogram
Opened at 4 cm · alert line 1 cm/h · action line 4 h later · cervix X, descent O (fifths)
Recording
FHR, pulse, contractions every 30 min · VE every 4 h (2 h on oxytocin) · liquor C/M/B · moulding · BP, urine
Contractions
Boxes per 10 min · dotted < 20 s, hatched 20–40 s, solid > 40 s · target 3–4/10 min
CTG reading (DR C BRAVADO)
Risk · contractions · baseline 110–160 · variability 5–25 · accelerations (15 bpm × 15 s) · decelerations · overall
Decelerations
Early: head · late: placenta · variable: cord · prolonged > 3 min
Augmentation
Oxytocin 5 IU in 500 mL, start 2 mU/min (4 drops/min), increase every 30 min
Exam traps
Head ≥ 3/5 palpable at full dilatation = CPD · reduced variability > 50 min is pathological · catheter 14 days after obstructed labour
7.7Normal delivery & episiotomy
Head crowning, perineum about to tear → Mediolateral episiotomy at 60°
Tight cord around the neck → Clamp twice and cut
Tear through the anal sphincter, more than 50% of the external sphincter → 3b: repair in theatre
Second stage
Primi 2 h, multi 1 h · FHR every 5 min · flex the head, guard the perineum · restitution, then anterior shoulder down, posterior up
Third stage (AMTSL)
Oxytocin 10 IU IM (exclude a second twin) · misoprostol 600 µg if none · clamp cord 1–3 min · controlled cord traction
Baby
Dry, warm, Apgar 1 and 5 min (≥ 7 normal) · golden minute · skin to skin, breastfeed in 1 h
Episiotomy
Mediolateral, 60° at crowning (45° after) · 3–4 cm · lidocaine 1% (max 3 mg/kg) · = 2nd-degree tear
Tears
1st skin · 2nd muscle · 3a < 50% EAS, 3b > 50%, 3c internal sphincter · 4th rectal mucosa
Repair
First stitch 1 cm above the apex · rapid polyglactin 2-0 · PR and swab count after
Exam traps
Check the placenta and membranes complete · PPH ≥ 500 mL · review OASI at 6–12 wk
