5. Pathophysiology: how it spreads
From the placental site outwards · local endometritis can become pelvic, then general, within hours (GAS).
6. Clinical features

General: fever ≥ 38 °C or < 36 °C, rigors, tachycardia, fast breathing, malaise. Severe: low BP, confusion, cold mottled skin, little urine, diarrhoea and vomiting, rash, pain out of proportion, needing opioids (GAS, necrotising infection).
