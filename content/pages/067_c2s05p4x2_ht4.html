12Magnesium sulphate
Prevents and treats eclamptic fits

Loading 4 g IV over 5–15 min → maintenance 1 g/h for 24 h after delivery or the last fit.

Toxicity in order: loss of knee jerk → respiratory depression (RR < 12) → cardiac arrest. Kidneys clear it: keep urine ≥ 30 mL/h.

Antidote: 10 mL of 10% calcium gluconate IV slowly. Fit on MgSO₄: extra 2 g IV; still fitting: phenytoin, then general anaesthesia.

Exam trap

Restrict fluids to 80 mL/h. Leaky capillaries flood the lungs: drips cause pulmonary oedema.

Knee → breath → heart. Magnesium switches off the reflexes first, then breathing, then the heart. Check the knee jerk and RR before every hour of infusion.
13Eclampsia

Definition: fits in pre-eclampsia with no other cause; before, during or after birth.

  1. Call for help. Left lateral, protect from injury, airway, O₂. Do not hold her down.
  2. MgSO₄ 4 g IV then 1 g/h. Diazepam only if magnesium is not available.
  3. Lower BP if ≥ 160/110: hydralazine or labetalol.
  4. Check glucose and a malaria test, catheter, fluid chart, FHR.
  5. Deliver once stable (within 6–12 h): vaginal or caesarean.
