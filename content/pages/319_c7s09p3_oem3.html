Eclampsia Drill

7. The station

Task (7–8 min): "Primigravida, 35 wk, brought by her family. She had a fit at home and now fits again in front of you. Manage her." A mannequin on a couch, a midwife, an eclampsia box. When you ask: BP 170/112, urine protein ++, FHR 150. The examiner then asks you to prepare MgSO₄ and to check for toxicity.

8. The drill
Step · whyماذا تقول
During the fit أثناء التشنج
1Call for help, note the time. Stay with her: most fits stop in 1–2 min(للأهل) هي جاتها تشنجات من ضغط الحمل. نحن معاها، أرجو تنتظروا برّه شوية.
2Left lateral, protect from injury (pad the sides), do not restrain, nothing in the mouth
3When the fit stops: airway, suction secretions, O₂ by mask, check breathing and pulse
Stop the next fit منع التشنج القادم
4IV cannula, bloods: FBC, platelets, LFT, RFT, urate, clotting, cross-match · glucose and malaria test: exclude other causes of fits(لما تفوق) يا حاجة، إنتي في المستشفى وكويسة هسي. حنركّب ليكي درب.
5MgSO₄ loading 4 g IV over 5–15 min, then 1 g/h for 24 h (after birth or the last fit). Why: drug of choice, better than diazepam or phenytoinده دواء بيمنع التشنج. ممكن تحسي بسخانة في جسمك، ده طبيعي.
Control BP & fluids الضغط والسوائل
6BP ≥ 160/110: hydralazine 5 mg IV over 10–15 min (saline 500 mL first), or labetalol IV. Aim about 130/85ضغطك عالي شوية، حنديك دواء ينزّله بالراحة.
7Foley catheter, hourly urine, fluids ≤ 80 mL/h: drips flood the lungsحنركّب قسطرة عشان نقيس البول كل ساعة.
Monitor & deliver المراقبة والولادة
8Every hour: knee jerk, RR, urine, SpO₂, BP every 15 min · fluid chartحنكشف على الركبة والنفس كل ساعة عشان نطمّن.
9FHR / CTG after she is stable (bradycardia during a fit usually recovers)حنسمع قلب الجنين هسي.
10Deliver once stable (within 6–12 h), any gestation; steroids if < 34 wk · 3rd stage: oxytocin, no ergometrineالعلاج الأساسي إنك تولدي بعد ما حالتك تستقر، وده بيحميكي إنتي والجنا.
What to say · to the husband after the fit
“Your wife had a fit caused by high blood pressure of pregnancy. She is breathing well now. We are giving her a medicine that prevents more fits and lowers the pressure. The real cure is delivery once she is stable; we will tell you each step.”
«مرتك جاتها تشنجات بسبب ضغط الحمل. هسي بتتنفس كويس. بنديها دواء بيمنع التشنجات وبينزّل الضغط. العلاج الأساسي إنها تولد بعد ما حالتها تستقر، وحنوريك كل خطوة.»

Viva after the drill: define eclampsia · first sign of MgSO₄ toxicity and its antidote · why no ergometrine · when fits can still happen (up to days after birth: MgSO₄ 24 h after delivery).
