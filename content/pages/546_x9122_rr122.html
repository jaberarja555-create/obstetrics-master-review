3Instrumental birth and evacuation
  • Ventouse: Kiwi (hand pump), metal cups
  • Forceps: Wrigley (outlet, CS), Neville-Barnes (low, mid), Kielland (rotation, sliding lock, little pelvic curve)
  • MVA syringe (60 mL) and Karman cannulae
  • Curette, sound, Hegar dilators
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Forceps with a sliding lock and little pelvic curve → Kielland (rotational)
4Induction, PPH and contraception
  • Foley catheter for induction
  • Bakri balloon (300–500 mL) and condom-catheter balloon for PPH tamponade
  • Copper IUD (within 48 h or after 4 wk), implant, DMPA, combined pill
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Uterine balloon filled with 300–500 mL → Bakri balloon: PPH tamponade
5Drugs

  • Carboprost
