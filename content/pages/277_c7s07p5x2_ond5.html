Common mistakes

Cutting before the local works · cutting between contractions (more bleeding) · no fingers under the scissors · forgetting the PR.

13. The station

Suturing model (perineum pad with a 2nd degree wound), needle holder, toothed forceps, scissors, 2-0 rapidly absorbed polyglactin, swabs, tampon. "Repair this episiotomy and tell me what you are doing."

14. Three layers, then check
Step · whyماذا تقول
1Explain, consent · lithotomy, good light · test the local (top up if needed)الجرح محتاج غُرز بسيطة. حنخدّر المكان، ولو حسيتي بوجع قولي لي.
2Count swabs and needle with the midwife · tampon with a tape in the upper vagina to keep the field dry
3Find the apex; first stitch 1 cm above it (to catch retracted vessels)
4Vaginal mucosa: continuous, not locked, to the hymenal ring; tie at the introitus
5Perineal muscles: continuous or interrupted; close all dead space (haematoma)
6Skin: subcuticular (less pain) or loose interrupted; edges meet, not tight
7Remove the tampon · PR: no stitch in the rectum · count swabs and needle againحأكشف من ورا تاني عشان أتأكد إنو الغُرز سليمة.
8Advice and document (type, layers, suture, counts correct, PR)نضّفي المكان بالموية بعد الحمام، وغيّري الفوطة كتير. الغُرز بتدوب براها.
Exam trap · 3rd and 4th degree

Not repaired in the labour room: theatre, regional or general anaesthesia, an experienced operator; EAS end-to-end or overlap, IAS and mucosa separately. Then antibiotics, laxatives, physiotherapy, review at 6–12 wk.
