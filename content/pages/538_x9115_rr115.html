🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Tight cord around the neck → Clamp twice and cut
  • Head retracts, no restitution → Shoulder dystocia: HELPERR
2Preparation
  • Consent, chaperone
  • Empty bladder
  • Wash, gown, sterile gloves
  • Delivery set: 2 artery forceps, cord scissors, episiotomy scissors, cord clamps
  • Oxytocin 10 IU drawn up
  • Resuscitaire warm
3Second stage
  • Primigravida up to 2 h, multipara 1 h of active pushing
  • FHR every 5 min
  • Push only with contractions; pant at crowning
4Baby
  • Dry, warm, skin to skin
  • Apgar at 1 and 5 min (≥ 7 normal)
  • Golden minute: breathing by 60 s, else ventilate
  • Breastfeed within 1 h
5Third stage (AMTSL)
  • Exclude a second twin
  • Oxytocin 10 IU IM (thigh) within 1 min
  • None: misoprostol 600 µg oral
  • Delayed cord clamping 1–3 min
  • Controlled cord traction with counter-pressure
  • Uterine tone checks every 15 min, rub up only if soft
  • Check placenta and membranes complete
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Pulling the cord without a contraction or counter-pressure → inversion
