Oligohydramnios

8. Definition

AFI < 5 cm or DVP < 2 cm (AFI 5–8 = borderline) · anhydramnios = no pool.

9. Aetiology
MechanismCauses
Fluid leaks outRupture of membranes (PROM, PPROM): the commonest cause
Less urine madeFGR, placental insufficiency (pre-eclampsia) · post-term · renal agenesis (Potter), dysplastic kidneys
Urine blockedPosterior urethral valves (boys) · urethral atresia
DrugsNSAIDs (indomethacin) · ACE inhibitors, ARBs
Clinical picture
Potter sequence
Renal agenesis+no liquor=Lung hypoplasia
Distinguishing features · The likely cause
Watery leak, pool on speculumRupture of membranes
Empty bladder, no kidneys on scanRenal agenesis (Potter)
Boy, large bladder, keyhole signPosterior urethral valves
42 wk, liquor low, CTG normalPost-term: induce
10. Clinical features

History: watery leak (ROM) · reduced fetal movements. Examination: uterus small for dates · fetal parts easily felt, fetus moulded by the uterine wall · often breech.

11. Investigations
  • Sterile speculum: pool (ROM)
  • If ROM: FBC, CRP, high vaginal swab
  • Temp and pulse 4-hourly
  • Scan: AFI, growth, kidneys, bladder
  • UA Doppler if growth is poor
  • CTG in the 3rd trimester
