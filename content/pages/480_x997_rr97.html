7.8Operative delivery stations
Prolonged 2nd stage, head 0/5 palpable, station +2, OA → Outlet/low ventouse or forceps
Need for delivery at 32 weeks in the 2nd stage → Forceps, not ventouse
Cord prolapse at 6 cm, live baby → Category 1 CS within 30 minutes
Prerequisites
Fully dilated, ROM · head ≤ 1/5 palpable, at or below the spines · position known · bladder empty · analgesia · consent
Ventouse
Cup on the flexion point (3 cm in front of the posterior fontanelle) · 0.8 kg/cm² · max 3 pulls, stop after 2 detachments · ≥ 34 wk
Forceps
Left blade first · Wrigley (outlet, at CS) · Neville-Barnes · Kielland (rotation) · Piper (aftercoming head)
CS urgency
Category 1 ≤ 30 min · Category 2 ≤ 75 min · elective at 39 wk
CS care
Cefuroxime 1.5 g before incision · oxytocin 5 IU slow IV · left tilt 15° · LMWH 10 d after emergency CS · catheter out 12 h
Perimortem CS
Start at 4 min of arrest, baby out by 5 min
Exam traps
Never ventouse below 34 wk or for a face · stop if no descent with each pull · classical scar ruptures in 2–9%
7.9Emergency drills
Soft soft uterus with heavy bleeding after birth → Call help, rub the uterus, oxytocin 10 IU
Fit at 36 weeks with BP 160/110 → Eclampsia: MgSO₄ 4 g IV
Floppy, apnoeic newborn → 5 inflation breaths
PPH drill
Help · ABC, O₂ 15 L/min · 2 × 14–16 G cannulae, cross-match 4–6 u · oxytocin → ergometrine 0.5 mg → misoprostol 800 µg → carboprost 250 µg · TXA 1 g within 3 h · bimanual, balloon, theatre
Eclampsia drill
Help · left lateral, airway, O₂ · MgSO₄ 4 g IV over 5–15 min (8 mL of 50% + 12 mL saline) → 1 g/h for 24 h · recurrent fit: 2 g · control BP · fluids ≤ 80 mL/h
Mg toxicity
Lost knee jerk → RR < 12 → stop, calcium gluconate 10 mL of 10%
Shoulder dystocia
Help · McRoberts + suprapubic (80%) · internal manoeuvres · posterior arm · all fours · no fundal pressure
Cord prolapse
Push the part up · knee–chest · fill bladder 500 mL · Category 1 CS
Newborn
Dry, warm · airway · 5 inflation breaths · HR < 60 after ventilation → compressions 3 : 1
Exam traps
First words in every drill: "call for help" · avoid ergometrine with BP 160/110 · keep the clot test: no clot at 7–10 min = DIC
