8. Postnatal care
  • Let her see, hold and name the baby
  • Stop milk: cabergoline 1 mg once
  • Rh-negative: anti-D 500 IU IM within 72 h, after a Kleihauer test
  • Review at 6 wk: results, cause, grief
  • Next pregnancy: folic acid, early booking, OGTT, aspirin if placental cause
  • Serial growth scans · deliver at term
