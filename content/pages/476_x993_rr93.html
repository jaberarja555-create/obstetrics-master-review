6.4Maternal mortality
Death from PPH 2 days after birth → Direct maternal death
Death from malaria at 30 weeks → Indirect maternal death
Woman who survived eclampsia with ICU care → Maternal near miss
Definitions
Death in pregnancy or within 42 days of its end · late: 42 d to 1 year · coincidental: accident
Direct vs indirect
Direct: obstetric complications (PPH, PE, sepsis, ectopic) · indirect: disease worsened by pregnancy (malaria, heart disease, anaemia)
Measures
MMR per 100 000 live births · maternal mortality rate per women 15–49 · lifetime risk
Figures (2023)
Sudan MMR 256 · world 197 · sub-Saharan Africa 448 · SDG 3.1: < 70 by 2030
Causes
World: haemorrhage · Sudan: puerperal sepsis, PPH, PE · UK: VTE
Three delays
1: deciding to seek care · 2: reaching care · 3: receiving care · comprehensive EmONC adds CS and transfusion
Exam traps
A road accident is coincidental, not counted · MMR denominator is live births, not women · skilled attendance is the key intervention

7.1The OSCE station
Entering any station → Hand hygiene, introduce, check name and age, consent
Male student about to examine a woman → Named female chaperone
Breaking bad news → SPIKES with a warning shot
Opening (30 s)
Greet, introduce · confirm identity · explain and consent · privacy, chaperone
Communication
ICE (ideas, concerns, expectations) · open then closed questions · signposting · facts in 2–3 chunks · teach-back
Bad news (SPIKES)
Setting · perception · invitation · knowledge (warning shot) · emotions · strategy and summary
Consent (BRAN)
Benefits · risks · alternatives · nothing (doing nothing) · capacity: understand, retain, weigh, communicate
Presenting
< 1 min: who, why, positives, relevant negatives, impression, plan · one main differential
Closing
Summarise · safety-net (bleeding, leaking, fewer movements) · thank · wash hands
Exam traps
The first words in an emergency viva are "call for help" · never leave a medical word unexplained · let her speak first for about a minute
