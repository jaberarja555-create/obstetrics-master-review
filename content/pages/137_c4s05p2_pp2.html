  1. Confirm dates. Pre-eclampsia, diabetes, FGR or APH: deliver earlier, do not wait.
  2. Membrane sweep at 40–41 wk: finger through the os frees membranes from the lower segment → prostaglandins. Not in praevia or after ROM.
  3. Offer induction at 41+3 (within 41+0 to 41+6). Fewer stillbirths, no rise in caesareans. Method by Bishop score (4.6).

9. Management: intrapartum

Continuous CTG · early ARM to see the liquor · meconium + abnormal CTG → CS · ready for shoulder dystocia · neonatal team at birth · active 3rd stage (PPH).

Exam trap

Suctioning the mouth on the perineum to prevent MAS: not done. Vigorous baby: nothing; floppy baby: resuscitaire.

10. Prevention

Early dating scan for all · sweep at term · offer induction before 42 wk.

Hospital protocol

Late booking is common: date by a sure LMP plus the earliest scan; never move the EDD from a late scan.
