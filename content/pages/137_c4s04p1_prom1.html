4.4Prelabour Rupture of Membranes

1. Definition
TermMeaning
PROM (term)Rupture before contractions, at ≥ 37 wk: physiological, ≈ 8–10% of term births
PPROMThe same before 37 wk: pathological; ≈ 2% of pregnancies, ≈ 40% of preterm births
Prolonged ROMRuptured > 24 h before birth: infection risk rises
Clinical picture
PPROM
Membranes rupture+no contractions+< 37 wk=PPROM
2. Aetiology and risk factors
  • Infection: BV, GBS, UTI, STIs
  • Previous PPROM, preterm birth
  • Twins, polyhydramnios
  • Cervical weakness · amniocentesis
  • APH · malpresentation
  • Smoking · poverty · low vit C

Pathophysiology: infection → bacterial proteases and prostaglandins digest membrane collagen; stretch and bleeding weaken it further.

3. Clinical features

Sudden gush, then a trickle of clear fluid, worse on standing. Ask: colour (meconium, blood), smell, fever, contractions.

4. Investigations: confirm the leak
Step 1History: gush
Step 2Speculum: pooling
Step 3Nitrazine, fern
Step 4US: low AFI
Sterile speculumPool in the posterior fornix or leak on coughing · HVS
NitrazineAlkaline liquor turns it blue · false +: blood, semen, BV
