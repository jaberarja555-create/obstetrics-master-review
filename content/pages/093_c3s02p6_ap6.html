42. Complications

Maternal: haemorrhage, shock, bladder injury, hysterectomy, infection, death · fetal: death in 50–75%, hypoxic brain injury.

43. Management

Call for help, resuscitate (as in abruption) → immediate laparotomy: deliver, then repair a clean tear (± tubal ligation, with consent) or hysterectomy if ragged, infected or unstable. Check the bladder.

Prevention: partograph, no oxytocin in obstructed labour, careful oxytocin with a scar; VBAC only in a hospital with theatre (and only after one lower-segment scar).
