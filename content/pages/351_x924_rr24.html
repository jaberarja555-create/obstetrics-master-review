7.9Emergency drills
Soft soft uterus with heavy bleeding after birth → Call help, rub the uterus, oxytocin 10 IU
Fit at 36 weeks with BP 160/110 → Eclampsia: MgSO₄ 4 g IV
Floppy, apnoeic newborn → 5 inflation breaths
PPH drill
Help · ABC, O₂ 15 L/min · 2 × 14–16 G cannulae, cross-match 4–6 u · oxytocin → ergometrine 0.5 mg → misoprostol 800 µg → carboprost 250 µg · TXA 1 g within 3 h · bimanual, balloon, theatre
Eclampsia drill
Help · left lateral, airway, O₂ · MgSO₄ 4 g IV over 5–15 min (8 mL of 50% + 12 mL saline) → 1 g/h for 24 h · recurrent fit: 2 g · control BP · fluids ≤ 80 mL/h
Mg toxicity
Lost knee jerk → RR < 12 → stop, calcium gluconate 10 mL of 10%
Shoulder dystocia
Help · McRoberts + suprapubic (80%) · internal manoeuvres · posterior arm · all fours · no fundal pressure
Cord prolapse
Push the part up · knee–chest · fill bladder 500 mL · Category 1 CS
Newborn
Dry, warm · airway · 5 inflation breaths · HR < 60 after ventilation → compressions 3 : 1
Exam traps
First words in every drill: "call for help" · avoid ergometrine with BP 160/110 · keep the clot test: no clot at 7–10 min = DIC
7.10Instruments, drugs & spotters
Bivalve self-retaining speculum → Cusco speculum
Forceps with a sliding lock and no pelvic curve → Kielland (rotational)
Uterine balloon filled with 300–500 mL → Bakri balloon: PPH tamponade
Specula
Cusco (self-retaining) · Sims (duck-bill, held by assistant: VVF, prolapse)
Forceps
Wrigley (outlet, CS) · Neville-Barnes (low) · Kielland (rotation, sliding lock) · Piper (aftercoming head)
Other instruments
Pinard (from 24 wk) · ventouse cup (flexion point) · MVA syringe 60 mL · amniohook · Foley (induction)
Drugs
Ergometrine (dark, fridge; not in HTN) · oxytocin (fridge) · misoprostol 200 µg tablets (no fridge) · MgSO₄ 50% ampoules · carboprost
Spotters
Fern pattern: liquor · snowstorm scan: mole · no clot at 10 min: DIC · lambda sign: dichorionic
How to answer
Name · use · how to use it · one complication or precaution
Exam traps
Copper T after birth: within 48 h or after 4 weeks · Sims needs an assistant · always name the precaution with a drug
