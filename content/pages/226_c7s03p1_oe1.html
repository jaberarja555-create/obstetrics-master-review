7.3Obstetric Abdominal Examination

1. What the station looks like
Task

"Amna, 30 years, G3P2, is 32 weeks pregnant. Examine her abdomen and present your findings." · 5–7 min · a real woman, a belly simulator or a mannequin with a doll inside. Tape, Pinard and Doppler are on the table.

2. Equipment & preparation
  • Non-stretch tape in cm
  • Pinard stethoscope · hand-held Doppler + gel
  • BP machine, urine dipstick (7.4)
  • Sheet to drape · pillow or wedge
  • Warm hands: cold hands tighten the abdomen
  • No gloves needed on intact skin

Preparation التحضير
5Empty bladder: a full one lifts the fundus and hurtsلو محتاجة الحمام، اتفضلي قبل ما نبدأ.
