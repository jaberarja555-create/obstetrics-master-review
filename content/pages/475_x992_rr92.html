6.2Puerperal pyrexia & sepsis
Fever day 3 after CS, foul lochia, tender uterus → Endometritis: ceftriaxone + metronidazole
Sudden toxic collapse after birth, child at home with a sore throat → Group A streptococcal sepsis: add clindamycin
Spiking fever despite antibiotics, no collection → Pelvic vein thrombophlebitis: heparin
Definition
Temperature ≥ 38 °C in the first 14 days · sepsis up to 42 days · leading direct killer
Causes (sites)
Endometritis commonest · wound · UTI · breast · chest (day 1 after GA: atelectasis, aspiration) · DVT
Risk factors
Caesarean section (strongest) · ROM > 24 h · many VEs · retained products · anaemia, diabetes
Assessment
MEOWS (one red trigger → urgent review) · qSOFA (RR ≥ 25, SBP < 90, confusion) · lactate ≥ 4 = severe
Sepsis Six (1 hour)
O₂ to 94–98% · 2 blood cultures · IV antibiotics · fluids 20 mL/kg · lactate · urine output ≥ 30 mL/h
Complications
Pelvic abscess (swinging fever, tenesmus) · septic shock · thrombophlebitis
Exam traps
Antibiotics at CS go before skin incision · hand washing prevents most · stop IV after 48 h fever-free
6.3Postpartum mood disorders
Day 4, tearful, manages the baby → Baby blues: reassure
Low mood for 4 weeks, EPDS 15 → Postnatal depression: sertraline, psychology
Day 5, confused, delusions about the baby → Puerperal psychosis: admit today
Baby blues
50–80% · peak day 4–5 · settles within 2 wk · still present at 2 wk → think depression
Postnatal depression
10–15% · within the first year · EPDS ≥ 13 (question 10 = self-harm) · sertraline 50 mg (safe in breastfeeding)
Puerperal psychosis
1–2 per 1000 · onset in 2 wk · risk: bipolar disorder, previous psychosis (50% recurrence)
Psychosis treatment
Mother and baby unit / psychiatric admission · antipsychotics · ECT if severe
Risks
Suicide, infanticide · PND recurrence ≈ 25%
Drugs
Avoid fluoxetine in newborn breastfeeding · lithium: bottle-feed
Exam traps
Psychosis is a psychiatric emergency · ask EPDS question 10 the same day · screen at 6 wk
