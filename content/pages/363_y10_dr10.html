7Which drug, which trimester
Before pregnancy
Give
  • Folic acid 0.4 mg daily (5 mg if high risk: epilepsy, diabetes, obesity, previous neural tube defect)
  • Change ACE inhibitors, sodium valproate, warfarin and statins to safer drugs
Avoid
  • Isotretinoin: stop at least 1 month before
  • Methotrexate and mycophenolate: stop 3 months before
First trimester (up to 13⁺⁶ weeks)
Give
  • Folic acid
  • Antiemetics: promethazine, cyclizine
  • Propylthiouracil (PTU) for Graves’ disease
  • Artemether-lumefantrine for malaria
  • Low-dose aspirin from 12 weeks if at risk of pre-eclampsia
Avoid
  • Sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine and trimethoprim (antifolate)
  • Carbimazole
  • Ondansetron if another antiemetic works
  • Tetracyclines
  • Live vaccines
  • X-rays without a clear need
Second trimester (14 to 27⁺⁶ weeks)
Give
  • Iron and folic acid
  • Sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine for malaria prevention after quickening
  • Calcium from 20 weeks if intake is low
  • Tetanus-diphtheria vaccine
  • Dihydroartemisinin-piperaquine and carbimazole are allowed
Avoid
  • Tetracyclines (teeth staining)
  • ACE inhibitors and angiotensin receptor blockers (fetal kidney damage)
Third trimester (from 28 weeks)
Give
  • Corticosteroids at 24–34 weeks if preterm birth is likely
  • Magnesium sulphate before 32 weeks for fetal brain protection
  • Stop low-dose aspirin at 36 weeks
Avoid
  • NSAIDs after 30 weeks; indomethacin after 32 weeks
  • Nitrofurantoin, chloramphenicol and sulphonamides near term
  • Pethidine within 2–3 hours of birth
Labour and after birth
Give
  • Oxytocin 10 IU IM for the third stage
  • Magnesium sulphate for 24 hours in eclampsia
  • Low-molecular-weight heparin for 6 weeks if at risk of clots
  • Anti-D within 72 hours if Rh-negative
  • Progestogen-only contraception
Avoid
  • Ergometrine in hypertension
  • Combined pill before day 21 (risk of venous thrombosis)
