Viva questions
Why see RFM the same day?
It often comes before stillbirth; early action saves babies.
PPROM at 32 wk. Which antibiotic, which to avoid?
Erythromycin; avoid co-amoxiclav (NEC).
21. Examiner marking points
  • RFM: onset, less vs none
  • RFM: FGR risks; seen at once
  • Leak: time, gush, colour
  • Leak: urine and discharge excluded
  • Infection signs in both
  • No digital VE in PPROM
