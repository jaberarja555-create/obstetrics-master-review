2.2Anaemia in pregnancy
Hb 9 g/dL at 28 weeks, low MCV, low ferritin → Iron deficiency: oral iron
Low MCV with normal ferritin → Thalassaemia trait: HbA2
Pale, febrile pregnant woman with a palpable spleen → Malaria
Definition
Hb < 11 g/dL (1st trimester), < 10.5 later · severe < 7 g/dL
Causes
Iron deficiency (80–90%) · folate (commonest macrocytic) · malaria, hookworm, schistosomiasis · haemoglobinopathies
Investigations
CBC, MCV (< 80 microcytic, > 100 macrocytic) · ferritin (< 15 low) · film · stool, malaria film · HbA2 (> 3.5% β-thal trait)
Treatment
Ferrous sulphate 200 mg 2–3 times daily · IV iron if intolerant or late · folic acid 5 mg if high risk
Monitoring
Reticulocytes rise at 7–10 days (first sign) · Hb rises about 1 g/dL a week
Severe anaemia
Hb < 7 after 36 wk → transfuse packed cells (each over 3–4 h) · before 36 wk → admit, iron, find the cause
Exam traps
Ferritin is the best test of iron stores · thalassaemia trait must not get extra iron · a normal Hb fall in pregnancy is dilutional
2.3Venous thromboembolism
Swollen painful left calf at 30 weeks → Treatment-dose LMWH now, then duplex
Sudden breathlessness after CS, sinus tachycardia, normal CXR → PE: V/Q scan
Collapse with massive PE → IV unfractionated heparin ± thrombolysis
Risk factors
Pregnancy (×5), first 6 wk postpartum highest · CS · age > 35, BMI > 30 · thrombophilia (antithrombin III worst) · immobility
Clinical
DVT: left leg (75%), calf difference > 2 cm · PE: dyspnoea, pleuritic pain, tachycardia, ↓ CO₂
Investigations
Duplex (repeat day 4 and 7 if negative and suspicion high) · PE: normal CXR → V/Q, abnormal CXR → CTPA · D-dimer not useful
Treatment
Enoxaparin 1 mg/kg twice daily · for the rest of pregnancy + 6 wk postpartum · warfarin only after delivery (INR 2–3)
Labour
Stop treatment dose 24 h before regional block (prophylactic dose 12 h)
Prevention
≥ 3 risk factors → LMWH from 28 wk · prophylaxis 40 mg daily
Exam traps
Do not wait for the scan to start LMWH · warfarin is avoided in 6–12 wk (embryopathy) · commonest ECG in PE is sinus tachycardia, not S1Q3T3
