13. Eclampsia

Definition: fits in pre-eclampsia with no other cause; before, during or after birth.

  1. Call for help. Left lateral, protect from injury, airway, O₂. Do not restrain.
  2. MgSO₄ 4 g IV then 1 g/h. Diazepam only if magnesium is not available.
  3. Lower BP if ≥ 160/110: hydralazine or labetalol.
  4. Check glucose and a malaria test, catheter, fluid chart, FHR.
  5. Deliver once stable (within 6–12 h): vaginal or caesarean.
14. HELLP syndrome

Severe variant of PE: Haemolysis (LDH ↑) · Elevated Liver enzymes · Low Platelets (< 100). BP may be only mildly raised. Treat as severe PE and deliver. Risks: DIC, liver rupture (shock). Bleeding: FFP, cryo, platelets.

Exam trap

Epigastric pain + vomiting at 34 wk = HELLP until proven otherwise, not gastritis: check BP, platelets and LFTs first.
