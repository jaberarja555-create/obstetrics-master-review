7After birth
  • Neonatal team at delivery: asphyxia, HIE
  • Cord blood gases
  • Active 3rd stage: PPH risk after tocolysis, CS or instruments
  • Document times: prolapse, decision, delivery; debrief the mother
8Cord presentation
  • Do not do ARM; abandon an ARM if the cord is felt
  • Preterm: conservative
  • Term: counsel; most end in caesarean
  • In labour: caesarean
9Prevention
  • No ARM with a high head
  • Needed ARM (polyhydramnios, unstable lie): controlled ARM in theatre, head held at the brim
  • Admit unstable lie from 37 wk
  • Check for cord after ECV or internal version
  • VE and FHR after every ROM in a woman at risk
5.2Uterine inversion
The uterus turns inside out, almost always in the 3rd stage of labour. About 1 in 2000–3000 deliveries; maternal death up to 15% if not treated fast.
1Classification
  • 1st degree: fundus in the cavity, not past the cervix
  • 2nd: through the cervix, in the vagina
  • 3rd: to or beyond the vulva
  • 4th: uterus and vagina turned out
