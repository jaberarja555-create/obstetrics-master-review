Station: Diabetes in Pregnancy

The station

"Amal, 34, G4 P3 at 26 weeks. Her OGTT: fasting 6.0, 2 h 8.4 mmol/L. Take a focused history." Then: diagnosis, risks to her and the baby, first plan.

Presenting the case

"Amal, 34, G4 P3 at 26 weeks, fasting 6.0 and 2 h 8.4: both above the cut-off. No diabetes before. Her last baby weighed 4.6 kg and her mother is diabetic; BMI 33. No thirst or weight loss. Impression: gestational diabetes. Plan: diet and glucose profile, metformin if targets are missed, growth scans every 4 weeks."

Common errors

Not asking if diabetes was known before · forgetting the previous baby's weight · calling it "high sugar" and leaving her scared.
