7. Common errors
Marks commonly lost

No chaperone · not inspecting the vulva · gel on the blades in ROM · inserting open or upright · cervix not found and not said · not asking her to cough · removing with the screw locked · swabs not labelled · then doing a digital VE "to check".

9. Examiner marking points
  • States the indication
  • No PV before the placenta is located
  • Chooses the right size
  • Names Cusco vs Sims correctly
  • Prepares swabs and media first
  • Uses a sterile tray in ROM
  • Consent + female chaperone
  • Vulva inspected before inserting
  • Sterile gloves, clean front → back
  • Closed, sideways, backwards
  • Rotate, open, cervix seen
  • Screw loosened before removing
  • Describes walls, fornices, os
  • Asks her to cough
  • HVS from the posterior fornix
  • Endocervical inside the os
  • Ectropion vs polyp named
  • Swabs labelled and sent
  • Pool + cough test described
  • Knows nitrazine colour and pH
  • Knows fern test and false positives
  • Concludes: ROM or not
  • Says no digital VE
  • Explains the plan to her
