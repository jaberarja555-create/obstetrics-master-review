5. Clinical features

Painful regular contractions · cyclical low backache, pelvic pressure · vaginal loss: show, bleeding, discharge, gush of fluid · ↓ fetal movements · uterine tenderness (infection, abruption) · progressive cervical change = the proof.

Exam trap

Contractions alone are not preterm labour: Braxton Hicks and UTI cause tightenings. No cervical change = threatened labour; most do not deliver.

6. Diagnosis

The diagnosis is clinical. Tests mainly show who is not in labour, sparing needless drugs and transfer.

  • History: regular rising pains, fluid, bleeding, fever, urinary symptoms; sure gestation
  • Examination: pulse, temperature, uterine tenderness · sterile speculum first: pooling, membranes at the os, swabs
  • Digital VE only if membranes are intact and placenta praevia is excluded
