9. Management: intrapartum

Plan by 36 wk (team, written). Await spontaneous labour at term. Vaginal birth is safer: caesarean only for obstetric reasons (or aortic root > 4 cm, recent MI, NYHA IV in failure).

  1. Position: sit up or left lateral. Never flat on the back (IVC compression).
  2. Avoid fluid overload: restrict IV fluid, fluid chart, catheter. Furosemide ready.
  3. Monitor: pulse, RR, SpO₂, BP (MEWS chart) · continuous CTG.
  4. Early epidural: removes pain and the rise in heart rate. Low dose, slow top-ups. LMWH stopped 24 h before.
  5. Shorten 2nd stage: no long pushing; forceps or ventouse if NYHA III–IV or slow progress.
  6. 3rd stage: oxytocin 5 IU slow IV or low-dose infusion. No ergometrine.
Exam trap

Ergometrine clamps the veins and pushes blood into the heart: acute pulmonary oedema. Fast oxytocin bolus drops BP; large oxytocin infusions overload with water. For PPH: oxytocin slowly, misoprostol; avoid carboprost (bronchospasm, pulmonary hypertension).

Delivery room: cardiac trolley (furosemide, digoxin, adrenaline), O₂, instruments for assisted birth, neonatal resuscitation. Endocarditis prophylaxis is not routine: only prosthetic valve or previous endocarditis.
