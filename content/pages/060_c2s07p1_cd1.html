2.7Cardiac Disease in Pregnancy

1. Physiological changes

Cardiac output ↑ 40% (stroke volume + heart rate ↑ 10–15/min). Blood volume ↑ 40–50%. Resistance ↓, so BP dips mid-pregnancy. A diseased heart cannot meet this load.

THREE DANGER TIMES · Plateau at 24–32 wk · each contraction pushes 300–500 mL into the circulation · after birth the emptied uterus returns blood (autotransfusion): the highest load of all.

2. Aetiology
Acquired
  • Rheumatic heart disease: commonest in Sudan
  • Mitral stenosis: commonest lesion
  • Peripartum cardiomyopathy · IHD
Congenital
  • Acyanotic: ASD, VSD, PDA
  • Cyanotic: Fallot, Eisenmenger
  • Coarctation · Marfan (aortic root)
