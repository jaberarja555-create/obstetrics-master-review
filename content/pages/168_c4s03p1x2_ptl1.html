4Risk factors
  • Previous preterm birth
  • Twins, polyhydramnios
  • Uterine anomaly · cone biopsy
  • Pyelonephritis, malaria, pneumonia
  • APH · pre-eclampsia · diabetes
  • Age < 17 or > 35 yr
  • BMI < 20 · interval < 1 yr
  • Primigravida · grand multipara
  • Smoking · poverty · stress
  • Family history (genetic)

Planned (iatrogenic) early birth: severe pre-eclampsia, severe FGR, APH, monochorionic monoamniotic twins at 32 wk.

5Clinical features

Painful regular contractions · cyclical low backache, pelvic pressure · vaginal loss: show, bleeding, discharge, gush of fluid · ↓ fetal movements · uterine tenderness (infection, abruption) · progressive cervical change = the proof.

Exam trap

Contractions alone are not preterm labour: Braxton Hicks and UTI cause tightenings. No cervical change = threatened labour; most do not deliver.

Clinical picture
Preterm labour
Regular contractions+cervical change+24–37 wk=Preterm labour
