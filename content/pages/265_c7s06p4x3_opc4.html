CTG Station

9. The station

Two forms: (1) put a CTG on a woman (actor or model) and explain it to her; (2) read a strip given with a short story ("G2, 40 wk, on oxytocin, 7 cm…"). Ask for the name, date, time and paper speed before you read.

Applying it
1Introduce, consent, privacy, empty bladder
2Left lateral or sitting up: never flat (aortocaval compression)
3Leopold: find the fetal back
4Toco on the fundus, no gel · US probe with gel over the back
5Check mother's pulse ≠ FHR · speed 1 cm/min · label strip
What to say
"This machine records your baby's heartbeat and your pains. It does not hurt. Lie on your left side; press this button when you feel the baby move."
«الجهاز ده بيسجّل دقات قلب الجنين والطلق، وما بيوجعك. أرقدي على جنبك الشمال، ولمّا تحسي الجنا اتحرك اضغطي الزرار ده.»
Distinguishing features · Before you read
"Rate" 88 that tracks the mother's pulseMaternal pulse: reposition
Flat 30 min, then accelerationsFetal sleep
Speed set at 3 cm/minTrace looks falsely flat
Viva questions
What does a reassuring acceleration tell you?
The fetus is not acidotic at that moment.
Why not flat on her back?
Aortocaval compression: hypotension, fetal decelerations.
