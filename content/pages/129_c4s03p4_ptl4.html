13. Magnesium sulphate: fetal neuroprotection

< 32 wk when birth is expected within 24 h (also for planned early birth): 4 g IV over 20 min, then 1 g/h until birth or 24 h. ↓ cerebral palsy by about one third. Toxicity: flushing, lost reflexes, slow breathing, heart block → stop it, calcium gluconate 10%, 10 mL IV.

14. Intrapartum Management & Prevention

Bacterial vaginosis (Amsel, 3 of 4): thin grey discharge · pH > 4.5 · fishy amine smell with KOH · clue cells > 20%.

15. Intrapartum management
  1. Place: deliver where there is a neonatal unit; transfer in utero is safer than after birth. Paediatrician at the birth, incubator warm, resuscitation ready.
  2. GBS prophylaxis: if GBS positive or not known: benzylpenicillin 3 g IV, then 1.8 g 4-hourly until birth (or ampicillin). Not needed if the swab is negative.
  3. Monitoring: continuous CTG once viable · pulse and temperature 4-hourly · partograph.
  4. Mode: vaginal for a cephalic baby; caesarean only for usual obstetric reasons. Preterm breech: discuss, caesarean often chosen.
  5. Second stage: gentle, controlled head · episiotomy if the perineum is tight · forceps if needed (poor effort, epidural) · no ventouse < 34 wk (scalp bleed).
  6. After birth: if the baby is well, delay cord clamping 1 min · dry, wrap, keep warm.
Antibiotics: when?

Intact membranes: antibiotics do not delay preterm labour, so give them only for GBS in labour, UTI or BV. With PPROM: erythromycin (see 4.4).
