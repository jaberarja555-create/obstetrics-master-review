12. Intrapartum management

Hospital birth · IV line, group and save · continuous CTG of both · epidural ideal · twin 1 as a singleton.

SECOND TWIN · Hold the lie longitudinal by hand while the uterus settles. If contractions fade, restart oxytocin.
Locked twins (rare)

Twin 1 breech, twin 2 cephalic: chins lock, twin 1's head is trapped. Prevent: caesarean when twin 1 is breech.

Third stage: PPH

Active management + oxytocin infusion for 2–4 h after birth. Uterus overstretched: watch closely.
