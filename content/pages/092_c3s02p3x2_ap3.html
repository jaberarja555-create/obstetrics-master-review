19. Clinical features
  • Constant pain; dark bleed or none
  • Shock > visible loss
  • Uterus hard, "woody", tender
  • Fetal distress or death
20. Investigations
  • Clinical: scan misses most clots
  • CTG · FBC, cross-match
  • Clotting, fibrinogen, clot test
  • Creatinine · Kleihauer
21. Complications

Maternal: shock · DIC · Couvelaire uterus → PPH · AKI · Sheehan. Fetal: hypoxia, death, prematurity.

Couvelaire uterus

Blood tracks into the myometrium: the uterus looks blue-purple and bruised at caesarean and contracts poorly → PPH. Not itself a reason for hysterectomy: uterotonics, massage, compression sutures first.

Clinical scenarios
Blood seeping into the myometrium in abruption is called?
Couvelaire uterus.
Couvelaire uterus at caesarean. Hysterectomy?
No: uterotonics, massage, compression sutures first.
22. Management
  • Mother unstable or DIC: resuscitate, correct clotting and deliver now
  • Fetus alive, distressed: emergency caesarean once resuscitation has started
  • Fetus dead, mother stable: vaginal, ARM + oxytocin; labour is fast
