11. Corticosteroids: 24–34 wk

Dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12-hourly × 4 or betamethasone 12 mg IM 24 h apart × 2. Act after 24 h, best at 2–7 days; ↓ RDS, IVH, NEC and neonatal death. Side effects: maternal hyperglycaemia (diabetic: raise insulin), masked infection, adrenal suppression; repeated courses ↓ fetal growth, so one course.

Contraindications to tocolysis

Chorioamnionitis · fetal distress, IUFD, lethal anomaly · heavy APH, abruption · severe pre-eclampsia · advanced labour · > 34 wk. β-agonists also not with heart disease or diabetes. Never give two tocolytics together.

13. Magnesium sulphate: fetal neuroprotection

< 32 wk when birth is expected within 24 h (also for planned early birth): 4 g IV over 20 min, then 1 g/h until birth or 24 h. ↓ cerebral palsy by about one third. Toxicity: flushing, lost reflexes, slow breathing, heart block → stop it, calcium gluconate 10%, 10 mL IV.

14. Intrapartum Management & Prevention

Bacterial vaginosis (Amsel, 3 of 4): thin grey discharge · pH > 4.5 · fishy amine smell with KOH · clue cells > 20%.
