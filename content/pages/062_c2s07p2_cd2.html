7. Management: pre-pregnancy

Joint team (cardiologist, obstetrician, anaesthetist). Echo + NYHA class. Explain risks; advise against pregnancy if high risk. Repair the valve before pregnancy if needed. Stop ACE inhibitors and statins; plan anticoagulation. Folic acid.

8. Management: antenatal
  • Joint cardiac–obstetric clinic, every 10–12 days. NYHA, pulse, BP, weight, lung bases each visit.
  • Remove extra loads: treat anaemia, infection (UTI, chest, dental), hypertension, thyroid disease. Rest; limit salt.
  • Drugs: furosemide for congestion · beta-blocker (slows the heart, longer filling in MS) · digoxin for AF rate · penicillin prophylaxis continues.
  • Fetus: dating scan · anomaly scan 18–20 wk · fetal echo 20–22 wk if CHD · growth scan every 4 wk from 24 wk (beta-blockers → small baby).
  • Admit NYHA III–IV, AF or any failure. Severe symptomatic MS: balloon valvotomy in 2nd trimester.
MECHANICAL VALVE · Never stop anticoagulation (valve thrombosis kills). Warfarin is oral; LMWH injections twice daily, dose by weight. AF + MS: LMWH too.
