Apgar does not decide when to start resuscitation: breathing and heart rate do, from the first seconds. The 5-minute score tells more about outcome than the 1-minute score. In dark-skinned babies judge colour on lips, tongue, palms and soles.

What to say · to the mother
“Your baby was tired at birth and did not breathe at first. We helped him breathe with a mask and he is breathing by himself now. We will keep him warm next to you and check him again.”
«الجنا كان تعبان شوية لما اتولد وما اتنفس طوالي. ساعدناه يتنفس بالكمامة، وهسي بيتنفس براه. حنخليه دافي جنبك ونكشف عليه تاني.»
Presenting the case

"Term boy, born floppy and apnoeic after a long 2nd stage. Dried, warmed, head neutral, 5 inflation breaths with chest rise, then ventilation for 1 min; HR rose from 70 to 140; breathing at 2 min. Apgar 3 at 1 min, 8 at 5 min. Now with mother skin-to-skin; for observation."

Common errors

Deep suction before breathing for the baby.

Giving inflation breaths with the head over-extended.

Starting compressions before the lungs are inflated.

Leaving a wet baby on a cold surface.
