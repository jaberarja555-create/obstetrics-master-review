4Complications
  • DIC (hypofibrinogenaemia) is the most serious: about 10% in the first 3 wk, about 30% if retained over 3 wk
  • Infection and sepsis (especially after ROM)
  • PPH
  • Grief and depression
5Management
  • Most choose induction: mifepristone 200 mg, then misoprostol 36–48 h later
  • Misoprostol: under 27 wk 200 µg 4–6-hourly; 27–28 wk 100 µg 4-hourly; over 28 wk 25 µg vaginal 6-hourly or 25 µg oral 2-hourly
  • Previous CS: lower doses; 2 or more CS or classical scar: Foley + oxytocin or CS
  • Expectant: 85% labour within 3 wk; clotting 2–3 times a week
  • Deliver now with ROM, fever, DIC, abruption or PE
  • Avoid ARM; CS only for usual indications; epidural only with normal clotting
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • IUFD with bleeding and low fibrinogen → Correct DIC, deliver now
  • IUFD with two previous CS → No routine misoprostol
6Postnatal care
  • See, hold and name the baby
  • Suppress lactation: cabergoline 1 mg once
  • Rh-negative: anti-D 500 IU within 72 h after Kleihauer
  • Review at 6 wk: results, cause, grief
  • Next pregnancy: folic acid, early booking, OGTT, aspirin if placental cause, serial growth scans, birth at term
