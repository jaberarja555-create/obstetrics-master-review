7. Investigations in pre-existing diabetes
Mother
  • HbA1c (aim < 6.5%)
  • Creatinine, urine protein (nephropathy)
  • Fundoscopy at booking + each trimester
  • FBC at booking and 28 wk · MSU
Fetus
  • Early dating scan
  • Anomaly scan + fetal echo, 18–20 wk
  • Growth scan every 4 wk, 24–36 wk
  • CTG late in pregnancy
Clinical scenario
Pre-existing diabetes: which extra fetal scan at 18–20 wk?
Fetal echocardiography.
8. Maternal complications
PregnancyHypoglycaemia · DKA · pre-eclampsia · polyhydramnios · preterm labour · UTI, candida · VTE · worse retinopathy, nephropathy
LabourObstructed labour · operative delivery · PPH
Long termType 2 diabetes: ~50% in 10 years after GDM
9. Fetal & neonatal complications
EarlyMiscarriage · congenital anomalies: pre‑existing diabetes only, risk rises with HbA1c · cardiac, neural tube, renal, caudal regression
GrowthMacrosomia (> 4.5 kg) · IUGR if maternal vascular disease
LateUnexplained stillbirth after 36 wk
BirthShoulder dystocia · Erb palsy · clavicle fracture
NeonateHypoglycaemia · RDS · polycythaemia · jaundice · hypocalcaemia
Three "most" answers

Commonest anomaly = cardiac. Most characteristic = caudal regression (sacral agenesis). Commonest neonatal problem = hypoglycaemia.
