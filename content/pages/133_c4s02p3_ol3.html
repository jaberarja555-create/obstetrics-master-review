7. Classification

Normal = all normal · suspicious = one suspicious feature · pathological = one pathological or two suspicious.

FHR > 160: fever, chorioamnionitis, hypoxia, β-agonists · < 100: cord prolapse, abruption, rupture, epidural hypotension · low variability: sleep, opioids, MgSO₄, hypoxia · sinusoidal: severe fetal anaemia.

Clinical scenario
Flat trace for 30 min, then accelerations return. Cause?
Fetal sleep cycle: normal.
8. Failure to progress

1st stage: < 2 cm in 4 h in the active phase, or the plot crosses the action line. 2nd stage: beyond 2 h primi (3 h epidural), 1 h multi (2 h epidural). Prolonged labour = > 18 h. Judge by time and dilatation, not by the pains.

Causes · the 3 Ps: power (inefficient contractions: commonest, mainly primi) · passenger (OP, malpresentation, macrosomia) · passage (CPD, tumour).

1ST STAGE DELAY · Slow progress in a multipara = obstruction until proved otherwise. 2nd stage delay: instrumental or CS.
9. Obstructed labour

Definition: no descent despite good contractions, from a mechanical block: CPD, brow, shoulder or mento-posterior face, hydrocephalus, locked twins, pelvic tumour.

Clinical features
  • Exhausted, dry, ketotic, febrile
  • Bandl's ring: rising ridge
  • Head ≥ 3/5 at full dilatation
  • Moulding +3, caput, swollen cervix
  • Haematuria · fetal distress
Complications
  • Uterine rupture (multi)
  • VVF, RVF (primi): pressure necrosis
  • Sepsis · PPH · foot drop
  • Fetal hypoxia, death, infection

Never give oxytocin.

Management: resuscitate (IV fluids, correct ketosis, broad-spectrum antibiotics, catheter) → caesarean section, even if the fetus is dead (craniotomy only by the experienced). Catheter 14 days after delivery to prevent fistula.
