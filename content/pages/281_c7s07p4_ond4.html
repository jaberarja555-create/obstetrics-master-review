8. Where to cut
Viva questions
Why mediolateral rather than midline?
Midline extends into the anal sphincter.
Best time to cut?
At crowning, at the height of a contraction.
9. Mediolateral vs midline

10. Complications

Early: bleeding · haematoma · extension to a 3rd/4th degree tear · infection, wound breakdown · pain, urinary retention. Late: dyspareunia · scarring, poor anatomy · rarely fistula · incontinence if the sphincter was missed.
