7.7Normal delivery & episiotomy
Head crowning, perineum about to tear → Mediolateral episiotomy at 60°
Tight cord around the neck → Clamp twice and cut
Tear through the anal sphincter, more than 50% of the external sphincter → 3b: repair in theatre
Second stage
Primi 2 h, multi 1 h · FHR every 5 min · flex the head, guard the perineum · restitution, then anterior shoulder down, posterior up
Third stage (AMTSL)
Oxytocin 10 IU IM (exclude a second twin) · misoprostol 600 µg if none · clamp cord 1–3 min · controlled cord traction
Baby
Dry, warm, Apgar 1 and 5 min (≥ 7 normal) · golden minute · skin to skin, breastfeed in 1 h
Episiotomy
Mediolateral, 60° at crowning (45° after) · 3–4 cm · lidocaine 1% (max 3 mg/kg) · = 2nd-degree tear
Tears
1st skin · 2nd muscle · 3a < 50% EAS, 3b > 50%, 3c internal sphincter · 4th rectal mucosa
Repair
First stitch 1 cm above the apex · rapid polyglactin 2-0 · PR and swab count after
Exam traps
Check the placenta and membranes complete · PPH ≥ 500 mL · review OASI at 6–12 wk
7.8Operative delivery stations
Prolonged 2nd stage, head 0/5 palpable, station +2, OA → Outlet/low ventouse or forceps
Need for delivery at 32 weeks in the 2nd stage → Forceps, not ventouse
Cord prolapse at 6 cm, live baby → Category 1 CS within 30 minutes
Prerequisites
Fully dilated, ROM · head ≤ 1/5 palpable, at or below the spines · position known · bladder empty · analgesia · consent
Ventouse
Cup on the flexion point (3 cm in front of the posterior fontanelle) · 0.8 kg/cm² · max 3 pulls, stop after 2 detachments · ≥ 34 wk
Forceps
Left blade first · Wrigley (outlet, at CS) · Neville-Barnes · Kielland (rotation) · Piper (aftercoming head)
CS urgency
Category 1 ≤ 30 min · Category 2 ≤ 75 min · elective at 39 wk
CS care
Cefuroxime 1.5 g before incision · oxytocin 5 IU slow IV · left tilt 15° · LMWH 10 d after emergency CS · catheter out 12 h
Perimortem CS
Start at 4 min of arrest, baby out by 5 min
Exam traps
Never ventouse below 34 wk or for a face · stop if no descent with each pull · classical scar ruptures in 2–9%
