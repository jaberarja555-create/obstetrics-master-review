4Morbidity and the near miss

Maternal morbidity = any ill health caused or made worse by pregnancy: for each death, many women live with fistula, prolapse, anaemia, infertility or depression. A maternal near miss = a woman who nearly died but survived a life-threatening complication in pregnancy, birth or within 42 days (e.g. shock, eclampsia, emergency hysterectomy). Near misses are more common than deaths and are reviewed to improve care.

Exam trap

The MMR denominator is live births, not total births or women; and it is a ratio, per 100 000, not per 1000 (perinatal mortality is per 1000).
