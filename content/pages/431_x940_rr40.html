High-Risk Pregnancy
3.1Bleeding in early pregnancy
Bleeding before 24 weeks. Miscarriage is the commonest cause, ectopic pregnancy the most dangerous, and molar pregnancy the one with very high hCG. Incidental causes: ectropion, polyp, cervicitis, cervical cancer.
1Miscarriage: definition and types
  • Miscarriage: spontaneous loss before 24 wk; about 15–20% of known pregnancies
  • Threatened: mild bleeding, os closed, live fetus; most continue
  • Inevitable: heavy bleeding, colic, os open, nothing passed yet
  • Incomplete: os open, tissue in the os, uterus smaller than dates, may be shocked
  • Complete: pain and bleeding settle, os closed, empty uterus (exclude ectopic)
  • Missed: dead fetus retained, os closed, brown loss, symptoms fade
  • Septic: fever, offensive loss, tender uterus
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • 10 wk, light bleed, os closed, live fetus on scan → Threatened miscarriage
  • Heavy bleed, colic, open os, nothing passed → Inevitable miscarriage
2Aetiology
  • Chromosomal (aneuploidy): about 50% of first-trimester losses
  • Infection and high fever: malaria, TORCH
  • Endocrine: diabetes, thyroid disease, PCOS
  • Immune: antiphospholipid syndrome, SLE
  • Uterine anomaly, fibroid, cervical weakness
  • Drugs: quinine, alcohol, tobacco
