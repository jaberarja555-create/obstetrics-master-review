9. Investigations and treatments
  • Hb if bled or anaemic · iron + folate for 3 months
  • Anti-D 500 IU IM within 72 h: RhD-neg, not sensitised, baby RhD-pos (+ Kleihauer)
  • Rubella non-immune → MMR before discharge; avoid pregnancy 1 month; breastfeeding fine
  • Tetanus (Td): complete the course · hepatitis B vaccine if at risk
  • GDM: fasting glucose / OGTT at 6 wk
  • Hypertension: BP check day 3–5; adjust drugs
  • 6-wk check: BP, Hb, weight, urine, perineum, prolapse, smear if due, contraception
Danger signs: come back at once

Heavy bleeding (a pad soaked in < 1 h, clots) · fever, foul lochia · severe headache, blurred vision, fits · breathlessness, chest pain · calf pain, swollen leg · severe abdominal pain · red painful breast · wound pus · thoughts of harming herself or the baby.

Clinical picture
Anti-D after birth
RhD-neg mother+RhD-pos baby+not sensitised=500 IU in 72 h
Clinical scenarios
Rubella non-immune mother, breastfeeding. Vaccinate now?
Yes, MMR before discharge; breastfeeding is not a bar.
RhD-negative mother, baby RhD-positive, born today. What and when?
Anti-D 500 IU IM within 72 h + Kleihauer.
