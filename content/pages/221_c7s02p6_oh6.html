12. Presenting the history
Presenting the history: one opening line

"Huda, 28, from Shendi, G3 P2+0, at 32⁺³ weeks by a sure LMP confirmed by an early scan, presented with painless vaginal bleeding for 3 hours. Her previous two births were by caesarean section." Then say only what is relevant, ending with a summary and your top differential.

Common mistakes in the long case
  • Parity written wrongly (twins, stillbirths, current pregnancy)
  • No gestational age, or GA not checked against the scan
  • Complaint not analysed (no amount of bleeding, no fetal movements)
  • Previous CS without the indication and the incision type
  • Ending without a summary and a diagnosis
