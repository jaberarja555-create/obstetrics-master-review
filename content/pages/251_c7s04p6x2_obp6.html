  • Clear midstream instructions
  • Expiry checked, bottle closed
  • Strip dipped fully, briefly
  • Held flat, read at the right times
  • Gloves and safe disposal
  • Records every pad
  • Reads all pads, not only protein
  • Links protein to the BP
  • Asks for PCR / 24 h urine
  • Nitrite + leucocytes → MSU first
  • Knows glycosuria is a poor test
  • Presents every pad in order
  • Gives a conclusion + plan
  • 24 h: discard first, keep last
  • Plain words, checks she understood
  • Knows significant proteinuria
