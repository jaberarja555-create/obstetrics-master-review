9Post-operative orders
  • Observations
  • Fluids until drinking
  • Analgesia
  • LMWH for emergency CS or risk factors
  • Catheter out at about 12 h
  • Early walking, feeding
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Ergometrine in a hypertensive woman; no thromboprophylaxis
7.9Emergency drills
Emergency drill stations on a mannequin with a team: PPH, eclampsia, shoulder dystocia, cord prolapse and newborn resuscitation. The first words in every drill are "call for help".
1PPH drill
  • Help, note the time
  • ABC, O₂ 15 L/min
  • 2 large cannulae, blood tests, cross-match 4–6 units, warm fluids
  • Rub up the uterus
  • Find the 4 Ts (tone, trauma, tissue, thrombin)
  • Catheter
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Soft uterus, heavy bleeding after birth → Rub up the uterus and give oxytocin
2PPH drugs
  • Oxytocin 10 IU slow IV, then 40 IU in 500 mL over 4 h
  • Ergometrine 0.5 mg IM (not in hypertension or heart disease)
  • Misoprostol 800 µg sublingual
  • Carboprost 250 µg deep IM every 15 min, max 8 (not in asthma)
  • Tranexamic acid 1 g IV within 3 h
  • Bimanual compression, balloon, theatre
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Ergometrine with BP 160/110
