14. Prevention
WhenWhat
AntenatalTreat anaemia and UTI (screen for bacteriuria) · teach breast care and hygiene
LabourHand washing or alcohol gel before every examination · sterile gloves and instruments · few vaginal examinations · partograph (no prolonged labour) · catheter only when needed, aseptically · antibiotics for prolonged ROM or chorioamnionitis
CaesareanProphylactic antibiotic before skin incision (0–60 min before): cefuroxime 1.5 g IV or cefazolin 2 g IV (allergy: clindamycin 900 mg IV) · avoid co-amoxiclav before birth (NEC) · good haemostasis
After birthCheck the placenta is complete · hand washing before and after pad change and toilet · tell her the danger signs · treat sore throats in the family
Hospital protocol

Peripheral unit: blood culture if possible, first dose of ceftriaxone + metronidazole before referral, IV fluids, malaria RDT, then transfer with a referral note. Never send a septic mother home on oral drugs alone.
