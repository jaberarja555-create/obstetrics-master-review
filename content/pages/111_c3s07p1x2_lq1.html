4. Clinical features
  • Uterus large for dates, tense, shiny
  • Fetal parts and heart hard to find · fluid thrill
  • Discomfort, dyspnoea, oedema
  • Unstable lie, malpresentation
5. Investigations
  • Detailed scan: anomaly, twins, hydrops
  • OGTT · TORCH, syphilis serology
  • Blood group + antibodies
  • Karyotype if anomaly
6. Complications

Maternal: preterm labour · malpresentation · at ROM: cord prolapse, abruption · PPH (atony) · dyspnoea. Fetal: prematurity · the anomaly · ↑ perinatal death.

7. Management
  • Treat the cause: tight glucose control · TTTS: laser to placental anastomoses
  • Severe (AFI > 40 cm), symptomatic: amnioreduction, drain slowly (risk: abruption)
  • Indomethacin 25 mg oral 6-hourly < 32 wk, ≤ 72 h (ductus closure)
  • Preterm risk: steroids · labour: check lie, controlled ARM, oxytocin for PPH

Oligohydramnios

8. Definition

AFI < 5 cm or DVP < 2 cm (AFI 5–8 = borderline) · anhydramnios = no pool.
