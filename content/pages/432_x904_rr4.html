9Weight gain
  • About 12.5 kg in a normal-weight woman, about 0.5 kg a week in the second half
  • Recommended gain by booking BMI: under 18.5: 12.5–18 kg; 18.5–24.9: 11.5–16 kg; 25–29.9: 7–11.5 kg; 30 or more: 5–9 kg
  • Fetus 3.5 kg, placenta 0.7, liquor 0.8, uterus 1, blood 1.5, fat 3–4 kg
  • Gain of 1 kg or more in a week with face and hand oedema: suspect pre-eclampsia
10Normal values in pregnancy
  • Hb: 11 or more (10.5 in the second and third trimester)
  • WBC: up to 15, up to 25 in labour
  • Platelets below 100: think HELLP or pre-eclampsia
  • Fibrinogen 4–6 g/L: 2 g/L during PPH means consumption (DIC)
  • Creatinine below 75 µmol/L
  • Uric acid lower; rises in pre-eclampsia
  • ALP up to 4 times
  • ESR up to 70 mm/h
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Fibrinogen 2 g/L during PPH → Not reassuring: consumption (DIC), replace it
1.2Diagnosis of pregnancy & dating
Pregnancy is suspected from the history, confirmed by hCG, and its site, number and age are set by ultrasound. Consider it in every woman of reproductive age with amenorrhoea, vomiting, abdominal pain or bleeding, and exclude it before X-rays, teratogenic drugs and surgery.
1Clinical features
  • Presumptive (noticed by the woman): amenorrhoea (first and most useful), nausea from about 6 wk, breast tenderness, frequency, tiredness
  • Quickening at 18–20 wk in a first pregnancy, 16–18 wk later
  • Probable (found by the examiner): positive hCG, Hegar's sign (soft isthmus, 6–10 wk), Goodell's (soft cervix), Chadwick's (bluish vagina, about 8 wk), Piskacek's (asymmetric uterus), ballottement
  • Positive (proof): fetal heart on TVS from 6 wk, Doppler from about 12 wk, Pinard from about 24 wk; fetal parts and movements felt by the examiner; fetus seen on scan
