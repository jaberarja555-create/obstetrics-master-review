7. Types of anaemia
Iron deficiencyThe commonest. Ferritin ↓, small pale cells. Oral iron first
MegaloblasticNo folate, no DNA: cells grow, cannot divide. Folic acid 5 mg + iron. Low B12: hydroxocobalamin 1 mg IM first
Sickle cellHbSS: crises, infection, VTE, IUGR. Folic acid 5 mg, no iron unless ferritin low, malaria prophylaxis, growth scans. Crisis: fluids, O₂, analgesia, antibiotics, LMWH. Trait: more UTIs
β-thal traitFolic acid 5 mg; iron only if ferritin low. Two carriers: 25% major
G6PDAvoid sulphonamides, nitrofurantoin, primaquine
