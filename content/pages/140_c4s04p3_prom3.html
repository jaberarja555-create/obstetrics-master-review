9. Management of term PROM (≥ 37 wk)

About 75% labour on their own within 24 h. Antenatal care is as for PPROM (observations, CTG, no VE) but no steroids. Counsel and let her choose:

Immediate induction

Oxytocin infusion (prostaglandin if the cervix is unfavourable).

↓ infection for mother and baby, fewer NICU admissions; but the risks of induction.

Expectant, up to 24 h

Wait for labour with 4-hourly temperature, pulse and fetal heart.

No labour by 24 h, or any sign of infection → induce.

10. Intrapartum management (PROM and PPROM)
  • Deliver in hospital; paediatrician called for the birth
  • Continuous CTG · MEOWS chart for sepsis
  • GBS prophylaxis (IV penicillin) if GBS positive, ROM > 24 h, or preterm
  • Induce now (no waiting) if meconium, GBS positive or any sign of infection
  • High head or malpresentation: check FHR at once for cord prolapse
  • Caesarean only for obstetric reasons; infection is not itself a reason for CS
