4.5Prolonged Pregnancy

1. Definition

Prolonged (post-term) pregnancy = ≥ 42+0 wk (294 days) from the LMP, with regular 28-day cycles.

TermMeaning
Post-datePast the EDD (40+1) but not yet 42 wk (5–10% of pregnancies reach 42 wk; under 5% when dated by an early scan); it recurs in about 30% after one prolonged pregnancy and 40% after two
PostmaturityDescribes the baby (signs of malnutrition), not the weeks
2. Accurate dating: the first step

The commonest reason a pregnancy "looks" post-term is wrong dates. Dates by LMP (Naegele: LMP + 9 months + 7 days) cannot be trusted with an unsure LMP (10–30% of women), irregular cycles, recent pill use, or conception in lactational amenorrhoea.

Best · CRL 11–14 wk± 5 days
HC 14–20 wk± 7–10 days
3rd trimester scan± 3 wk: useless

If the early scan and LMP differ by > 5 days, use the scan date. Never re-date from a late scan.

3. Aetiology / risk factors
  • Unknown in most
  • Previous post-term pregnancy
  • Primigravida · obesity · male fetus
  • Family history
  • Anencephaly (no hydramnios)
  • Fetal adrenal hypoplasia
  • Placental sulphatase deficiency
  • Why: low fetal cortisol
Clinical scenarios
Booked at 30 wk, "42 wk" by LMP, irregular cycles. First step?
Confirm gestational age (earliest scan + history).
Hormone whose fetal deficiency delays labour onset?
Cortisol (adrenal hypoplasia, anencephaly).
