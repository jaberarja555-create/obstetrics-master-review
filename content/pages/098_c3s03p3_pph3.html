10. Mechanical measures

Uterine massage: rub up the fundus through the abdomen to expel clots and start a contraction.

Bimanual compression: fist in the anterior fornix, other hand presses the fundus onto it. Buys time for drugs.

Balloon tamponade: Bakri, Sengstaken or condom catheter with 300–500 mL saline, 12–24 h, with oxytocin and antibiotics.

Catheter: a full bladder stops the uterus contracting.

11. Management: surgery when drugs fail
Step 1B-Lynch brace suture
Step 2Uterine artery ligation
Step 3Internal iliac (anterior div.)
LastHysterectomy

Laparotomy: repair any tear first. B-Lynch works in about 9 of 10 and keeps the uterus.

Hysterectomy: decide early if she is getting worse; a living mother matters more than her uterus. Then ICU, antibiotics, LMWH.
