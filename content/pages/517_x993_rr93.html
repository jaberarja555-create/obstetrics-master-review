6Postnatal care
  • Stay ≥ 24 h after a vaginal birth
  • Visits: 48–72 h, day 7–14, 6 wk (at least 4 contacts, WHO)
  • Check: vital signs, uterus and lochia, perineum or wound, breasts, bladder, legs, mood, baby
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Day 4 headache, BP 160/110 → Postpartum pre-eclampsia
  • Pad soaked hourly on day 9 → Secondary PPH
7Investigations and treatments
  • Iron + folate for 3 months
  • Anti-D 500 IU IM within 72 h: RhD-negative, not sensitised, baby RhD-positive (+ Kleihauer)
  • Rubella non-immune: MMR before discharge; avoid pregnancy 1 month; breastfeeding fine
  • GDM: glucose test at 6 wk
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Rubella non-immune, breastfeeding → MMR before discharge
8Breastfeeding
  • Start within 1 hour, skin to skin
  • Exclusive for 6 months, on demand, 8–12 feeds a day
  • Continue to 2 years
  • Good attachment: mouth wide, chin on the breast, lower lip out
  • Benefits: fewer infections, less PPH, LAM, less breast and ovarian cancer
9Contraindications to breastfeeding
  • Galactosaemia (absolute)
  • Untreated active TB: treat, express, isoniazid to the baby
  • HIV: exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months on ART, no mixed feeding
  • Drugs: cytotoxics, radioactive iodine, lithium, amiodarone, ergots, tetracycline, chloramphenicol
  • Not contraindications: hepatitis B (vaccine + HBIG), mastitis, caesarean
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Mastitis is not a reason to stop: keep feeding from that breast
