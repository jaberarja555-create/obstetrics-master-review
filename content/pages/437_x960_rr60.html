Chapter4
Labour
4.1Normal labour
Labour is regular painful contractions that cause progressive effacement and dilatation of the cervix, ending in the birth of the baby and placenta. Normal labour: spontaneous onset at term (37–42 wk), single cephalic vertex, vaginal birth, 3–18 h, no complications.
1Onset and diagnosis
  • Diagnose by painful regular contractions + a changing cervix; show and bulging forewaters support it
  • True labour: regular, closer, stronger; abdomen to back; cervix dilates; not relieved by sedation
  • False (Braxton Hicks): irregular, unchanged, lower abdomen, no cervical change, relieved by rest
🩺 Exam scenario
  • 39 wk, irregular tightenings relieved by rest, cervix closed → False labour: send home
2Stages and phases
  • First stage: onset to full dilatation
  • Latent 0–4 cm; active from 4 cm (open the partogram)
  • Primi 12–18 h, multi 6–8 h; about 1 cm/h or more in the active phase
  • Second stage: full dilatation to birth: primi up to 2 h (3 h with epidural), multi 1 h (2 h with epidural)
  • Third stage: birth to placenta, 5–15 min; retained if over 30 min with active management
  • Fourth stage: recovery; highest PPH risk: observe at least 4 h
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Cervix 5 cm, 3 contractions in 10 min → Active phase: start the partogram
