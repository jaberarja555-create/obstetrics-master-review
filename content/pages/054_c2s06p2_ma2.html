6. Differential diagnosis
PresentationAlso consider
FeverUTI / pyelonephritis, typhoid, chorioamnionitis, brucellosis
Fits / comaEclampsia, meningitis, hypoglycaemia, epilepsy
JaundiceHepatitis (E is severe in pregnancy), HELLP, acute fatty liver
Clinical picture
Cerebral malaria
Fit or coma+fever + parasites+BP normal=Cerebral malaria
Distinguishing features
Fit, fever, BP 110/70, film +Cerebral malaria
Fit, BP 165/110, protein 3+Eclampsia
Fever, loin pain, film negativePyelonephritis
Newborn with fever + anaemia, mother had malariaCongenital malaria
Clinical scenarios
Febrile pregnant woman, RDT negative, still strongly suspected. Next?
Repeat the blood film.
Fit at 34 wk, fever 39 °C and BP 160/110. Cannot tell the cause. Treat?
Both: MgSO₄ + IV artesunate.
