6. Diagnosis: 75 g OGTT
  1. Normal diet for 3 days, then fast 8–14 h overnight.
  2. Fasting sample, then 75 g glucose in 250–300 mL water.
  3. Second sample at 2 h.
Plasma glucoseGDM · any one valueOvert diabetes
Fasting≥ 5.6 mmol/L (100 mg/dL)≥ 7.0 (126 mg/dL)
2 hours≥ 7.8 mmol/L (140 mg/dL)≥ 11.1 (200 mg/dL)

Use a venous plasma sample sent to the lab. To convert: mg/dL ÷ 18 = mmol/L. Diagnosed GDM? Start treatment at once (see Management).

Exam trap

The load is 75 g of glucose, not 75 mg. And one abnormal value is enough for GDM: you do not need two. No OGTT available? Use fasting + 2 h post-prandial glucose on a lab sample, never the urine dipstick.

7. Investigations in pre-existing diabetes
Mother
  • HbA1c (aim < 6.5%)
  • Creatinine, urine protein (nephropathy)
  • Fundoscopy at booking + each trimester
  • FBC at booking and 28 wk · MSU
Fetus
  • Early dating scan
  • Anomaly scan + fetal echo, 18–20 wk
  • Growth scan every 4 wk, 24–36 wk
  • CTG late in pregnancy
