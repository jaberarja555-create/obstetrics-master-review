5Antiepileptic drugs and anomalies
MAJOR ANOMALY RISK · Risk rises with the dose and the number of drugs: on average about 6–8% with one drug and 8–16% with polytherapy.
DrugTypical anomaly
ValproateNeural tube defect (1–2%), heart, cleft, low IQ. Avoid if she may conceive
CarbamazepineNeural tube defect (about 1%), cleft · enzyme inducer
PhenytoinFetal hydantoin syndrome: cleft, nail and finger hypoplasia, heart
PhenobarbitoneHeart, cleft · neonatal drowsiness, withdrawal
LamotrigineLowest risk · levels fall in pregnancy, so fits may rise
