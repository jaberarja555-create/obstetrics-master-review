10. Risk factors the history must find
  • Previous CS, myomectomy, uterine surgery
  • Previous PPH, retained placenta, abruption
  • Previous pre-eclampsia, stillbirth, preterm birth
  • Recurrent miscarriage, previous baby with anomaly
  • Medical disease: heart, diabetes, hypertension, epilepsy, sickle cell
  • Age < 18 or ≥ 35; grand multipara (≥ 5 births: PPH risk); short interval
  • Rh negative with antibodies; HIV, hepatitis B
  • Twins now; infertility treatment

History in an emergency

A bleeding or fitting woman cannot give a full history: resuscitate first, and ask the companion in a few lines: gestation, parity, previous CS, what happened and when, bleeding amount, fits or headache, fetal movements, allergies, drugs, last meal. Complete the history once she is stable.
