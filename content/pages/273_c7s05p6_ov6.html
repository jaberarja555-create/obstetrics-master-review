14. The cervix
DilatationOs diameter in cm, 0–10 · full (10) = no cervix around the head
EffacementLength: ≥ 3 cm = not effaced (0%) · 2 cm ≈ 50% · 1 cm ≈ 75% · paper-thin = 100%
ConsistencyFirm (like the nose tip) · medium · soft (like the lips)
PositionPosterior (hard to reach) → mid → anterior (ripe)
ApplicationWell applied = good · loose, hanging = poor fit
DILATATION IN CM · Circles drawn to one scale. Train your fingers on a dilatation board before the exam.
15. Membranes and liquor

Intact: a smooth bag that bulges in contractions (forewaters) · ruptured: hair felt directly. Liquor: clear, meconium (green: fetal distress?), blood-stained (abruption?). Feel for a pulsating cord beside the head.
