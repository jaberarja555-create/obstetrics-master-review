5Pre-pregnancy and antenatal care
  • Joint team: cardiologist, obstetrician, anaesthetist
  • Repair the valve before pregnancy if needed; stop ACE inhibitors and statins
  • Clinic every 10–12 days; NYHA, pulse, lung bases each visit
  • Remove extra loads: treat anaemia, infection, hypertension, thyroid disease
  • Furosemide for congestion, beta-blocker (longer filling in MS), digoxin for AF, penicillin prophylaxis
  • Fetal echo at 20–22 wk if CHD; growth scans
  • Admit NYHA III–IV, AF or failure
  • Severe MS: balloon valvotomy in the second trimester
  • Mechanical valve: never stop anticoagulation; warfarin → LMWH at 6–12 wk
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • MS, NYHA II, Hb 8 → Treat the anaemia first
  • MS with a new irregular pulse → AF: rate control + LMWH
6Labour
  • Written plan by 36 wk; await spontaneous labour
  • Vaginal birth is safer; CS for obstetric reasons (or aortic root over 4 cm, failure)
  • Sit up or left lateral; restrict IV fluids, catheter
  • Early low-dose epidural (LMWH stopped 24 h before)
  • Shorten the second stage: forceps or ventouse
  • Third stage: oxytocin 5 IU slowly or low-dose infusion
  • PPH: oxytocin slowly, misoprostol; avoid carboprost
  • Endocarditis prophylaxis only for a prosthetic valve or previous endocarditis
⚠️ Exam trap
  • No ergometrine: it pushes blood into the heart and causes acute pulmonary oedema
7After birth and contraception
  • HDU for at least 3 days: autotransfusion → pulmonary oedema in the first hours
  • Breathless with crackles: sit up, O₂, stop fluids, furosemide 40 mg IV
  • Restart LMWH 8–12 h after birth; mechanical valve → warfarin from day 5
  • Breastfeeding safe
  • Contraception: progestogen-only (implant, injection, LNG-IUS); no combined pill; sterilisation if pregnancy is contraindicated
