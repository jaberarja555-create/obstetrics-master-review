14. Fetal heart: where and how
WHERE TO LISTEN · Over the fetal anterior shoulder, on the side of the back. Red dot = Pinard or Doppler.
14. Fetal heart: where and how
  1. Find the back (lateral grip). Place the Pinard over the anterior shoulder; ear on it, hands off, so you hear the baby, not your hand.
  2. Count for 1 full minute while feeling her radial pulse: two different rates prove it is the fetus.
  3. Say it: "Fetal heart heard, 140/min, regular." Normal 110–160. Let her hear it on the Doppler.
What to say
“Can you hear that? This is your baby’s heart. It is beating well.”
«سامعة الصوت ده؟ ده قلب الجنا، بيدق كويس الحمد لله.»
