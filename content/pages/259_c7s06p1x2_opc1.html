The fetal condition is … Progress: the cervix went from … to … in … hours, [left of / crossing] the [alert / action] line; the head descended from …/5 to …/5; contractions were … The mother's pulse, BP, temperature and urine show … So this is [diagnosis], and my plan is …"
«هذا البارتوقرام للسيدة (فلانة)، عمرها …، حامل للمرة … في الأسبوع …، دخلت الساعة … وعنق الرحم … سم. حالة الجنين … وتقدم الولادة … وحالة الأم … إذن التشخيص هو … والخطة …»
Distinguishing features · Symbol → meaning
X flat, O flat at 3/5No progress: consider CPD
Dotted boxes, 2 per 10 minWeak, short contractions
Moulding +3 at 6 cmObstruction until proved otherwise
Common mistakes

First X not on the alert line · descent as station, not fifths · boxes = minutes, not contractions · forgetting fetus and mother · diagnosis with no plan.
