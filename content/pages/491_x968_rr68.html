⚠️ Exam trap
  • No tocolysis with chorioamnionitis, fetal distress, IUFD, heavy APH, severe pre-eclampsia, advanced labour or over 34 wk
6Magnesium sulphate for neuroprotection
  • Under 32 wk when birth is expected within 24 h (also planned early birth)
  • 4 g IV over 20 min, then 1 g/h until birth or 24 h
  • Reduces cerebral palsy by about one third
  • Toxicity: lost reflexes, slow breathing → stop, calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL IV
7Intrapartum care
  • Deliver where there is a neonatal unit; in-utero transfer is safer
  • GBS prophylaxis if positive or unknown: benzylpenicillin 3 g IV, then 1.8 g 4-hourly; not if the swab is negative
  • Continuous CTG once viable
  • Vaginal for cephalic; preterm breech often by CS
  • Forceps if needed; no ventouse before 32 wk, care at 32–36 wk
  • Delay cord clamping 1 min if well
  • Antibiotics with intact membranes only for GBS, UTI or BV
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Preterm 32 wk, delay in the second stage → Forceps, not ventouse
8Prevention
  • History-indicated cerclage at 12–14 wk after 3 or more mid-trimester losses or preterm births
  • 1–2 losses: TV cervical length every 2 wk at 16–24 wk; under 25 mm: cerclage or vaginal progesterone (200 mg nightly to 34 wk)
  • Emergency (rescue) cerclage for painless dilatation without infection
  • McDonald (simple) or Shirodkar (higher)
  • Remove at 36–37 wk, or at once with labour, ROM, APH or IUFD
  • Treat BV, bacteriuria, malaria; stop smoking; space pregnancies
🩺 Exam scenario
  • One previous birth at 30 wk; now 20 wk, cervix 18 mm → Cerclage or vaginal progesterone
