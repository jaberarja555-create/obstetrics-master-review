9. Management of severe malaria & complications
  1. Call for help, HDU. Left lateral, airway, O₂, IV access.
  2. RBG at once (treat low first), film, Hb, creatinine · IV artesunate now.
  3. Fits: diazepam, or MgSO₄ if eclampsia is possible.
  4. Fluids with care: catheter, hourly urine, chest crepitations.

10. Intrapartum & postpartum
  • Paracetamol, cooling · continuous CTG · CS for obstetric reasons only
  • Shorten 2nd stage if very sick · active 3rd stage (PPH) · check Hb, RBG
  • Placenta to histology · blood film on the newborn
11. Prevention
IPTp with SP · treated bed net

SP 3 tabs 500/25 mg observed at ANC: 2 doses, first after quickening (16–20 wk), second ≥ 4 wk later; high-transmission areas · treated net nightly from the first visit · indoor spraying · iron, folate.

Exam trap

No SP in the 1st trimester or with cotrimoxazole; folic acid 0.4 mg, not 5 mg. No primaquine (fetal G6PD haemolysis).

Clinical scenario
Confused, sweating, on IV quinine. First test?
RBG: quinine releases insulin.
