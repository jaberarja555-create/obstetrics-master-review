LevothyroxineThyroid hormone
Main use
Hypothyroidism
Dose · route · how long
Increase dose by 25–30% once pregnant · TSH each trimester
Side effects · monitor
Over-replacement: palpitations
When · cautions
Target TSH < 2.5 in 1st trimester · back to pre-pregnancy dose after birth
Propylthiouracil · carbimazoleBlock thyroid hormone synthesis
Main use
Hyperthyroidism (Graves)
Dose · route · how long
PTU 150–400 mg/day in 1st trimester · carbimazole 15–40 mg/day in 2nd–3rd · propranolol 10–40 mg TDS for symptoms
Side effects · monitor
PTU: BOX liver failure · carbimazole: aplasia cutis, choanal atresia (1st trimester) · agranulocytosis (sore throat → FBC)
When · cautions
Lowest dose; keep free T4 high-normal
Promethazine · cyclizineAntihistamine antiemetics
Main use
Hyperemesis: 1st line
Dose · route · how long
Promethazine 25 mg TDS · cyclizine 50 mg TDS (PO / IM / IV)
Side effects · monitor
Drowsiness
When · cautions
Any trimester
Metoclopramide · ondansetronD₂ blocker · 5-HT₃ blocker
Main use
Hyperemesis: 2nd line
Dose · route · how long
Metoclopramide 10 mg TDS, max 5 days · ondansetron 4–8 mg TDS
Side effects · monitor
Metoclopramide BOX tardive dyskinesia, dystonia · ondansetron: constipation, QT
When · cautions
Ondansetron: small cleft risk in 1st trimester → after 12 wk if possible
ThiamineVitamin B₁
Main use
Hyperemesis, before any dextrose
Dose · route · how long
100 mg IV before dextrose, then PO
Side effects · monitor
Rare allergy
When · cautions
Prevents Wernicke encephalopathy
Ursodeoxycholic acidBile acid displacement
Main use
Obstetric cholestasis
Dose · route · how long
8–12 mg/kg/day in 2 doses (≈ 500 mg BD)
Side effects · monitor
Diarrhoea
When · cautions
Relieves itch · vitamin K 10 mg PO OD if clotting abnormal
Lamotrigine · levetiracetamAntiepileptics
Main use
Epilepsy
Dose · route · how long
Continue the same dose · lamotrigine levels fall: check, adjust
Side effects · monitor
Lamotrigine BOX serious rash (SJS)
When · cautions
Lowest-risk antiepileptics · folic acid 5 mg · baby vitamin K 1 mg IM
FurosemideLoop diuretic
Main use
Pulmonary oedema, heart failure
Dose · route · how long
20–40 mg IV
Side effects · monitor
Hypokalaemia, hypovolaemia
When · cautions
Not for pre-eclampsia oedema
