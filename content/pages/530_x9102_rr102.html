🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Dies of PPH on day 1 → Direct
  • Severe malaria kills a woman at 28 wk → Indirect
  • Peripartum cardiomyopathy, dies at 4 months → Late maternal death
2Measurement
  • MMR = maternal deaths ÷ live births × 100 000
  • Maternal mortality rate: per 100 000 women aged 15–49
  • Lifetime risk: Sudan about 1 in 91
  • Sources: vital registration, surveys, sisterhood method, confidential enquiries; deaths are under-reported
⚠️ Exam trap
  • The MMR denominator is live births, per 100 000 (perinatal mortality is per 1000)
3Morbidity and the near miss
  • For each death many women live with fistula, prolapse, anaemia, infertility, depression
  • Near miss: nearly died but survived a life-threatening complication; reviewed to improve care
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Survives eclampsia and an emergency hysterectomy → Maternal near miss
4Sudan and the world (WHO 2023)
  • Sudan MMR 256 (298 in 2015)
  • Sub-Saharan Africa 448, about 70% of deaths
  • World 197, about 260 000 deaths a year
  • SDG 3.1: under 70 by 2030
5Causes
  • Sudan: sepsis, haemorrhage, hypertensive disorders, VTE; obstructed labour, unsafe abortion, anaemia
  • World: haemorrhage (about 27%)
  • UK, USA: VTE leading direct cause, cardiac disease overall
  • First-trimester death: ectopic, abortion
