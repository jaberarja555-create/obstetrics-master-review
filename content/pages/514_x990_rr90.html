  • Pressure: suprapubic (Rubin I) from the fetal back side
  • Enter: Rubin II, Woods screw, reverse Woods
  • Remove the posterior arm
  • Roll onto all fours (Gaskin)
  • 30–60 s per manoeuvre
  • McRoberts + suprapubic pressure deliver about 80%
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Turtle sign; after calling for help, first manoeuvre → McRoberts
  • McRoberts and suprapubic pressure fail → Posterior arm or internal rotation
⚠️ Exam traps
  • Never fundal pressure: it impacts the shoulder and ruptures the uterus
  • Episiotomy is not the first step
6When HELPERR fails
  • Deliberate clavicle fracture
  • Zavanelli: head pushed back (terbutaline 0.25 mg SC), then caesarean
  • Symphysiotomy under local anaesthetic where theatre is far
  • Dead baby: cleidotomy
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • All manoeuvres fail, theatre ready → Zavanelli + caesarean
  • All fail, baby dead → Cleidotomy
7After delivery
  • Mother: expect PPH (active 3rd stage, oxytocin infusion); examine and repair the whole genital tract
  • Baby: paediatrician, cord gases (arterial pH < 7.20 = acidaemia), examine arms and clavicles
  • Document head and body times, anterior shoulder, each manoeuvre and who did it
