6Choosing the route
  • Assisted vaginal breech: frank or complete, EFW 2.0–3.8 kg (RCOG; local units often 2.5 kg), flexed head, no FGR or other problem, adequate pelvis, skilled staff
  • Elective CS at 39 wk: footling, EFW over 3.8 or under 2.0 kg, star-gazing head, other CS indication, her choice, first twin breech
  • Perinatal death about 0.5 per 1000 with planned CS vs 2 per 1000 with planned vaginal breech (RCOG)
  • In labour: criteria not met or early labour → CS
  • Preterm breech: CS often chosen; steroids, MgSO₄
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Breech with a star-gazing head on scan → Caesarean
7Vaginal breech delivery
  • Continuous CTG, paediatrician, no early ARM; slow progress = CS, not oxytocin
  • Hands off: push from above, never pull from below
  • Umbilicus to head in about 4 min; head delivered slowly
  • Pinard for extended legs
  • Løvset for extended arms
  • Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit for the head (first choice)
  • Piper forceps when MSV fails
  • Breech extraction only for the second twin or acute distress at full dilatation
  • After: check clavicle, humerus, femur, Erb palsy; hip check for DDH
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Arms extended above the head → Løvset manoeuvre
  • Arm cannot move after a breech birth → Erb palsy or fractured humerus/clavicle
