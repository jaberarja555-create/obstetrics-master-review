2.12HIV in pregnancy
HIV positive at booking → Start ART (TLD) at once, lifelong
Viral load ≥ 1000 copies/mL at 36 weeks → Elective CS at 38 weeks + IV zidovudine
Viral load < 50 at 36 weeks → Vaginal birth
Transmission
Labour and delivery commonest (not breastfed) · high viral load strongest risk · no treatment ≈ 25%, ART with undetectable VL 1–2%
Screening
Opt-out testing at booking
ART
TLD (tenofovir, lamivudine, dolutegravir) from diagnosis, lifelong · cotrimoxazole if CD4 ≤ 350
Delivery plan
Viral load at 34–36 wk · < 50: vaginal · ≥ 1000: CS at 38 wk · avoid ARM early, instruments
Baby
Nevirapine 6 wk (low risk) · DNA PCR at 6 wk · antibody test reliable at 18 mo
Feeding
Exclusive breastfeeding 6 months with ART · never mixed feeding
Exam traps
On cotrimoxazole no SP for IPTp · mixed feeding carries the highest risk · antibody tests in infants are misleading before 18 months
Chapter3
High-Risk Pregnancy
3.1Bleeding in early pregnancy
Bleeding at 8 weeks, os closed, live fetus → Threatened miscarriage
Positive hCG, empty uterus, adnexal tenderness, shoulder-tip pain → Ruptured ectopic: resuscitate, laparotomy
Bleeding, uterus large for dates, snowstorm scan → Complete mole: suction evacuation
Miscarriage types
Threatened (os closed, live) · inevitable (os open) · incomplete (tissue in os) · complete · missed (CRL ≥ 7 mm, no heartbeat; sac ≥ 25 mm empty) · septic
Miscarriage treatment
Expectant (missed: up to 4 wk, DIC risk) · misoprostol 800 µg PV (missed) or 600 µg oral (incomplete) · MVA ≤ 12–14 wk · septic: antibiotics + evacuation
Recurrent (≥ 3)
Antiphospholipid: aspirin + LMWH · cervical weakness (painless 2nd-trimester loss): cerclage 12–14 wk
Ectopic
hCG > 2000 with empty uterus · stable, hCG < 5000, mass < 3.5 cm: methotrexate 50 mg/m² (hCG must fall 15% day 4→7) · surgery: salpingectomy if other tube healthy
Molar pregnancy
Complete 46,XX (paternal) · partial triploid 69 · hyperemesis, early PE, thyrotoxicosis · follow hCG · rising hCG = GTN (methotrexate if FIGO ≤ 6)
Anti-D
Rh-negative: 250 IU before 20 wk, 1500 IU from 20 wk
Exam traps
Never send a woman with pain and a positive hCG home without excluding ectopic · about half of first-trimester miscarriages are chromosomal · no pregnancy until hCG normal
