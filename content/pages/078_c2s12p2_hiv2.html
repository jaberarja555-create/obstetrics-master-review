8. Intrapartum management
  1. Continue oral ART through labour and after. Do not stop it.
  2. IV zidovudine if VL ≥ 1000 or unknown: 2 mg/kg over 1 h, then 1 mg/kg/h until the cord is clamped.
  3. Avoid early ARM, prolonged ROM, scalp electrodes, fetal blood sampling, routine episiotomy.
  4. ROM at term: speed up delivery. Active 3rd stage. Universal precautions.

TestingDNA PCR at 6 wk and after weaning. Antibody test only from 18 mo
In our hospitals · infant feeding

Breastfeed exclusively for 6 months while the mother takes ART, then add foods and continue to 12–24 months. No mixed feeding in the first 6 months (gut damage lets the virus in). Formula only if safe and affordable: here, diarrhoea and malnutrition kill more babies than HIV.

10. Postpartum management
  • ART for life; viral load every 6–12 months. Routine postnatal care.
  • Contraception: all methods suitable; condoms always (dual protection). Efavirenz and rifampicin lower implant and pill levels; dolutegravir does not.
