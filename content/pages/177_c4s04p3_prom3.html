Exam trap

Do not use co-amoxiclav in PPROM: it raises necrotising enterocolitis in the baby. Erythromycin is the drug.

Hospital protocol

Sepsis is a leading cause of death of mothers and newborns locally: MEOWS chart, no VEs, act on the first fever or fetal tachycardia.

Distinguishing features
PPROM 30 wk, mother and fetus wellErythromycin + steroids, wait
PPROM 30 wk + FHR 175, feverAntibiotics IV + deliver
PPROM 30 wk + contractionsNo tocolysis; MgSO₄, GBS cover
PPROM, cord at the introitusCord prolapse: CS now
Clinical scenario
Antibiotic of choice in PPROM, with dose?
Erythromycin 250 mg 6-hourly for 10 days.
9Management of term PROM (≥ 37 wk)

About 75% labour on their own within 24 h. Antenatal care is as for PPROM (observations, CTG, no VE) but no steroids. Counsel and let her choose:

Immediate induction

Oxytocin infusion (prostaglandin if the cervix is unfavourable).

↓ infection for mother and baby, fewer NICU admissions; but the risks of induction.

Expectant, up to 24 h

Wait for labour with 4-hourly temperature, pulse and fetal heart.

No labour by 24 h, or any sign of infection → induce.
