19. Examiner marking points
  • Consent and a chaperone
  • Bladder emptied before measuring
  • Left tilt named and done

  • Asks about pain before touching
  • Hand hygiene before and after

  • Checks pallor and oedema

  • Inspects before touching

  • Fundus found with the hand edge, not fingertips
  • Tape blind, fundus → symphysis
  • States if it fits the dates
  • Hands < 24 wk, tape after
  • Records in cm, one reading
  • Right order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4

  • Converge / diverge explained
  • Gives a cause for an abnormal lie
  • Comments on liquor
