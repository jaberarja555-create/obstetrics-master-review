5.3Amniotic fluid embolism
Sudden breathlessness, hypotension and collapse at ROM → Amniotic fluid embolism
Survivor of collapse now bleeding from every site → DIC: FFP, cryoprecipitate
Cardiac arrest at 34 weeks, no output at 4 minutes → Perimortem CS: baby out by 5 min
Facts
1 : 8000–30 000 · anaphylactoid reaction · 70% in labour around ROM, 19% at CS
Phases
1: cardiorespiratory collapse, hypoxia, fits · 2: DIC and haemorrhage (within 4 h)
Diagnosis
Clinical, by exclusion · post-mortem: fetal squames in the lung · CXR: pulmonary oedema
Treatment
ABC, CPR (30 : 2) · left uterine displacement · O₂ · vasopressors · correct DIC: FFP 15 mL/kg, cryo if fibrinogen < 2 g/L, platelets < 50 · TXA 1 g
Perimortem CS
From 20 wk (uterus compresses IVC) · start at 4 min, baby out by 5 min
Differential
PE (pleuritic pain) · eclampsia (MgSO₄ 4 g) · anaphylaxis (adrenaline 0.5 mg IM) · LA toxicity (lipid 20% 1.5 mL/kg) · haemorrhage
Exam traps
Perimortem CS is done to save the mother · mortality 13–80% · no test confirms AFE in life
5.4Shoulder dystocia
Head delivered then pulled back against the perineum (turtle sign) → Shoulder dystocia: call help, McRoberts
McRoberts and suprapubic pressure fail → Internal manoeuvres or posterior arm delivery
Baby after shoulder dystocia with arm hanging, waiter's tip → Erb palsy (C5–C6)
Definition
Head-to-body interval > 60 s or extra manoeuvres needed · anterior shoulder impacted behind the symphysis
Risk factors
Macrosomia, diabetes · obesity · post-term · prolonged 2nd stage, instrument · previous SD (10%)
HELPERR
Help · evaluate episiotomy · legs (McRoberts) · pressure (suprapubic, Rubin I) · enter (Rubin II, Woods screw) · remove posterior arm · roll (all fours, Gaskin)
Last resort
Zavanelli (head pushed back, CS) · cleidotomy · symphysiotomy
Complications
Baby: Erb palsy, Klumpke (C8–T1), fractured clavicle/humerus, hypoxia (pH falls 0.04/min) · mother: PPH, 3rd/4th degree tear
Prevention
Diabetic, EFW > 4.5 kg → elective CS (non-diabetic > 5 kg)
Exam traps
Never fundal pressure · avoid strong lateral traction · McRoberts + suprapubic free 80%
