Presenting the findings
"Fatima, 34, para 5 at 41 weeks. Her cervix stayed at 7 cm for 4 hours, past the action line, and the head stayed 3/5 despite strong contractions, moulding +3. Fetal tachycardia with meconium; she is febrile, ketotic, with bloody urine. This is obstructed labour with fetal distress; I will resuscitate her and do an emergency caesarean."
«فاطمة، ٣٤ سنة، ولدت خمس مرات. الفتح وقف في ٧ سم لمدة ٤ ساعات وتعدّى خط التدخل، والرأس ما نزل رغم الطلق القوي. الجنين متعب، وهي عندها حمى وجفاف. دي ولادة متعسرة، ومحتاجين عملية قيصرية عاجلة.»

Why is oxytocin dangerous in this woman?
Obstruction + multiparity: uterine rupture.
Why is her urine blood-stained?

8Examiner marking points
  • Starts at 4 cm on the alert line
  • Draws alert and action lines right
  • X and O in the correct cells
  • Contraction boxes: number + shading
  • Reads fetus → progress → mother
  • Ends with diagnosis and plan
  • Names her, parity, GA, time
  • Says "left of the alert line"
  • Gives the rate in cm/h
  • Notes SROM with clear liquor
  • Comments on contractions and mother
  • Calls it normal labour
