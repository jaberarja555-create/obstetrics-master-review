15. Full-mark answers: say them like this
SpotterModel answer (name · what · use · danger · viva fact)
Cusco"Cusco bivalve self-retaining speculum. Used to see the cervix, take swabs and look for pooling of liquor. Avoid force: it pinches the cervix. Insert closed and oblique."
Kielland"Kielland forceps: long, almost no pelvic curve, sliding lock. Used to rotate OT or OP heads. Risk: vaginal and cervical tears. Sliding lock corrects asynclitism."
Ergometrine"Ergometrine 0.5 mg ampoule: ergot uterotonic. For atonic PPH, IM or slow IV. Not in hypertension or heart disease. Store in the fridge, away from light."
Bakri"Bakri balloon: uterine tamponade. For atonic PPH when drugs fail. Fill with 300–500 mL saline, leave 12–24 h with oxytocin and antibiotics. Bleeding goes on → theatre."
Copper IUCD"Copper T IUCD: long-acting reversible method, about 10 years. Also emergency contraception within 5 days. Risk: heavy periods, expulsion. After birth: within 48 h or after 4 wk."
Snowstorm scan"Ultrasound of the uterus filled with small cysts and no fetus: complete molar pregnancy. Check β-hCG, chest X-ray; suction evacuation, then follow hCG to normal."
16. Look-alikes: how to tell them apart
Cusco vs SimsTwo blades that stay open vs a single duck-bill someone must hold
Wrigley vs Neville-Barnes vs KiellandShort (outlet) vs long with pelvic curve (low, mid-cavity) vs long, straight, sliding lock (rotation)
Kiwi vs metal cupPlastic cup, hand pump, single use vs rigid cup with chain and separate pump
Hegar dilator vs uterine soundThick graded rods in a set vs one thin malleable rod marked in cm
Nitrazine vs fernColour change on paper (pH) vs crystal pattern under the microscope
Dexamethasone vs betamethasone6 mg × 4, 12-hourly vs 12 mg × 2, 24 h apart: same purpose
Common examiner follow-up questions

Instrument: "prerequisites before you use it?" · "one maternal and one fetal complication?"

Drug: "dose and route?" · "one contraindication?" · "how is it stored?"

Picture or scan: "what do you see?" first, then "diagnosis?", then "next step?"

Traps

Saying "forceps" without the type, or "speculum" without Cusco or Sims.

Giving a dose without the route.

Describing the picture for a minute and never naming it.

Brand names: say the generic drug name.
