9. Management: when HELPERR fails
ManoeuvreWhat and when
Clavicle fracturePress the anterior clavicle outward (away from the lung) to break it; shoulder width falls. Heals well
ZavanelliPush the head back into the vagina by reversing the mechanism (flex, turn back), with a tocolytic terbutaline 0.25 mg SC, then caesarean
SymphysiotomyCut the symphysis under local anaesthesia, urethra pushed aside with a catheter. Risks: urethral and bladder injury, incontinence, pain, walking problems
Dead babyCleidotomy (cut the clavicles) to spare the mother
Distinguishing features
All fail, baby deadCleidotomy
All fail, no theatre nearbySymphysiotomy
GDM, EFW 4.2 kg, good controlIndividual plan, not automatic CS
All manoeuvres fail, theatre readyZavanelli + CS
10. Management: after delivery
Mother
  • Expect PPH: active 3rd stage + oxytocin 40 IU/500 mL over 4 h
  • Examine and repair the whole genital tract (cervix, vagina, perineum: 3rd/4th degree)
  • Bladder: catheter if injury suspected
Baby
  • Paediatrician at delivery: resuscitation often needed
  • Cord blood gases (pH < 7.25 = acidosis)
  • Examine arms, clavicles, humerus; watch for HIE
Document & debrief

Time of head and body delivery, which shoulder was anterior, each manoeuvre in order and who did it, baby's condition and gases. Explain to the parents; debrief the team.
