2.4Diabetes in Pregnancy

1. Definition & classification
TypeMeaning
Pre-existingType 1 or type 2 known before pregnancy
Gestational (GDM)Glucose intolerance first found in pregnancy (usually 20–24 wk), below the overt level; gone after birth. 85–90%
Overt diabetes in pregnancyFirst found in pregnancy at diabetes levels (fasting ≥ 7.0, 2 h ≥ 11.1). Treat as pre-existing
Clinical picture
Gestational diabetes
First found in pregnancy+gone after birth=GDM
Distinguishing features
High glucose at 26 wk, normal at 6 wk after birthGDM
Fasting 7.8 at first visit, 10 wkOvert diabetes: treat as pre-existing
Type 1, needs less insulin right after birthPlacenta gone: resistance gone
2. Risk factors for GDM
Strong · screen with OGTT
  • Previous GDM · BMI > 30
  • Previous macrosomic baby
  • First-degree relative with diabetes
Others
  • Age ≥ 35 · PCOS · multiparity
  • Previous unexplained stillbirth
  • Corticosteroids
3. Pathophysiology
INSULIN RESISTANCE · From 20 wk placental hormones oppose insulin, keeping glucose for the baby. A healthy pancreas copes; a weak or obese one cannot → hyperglycaemia.
