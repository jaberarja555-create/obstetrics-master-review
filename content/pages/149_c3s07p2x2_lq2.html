Exam trap

ECV is usually not offered: no fluid cushion and lower success (a relative contraindication, RCOG). ACE inhibitors are banned in pregnancy: fetal renal failure, oligohydramnios, hypocalvaria, death.

Clinical scenarios
Breech at 37 wk, AFI 3 cm. Offer ECV?
No: oligohydramnios contraindicates ECV.
Newborn: flat nose, low-set ears, talipes, breathing failure. Cause?
Potter sequence: renal agenesis.
