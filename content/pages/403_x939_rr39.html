4Mode of birth and labour
  • The viral load decides
  • VL under 50 at 36 wk: vaginal birth, oral ART, no IV zidovudine
  • VL ≥ 1000 or unknown: elective CS at 38 wk + IV zidovudine (2 mg/kg over 1 h, then 1 mg/kg/h until the cord is clamped)
  • Continue oral ART through labour
  • Avoid early ARM, prolonged ROM, scalp electrodes, fetal blood sampling, routine episiotomy
🩺 Exam scenario
  • VL under 50, breech at 38 wk → Mode by obstetric reasons only
5The newborn
  • Wash, vitamin K, BCG, eye ointment
  • Low risk: nevirapine 15 mg daily for 6 wk
  • High risk (VL ≥ 1000, ART under 4 wk, found in labour): AZT + NVP 6 wk, then NVP 6 wk
  • Cotrimoxazole from 6 wk until HIV is excluded
  • DNA PCR at 6 wk and after weaning; antibody test only from 18 months
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Baby antibody positive at 9 months → Maternal antibody: do a PCR
6Feeding, after birth and prevention
  • Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months while the mother takes ART, then foods and continue to 12–24 months
  • No mixed feeding in the first 6 months
  • ART for life; viral load every 6–12 months
  • Contraception: all methods, plus condoms; efavirenz and rifampicin lower implant and pill levels
  • Four prongs: prevent HIV, family planning, stop MTCT, treat the family
