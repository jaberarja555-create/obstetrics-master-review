4.5Prolonged pregnancy
Pregnancy reaches 42 weeks by an early scan → Post-term: induce
Pregnant at 41⁺³ weeks, cervix favourable → Offer induction of labour
Post-term baby with peeling skin, long nails, alert look → Postmaturity syndrome
Definitions
Post-term ≥ 42⁺⁰ wk (294 d) · post-date: past the EDD, < 42 wk
Causes
Wrong dates commonest · previous post-term (30%) · anencephaly · primigravida, obesity
Risks
Stillbirth and intrapartum death ↑ · meconium aspiration · macrosomia, shoulder dystocia · oligohydramnios · polycythaemia
Management
Membrane sweep at 40–41 wk · induce at 41⁺³ wk (to 42 wk)
Declines induction
CTG + liquor (DVP) twice a week · DVP < 2 cm → induce
Labour
Continuous CTG · thick meconium + abnormal CTG → CS
Exam traps
Early CRL dating prevents most "post-term" · recurrence ≈ 30% · a sweep brings labour in about 30% within 7 days
4.6Induction of labour
Bishop score 4 at 41⁺³ weeks → Ripen first: dinoprostone 3 mg PV
Previous CS, unripe cervix → Foley balloon, not prostaglandin
More than 5 contractions in 10 minutes with FHR 80 → Tachysystole: stop oxytocin, terbutaline 0.25 mg
Indications
Prolonged pregnancy commonest · PE, diabetes, PROM, FGR, IUFD
Bishop score
Dilatation, length, station, consistency, position · max 13 · ≥ 8 favourable · ≤ 6 ripen
Ripening
Live fetus: dinoprostone 3 mg or misoprostol 25 µg · IUFD: misoprostol · previous CS: Foley 30–50 mL
Oxytocin
5 IU in 500 mL · start 2 mU/min, increase every 30 min, max 32 mU/min · ≥ 6 h after prostaglandin
Complications
Tachysystole · cord prolapse (ARM with high head) · water intoxication · uterine rupture · failed induction
Contraindications
Praevia, transverse lie, ≥ 2 previous CS, active herpes
Exam traps
Never ARM with a high head · long oxytocin with dextrose causes fits (hyponatraemia) · induced VBAC carries 2–3× the rupture risk
