4Supplements and preventive drugs
  • Folic acid 0.4 mg from before conception to 12 wk
  • 5 mg with epilepsy, diabetes, previous NTD, obesity, sickle cell disease
  • Iron 30–60 mg elemental daily throughout pregnancy
  • Calcium 1.5–2 g daily from 20 wk if dietary intake is low
  • Aspirin 75 mg at night, 12–36 wk: one high or two moderate risk factors for pre-eclampsia
  • Albendazole 400 mg once after the first trimester where hookworm is common
⚠️ Exam trap
  • A woman taking SP for IPTp gets folic acid 0.4 mg, not 5 mg (high dose reduces the effect of SP)
5Tetanus–diphtheria vaccine
  • Prevents neonatal tetanus (umbilical stump infection, usually fatal)
  • Never vaccinated: first dose at the first visit, second at least 4 wk later, at least 2 wk before birth
  • Five doses give lifelong protection
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Never vaccinated, books at 32 wk → Td now and 4 wk later
6Prevention of malaria
  • Malaria is more severe in pregnancy; parasites collect in the placenta: anaemia, miscarriage, low birth weight, stillbirth
  • IPTp-SP: 3 tablets swallowed under observation, from 13 wk, at every scheduled visit at least 4 wk apart, at least 3 doses (WHO)
  • Local practice: often two doses (after quickening and 4 wk later)
  • No SP in the first trimester or with cotrimoxazole
