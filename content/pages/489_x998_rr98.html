12Complications
  • Septic shock, MODS
  • DIC
  • Pelvic abscess: swinging fever, diarrhoea, tenesmus → drain
  • Septic pelvic thrombophlebitis: ovarian vein after CS, spiking fever on antibiotics, looks well → add heparin
  • Necrotising fasciitis
  • Secondary PPH
  • Late: tubal block, infertility
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Fever on antibiotics is rarely "resistant bacteria": look for pus, retained products, pelvic vein thrombosis, DVT, malaria
13Prevention
  • Treat anaemia and bacteriuria
  • Hand washing before every examination, few VEs, partograph
  • CS prophylaxis before skin incision: cefuroxime 1.5 g or cefazolin 2 g IV (allergy: clindamycin); avoid co-amoxiclav before birth (NEC)
  • Check the placenta is complete
  • Peripheral unit: first IV dose before referral
6.3Postpartum mood disorders
Mood disorders that start in the puerperium or the months after birth, from a mild self-limited low mood to a psychiatric emergency: baby blues (50–80%), postnatal depression (10–15%), puerperal psychosis (1–2 per 1000).
1The three compared
  • Blues: day 3–10, peak day 4–5, gone by 2 wk; tearful, labile; still cares for the baby; insight full
  • Depression: usually by 3 months, lasts months; anhedonia, guilt, poor bonding; insight present
  • Psychosis: first 2 weeks, sudden; mania or mixed, delusions, confusion; insight lost; suicide and infanticide risk
