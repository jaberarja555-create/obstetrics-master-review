Mode of delivery

Normal Doppler, reassuring CTG: induction and vaginal birth with continuous CTG. Absent/reversed EDF or abnormal CTG: caesarean (cannot tolerate contractions).

Neonatal management

Paediatrician at birth · resuscitation ready · early feeding, glucose checks · keep warm · check PCV · look for anomalies, infection.

Clinical scenario
FGR newborn, 2 h old, jittery. First test?
Blood glucose: hypoglycaemia (low glycogen).
10. Prevention

Early booking and dating scan · stop smoking · treat anaemia, malaria, HTN · low-dose aspirin from 12 wk if high risk of placental disease (previous FGR or PE) · interpregnancy gap > 6 months.
