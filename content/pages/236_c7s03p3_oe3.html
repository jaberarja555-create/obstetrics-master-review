7. Fundal height: hands, then tape
  1. Bring the uterus to the midline: it is usually turned to the right (dextrorotation).
  2. Find the fundus with the ulnar border of the left hand, moving down from the xiphisternum until you meet firm uterus.
  3. Hold the tape at the fundus, run it in the long axis of the uterus to the upper border of the symphysis; numbers hidden so you are not biased. Read once.
  4. Compare with the dates: "SFH 31 cm at 32 wk: compatible with dates."
