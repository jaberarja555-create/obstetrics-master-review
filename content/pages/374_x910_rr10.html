10Risk assessment and birth preparedness
  • Low risk: midwife care, birth in a health facility
  • High risk: doctor-led care, hospital with theatre and blood
  • Previous pregnancies: CS or uterine surgery, PPH, pre-eclampsia, stillbirth, preterm birth, ≥ 3 miscarriages, anomaly, grand multiparity
  • Medical: heart disease, hypertension, diabetes, epilepsy, kidney or thyroid disease, sickle cell, HIV, hepatitis B
  • Current: age under 18 or 35+, BMI 30+ or under 18.5, height under 150 cm, twins, Rh antibodies, Hb below 9, BP 140/90+, APH, SFH not matching dates, malpresentation at 36 wk
  • Birth plan: place, transport and money, blood donor, companion; high risk moves near the hospital about 2 wk before the EDD
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Two previous CS → Hospital: planned caesarean
  • Breech at 36 wk → Hospital: ECV or delivery plan
11Minor complaints
  • Nausea: small dry meals, ginger; ketonuria or weight loss = hyperemesis
  • Heartburn: small meals, no late meals, raise the bed head, antacids
  • Constipation, piles: fluids, fibre, lactulose (iron worsens it)
  • Backache: posture, flat shoes, paracetamol
  • Cramps, varicose veins: stretching, leg raising, stockings
  • Frequency is normal; dysuria, fever or loin pain need a urine culture
  • Itchy curd-like discharge: clotrimazole pessary
  • Fainting: avoid lying flat; carpal tunnel: night splint
