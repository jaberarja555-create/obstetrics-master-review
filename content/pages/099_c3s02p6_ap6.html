Uterine Rupture

38. Definition

Complete = all layers torn, endometrium to serosa · incomplete (dehiscence) = serosa intact, often silent, found at CS. Most happen in labour.

39. Risk factors
  • Scar: VBAC, classical CS, myomectomy
  • Obstructed labour in a multipara: malpresentation, macrosomia, hydrocephalus, contracted pelvis
  • Oxytocin or misoprostol overdose
  • Grand multiparity · trauma · internal version
40. Clinical features

Impending rupture (obstructed labour): Bandl's ring rising, tender thin lower segment, maternal tachycardia, haematuria. A catheter in labour shows early haematuria.
