Viva questions
Why tilt her to the left after 20 weeks?
The uterus presses the IVC and aorta: low BP, less placental flow.
Why stand on her right side?
Right hand free to palpate; standard, easy to face head or feet.

4. Inspection: say what you see
Look atFinding → meaning
SizeMatches the dates? Too large → twins, polyhydramnios · too small → FGR, oligohydramnios
ShapeOvoid, long = longitudinal lie · broad, wide = transverse · flat below the umbilicus = OP · pendulous = grand multipara, contracted pelvis
Fetal movementsSeen after ≈ 24 wk: baby alive · ask her too
SkinLinea nigra · striae gravidarum: red = new, silvery = old (previous pregnancy)
ScarsPfannenstiel (lower segment CS) · midline (classical CS, laparotomy) · laparoscopy ports
UmbilicusFlat or everted near term; tense, shiny skin with polyhydramnios
What to say
“Is the baby moving well? Have you had a caesarean or any operation on your tummy?”
