Viva questions
Why is a Cusco called self-retaining?
The screw locks the blades open, so both hands are free.
Why no digital VE in PPROM?
Carries bacteria up: chorioamnionitis, shorter latency.

Before قبل الكشف
1Hand hygiene · greet · identify · explain · consentحأكشف بجهاز صغير اسمه المنظار، عشان أشوف عنق الرحم.
2Female chaperone, privacy, empty bladderالسستر معانا، والستارة مقفولة. فضّيتي المثانة؟
3Position: on her back, heels together, knees bent and fall apart; sheet over the thighs; light readyأرقدي على ضهرك، لمّي كعوبك مع بعض، وخلي ركبك تقع على الجنبين.
4Open the sterile tray; hand rub; sterile gloves
5Inspect the vulva: liquor, blood, discharge, warts, ulcers, FGM scar, varicosities
6Clean the vulva front to back with antiseptic swabs on sponge-holding forcepsحتحسي بحاجة باردة، دا مطهّر.
Insert إدخال المنظار
7Choose the size; warm it; wet with sterile water (gel spoils swabs and nitrazine); test the screw
8Left thumb and index part the labiaخذي نفس عميق وارخي جسمك.
9Insert the closed blades sideways (blades vertical), directed backwards and slightly down along the posterior wall, away from the urethraحتحسي بضغط بسيط، لو وجعك قولي لي وأوقف.
10Once fully in, rotate 90° so the handle points up; open the blades slowly until the cervix sits between them; lock the screw
Look, test, remove الفحص والإخراج
11Look at the cervix and fornices (next page); ask her to coughاكحّي مرة واحدة لو سمحتِ.
12Take swabs: HVS from the posterior fornix, endocervical from the os; nitrazine on the poolحآخد مسحة صغيرة للمعمل، ما بتوجع.
