4Management by type
  • Threatened: reassure; bed rest is unproven
  • Previous miscarriage + bleeding: vaginal micronised progesterone 400 mg twice daily until 16 wk (NICE 2021)
  • Complete: confirm the empty uterus; pregnancy test negative after 2–3 wk
  • Inevitable, incomplete or missed: three options
  • Expectant: wait 2–4 wk; best under 8 wk
  • Medical: misoprostol; preferred from 12 wk
  • Surgical: MVA up to 12–14 wk; the choice for heavy bleeding, shock or sepsis
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Incomplete miscarriage, BP 80/50, heavy bleeding → Resuscitate, then evacuate (MVA)
⚠️ Exam trap
  • A missed miscarriage should not wait longer than 4 wk: a retained dead fetus can cause DIC
5Misoprostol doses
  • Incomplete, under 13 wk: 600 µg oral or 400 µg sublingual once
  • Missed, under 13 wk: 800 µg vaginal or 600 µg sublingual; repeat after 3 h if needed
  • 13–24 wk: 400 µg sublingual or vaginal every 3 h
  • Priming before evacuation: 400 µg vaginal 3 h before, to soften the cervix
6Surgical evacuation
  • MVA: plastic cannula and 60 mL hand syringe; no electricity; up to 12–14 wk under paracervical block
  • Prime the cervix first in a nullipara or near 12 wk
  • After 14 wk: D&E in theatre
  • Risks: bleeding, perforation, cervical tear, infection, Asherman syndrome, cervical weakness
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Amenorrhoea after repeated D&C → Asherman syndrome (intrauterine adhesions)
