5. Clinical features
  • Head delivers slowly; face and chin are hard to deliver
  • Turtle sign: the head retracts tight against the perineum
  • No restitution: the head does not turn to the side
  • Gentle axial traction with the next contraction fails
  • Baby's face becomes congested, blue
  • Often a warning: slow late 1st or 2nd stage, macrosomia
Best next step

Say "shoulder dystocia" aloud, note the time, call for help, stop her pushing, start HELPERR. Do not pull harder.
