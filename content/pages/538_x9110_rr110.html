3Errors that change the reading
  • Small cuff on a large arm: falsely high
  • Large cuff: low
  • Arm hanging below the heart: high
  • Lying flat: supine hypotension
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Obese arm, standard cuff, 150/100 → Repeat with a large cuff
4What the number means
  • Hypertension ≥ 140/90 twice, 4 h apart
  • Severe ≥ 160/110: treat now, re-check in 15 min
  • One high reading is not pre-eclampsia
🩺 Exam scenario
  • 145/95 twice, 4 h apart, at 30 wk → Hypertension: test urine for protein
5Urine dipstick
  • Midstream (clean-catch) sample
  • Check expiry
  • Dip all pads about 1 s
  • Read each pad at its own time against the chart
6Pad by pad
  • Protein 1+ ≈ 0.3 g/L → PCR
  • Glucose 2+ → OGTT
  • Ketones → hyperemesis, starvation
  • Nitrites + leucocytes → MSU for culture (bacteriuria in 2–10%, always treat)
  • Blood → infection, stones
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Nitrites and leucocytes positive → UTI: send MSU for culture
7Measuring protein
  • Significant proteinuria: PCR ≥ 30 mg/mmol or ≥ 0.3 g/24 h
  • 24-hour urine: discard the first sample, collect all for 24 h including the next morning's first
