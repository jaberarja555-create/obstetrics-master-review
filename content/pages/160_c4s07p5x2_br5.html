16. Breech manoeuvres
PinardPress popliteal fossa → knee flexes → foot out
LøvsetHold pelvis, rotate 180°, back anterior: posterior arm comes under the pubis; reverse for the other
Burns-MarshallHang, then swing feet up in an arc. Not advised: can extend the head
MSVJaw flexion + shoulder traction · first choice
PragueBack stays posterior: hold the shoulders, swing the body up over the mother's abdomen
Piper forcepsAftercoming head when MSV fails; body held up in a towel

Head trapped in a cervix not fully dilated: Dührssen incisions at 2 and 10 o'clock, then MSV. Breech extraction (whole body pulled out): only for the second twin or acute fetal distress at full dilatation.

After a breech birth
  • Baby: clavicle, humerus, femur · Erb palsy · bruised, swollen genitalia
  • Hips: breech is a risk factor for DDH: Ortolani and Barlow at birth, hip ultrasound at 6 wk
  • Mother: AMTSL, inspect for tears, watch for PPH
