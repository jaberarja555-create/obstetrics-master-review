Exam trap · anticoagulation

No warfarin in weeks 6–12; LMWH is the anticoagulant of pregnancy. With a mechanical valve the team may use warfarin from 12 to 36 wk; LMWH again from 36 wk. Her monthly penicillin is rheumatic fever prophylaxis. Endocarditis cover at birth is not routine in heart disease, but she qualifies: prosthetic valve.

What to say · the injections
"These injections thin the blood like warfarin, but they do not reach the baby. You or your sister can learn to give them."

Viva questions
Mechanical valve, positive test at 5 wk. Next step?
Switch warfarin to weight-based LMWH twice daily before 6 wk.
Why treat anaemia hard in heart disease?
Anaemia raises cardiac output: can tip her into failure. Cardiac output already rises about 40% in pregnancy.
