3.4Multiple Pregnancy

1. Definition

More than one fetus in the uterus; twins are the common form. Natural rate ≈ 1 in 80 (Hellin's law: triplets 1 in 80²).

2. Classification
Dizygotic (non-identical) (≈ ⅔ of twins)Monozygotic (identical)
Origin2 ova + 2 sperm1 ovum + 1 sperm, then splits
ShareTwo-thirds of twinsOne-third · constant, by chance
Genes, sexLike siblings · either sexIdentical · always same sex
PlacentaAlways DCDABy day of split: ⅔ MCDA
CHORIONICITY · DC/MC = di-/monochorionic · DA/MA = di-/monoamniotic. Chorionicity decides the risk.
Distinguishing features
Twins of different sexDizygotic, DCDA
Thin membrane, T sign at 12 wkMonochorionic
No dividing membraneMCMA
Split on day 14Conjoined twins
3. Risk factors (DZ)
  • IVF (2 embryos) 20–30% · IUI · clomiphene
  • Previous twins · family history
  • Age ↑ · parity ↑ · African race
4. Clinical features
  • Severe hyperemesis (high hCG)
  • Uterus large for dates
  • ≥ 3 fetal poles after 24 wk
  • Two fetal hearts (rates differ)
