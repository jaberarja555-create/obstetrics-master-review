HELLP
Haemolysis+↑ liver enzymes+platelets < 100=HELLP
15. Delivery
WhenPIH / mild PE: 38 wk. Severe PE, eclampsia, HELLP: once stable, whatever the gestation. Steroids if < 34 wk
HowInduction and vaginal birth preferred. Caesarean if Bishop < 6 and birth not expected soon, or fetal distress
LabourContinuous CTG · BP every 15 min · fluids restricted · epidural helps BP (if platelets > 80)
3rd stageOxytocin 10 IU IM. No ergometrine (raises BP)
Distinguishing features
Severe PE at 30 wk, stable on drugsSteroids, then deliver
PPH in a woman with PEOxytocin, not ergometrine
Fit on day 2 after birth, BP 160/110Postpartum eclampsia
Clinical scenarios
Eclamptic fit stopped. BP 165/112. Next two steps?
MgSO₄ loading + IV hydralazine.
HELLP with BP 145/95. Is this mild disease?
No: HELLP is severe whatever the BP.
16. Postpartum care
  • Fits can still happen: MgSO₄ for 24 h. BP peaks on days 3–5.
  • Wean drugs slowly; stop methyldopa (depression). Breastfeeding safe.
  • Progestogen-only pill. 6 wk review: still high → chronic HTN work-up.
17. Prevention
Aspirin

75 mg at night from 12 wk to 36 wk: 1 major or 2 moderate risk factors.

Calcium

1.5–2 g/day from 20 wk when dietary calcium is low.
