3Classification: stages and phases
STAGES OF LABOUR · Active phase starts at 4 cm: open the partogram here. Rate in the active phase: primi ≈ 1.2 cm/h, multi ≈ 1.5 cm/h.
StageFrom → toKey point
1stOnset → full dilatation (10 cm)Latent 0–4 cm (slow, effacement) · active 4–10 cm: acceleration, maximum slope, deceleration
2ndFull dilatation → birth of babyPassive (descent) then active (pushing)
3rdBaby → placenta and membranesUsually 5–15 min; retained if > 30 min (active)
4thPlacenta → recoveryHighest PPH risk: observe ≥ 4 h
Distinguishing features
Pains at 38 wk, cervix unchanged in 2 hFalse labour: reassure
Pains + cervix 2 cm, soft, effacedLatent phase
Cervix 5 cm, 3 contractions / 10 minActive phase: partogram
Cervix 10 cm, head on perineum2nd stage
