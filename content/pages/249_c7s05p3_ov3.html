4. Taking swabs
High vaginal swab (HVS)

Roll the swab in the posterior fornix for a few seconds, into its transport medium. For candida, BV, trichomonas, GBS.

Endocervical swab

Insert 1–2 cm into the os, rotate gently, withdraw without touching the walls. For gonorrhoea, chlamydia.
