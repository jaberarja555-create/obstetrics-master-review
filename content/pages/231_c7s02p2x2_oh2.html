4. Examiner marking points
  • Age and residence asked
  • LMP, sure and regular, cycle length
  • EDD and GA worked out correctly
  • G/P written correctly, with living children
  • Asks in plain words, one at a time
  • HPI analysed, not just listed
  • Fetal movements asked
  • Each previous birth: mode and why
  • Medical and drug history
  • Family + social, incl. support
  • Summary in plain words
