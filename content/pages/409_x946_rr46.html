5Praevia: management
  • Heavy bleeding, unstable: resuscitate, caesarean at any gestation
  • Bleed settles, preterm: admit, steroids under 34 wk, blood ready
  • No bleeding: elective CS at 36–37 wk (RCOG 2018; local 37–38 wk)
  • Edge more than 2 cm from the os: vaginal birth can be tried
  • Bed bleeding at CS: oxytocin → sutures → balloon → artery ligation → hysterectomy
🩺 Exam scenario
  • G3, 34 wk, painless bright bleed, head high → Ultrasound for the placental site; no PV exam
6Abruption
  • Premature separation of a normally sited placenta
  • Concealed or revealed
  • Risks: previous abruption, pre-eclampsia, trauma, smoking, cocaine, polyhydramnios with sudden ROM, thrombophilia
  • Clot strips the placenta → fetal hypoxia
  • Blood into the muscle → Couvelaire uterus
  • Thromboplastin → DIC (commonest cause of DIC in pregnancy)
  • Scan misses most clots
  • Complications: shock, DIC, PPH, AKI, Sheehan, fetal death
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Couvelaire uterus is not itself a reason for hysterectomy: uterotonics, massage, compression sutures first
7Abruption: management
  • Mother unstable or DIC: resuscitate, correct clotting (tranexamic acid 1 g IV, platelets if under 50, FFP 15 mL/kg), deliver now; no IM injections
  • Fetus alive and distressed: emergency caesarean
  • Fetus dead, mother stable: vaginal birth, ARM + oxytocin (labour is fast)
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Abruption, FHR absent, mother stable → ARM + oxytocin, vaginal birth
  • Bleeding from cannula sites → DIC: FFP, cryoprecipitate
