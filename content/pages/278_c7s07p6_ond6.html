Exam trap · 3rd and 4th degree

Not repaired in the labour room: theatre, regional or general anaesthesia, an experienced operator; EAS end-to-end or overlap, IAS and mucosa separately. Then antibiotics, laxatives, physiotherapy, review at 6–12 wk.
