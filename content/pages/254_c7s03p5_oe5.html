Four grips, always in this order. Use the flat of the palms, slow and gentle; talk to her so the wall stays soft. Grips 1–3: face her head. Grip 4: face her feet.

Grip · how · what it tellsماذا تقول
1 · Fundal grip قبضة القاع
1Both palms, fingers together, cup the fundus. What is in the fundus? Breech = soft, broad, irregular, moves with the trunk · head = hard, round, ballotable · empty = transverse lieحأضغط بإيديني على أعلى البطن، ممكن تحسي بضغط بسيط.
2 · Lateral grip القبضة الجانبية
2Hands at umbilical level, one each side. One steadies, the other palpates top → bottom, then swap. Back = smooth, firm, continuous curve · limbs = small knobs, kicks. Back side gives the position (left or right) and where to listenارخي بطنك وخذي نفس عادي.
3 · Pawlik grip (1st pelvic) قبضة باولك
3Right hand only, thumb and fingers wide, just above the symphysis; grasp the lower pole gently. What presents? Head: hard, round, ballotable if not engaged. Fixed = in the pelvisده ممكن يضايقك شوية، حأكون بالراحة. لو وجعك قولي لي.

4 · Pelvic grip (2nd pelvic) القبضة الحوضية
4Face her feet. Both hands flat on the lower abdomen, fingers slide down and in towards the pelvis. Hands converge = head above the brim (not engaged) · diverge = engaged. Count the fifths felt (next page). Cephalic prominence (sinciput) on the side opposite the back = flexed; on the same side as the back = extended (face)ثني ركبك أكتر شوية، وحأضغط تحت البطن فوق العظم.
9. Abnormal lie or presentation: consider why

Uterus: lax (grand multipara), fibroid, septate · Liquor: polyhydramnios · Placenta: praevia · Baby: preterm, twins, anomaly · Pelvis: contracted. Any of these after 36 wk → scan.
