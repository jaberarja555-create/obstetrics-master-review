7Severe malaria and complications
  • HDU, left lateral, airway, O₂
  • Glucose at once, film, Hb, creatinine; IV artesunate now
  • Fits: diazepam, or MgSO₄ if eclampsia is possible
  • Careful fluids, catheter, hourly urine
  • Hypoglycaemia: 50 mL 50% dextrose then 10% infusion
  • Severe anaemia: Hb below 5 (below 7 after 36 wk): slow packed cells
  • Pulmonary oedema: sit up, O₂, stop fluids, furosemide
  • Shock: fluids, cultures, ceftriaxone
  • Labour: CTG, CS for obstetric reasons only; film on the newborn
8Prevention
  • IPTp-SP (3 tablets, observed) from 13 wk at every scheduled visit at least 4 wk apart, at least 3 doses (WHO); local clinics often give two
  • Insecticide-treated net every night, indoor spraying, iron and folate
⚠️ Exam traps
  • No SP in the first trimester or with cotrimoxazole; folic acid 0.4 mg, not 5 mg
  • No primaquine in pregnancy (fetal G6PD haemolysis)
2.7Cardiac disease in pregnancy
Heart disease is a leading indirect cause of maternal death. Cardiac output rises by 40% and a diseased heart cannot meet the load. Rheumatic heart disease is the commonest cause locally and mitral stenosis the commonest lesion.
1Physiological changes and danger times
  • Cardiac output +40%, blood volume +40–50%, resistance falls
  • Three danger times: 24–32 wk (plateau), labour (each contraction adds 300–500 mL), and just after birth (autotransfusion: the highest load)
  • Normal: mild breathlessness, ankle oedema, bounding pulse, ejection systolic murmur, third sound
  • Investigate: orthopnoea, PND, haemoptysis, syncope, pulse over 100 or irregular, any diastolic murmur, raised JVP, basal crackles, cyanosis
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Cyanosis, loud P2, known VSD → Eisenmenger syndrome
