Station: Fever and Malaria

The station

"Rasha, 19, primigravida at 24 weeks, from Gezira, has had fever for 3 days. Take a focused history." Then: differential, first test, danger signs.

Ask · points towardماذا تقول
Opening (7.1) · dates · G/P البداية

2Rigors, sweating, headache, joint pains → malariaبتجيك رجفة وبرد وبعدها عرق؟ عندك صداع؟

Presenting the case

"Rasha, 19, primigravida at 24 weeks from Gezira, has fever with rigors and headache for 3 days, vomiting twice. No fits, confusion, jaundice or dysuria; baby moving. She sleeps without a net and has had no IPTp. Impression: uncomplicated malaria; differential pyelonephritis. I would do a blood film or RDT, RBG, Hb and urine."

Common mistake

Stopping at "malaria" without asking the severe features: they decide oral ACT versus IV artesunate.
