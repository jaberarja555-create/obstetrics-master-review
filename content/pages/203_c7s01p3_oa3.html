7. Asking well
  • Funnel: open → focused → closed. "Tell me about the bleeding" → "how many pads?" → "any clots?"
  • One question at a time: not "any headache or vision or pain?"
  • No leading questions: not "you have no headache, right?"
  • Sensitive questions: say why you ask, then ask plainly.
What to say · ICE
"What do you think is causing this? What worries you most? What were you hoping we would do today?"
