3Eclampsia drill
  • Help
  • Left lateral, airway, O₂
  • MgSO₄ 4 g IV over 5–15 min (8 mL of 50% diluted to 20 mL) → 1 g/h for 24 h
  • Recurrent fit: 2 g
  • Control BP (labetalol, hydralazine)
  • Fluids ≤ 80 mL/h
  • Deliver once stable
  • No pump: 4 g IV + 10 g IM (5 g each buttock), then 5 g IM 4-hourly
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Fit at 36 wk, BP 170/112 → Eclampsia: MgSO₄ 4 g IV
4Magnesium toxicity
  • Before each dose: knee jerk present, RR ≥ 12, urine ≥ 30 mL/h
  • Order of signs: lost reflexes → respiratory depression → arrest
  • Antidote calcium gluconate 10 mL of 10% IV over 10 min
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Reflexes absent, RR 16 → Stop MgSO₄, check level
5Shoulder dystocia drill (HELPERR)
  • Help
  • Evaluate episiotomy
  • Legs (McRoberts)
  • Suprapubic pressure from the baby's back (80% delivered)
  • Enter (Rubin II, Woods screw)
  • Remove the posterior arm
  • Roll onto all fours
  • Record each manoeuvre, its time and who did it
  • No fundal pressure
