7.8Operative Delivery Stations

1. The station

(1) A viva: "When would you use a ventouse? What must be present first?" · (2) apply a ventouse or forceps on a model pelvis · (3) consent a woman for assisted birth (10–15% of births need instruments).

2. Indications
Maternal
  • Exhaustion
  • Prolonged 2nd stage: primi > 2 h (3 h epidural), multi > 1 h (2 h epidural)
  • Must not push: heart disease, hypertensive crisis, cerebral aneurysm, retinal detachment
Fetal
  • Fetal distress in the 2nd stage
  • Aftercoming head of breech: forceps (Piper)
  • Malposition with delay (OT, OP): rotation by Kiwi or Kielland
