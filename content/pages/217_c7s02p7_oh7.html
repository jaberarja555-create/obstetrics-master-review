Presenting the case

"Fatima, 26, G3 P1+1 at 9 weeks, has heavy bleeding with clots and central colicky pain since this morning; she passed some tissue. No shoulder pain, no fever. Impression: incomplete miscarriage; ectopic must be excluded. I would check pulse and BP, do a speculum exam and a scan, FBC, group and Rh."
