Test · Treat · Suppress · Protect. Test at booking, treat for life, suppress the viral load by 36 wk, protect the baby.
6. Antenatal management
ART for allStart as soon as diagnosed (same day if ready), any CD4, any gestation, for life. Already on ART: continue it
RegimenTLD = tenofovir + lamivudine + dolutegravir, 1 tablet daily (300/300/50 mg). Folic acid
MonitorViral load 3 months after starting and at 34–36 wk (birth plan). Check adherence
Cotrimoxazole960 mg daily if CD4 ≤ 350 or WHO stage 3–4. Protects against PCP, toxoplasma and malaria
Routine ANCLook for diarrhoea, thrush, chest infection, zoster, STIs. Avoid amniocentesis, CVS
Exam trap

On cotrimoxazole? No SP (Fansidar) IPTp: two sulfa drugs = severe skin reactions, and cotrimoxazole already prevents malaria.

Clinical scenario
Newly diagnosed at 16 wk, CD4 500. When to start ART and for how long?
Now, and lifelong.
7. Mode of delivery
Clinical picture
Elective caesarean
VL ≥ 1000 at 36 wk+not in labour=CS 38 wk + IV AZT
Clinical scenario
On ART, VL < 50 at 36 wk, wants a normal birth. Advice?
Vaginal birth; continue oral ART; no IV AZT.
The viral load decides everything: mode of birth, IV zidovudine and how long the baby takes prophylaxis.
