12. Supportive and surgical care
  • IV fluids, oxygen, paracetamol, NSAID if no AKI
  • Enoxaparin 40 mg SC daily (sepsis = VTE risk)
  • Correct anaemia; blood if Hb low
  • Breastfeed or express; baby checked (GAS)
  • Ice packs: perineum, mastitis
  • Evacuate retained products gently, after antibiotics start
  • Drain pus: breast, wound, pelvic abscess (US-guided, colpotomy, laparotomy)
  • Debride necrotising fasciitis early
  • Hysterectomy if the uterus is necrotic or gas-forming and she is not improving
Clinical scenario
Endometritis, 3 days of antibiotics, still febrile, bulky uterus. Next step?
Ultrasound for retained products; evacuate.
