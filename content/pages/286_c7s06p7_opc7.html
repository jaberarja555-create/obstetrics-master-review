Viva questions
Commonest deceleration in labour?
Variable (cord compression).
Late decelerations with normal variability: worse or better than with low variability?
Better: low variability means the fetus is decompensating.
15. Example 5 · prolonged deceleration (bradycardia)
READ IT · Normal trace, then the rate drops to about 80 and stays for more than 3 min (B). Pathological, emergency. Consider: cord prolapse, abruption, uterine rupture, hypotension after spinal, tachysystole.
