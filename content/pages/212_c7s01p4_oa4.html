Exam trap

Do not give the news while standing, outside a private room, or while still busy with the scan probe. Do not fill the silence with numbers. Do not say "you can try again" before she has taken in the news: let her lead.

Distinguishing features · What she does
She goes silentWait; do not fill the gap
She denies: "the scan is wrong"Offer a second scan, gently repeat
She is angry at staffAcknowledge, do not argue
She refuses a CS after counsellingRespect, document, keep caring
Husband asks you to hide the newsShe has the right to know
10. Informed consent
  • Capacity: she understands, retains, weighs and tells her choice
  • Information: what, why, benefits, risks (common and serious), alternatives, doing nothing
  • Voluntary: no pressure from staff or family
  • Written for operations: signed, dated, with a witness
  • Emergency, she cannot consent (eclampsia, collapse): treat to save life, explain later
  • The family expects to be involved: involve the husband if she wishes, but her own consent counts
  • She may change her mind at any time
What to say · checking understanding (teach-back)
"I have said a lot. To be sure I explained it well, can you tell me in your own words what we will do?"
