Artemether–lumefantrine (AL)Artemisinin + lumefantrine
Main use
Uncomplicated malaria: 1st line
Dose · route · how long
20/120 mg: 4 tablets per dose × 6 doses (0, 8, 24, 36, 48, 60 h = 3 days) with fatty food or milk
Side effects · monitor
Headache, dizziness · vomit < 30 min → repeat dose
When · cautions
All trimesters (Sudan 2023)
Dihydroartemisinin–piperaquineArtemisinin + piperaquine
Main use
Uncomplicated malaria: 2nd line
Dose · route · how long
Once daily × 3 days, dose by weight
Side effects · monitor
QT prolongation
When · cautions
2nd and 3rd trimester only
Artesunate IV / IMRapid parasite clearance
Main use
Severe malaria: 1st line
Dose · route · how long
2.4 mg/kg IV at 0, 12, 24 h, then daily · after ≥ 24 h and able to swallow → full AL course
Side effects · monitor
Delayed haemolysis (check Hb at 2–4 wk)
When · cautions
All trimesters · IM if no vein
QuinineBlood schizonticide
Main use
Severe malaria: 2nd line · no ACT
Dose · route · how long
10 mg/kg 8-hourly IV over 4 h in dextrose · or PO × 7 days
Side effects · monitor
Hypoglycaemia, tinnitus (cinchonism), arrhythmia
When · cautions
Monitor glucose hourly
Sulfadoxine–pyrimethamineAntifolate
Main use
IPTp (malaria prevention)
Dose · route · how long
3 tablets (500/25 mg) stat, observed · 2 doses ≥ 4 wk apart
Side effects · monitor
Rash (rare SJS)
When · cautions
Not 1st trimester, not with cotrimoxazole · folic acid 0.4 mg (not 5)
Cefalexin · nitrofurantoinCell wall · bacterial DNA
Main use
Cystitis, asymptomatic bacteriuria
Dose · route · how long
Cefalexin 500 mg PO TDS × 7 d · nitrofurantoin 100 mg PO BD × 5–7 d
Side effects · monitor
GI upset
When · cautions
Nitrofurantoin: avoid at term (neonatal haemolysis)
Ceftriaxone3rd-gen cephalosporin
Main use
Pyelonephritis · sepsis, endometritis · septic abortion
Dose · route · how long
1–2 g IV OD (+ metronidazole 500 mg IV TDS for pelvic sepsis)
Side effects · monitor
Allergy, diarrhoea
When · cautions
All trimesters
