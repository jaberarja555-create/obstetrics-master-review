22. Viva questions
Cervix 4 cm at 10 am. When is the next VE?2 pm (4 h); expect ≥ 8 cm
Cervix 6 cm, then 6 cm 4 h later. Term?Arrest: crosses the action line (7.6)
VE finds a breech: what do you feel?Ischial tuberosities + anus in a line, ± sacrum; meconium on the glove
Face: how is it told from breech?Mouth (gums) and malar bones make a triangle; anus and tuberosities a line
Caput vs cephalhaematoma?Caput crosses sutures, gone in days; cephalhaematoma stops at sutures
Distinguishing features · What you report
Soft swelling crosses the suturesCaput
Bones overlap, push back easilyMoulding +2
Anus and tuberosities in a lineBreech
Moulding +3, caput +3, head 3/5Obstructed: CS
Orbits, nose, mouth, gumsFace presentation
23. Examiner marking points
  • Abdomen before the VE
  • Asks about praevia / scan
  • Between contractions, gently
  • All cervix items + station
  • Checks for cord, looks at the glove
  • FHR after, partogram
  • Dilatation in cm, not fingers
  • Gives length or % effacement
  • Consistency and position
  • Membranes, liquor, no cord
  • Bishop added correctly
  • Station against the spines
  • Bone, not caput, measured
  • Finds the sagittal suture first
  • Posterior fontanelle = occiput
  • Names the position (e.g. LOA)
  • Grades moulding 0 to +3
  • Moulding vs caput told apart
  • Knows +3 moulding = obstruction
  • Findings in the right order
  • Full, ordered presentation
  • Explains result to her
