3.2Antepartum haemorrhage
Painless bright red bleeding at 34 weeks, soft uterus, high head → Placenta praevia: no PV, scan
Painful bleeding, woody hard uterus, shock worse than the visible loss → Abruption (concealed)
Bleeding at ROM with sudden fetal bradycardia → Vasa praevia: immediate CS
Definition
Bleeding from the genital tract after 28 wk (24 wk in some guidelines) before birth
Praevia
Painless, soft uterus, malpresentation · ultrasound diagnosis · low at 20 wk → rescan 32 wk · major praevia: CS at 36–37 wk · edge > 2 cm from os: vaginal birth possible
Abruption
Pain, tender hard uterus, fetal distress or death · clinical diagnosis · risks: PE, trauma, smoking, cocaine · commonest cause of DIC
First steps
Two 14–16 G cannulae, cross-match 4 units · warm fluids · urine ≥ 30 mL/h · clot test (no clot at 7–10 min = DIC) · steroids < 34 wk
Abruption, dead fetus
Mother stable → vaginal delivery (ARM + oxytocin) · correct DIC: FFP 15 mL/kg, cryo
Accreta (PAS)
Praevia + previous CS (2 CS ≈ 40%) · planned CS at 36 wk with 6–8 units · leave placenta, hysterectomy
Exam traps
No digital VE until praevia is excluded · Apt test detects fetal blood · Rh-negative: anti-D 500 IU within 72 h
3.3Postpartum haemorrhage
800 mL blood loss after vaginal birth, soft uterus → Atonic PPH: rub uterus, oxytocin
Contracted uterus, still bleeding → Genital tract trauma: examine and repair
Shock after delivery with no fundus felt and a mass in the vagina → Uterine inversion
Definitions
Primary ≥ 500 mL (vaginal) or ≥ 1000 mL (CS) in 24 h · secondary 24 h to 12 wk
4 Ts
Tone (70%) · trauma · tissue (retained placenta) · thrombin (DIC)
Drugs
Oxytocin 10 IU IV/IM then 40 IU infusion · ergometrine 0.5 mg (not in hypertension) · misoprostol 800 µg · carboprost 250 µg (not in asthma) · tranexamic acid 1 g within 3 h
Mechanical and surgery
Bimanual compression · balloon tamponade · B-Lynch · uterine/internal iliac ligation · hysterectomy
Resuscitation
Help, O₂ 15 L/min, 2 cannulae, cross-match · FFP : red cells 4 : 6 · platelets < 50 · cryo if fibrinogen < 2 g/L
Prevention
AMTSL: oxytocin 10 IU IM · controlled cord traction · uterine massage · placenta not out at 30 min → manual removal
Exam traps
Watch every woman for 4 h after birth · secondary PPH = endometritis ± retained products · no lactation and amenorrhoea after PPH = Sheehan syndrome
