2Aetiology and classification
  • Acquired: rheumatic heart disease, mitral stenosis, peripartum cardiomyopathy, IHD
  • Congenital: ASD, VSD, PDA; Fallot, Eisenmenger; coarctation; Marfan
  • NYHA I no symptoms
  • II moderate effort
  • III mild effort
  • IV at rest
  • Often worsens by one class as pregnancy advances
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Rheumatic history, mid-diastolic murmur, breathless at 28 wk → Mitral stenosis
3Pregnancy contraindicated
  • Pulmonary hypertension and Eisenmenger (maternal death 40–60%)
  • Severe MS or AS
  • EF below 30% or NYHA III–IV
  • Previous peripartum cardiomyopathy with poor recovery
  • Marfan with aortic root over 4 cm
  • Advise against pregnancy; offer termination
  • Stenotic lesions are worse than regurgitant (fixed output)
4Investigations and complications
  • Echocardiography is the key test (valve area, EF, pulmonary pressure, aortic root)
  • ECG, FBC (anaemia strains the heart), INR on warfarin
  • Maternal: heart failure and pulmonary oedema, AF and stroke, valve thrombosis, endocarditis, PPH, death
  • Fetal: FGR, preterm birth, stillbirth, congenital heart disease 3–5% if the mother has CHD, warfarin effects
🩺 Exam scenario
  • MS, 28 wk or just after birth, pink frothy sputum → Pulmonary oedema: sit up, furosemide
