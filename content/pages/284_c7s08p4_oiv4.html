12. Common indications
Maternal≥ 2 previous CS · classical scar, cavity-entering myomectomy · obstructing fibroid · contracted pelvis · HIV with high viral load · primary genital herpes
FetalFetal distress in the 1st stage · malpresentation: breech, transverse lie, brow, mento-posterior · cord prolapse · monoamniotic twins, first twin non-cephalic
LabourObstructed labour / CPD · failure to progress after augmentation · failed induction · failed instrumental delivery
PlacentalMajor placenta praevia · abruption with a live, distressed fetus · vasa praevia
13. Consent: points to cover
  • Why CS, and the alternatives (and their risks)
  • What happens: spinal, catheter, drip, the cut (lower, bikini line)
  • Common risks: pain, bleeding, infection, VTE, longer stay
  • Serious: bladder or bowel injury, hysterectomy for bleeding, transfusion, baby's skin cut
  • Future: placenta praevia/accreta, scar rupture; space births ≥ 18 months
  • Questions; sign; family informed if she wishes
What to say · consent
"The baby is lying across / the labour is not progressing, so the safest way is a caesarean. You will have an injection in the back so you are awake but feel no pain. Main risks: bleeding, infection, clots, rarely bladder injury. Do you have any questions?"
«الجنا راقد بالعرض / الولادة ما ماشية، فأسلم طريقة العملية القيصرية. حنديك حقنة في الضهر، حتكوني صاحية وما حتحسي بوجع. المخاطر: نزيف أو التهاب أو جلطة، ونادراً إصابة المثانة. عندك أي سؤال؟»
