Normal lochia

Smell is mild, not foul. Amount falls day by day. Total about 250 mL. Heavier on standing and when feeding.

Abnormal lochia

Foul → infection. Red again or persistent rubra → retained tissue, subinvolution. None + fever → blocked drainage (lochiometra).
