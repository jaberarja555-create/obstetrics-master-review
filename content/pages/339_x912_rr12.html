Chapter4
Labour
4.1Normal labour
Regular painful contractions with a cervix changing from 2 to 5 cm → Established labour
Irregular pains, cervix unchanged after 2 hours → False labour
Placenta not delivered 30 minutes after active management → Retained placenta: manual removal
Stages
1st: active phase from 4 cm to full dilatation (primi 12–18 h, multi 6–8 h) · 2nd: full to birth (primi 2 h, 3 h with epidural; multi 1 h, 2 h) · 3rd: placenta
Mechanism
Engagement → descent → flexion → internal rotation → extension → restitution → external rotation → expulsion
Diameters
SOB 9.5 cm (flexed vertex) · inlet AP 11 cm · interspinous 10.5 cm (narrowest)
Monitoring
Low risk: intermittent auscultation (15–30 min in 1st stage, 5 min in 2nd) · VE every 4 h · contractions 3–5/10 min
Third stage
AMTSL: oxytocin 10 IU IM within 1 min · controlled cord traction · massage · delayed cord clamping 1–3 min
Separation signs
Cord lengthens, fundus rises and becomes globular, gush of blood
Exam traps
Observe every woman at least 4 h after birth · head 2/5 palpable = engaged · station 0 = ischial spines
4.2Monitoring in labour
Cervix crosses the action line → Slow progress: ARM + oxytocin, reassess in 2 h
Good contractions, no descent, moulding +3, caput → Obstructed labour: CS
Late decelerations with reduced variability → Placental insufficiency: fetal blood sample or deliver
Partogram
Start at 4 cm · alert line 1 cm/h · action line 4 h to the right · < 2 cm in 4 h = slow
Partogram symbols
Cervix X · descent O (fifths) · liquor C/M/B · moulding +1 to +3 · contraction boxes (solid > 40 s)
CTG normal
Baseline 110–160 · variability 5–25 bpm · accelerations · no decelerations
Decelerations
Early: head compression (mirror) · late: placental insufficiency · variable: cord · prolonged > 3 min · bradycardia < 100
Obstructed labour
Prolonged labour > 18 h · Bandl's ring · haematuria, oedematous vulva · catheter 14 days after (fistula)
Fetal scalp pH
≤ 7.20 → deliver
Exam traps
Oxytocin in an obstructed multipara → uterine rupture · head ≥ 3/5 palpable at full dilatation = CPD · decide by the woman and fetus, not the line alone
