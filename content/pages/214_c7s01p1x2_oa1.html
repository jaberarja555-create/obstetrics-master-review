3. The 5-minute plan
Viva questions
Task: "Take a history from this woman with bleeding at 34 wk." Do you do a PV?
No: history only. Say you would avoid PV until praevia is excluded.
The bell rings at 4 min and you are not done. What now?
Stop, summarise to the patient, thank her, close.
4. Before you knock
  1. Read the task twice. Who, how many weeks, and the verb: history, examine, counsel?
  2. Note what is given (model, chart, result) and any "do not" line.
  3. Run the checklist in your head: opening → task → closing.
  4. Knock, enter, greet the patient first.
Where students lose easy marks

No hand hygiene or consent · doing more than asked · talking to the examiner, not the patient · rough with the model · no summary · a 2-minute opening.

5. Universal Opening & Closing

The same opening and closing start and end every station with a patient. They carry easy marks and take 30 seconds each.
