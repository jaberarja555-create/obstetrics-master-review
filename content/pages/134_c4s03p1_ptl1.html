4.3Preterm Labour

1. Definition

Preterm labour = regular contractions with cervical change after 24 wk and before 37 completed weeks. About 10% of births; the leading cause of perinatal death and handicap.

Clinical types: threatened = true contractions, cervix not changing · established = contractions + progressive dilatation.

3. Aetiology and pathophysiology
FOUR PATHWAYS, ONE ROAD · Infection is the commonest proven cause: 10–15% of amniotic fluid cultures are positive; histological chorioamnionitis is far commoner than clinical.
4. Risk factors
  • Previous preterm birth
  • Twins, polyhydramnios
  • Uterine anomaly · cone biopsy
  • Pyelonephritis, malaria, pneumonia
  • APH · pre-eclampsia · diabetes
  • Age < 17 or > 35 yr
  • BMI < 20 · interval < 1 yr
  • Primigravida · grand multipara
  • Smoking · poverty · stress
  • Family history (genetic)

Planned (iatrogenic) early birth: severe pre-eclampsia, severe FGR, APH, monochorionic monoamniotic twins at 32 wk.
