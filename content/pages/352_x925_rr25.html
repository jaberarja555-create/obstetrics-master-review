7.11Counselling stations
Counselling after IUFD → Explain options: induction most common; mifepristone then misoprostol
Woman on warfarin for a valve planning pregnancy → Switch to LMWH before 6 weeks
Woman with epilepsy on valproate → Change drug, folic acid 5 mg before conception
Frame
Introduce · establish what she knows · facts in 2–3 chunks · options · check understanding · ICE · plan · leaflets and follow-up
IUFD
Labour within 3 wk in 85% · DIC ≈ 30% after 3 wk · mifepristone 200 mg, then misoprostol · cabergoline 1 mg · investigations · 6-wk review
Cardiac disease
Cardiac output +40% · joint clinic · fetal echo 20–22 wk · LMWH · no ergometrine · HDU ≥ 3 days
Epilepsy
Fit-free 2 years ideal · avoid valproate · folic acid 5 mg 3 months before · baby vitamin K 1 mg
Contraception
LAM 98% · IUD within 48 h or after 4 wk · no method needed before 21 d
VBAC
Success 72–75% · rupture ≈ 0.5% · deliver in hospital with continuous CTG
Exam traps
Never leave a medical word unexplained · check she understood (teach-back) · offer written information and a follow-up date
