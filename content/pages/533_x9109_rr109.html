7.4Blood pressure & urine testing
Two procedure stations: measuring blood pressure correctly in pregnancy, and testing urine with a dipstick (with explaining a midstream or 24-hour collection).
1Blood pressure technique
  • Rest 5 min, sitting or semi-recumbent 45°, feet flat, arm at heart level
  • Cuff bladder covers ≥ 80% of the arm (large cuff if arm > 33 cm)
  • Palpate systolic, inflate 30 mmHg above
  • Deflate 2 mmHg/s
  • Record to the nearest 2 mmHg
2Korotkoff sounds
  • Systolic = K1
  • Diastolic = K5 (disappearance)
  • Use K4 only if sounds go on to 0
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Silent gap from 150 to 130 → Auscultatory gap: palpate systolic first
