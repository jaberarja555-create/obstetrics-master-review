⚠️ Exam trap
  • The load is 75 g, not 75 mg; never diagnose from the urine dipstick
4Investigations in pre-existing diabetes
  • HbA1c (aim under 6.5%), creatinine and urine protein, fundoscopy at booking and each trimester, FBC, MSU
  • Fetus: early dating scan, anomaly scan + fetal echocardiography at 18–20 wk, growth scans every 4 wk from 24–36 wk, CTG late
5Maternal and fetal complications
  • Mother: hypoglycaemia, DKA, pre-eclampsia, polyhydramnios, preterm labour, infections, worse retinopathy and nephropathy; obstructed labour, operative birth, PPH
  • Type 2 diabetes in about 50% within 10 years after GDM
  • Fetus: miscarriage, anomalies (pre-existing only)
  • Commonest anomaly: cardiac; most characteristic: caudal regression
  • Macrosomia; FGR with vascular disease; unexplained stillbirth after 36 wk
  • Birth: shoulder dystocia, Erb palsy
  • Neonate: hypoglycaemia (commonest), RDS, polycythaemia, jaundice, hypocalcaemia
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Macrosomic baby, shaky at 2 h → Hypoglycaemia
  • Type 1 with nephropathy, small fetus → FGR (vascular disease)
6Pre-pregnancy and antenatal care
  • HbA1c under 6.5% before conceiving; above 10%: avoid pregnancy for now
  • Folic acid 5 mg from 3 months before conception to 12 wk
  • Stop ACE inhibitors, ARBs, statins; oral drugs except metformin → insulin
  • MDT clinic, aspirin 75 mg from 12 wk, BP and urine every visit
  • Targets: fasting under 5.3, 1 h after meals under 7.8, 2 h under 6.4 mmol/L
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Type 2 on enalapril and atorvastatin, planning pregnancy → Stop both; folic acid 5 mg
