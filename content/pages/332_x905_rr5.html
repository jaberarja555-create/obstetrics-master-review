2.6Malaria in pregnancy
Primigravida, fever, rigors, positive film, able to eat → Artemether-lumefantrine
Fever, convulsion, normal BP and no proteinuria → Cerebral malaria: IV artesunate
Fit with fever, BP 150/100 and proteinuria → Treat both eclampsia and malaria
At risk
Primigravidae · parasites hide in the placenta (CSA binding) → low birth weight, stillbirth
Investigations
Thick and thin films · RDT · RBG (hypoglycaemia) · Hb · BP and urine protein in every fit
Uncomplicated
AL 4 tablets twice daily for 3 days (any trimester) · 2nd line in 2nd/3rd trimester: DHA-piperaquine
Severe
IV artesunate 2.4 mg/kg at 0, 12, 24 h then daily · 2nd line IV quinine 10 mg/kg 8-hourly
Severe features
Fits, coma · hypoglycaemia < 2.2 mmol/L · parasitaemia > 5% · shock · severe anaemia · pulmonary oedema
Prevention
IPTp-SP after quickening, 2 or more doses ≥ 4 wk apart · bed nets
Exam traps
Quinine causes hypoglycaemia · primaquine is never given in pregnancy · anaemia is the commonest maternal complication
2.7Cardiac disease in pregnancy
Breathless, orthopnoea, diastolic murmur in a Sudanese woman → Rheumatic mitral stenosis
Heart failure in the month after delivery, EF < 45% → Peripartum cardiomyopathy
Mechanical valve, now 7 weeks pregnant → Change warfarin to LMWH (6–12 wk embryopathy)
Physiology
Cardiac output +40% · danger peaks: 24–32 wk, labour, immediately after delivery (+60–80%)
Causes
Rheumatic heart disease commonest · mitral stenosis commonest lesion · congenital heart disease · PPCM
Assessment
NYHA class · echocardiography · ECG · fetal echo at 20–22 wk if CHD
Antenatal care
Joint clinic · treat anaemia, infection · LMWH for valves · severe MS: balloon valvotomy
Labour
Early epidural · left tilt · short 2nd stage (instrument) · oxytocin 5 IU slowly · no ergometrine · HDU 3 days
Contraindicate pregnancy
Pulmonary hypertension, Eisenmenger · severe MS/AS · Marfan root > 4 cm · EF < 30%
Exam traps
A diastolic murmur is always pathological · the most dangerous moment is just after delivery (autotransfusion) · avoid ergometrine
