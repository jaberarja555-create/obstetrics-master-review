6.3Postpartum Mood Disorders

1. Definition

Mood disorders that start in the puerperium or the months after birth. They range from a mild, self-limited low mood to a psychiatric emergency.

2. Classification
Mild · 50–80%Baby blues · normal, passes
Moderate · 10–15%Postnatal depression · treat
Severe · 1–2 / 1000Puerperal psychosis · emergency
3. Onset by day after delivery
TIMING · Day of onset and length of illness are the first clues: psychosis early and fast, depression later and slow.
