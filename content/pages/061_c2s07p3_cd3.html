Exam trap

Ergometrine clamps the veins and pushes blood into the heart: acute pulmonary oedema. Fast oxytocin bolus drops BP; large oxytocin infusions overload with water. For PPH: oxytocin slowly, misoprostol; avoid carboprost (bronchospasm, pulmonary hypertension).

Delivery room: cardiac trolley (furosemide, digoxin, adrenaline), O₂, instruments for assisted birth, neonatal resuscitation. Endocarditis prophylaxis is not routine: only prosthetic valve or previous endocarditis.

10. Management: postpartum
  • HDU, stay ≥ 3 days: autotransfusion → pulmonary oedema in the first hours. Watch pulse, RR, crackles.
  • Restart LMWH 8–12 h after birth; mechanical valve → warfarin from day 5. Early walking, VTE risk check.
  • Breastfeeding is safe (warfarin, LMWH, beta-blockers).
Best next step

Breathless with crackles and falling SpO₂ after birth: sit up, O₂, stop IV fluids, furosemide 40 mg IV, call the cardiologist.

11. Contraception

Progestogen-only: implant, injection, LNG-IUS, POP. Avoid combined pill (thrombosis). Pregnancy contraindicated or family complete: sterilisation.

Peripartum cardiomyopathy

Heart failure from 32 wk of pregnancy to 6 months after birth, no other cause, EF < 45%. Risks: multiparity, twins, pre-eclampsia, age > 30, African women. Treat: furosemide, beta-blocker, hydralazine + nitrate (ACE inhibitor after birth), LMWH if EF < 35%. Half recover. Recurs in next pregnancy, worst if EF not normal: advise against.
