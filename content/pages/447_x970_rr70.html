5Term PROM
  • About 75% labour within 24 h
  • Observations and CTG as for PPROM, no steroids
  • Induce now (oxytocin; prostaglandin if unfavourable) or wait up to 24 h, then induce
  • Induce at once with meconium, GBS or any sign of infection
  • GBS prophylaxis if positive, ROM over 24 h or preterm
  • High head or malpresentation: check the FHR for cord prolapse
  • CS only for obstetric reasons
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Term PROM 26 h, no labour → Induce with oxytocin
  • Term PROM, GBS positive → IV penicillin, induce now
6Chorioamnionitis
  • Ascending infection of membranes, liquor and placenta (GBS, E. coli, anaerobes)
  • Mother: fever over 37.8 °C, tachycardia, uterine tenderness, offensive liquor
  • Fetal tachycardia over 160, often the first sign
  • Rising WBC and CRP; HVS and blood cultures
  • Ampicillin + gentamicin + metronidazole IV, continued after birth
  • Deliver at any gestation; CS only for obstetric reasons; no tocolysis; never wait for steroids
  • Complications: sepsis, PPH, endometritis; neonatal sepsis, meningitis, cerebral palsy
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Chorioamnionitis at 28 wk → IV antibiotics and deliver; do not wait for steroids
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Antibiotics alone cannot clear an infected uterus
