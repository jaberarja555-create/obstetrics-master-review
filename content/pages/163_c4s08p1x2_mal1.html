3. Outcome in labour
THREE WAYS OUT · Good contractions, a roomy pelvis and a firm pelvic floor favour long anterior rotation.
4. Management

1st stage: partograph, fluids, analgesia, empty bladder; oxytocin only for weak contractions without CPD. Face to pubis: deliver by flexion, generous episiotomy. Deep transverse arrest (sagittal suture transverse at the spines, no descent): manual rotation, Kielland or ventouse if skilled, else caesarean.

Complications: prolonged labour, PROM, 3rd-degree tear, PPH, sepsis · fetal hypoxia, birth injury.

Face and Brow Presentation

5. Definition

Face: head fully extended, occiput on the back; denominator = mentum (chin). Brow: head midway between flexion and extension; denominator = frontal bone.
