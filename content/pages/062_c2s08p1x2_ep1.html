6. Management: pre-pregnancy
  • Team: obstetrician + neurologist. Plan pregnancy; ideally fit-free for 2 years. One drug, lowest dose; switch from valproate before conception.
  • Folic acid 5 mg daily from 3 months before conception and throughout pregnancy.
  • Never stop drugs suddenly on finding pregnancy: status epilepticus is more dangerous than the drug.
7. Management: antenatal
  • Continue the same drugs, even if vomiting; change only for frequent fits; levels not routine.
  • Scans: dating · anomaly scan 18–20+6 wk · fetal echo 22–24 wk · AFP 15–22 wk · serial growth 24–36 wk. BP + urine every visit.
  • Vitamin K: enzyme inducers → baby's vitamin K deficiency. Give the baby 1 mg IM at birth.
