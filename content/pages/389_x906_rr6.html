5Expected date of delivery and dating
  • Gestation counts from the first day of the LMP; term = 280 days
  • Naegele's rule: LMP + 7 days + 9 months (LMP 10 Jan 2026 → EDD 17 Oct 2026)
  • Longer cycle: add the extra days (35-day cycle: +7)
  • LMP unreliable (unsure, irregular, pill or DMPA in the last 3 months, breastfeeding, early bleeding): date by scan
  • CRL at 11–14 wk: ± 5 days; head at 14–20 wk ± 7–10 days; third trimester ± 3 wk (not for dating)
  • Scan and LMP differ by more than 5 days: use the scan
  • Never re-date after a reliable early scan: a small fetus later is growth restriction
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • LMP 10 Jan 2026, regular 35-day cycles → EDD 24 Oct 2026
  • First scan at 34 wk shows a small baby → Do not re-date: assess growth
6Terms for gestational age
  • First trimester to 13⁺⁶ wk; second 14–27⁺⁶; third from 28 wk
  • Loss before 24 wk = miscarriage; after = stillbirth
  • Preterm under 37 wk: extremely preterm under 28, very preterm 28–31⁺⁶, moderate to late 32–36⁺⁶
  • Term 37–41⁺⁶: early term 37–38⁺⁶, full term 39–40⁺⁶, late term 41–41⁺⁶
  • Post-term 42 wk or more
