10. Uterine hyperstimulation
Tachysystole> 5 contractions / 10 min
Hypertonusone contraction > 2 min
Hyperstimulationeither + abnormal FHR
  1. Stop the oxytocin; remove a prostin tablet if it can be reached.
  2. Left lateral position · IV fluid bolus if she is hypotensive · continuous CTG.
  3. Tocolysis: terbutaline 0.25 mg SC if the FHR stays abnormal.
  4. FHR recovers → restart oxytocin after 30 min at a lower rate. Bradycardia persists → caesarean.
