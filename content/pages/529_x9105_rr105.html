  • Present in under 1 minute: who, why, positives, relevant negatives, impression, plan

7.2Focused history stations
A focused history station: about 5 minutes with a simulated patient, then the examiner asks your diagnosis and plan. Use the full frame and go deep only where her complaint points.
1The frame
  • Personal data
  • Presenting complaint
  • History of the pregnancy
  • Past obstetric
  • Gynaecological
  • Medical and surgical
  • Drugs, allergy
  • Family
  • Social
2Dates and parity
  • LMP, cycle, pill or lactation
  • EDD by Naegele: + 7 days − 3 months + 1 year (cycle 35 d: add 7 days)
  • Dating scan (CRL 8–13 wk) beats LMP
  • Gravida = all pregnancies
