8. Management: route of delivery
DECIDE BY · fetus alive or dead, gestation, stage of labour, presentation.
Alive, 1st stageCategory 1 CS, decision to delivery ≤ 30 min; GA, or spinal if it is quick
Alive, fully dilatedVertex, head low, quick birth likely: forceps or ventouse in the room
Fully dilated, head highHead not engaged or transverse lie: caesarean
Second twinBreech extraction, or ventouse if cephalic, at full dilatation
Dead fetus (scan)Vaginal: await labour or induce; no CS for the baby
Before 26 wkTreat as a miscarriage: vaginal, no caesarean

Fully dilated, transverse lieCaesarean

Cord felt, membranes intact, termNo ARM: CS
