Viva questions
What does the alert line represent?
The slowest normal rate: 1 cm/h from 4 cm.
Commonest cause of slow 1st stage in a primigravida?
Inefficient uterine contractions (power).
Exam trap

Crossing the alert line is not a reason for CS. In a health centre it means refer now. Exclude obstruction before oxytocin; extra care in a multipara.
