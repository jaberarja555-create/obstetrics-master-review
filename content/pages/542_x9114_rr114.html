4The CTG: DR C BRAVADO
  • Define risk
  • Contractions
  • Baseline rate 110–160
  • Variability 5–25
  • Accelerations (15 bpm for 15 s)
  • Decelerations
  • Overall
5Decelerations and categories
  • Early: head compression, benign
  • Variable: cord compression
  • Late: placental insufficiency
  • Prolonged > 3 min
  • Reduced variability > 50 min is pathological
  • Normal → continue; suspicious → correct causes; pathological → act, consider delivery
🩺 Exam scenario
  • FHR below 100 for more than 3 min → Prolonged deceleration: act now
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Head ≥ 3/5 palpable at full dilatation = CPD
7.7Normal delivery & episiotomy
Procedure station on a birthing model: conduct a normal delivery and the third stage, talking to the mother; often followed by an episiotomy and its repair.
1The drill
  • Crowning: keep the head flexed, guard the perineum
  • Head out slowly between contractions
  • Feel for cord round the neck
  • Wait for restitution
  • Anterior shoulder down, posterior up
  • Baby onto the mother
  • AMTSL
