9. Complications of prematurity
SystemShort-term (neonatal) · long-term
Lungs, heartRDS (no surfactant) (the commonest neonatal problem) · apnoea · chronic lung disease · PDA
BrainIntraventricular haemorrhage · periventricular leukomalacia → cerebral palsy, learning problems
Gut, infectionNecrotising enterocolitis · feeding difficulty · sepsis, meningitis
MetabolicHypothermia · hypoglycaemia · jaundice
Eyes, earsRetinopathy of prematurity → blindness · deafness
Why the preterm baby suffers

Every organ is unfinished: little surfactant, a fragile germinal matrix, thin gut and skin, no fat or glycogen. Each week in utero counts.

10. Management by gestation

Before any drug: admit · confirm gestation (LMP or early scan) · fetal wellbeing, growth, anomalies · tell the parents and the neonatal team early.
