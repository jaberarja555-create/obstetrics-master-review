5. How often in practice
Every antenatal visitBP + urine dipstick
Normal labourBP 4-hourly, pulse every 30 min
Severe pre-eclampsiaBP every 15 min until stable
After birth (PE)Keep measuring: eclampsia can follow birth
6. Viva questions
Why does BP fall in the 2nd trimester?Vessels relax: lowest at 20–24 wk
Drug for BP 170/115 at 36 wk?Labetalol IV or hydralazine 5 mg IV; nifedipine oral
Which drugs never in pregnancy?ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics
Marks commonly lost

Wrong cuff · arm hanging · no palpatory estimate · deflating fast · K4 by habit · one high reading called "pre-eclampsia" · no repeat, no urine test.

7. Examiner marking points
  • Right cuff chosen, and why
  • Seated, arm at heart level
  • Palpatory systolic first
  • Slow deflation, 2 mmHg/s
  • K5 for diastolic
  • Reading said and recorded
  • Names the small-cuff error
  • Knows when to use K4
  • Explains the auscultatory gap
  • Arm at heart level, supported
  • No talking, 5 min rest
  • Knows 140/90 and 160/110
  • Says what to do with a high reading
  • Reports arm, position, cuff, K5
  • Links BP to urine protein
  • Warns her of PE symptoms
