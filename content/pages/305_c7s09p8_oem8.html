Newborn Resuscitation

21. The station

Task (6 min, on a newborn mannequin): "A term baby is born after a long 2nd stage. He is floppy and not breathing. Start resuscitation and give the Apgar score." Equipment: warmer, dry towels, hat, bag-valve-mask with term (size 1) and preterm (size 0) masks, suction, stethoscope, clock.

Before birth: check the bag and mask fit, warmer on, towels warm, clock started at birth. Delay cord clamping only if the baby is breathing; a baby who needs help: clamp and move to the warmer.
