14. Pre-operative preparation
AreaWhat to do · why
1HistoryConfirm GA and indication · previous CS (type, number) · medical illness · allergies, anaesthetic problems · last meal · blood transfusions
2ExaminationPulse, BP, pallor, chest and heart · abdomen: lie, presentation, scar, FHR
3TestsHb, group and cross-match 2 units (4–6 if praevia, abruption or anaemia) · urine · scan for placental site if previous CS · CTG if fetal risk · ECG if heart disease or ≥ 40 y
4Fasting · antacidSolids 6 h, clear fluids 2 h (elective) · ranitidine 150 mg oral night before and morning (emergency 50 mg IV) + metoclopramide 10 mg: less acid aspiration
5LinesIV cannula 16–18 G, fluids · Foley catheter (empty bladder away from the knife)
6AntibioticSingle dose before the skin incision: cefuroxime 1.5 g IV (or ceftriaxone 1 g IV): halves wound and uterine infection
7In theatreSpinal preferred · left lateral tilt 15° · baby team ready · oxytocin 5 IU slow IV after the baby, then 10 IU in the drip
