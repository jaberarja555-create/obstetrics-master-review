Present it · to the examiner

“Amna, 32 weeks. On inspection: a distended abdomen, linea nigra, no scars, fetal movements seen. SFH 31 cm: compatible with dates. A singleton, longitudinal lie, cephalic presentation, back on the left: LOA. Head 5/5 palpable, not engaged. Liquor clinically normal. Fetal heart 140/min, regular. In summary: a normal singleton pregnancy at 32 weeks.”
