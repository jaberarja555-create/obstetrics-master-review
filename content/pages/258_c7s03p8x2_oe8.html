Viva questions
Define the presentation.
The fetal part that lies over the pelvic brim.
SFH 36 cm at 32 wk. Two causes you can find by hand?
Twins (extra poles) and polyhydramnios (fluid thrill).
What is the denominator of a vertex presentation?
The occiput.
18. Common errors
Marks commonly lost

Palpating before inspecting · fingertip poking · tape read with numbers up · forgetting the left tilt · Pawlik with both hands or roughly · listening on the limb side · counting the FHR for 15 s · never saying "engaged / not engaged" · not covering her before presenting.

19. Examiner marking points
  • Consent and a chaperone
  • Bladder emptied before measuring
  • Left tilt named and done
  • Exposure: no more than needed
  • Asks about pain before touching
  • Hand hygiene before and after
  • General look first, briefly
  • Checks pallor and oedema
  • Mentions BP and urine
  • Inspects before touching
  • Names scars, asks about CS
  • Comments on fetal movements
  • Centralises the uterus first
  • Fundus found with the hand edge, not fingertips
  • Tape blind, fundus → symphysis
  • States if it fits the dates
  • Hands < 24 wk, tape after
  • Records in cm, one reading
