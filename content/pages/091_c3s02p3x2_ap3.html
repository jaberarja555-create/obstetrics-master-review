22. Management
  • Mother unstable or DIC: resuscitate, correct clotting (tranexamic acid 1 g IV; platelets if < 50 with bleeding) and deliver now; no intramuscular injections while clotting is abnormal
  • Fetus alive, distressed: emergency caesarean once resuscitation has started
  • Fetus dead, mother stable: vaginal, ARM + oxytocin; labour is fast
Distinguishing features
Abruption, FHR absent, mother stableARM + oxytocin, vaginal
Abruption, FHR 90, 35 wkEmergency caesarean
Pain + bleed after road accidentTraumatic abruption
Bleeding from cannula sitesDIC: FFP, cryo (FFP 15 mL/kg)

23. Definition

Deficient decidua: villi attach too deep and the placenta will not separate.

24. Classification
TypeDepth of villiShare of PAS
AccretaAttached on the myometrium (no decidua)≈ 75%
IncretaInvade into the myometrium≈ 15–20%
PercretaGo through the serosa, into bladder or bowel≈ 5%
25. Risk factors
  • Praevia + previous caesarean
  • Myomectomy, D&C, Asherman
  • IVF · age > 35 · multiparity
26. Pathophysiology
  • Scar: no decidua (Nitabuch layer)
  • Villi anchor in muscle
  • No cleavage plane → very heavy bleed
