2.10Acute Fatty Liver of Pregnancy

1. Definition

AFLP = acute liver failure from microvesicular fat in liver cells, in the 3rd trimester (or immediately after birth). Rare (about 1 in 10 000) but deadly: an obstetric emergency.

2. Risk factors
  • Twin pregnancy (9–25% of cases)
  • Male fetus (about 3 : 1)
  • Coexisting mild PE (30–60%)
  • First pregnancy · low BMI
3. Pathophysiology
PATHOPHYSIOLOGY · A mitochondrial fault in fatty-acid oxidation (often a fetal LCHAD deficiency) floods the mother's liver with fat.
Clinical scenario
AFLP mother, newborn later has low glucose. Which defect to test the baby for?
LCHAD (fatty-acid oxidation) deficiency.
4. Clinical features
  • Days of nausea, vomiting, malaise, anorexia
  • Abdominal pain (epigastric / RUQ)
  • Jaundice · headache · fever
  • Polyuria, polydipsia (transient diabetes insipidus)
  • Confusion → coma (encephalopathy)
  • Mild hypertension ± proteinuria
