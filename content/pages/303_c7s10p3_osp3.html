5. Operative: cervix, induction & PPH balloons
Hegar dilators
Hegar dilatorsموسعات هيجار
Graded metal rods, numbered in mm. Dilate the cervix for evacuation.
Viva · One size at a time; forced dilatation weakens the cervix.
Foley catheter (16–18 F)
Foley catheter (16–18 F)قسطرة فولي
Balloon past the internal os for cervical ripening; bladder drainage.
Viva · Induction: 30–50 mL saline, traction 12–24 h. Safer than prostaglandin after a scar.
Bakri balloon
Bakri balloonبالون باكري
Silicone balloon with a drain lumen. Tamponade for atonic PPH.
Viva · 300–500 mL saline, 12–24 h, with oxytocin and antibiotics.
Condom-catheter balloon
Condom-catheter balloonبالون الواقي الذكري
Condom tied over a Foley, filled with saline: the low-cost tamponade.
Viva · Bleeding stops = tamponade works; still bleeding → theatre.
6. Contraception spotters
Copper IUCD
Copper IUCDاللولب النحاسي
T-shaped frame with copper. Long-acting; emergency contraception within 5 days.
Viva · Postpartum: within 48 h or after 4 wk. Lasts about 10 years.
Contraceptive implant
Contraceptive implantالكبسولة تحت الجلد
1–2 progestogen rods in the inner upper arm. 3–5 years; safe in breastfeeding.
Viva · Main side effect: irregular bleeding. Enzyme inducers reduce effect.
Injectable (DMPA)
Injectable (DMPA)حقنة منع الحمل
Medroxyprogesterone 150 mg deep IM every 12 wk.
Viva · From 6 wk if breastfeeding. Fertility may return up to a year late.
Combined pill (COC)
Combined pill (COC)حبوب منع الحمل المركّبة
Oestrogen + progestogen; 21 active tablets then 7 days.
Viva · Not while breastfeeding < 6 months; not before day 21 (VTE).
7. Bedside & lab spotters
Urine dipstick
Urine dipstickشريط تحليل البول
Pads: protein, glucose, ketones, blood, nitrite, leucocytes. Every ANC visit.
Viva · Protein ≥ 1+ with BP ≥ 140/90 → PE work-up; PCR ≥ 30 mg/mmol.
Fern test
Fern testاختبار السرخس
Dried fluid on a slide shows a fern pattern under the microscope: liquor.
Viva · Mid-cycle cervical mucus also ferns; blood spoils the test.
CTG stripشريط تخطيط قلب الجنين
FHR above, contractions below. Here: a dip after each contraction peak.
Viva · Late decelerations = placental insufficiency.
Bedside clot test
Bedside clot testاختبار التجلط بجانب السرير
2–5 mL blood in a plain glass tube, tilted every 30 s.
Viva · No clot in 7–10 min = coagulopathy (DIC).
