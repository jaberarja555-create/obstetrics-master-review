8. PPH drugs
Oxytocin
Oxytocinالأوكسيتوسين
Ampoule, kept in the fridge. AMTSL 10 IU IM; PPH; induction drip.
Viva · Fast IV bolus drops BP; large volumes → water intoxication.
Ergometrine
Ergometrineالإرغومترين
0.5 mg ampoule, fridge, dark. Tonic contraction for PPH.
Viva · Not in HTN, pre-eclampsia, heart disease. Vomiting, BP rise.
Misoprostol
Misoprostolالميزوبروستول
200 µg tablets (PGE₁), no fridge. PPH 800 µg; AMTSL 600 µg oral.
Viva · Fever, shivering. Not for induction with a uterine scar.
Carboprost
Carboprostالكاربوبروست
250 µg ampoule (PGF₂α), fridge. Atony not responding to others.
Viva · Deep IM every 15 min, max 8. Not in asthma.
9. PPH & eclampsia
Tranexamic acid
Tranexamic acidحمض الترانيكساميك
Antifibrinolytic ampoule. PPH: 1 g IV over 10 min.
Viva · Only within 3 h of birth.
Magnesium sulphate 50%
Magnesium sulphate 50%سلفات المغنيسيوم
1 g in 2 mL. Eclampsia, severe PE; neuroprotection < 32 wk.
Viva · Toxicity: knee jerk lost first. Antidote calcium gluconate.
Calcium gluconate 10%
Calcium gluconate 10%جلوكونات الكالسيوم
10 mL ampoule. Antidote for MgSO₄ toxicity.
Viva · 10 mL IV slowly over 10 min, with O₂.
Hydralazine
Hydralazineالهيدرالازين
Powder vial to dissolve. Severe HTN ≥ 160/110.
Viva · 5 mg IV over 10–15 min, every 15 min; 500 mL saline first.
10. Blood pressure & prevention
Labetalol
Labetalolاللابيتالول
Tablets and IV ampoules. 1st line in pregnancy: 200 mg 8-hourly.
Viva · α + β blocker: not in asthma.
Nifedipine
Nifedipineالنيفيديبين
Tablets. HTN 20 mg twice daily; tocolysis 30 mg, then 20 mg 8-hourly.
Viva · Never sublingual. Headache, flushing, ankle oedema.
Methyldopa
Methyldopaالميثيل دوبا
250 mg tablets. HTN: 250 mg 8-hourly, up to 3 g/day.
Viva · Change it after birth: depression.
Low-dose aspirin
Low-dose aspirinالأسبرين بجرعة منخفضة
75 mg tablets. Prevents pre-eclampsia: 12 → 36 wk, at night.
Viva · For 1 major or 2 moderate risk factors.
