3.7Abnormal Amniotic Fluid

Differential (large for dates): wrong dates · twins · macrosomia · fibroids · ovarian cyst · ascites.
1. Normal amniotic fluid

After 20 wk liquor is mostly fetal urine, removed by fetal swallowing. Volume peaks at 34–36 wk (≈ 900 mL), ≈ 600 mL at 40 wk.

MEASUREMENT · AFI = deepest vertical pool in each of 4 quadrants, added · DVP = deepest single pool.
Clinical picture
Polyhydramnios
Large for dates+fetal parts hard to feel+AFI > 25=Polyhydramnios
Clinical scenario
Polyhydramnios: membranes rupture, sudden pain, dark bleed. Diagnosis?
Abruption from sudden decompression.
2. Polyhydramnios: definition & classification

AFI > 25 cm or DVP > 8 cm. Chronic (common): gradual, late. Acute (rare): over days, < 20 wk, usually monozygotic twins.
