7. Oxytocin regimen

5 IU oxytocin in 500 mL normal saline = 10 mU/mL (20-drop set: 1 drop/min = 0.5 mU/min). Pump if available.

Start · 4 drops2 mU/min
Every 30 min↑ 2–4 mU/min
Aim · 40–60 s each3–4 in 10 min
Maximum32 mU/min

Keep the rate once 3–4 good contractions come. Use saline, not large volumes of dextrose (water intoxication). Watch every contraction and the FHR; record on the partograph.

8. Choosing the method
METHOD CHOICE · Bishop score first; then the fetus (alive or dead) and the uterus (scarred or not) pick the ripening agent.
Hospital protocol

Prostin is used for the live baby and misoprostol for the dead baby. A multipara has a strong uterus: prefer ARM + careful oxytocin, low drug doses.
