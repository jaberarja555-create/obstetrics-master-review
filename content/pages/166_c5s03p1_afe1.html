5.3Amniotic Fluid Embolism

1. Definition

Sudden collapse in labour, at delivery or soon after, when amniotic fluid and fetal cells enter the mother's blood. Rare (1 in 8 000–30 000 births), unpredictable, not preventable, often fatal. Timing: 70% in labour around rupture of membranes · 19% at caesarean · 11% after delivery (within 48 h).

2. Risk factors
  • Induction, ARM, strong contractions (oxytocin)
  • Caesarean or instrumental delivery
  • Multiple pregnancy · polyhydramnios
  • Older mother · eclampsia
  • Placenta praevia · abruption
  • Cervical laceration · uterine rupture
  • Rarely: TOP, manual removal, amniocentesis
  • Oxytocin before a molar uterus is half emptied
3. Pathophysiology
TWO PHASES · Only 12% have DIC at the start, but almost all who survive the collapse develop it within 4 h.
