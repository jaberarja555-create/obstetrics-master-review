4Forceps
  • Left blade first (left hand, left side)
  • Lock without force
  • Wrigley (outlet, at CS)
  • Neville-Barnes (low, mid-cavity)
  • Kielland (rotation, sliding lock)
  • Piper (aftercoming head)
5Ventouse vs forceps
  • Ventouse: more failure, less maternal trauma; cephalhaematoma, chignon, retinal bleeds
  • Forceps: more 3rd/4th degree tears; facial bruising, facial nerve palsy
6Caesarean section: urgency
  • Category 1: threat to life, ≤ 30 min
  • Category 2: compromise, ≤ 75 min
  • 3: needs early delivery
  • 4: elective at 39 wk
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Cord prolapse at 6 cm, live baby → Category 1 CS
7Consent and preparation
  • Benefits, risks (bleeding, infection, VTE, bladder or bowel injury, future praevia and accreta)
  • Fast, antacid
  • Group and save
  • Cefuroxime 1.5 g before the incision
  • Spinal preferred
  • Left tilt 15°
  • Oxytocin 5 IU slow IV after the baby
8Lower segment vs classical
  • Lower segment: less bleeding, scar ruptures in about 0.5%
  • Classical: very preterm, transverse lie with back down, lower-segment fibroid, perimortem; rupture 2–9%, no labour next time
