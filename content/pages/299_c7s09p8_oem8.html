Newborn Resuscitation

21. The station

Task (6 min, on a newborn mannequin): "A term baby is born after a long 2nd stage. He is floppy and not breathing. Start resuscitation and give the Apgar score." Equipment: warmer, dry towels, hat, bag-valve-mask with term (size 1) and preterm (size 0) masks, suction, stethoscope, clock.

Before birth: check the bag and mask fit, warmer on, towels warm, clock started at birth. Delay cord clamping only if the baby is breathing; a baby who needs help: clamp and move to the warmer.

22. The sequence

2Assess tone, breathing, heart rate (stethoscope at the apex, or feel the cord)
3Head neutral (folded towel under the shoulders); jaw lift · suction only if the airway is blocked
45 inflation breaths, each 2–3 s, air, firm mask seal over mouth and nose; watch the chest rise
5Reassess HR: rising = lungs inflated → ventilate 30/min until breathing well; no chest rise → reposition, jaw thrust, repeat
6HR < 60 after 30 s of good breaths: chest compressions, two thumbs, lower sternum, 3 : 1, call senior help
