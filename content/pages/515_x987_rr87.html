5Differential diagnosis
  • PE: postnatal, pleuritic pain, swollen calf, no early DIC (CTPA, heparin)
  • Eclampsia: high BP, fit first, SpO₂ normal (MgSO₄ 4 g IV)
  • Haemorrhage: blood loss before shock
  • Anaphylaxis: after a drug, rash, wheeze (adrenaline 0.5 mg IM)
  • Local anaesthetic toxicity: after an epidural top-up (lipid 20% 1.5 mL/kg)
  • MI, septic shock, aspiration
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Day 5 after CS, pleuritic pain, swollen calf → Pulmonary embolism
  • Collapse 10 min after IV ampicillin, urticaria, wheeze → Adrenaline 0.5 mg IM
6Management: the drill
  • Save the mother first; never wait to prove the diagnosis
  • Phase 1: O₂ 15 L/min, intubate early
  • Left tilt or push the uterus left
  • Fluids with care (right heart failing)
  • Low BP after fluids: vasopressor ± inotrope
  • CPR 30:2
  • Phase 2: blood + FFP 15 mL/kg
  • Cryoprecipitate if fibrinogen < 2 g/L
  • Platelets if < 50
  • TXA 1 g IV
  • Atony: oxytocin infusion, carboprost 250 µg IM
  • Still bleeding: hysterectomy
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Giving 3 L of fluid fast floods the lungs through the failing right heart
7Perimortem caesarean
  • Cardiac arrest at ≥ 20 wk (fundus at or above the umbilicus), no output after 4 min of good CPR
  • Start at 4 min, baby out by 5 min
  • Where she lies: no transfer, no consent, CPR continues
  • Purpose: uterus off the IVC and aorta so CPR works: mainly for the mother
