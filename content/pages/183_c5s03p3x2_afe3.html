Exam trap

"Move her to theatre for the caesarean": wrong, transfer wastes minutes and stops CPR. "Give 3 L of fluid fast": wrong, the failing right heart floods the lungs. "Wait for clotting results": wrong, use the bedside clot test and give products.

Hospital protocol

No ICU on site: CPR, O₂, two cannulae, left tilt, call the theatre team; perimortem CS in the labour room. Ask relatives to donate early: fresh whole blood gives clotting factors and platelets. Transfer only once stable.

Clinical picture
Perimortem caesarean
Arrest+≥ 20 wk+4 min, no pulse=Deliver by 5 min
Clinical scenario
AFE, cardiac arrest at 36 wk, no output after 4 min of CPR. Next step?
Perimortem caesarean where she lies.
10. Prognosis

Maternal death 13–80%; up to 85% of survivors have hypoxic brain damage; perinatal death high. Debrief, document, report for maternal death review. Recurrence rare; next delivery in a hospital with ICU.
