Cardiotocography (CTG)

4. Definition

Record of fetal heart rate with uterine contractions to detect fetal hypoxia, for high-risk labour. Sensitive, not specific: in low-risk women it only raises caesareans.

5. Reading a CTG: DR C BRAVADO
DR · define riskWhy on CTG? Gestation, oxytocin, fever, meconium
C · contractionsNumber in 10 min (3–5 normal) · > 5 = tachysystole
BRa · baseline rate110–160 bpm, mean over 10 min
