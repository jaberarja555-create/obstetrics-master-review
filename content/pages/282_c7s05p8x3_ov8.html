23. Examiner marking points
  • Abdomen before the VE
  • Asks about praevia / scan
  • Between contractions, gently
  • All cervix items + station
  • Checks for cord, looks at the glove
  • FHR after, partogram
  • Dilatation in cm, not fingers
  • Gives length or % effacement
  • Consistency and position
  • Membranes, liquor, no cord
  • Bishop added correctly
  • Station against the spines
  • Bone, not caput, measured
  • Finds the sagittal suture first
  • Posterior fontanelle = occiput
  • Names the position (e.g. LOA)
  • Grades moulding 0 to +3
  • Moulding vs caput told apart
  • Knows +3 moulding = obstruction
  • Findings in the right order
  • Full, ordered presentation
  • Explains result to her
