Presenting the case

"Rasha, 19, primigravida at 24 weeks from Gezira, has fever with rigors and headache for 3 days, vomiting twice. No fits, confusion, jaundice or dysuria; baby moving. She sleeps without a net and has had no IPTp. Impression: uncomplicated malaria; differential pyelonephritis. I would do a blood film or RDT, RBG, Hb and urine."

Common mistake

Stopping at "malaria" without asking the severe features: they decide oral ACT versus IV artesunate.

Distinguishing features · Fever + …
Fit, BP normal, parasitesCerebral malaria
Fit, BP 170/110, protein 3+Eclampsia
Loin pain, dysuriaPyelonephritis
Leak, tender uterus, fetal tachyChorioamnionitis
Viva questions
Why is a primigravida more at risk?
No immunity yet to parasites that bind the placenta.
Drug for uncomplicated malaria in pregnancy (Sudan)?
Artemether-lumefantrine, any trimester, with fatty food.
18. Examiner marking points
  • Fever pattern, rigors
  • Severe features asked one by one
  • Can she swallow tablets?
  • UTI, chorioamnionitis excluded
  • Net, IPTp, drugs, allergy
  • Fetal movements, contractions
