3. Pathophysiology (continued)
PEDERSEN HYPOTHESIS · Glucose crosses the placenta, insulin does not. Macrosomia, RDS and neonatal hypoglycaemia all come from fetal hyperinsulinaemia.
Why GDM does not cause anomalies

Anomalies need high glucose in weeks 3–8 (organogenesis); GDM starts after 20 wk. Anomalies and miscarriage = pre-existing diabetes only.

4. Clinical features
  • History: usually none. Polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss suggest overt diabetes
  • Recurrent candida, UTI
  • Large-for-dates uterus
  • Polyhydramnios (fetal polyuria)
  • Glycosuria: poor test (renal threshold falls)

6. Diagnosis: 75 g OGTT
  1. Normal diet for 3 days, then fast 8–14 h overnight.
  2. Fasting sample, then 75 g glucose in 250–300 mL water.
  3. Second sample at 2 h.
