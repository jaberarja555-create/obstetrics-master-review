Viva questions
Why mediolateral rather than midline?
Midline extends into the anal sphincter.
Best time to cut?
At crowning, at the height of a contraction.
9Mediolateral vs midline
MediolateralMidline (median)
DirectionFrom the fourchette, 60° to one sideFrom the fourchette straight toward the anus
Anal sphincterProtected: extension goes into the ischiorectal fatExtension = 3rd/4th degree tear
Bleeding, repairMore bleeding, harder to repairLess bleeding, easy repair
Healing, painMore pain, more dyspareuniaHeals well, less pain, better look
UseOur standardAvoided in our units
10Complications

Early: bleeding · haematoma · extension to a 3rd/4th degree tear · infection, wound breakdown · pain, urinary retention. Late: dyspareunia · scarring, poor anatomy · rarely fistula · incontinence if the sphincter was missed.
