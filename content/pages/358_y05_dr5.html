TLD (tenofovir + lamivudine + dolutegravir)Antiretroviral
Main use
HIV
Dose · route · how long
1 tablet PO OD, lifelong · labour: IV zidovudine 2 mg/kg over 1 h → 1 mg/kg/h if VL ≥ 1000 · baby: nevirapine daily × 6 wk
Side effects · monitor
Nausea, headache · check VL
When · cautions
Start at diagnosis, any trimester · cotrimoxazole 960 mg OD if CD4 low

Main use

Dose · route · how long
