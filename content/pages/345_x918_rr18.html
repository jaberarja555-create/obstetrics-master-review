Chapter6
Puerperium and Maternal Health
6.1Normal puerperium
Day 4, tearful but caring for the baby → Baby blues: reassure
Fundus still palpable at day 14 with heavy red lochia → Subinvolution: scan for retained products, infection
Breastfeeding mother, red tender wedge on the breast, fever → Mastitis: keep feeding, flucloxacillin
Involution
Fundus falls 1 cm a day · not palpable by day 10–14 · uterus 1000 g → 60–100 g at 6 wk
Lochia
Rubra day 1–4 · serosa day 5–9 · alba day 10 to 6 wk · total ≈ 250 mL
Lactation
Prolactin makes milk · oxytocin for let-down and after-pains · start within 1 h, 8–12 feeds a day · exclusive 6 months
Postnatal care
Pass urine by 6 h · anti-D 500 IU within 72 h · MMR if rubella non-immune · iron 3 mo · WHO 4 postnatal contacts · check at 6 wk
Contraception
No method needed before 21 d · LAM 98% if rules met · copper IUD ≤ 48 h or ≥ 4 wk · DMPA at 6 wk if breastfeeding
Breastfeeding contraindications
Galactosaemia · untreated active TB (first 2 wk) · some drugs
Exam traps
Puerperium is 6 weeks (42 days) · earliest ovulation in a non-breastfeeding woman is about day 25 · avoid pregnancy for 1 month after MMR
6.2Puerperal pyrexia & sepsis
Fever day 3 after CS, foul lochia, tender uterus → Endometritis: ceftriaxone + metronidazole
Sudden toxic collapse after birth, child at home with a sore throat → Group A streptococcal sepsis: add clindamycin
Spiking fever despite antibiotics, no collection → Pelvic vein thrombophlebitis: heparin
Definition
Temperature ≥ 38 °C in the first 14 days · sepsis up to 42 days · leading direct killer
Causes (sites)
Endometritis commonest · wound · UTI · breast · chest (day 1 after GA: atelectasis, aspiration) · DVT
Risk factors
Caesarean section (strongest) · ROM > 24 h · many VEs · retained products · anaemia, diabetes
Assessment
MEOWS (one red trigger → urgent review) · qSOFA (RR ≥ 25, SBP < 90, confusion) · lactate ≥ 4 = severe
Sepsis Six (1 hour)
O₂ to 94–98% · 2 blood cultures · IV antibiotics · fluids 20 mL/kg · lactate · urine output ≥ 30 mL/h
Complications
Pelvic abscess (swinging fever, tenesmus) · septic shock · thrombophlebitis
Exam traps
Antibiotics at CS go before skin incision · hand washing prevents most · stop IV after 48 h fever-free
