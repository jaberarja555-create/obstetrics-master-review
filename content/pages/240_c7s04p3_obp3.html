Presenting the case

“Blood pressure 152/100 mmHg, right arm, sitting, large cuff, diastolic at K5. This is moderate hypertension. I would repeat it, test her urine for protein, ask about headache and visual change, and send FBC, LFT and RFT.”

What to say · high reading
“Your blood pressure is a bit high today. We will check it again after a short rest and test your urine. If you get a bad headache, blurred vision, pain under the ribs or swelling of the face, come to hospital at once.”
«ضغطك عالي شوية اليوم. حنقيسه تاني بعد ما ترتاحي، وحنفحص البول. لو جاك صداع شديد، أو زغللة في النظر، أو ألم تحت الضلوع، أو وشّك ورم، تعالي المستشفى طوّالي.»

Viva questions
BP 162/112 once. Wait 4 h to confirm?
No: severe; repeat within 15 min and treat.
BP 150/100 at 14 wk with protein. Likely diagnosis?
Molar pregnancy (PE before 20 wk).
