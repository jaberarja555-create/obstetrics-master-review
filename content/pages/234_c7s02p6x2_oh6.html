Presenting the case

"Samia, 22, primigravida at 35 weeks, BP 150/100 today. Severe headache and blurred vision; no fits, baby moving well. No BP before pregnancy. Impression: pre-eclampsia with severe features. I would confirm proteinuria, check platelets, LFT, creatinine, CTG and growth scan."

Common errors

No fetal movements · not asking when BP first rose · epigastric pain taken for "gastritis".

Distinguishing features · Which disorder?
BP 150/95 at 10 wkChronic HTN
New BP at 34 wk, urine clearGestational HTN
Known HTN, new protein at 30 wkSuperimposed PE
BP + protein at 14 wk, vesiclesMolar pregnancy
Viva questions
One symptom that makes PE severe at once?
Severe headache, visual disturbance or epigastric pain: any one.
Which past history earns aspirin by itself?
Previous PE, chronic HTN, renal disease, DM, SLE/APS.
13. Examiner marking points
  • Headache, vision, epigastric pain
  • Fits and breathlessness
  • Onset: before or after 20 wk
  • Past PE, renal, DM; family
  • Fetal movements
  • Diagnosis + severity
