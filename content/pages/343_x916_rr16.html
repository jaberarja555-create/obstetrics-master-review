Chapter5
Obstetric Emergencies
5.1Cord prolapse
Sudden fetal bradycardia just after ARM with a high head → Cord prolapse: immediate VE
Cord felt in the vagina, cervix 5 cm, live fetus → Push the part up, Category 1 CS
Cord prolapse at full dilatation, head low → Expedite vaginal birth (instrument)
Types
Presentation (membranes intact) · prolapse (ruptured) · occult (beside the part)
Risk factors
Footling breech (15%) · transverse lie · polyhydramnios · prematurity · ARM with a high head · twin 2
Immediate actions
Call for help · manually elevate the presenting part (2 fingers) · knee–chest or left Sims · stop oxytocin
Relieve compression
Fill bladder with 500 mL warm saline · terbutaline 0.25 mg SC
Delivery
Category 1 CS (decision to delivery ≤ 30 min) · fully dilated: instrument · dead fetus or < 26 wk: vaginal
Cord care
Do not handle the cord (spasm) · keep it warm and moist in the vagina
Exam traps
Never try to replace the cord · empty the bladder before CS · ARM only with an engaged head
5.2Uterine inversion
Shock out of proportion to bleeding, bradycardia, no fundus felt → Uterine inversion: replace at once (Johnson)
Inverted uterus with the placenta still attached → Leave the placenta until replaced
Manual replacement fails → O'Sullivan hydrostatic method
Facts
1 in 2000–3000 · most in the 3rd stage · cause: cord traction before separation, fundal pressure
Degrees
1st: within the cavity · 2nd: through the cervix · 3rd: to the introitus · 4th: uterus and vagina outside · chronic > 4 wk
Features
PPH (94%), pain, shock with bradycardia (vagal) · no fundus abdominally · mass in the vagina
Treatment
Call help · Johnson manoeuvre at once (fundus pushed up last-out first-in) · relax ring: terbutaline, MgSO₄ 4 g, GA
Failure
O'Sullivan (2–5 L warm saline, exclude rupture first) · laparotomy: Huntington, Haultain
After
Oxytocin infusion, hold fist in fundus 3–5 min · antibiotics · remove placenta
Exam traps
Do not remove the placenta before replacement · no oxytocics until replaced · vagal shock is treated with atropine and fluids
