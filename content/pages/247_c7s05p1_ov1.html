7.5Speculum & Vaginal Examination

Task

"Sara, 33 weeks, felt a gush of fluid 3 hours ago. Perform a sterile speculum examination on this model, take any swabs needed and describe your findings." · 5–7 min · pelvic model, Cusco specula of 3 sizes, sponge-holding forceps, swabs, gloves, light.

1. When to pass a speculum in pregnancy
IndicationWhat you look for
Suspected ROM / PPROMPooling of liquor in the posterior fornix, leak on coughing · HVS · nitrazine, fern. No digital VE if not in labour
APHOnly after the scan has located the placenta: local cause (ectropion, polyp, cancer, trauma), how much bleeding, os open?
Abnormal dischargeCandida, BV, trichomonas, gonorrhoea: HVS + endocervical swabs
Preterm labourIs the os open? Membranes bulging? Swabs (GBS) · avoids repeated digital VEs
Cervical screeningPap smear: best at the postnatal visit (6 wk), not in pregnancy unless due
After deliveryCervical and high vaginal tears as a cause of PPH (in theatre, good light)
Safety

APH: no digital VE until the scan excludes praevia; a gentle speculum is then safe. Suspected ROM: speculum only, never fingers, unless she is in labour (7.5, ROM tests).

Distinguishing features · Speculum or fingers?
Gush of fluid, 32 wk, no painsSpeculum only
Painless bleed, placenta not yet seenScan first, no PV
Bleed, scan: fundal placentaSpeculum: local cause?
Leaking, contracting 3 / 10 minIn labour: VE allowed
Urine leaks after obstructed labourVVF: Sims speculum
