6. Differential diagnosis
DVTCellulitis · ruptured Baker's cyst · lymphatic obstruction · muscle strain · physiological oedema
PEPneumonia · ischaemic heart disease · pneumothorax · pulmonary oedema (peripartum cardiomyopathy) · pleural effusion · aortic dissection · amniotic fluid embolism
7. Complications
  • PE (from DVT) · recurrence in a later pregnancy
  • Post-thrombotic syndrome: chronic leg swelling, pain, pigmentation, ulcers
  • Venous gangrene (massive iliofemoral clot) → amputation
  • Of treatment: bleeding, heparin-induced thrombocytopenia, osteoporosis
8. Management · antenatal

LMWH is the anticoagulant of pregnancy (no placental crossing). Dose by booking weight: enoxaparin 1 mg/kg twice daily (50–69 kg 60 mg bd · 70–89 kg 80 mg bd) for the rest of pregnancy + ≥ 6 wk postpartum (≥ 3 months total). Plus leg elevation, stockings, early mobilisation.

Exam trap

Warfarin (oral) crosses the placenta: embryopathy at 6–12 wk, fetal brain bleeds later. Avoid in pregnancy (except some mechanical valves); safe after birth.
