7Health advice
  • Only about 300 extra kcal a day, second half only; 'eating for two' is a myth
  • No strong tea or coffee with meals (less iron absorption)
  • Avoid smoking, shisha, toombak, alcohol
  • NSAIDs avoided from 20 wk (less fetal urine; after 30 wk closure of the ductus arteriosus); also tetracycline, warfarin
  • Normal work, walking, left-side rest late; seat belt below the abdomen
  • Exclusive breastfeeding 6 months, starting within the first hour
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Asks for ibuprofen at 32 wk → Avoid it; use paracetamol
8Special situations
  • RhD-negative: antibody screen at booking and 28 wk; anti-D within 72 h of any sensitising event (bleeding, miscarriage, ectopic, ECV, trauma, amniocentesis, birth of an Rh-positive baby); routine anti-D at 28 wk where available
  • Late booking (after 20 wk): all tests the same day, date with every clue, iron and folic acid, two Td doses 4 wk apart, SP, bed net; look for anaemia, hypertension, malpresentation, twins
9Danger signs
  • Vaginal bleeding
  • Fits, severe headache, blurred vision
  • Fever, too weak to get up
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Fast or difficult breathing
  • Reduced fetal movements
  • Leaking fluid
  • Swelling of the face and hands
