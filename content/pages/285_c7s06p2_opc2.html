Presenting the findings
"Amna, 24, primigravida at 39 weeks, admitted at 08:00 at 4 cm. The fetus stayed well with clear liquor. The cervix dilated about 1 cm per hour, left of the alert line, and the head came down to 0/5 with strong contractions. Her observations were normal. This is normal labour; she delivered at 14:00."
«آمنة، ٢٤ سنة، أول حمل، ٣٩ أسبوع. دخلت الساعة ٨ وعنق الرحم ٤ سم. الجنين كويس والموية صافية. الفتح كان حوالي سم في الساعة على يسار خط الإنذار، والرأس نزل تماماً. علاماتها الحيوية طبيعية. ولادة طبيعية، ولدت الساعة ٢ الظهر.»

Viva questions
How long may the 2nd stage last in a primigravida without epidural?
2 hours (3 h with epidural).
How often is FHR heard in the 2nd stage, low risk?
Every 5 minutes, after a contraction.
Examiner follow-up

"Why a VE at 13:30, not 16:00?" Signs of the 2nd stage: urge to push, anus gaping, head visible. Write the reason.
