6. Examiner marking points
  • Consent and a chaperone
  • Kit checked before crowning
  • Oxytocin drawn up, baby area warm
  • Asepsis: wash, gown, gloves, drape
  • Coaches pushing, then panting
  • FHR every 5 min in 2nd stage (normal length: primi up to 2 h, multi up to 1 h)
  • Flexion + perineal guard together
  • Head out slowly, between contractions
  • Checks cord round the neck
  • Down for anterior, up for posterior
  • Time of birth, baby on chest, dried
  • Excludes a 2nd twin first
  • Oxytocin dose, route, timing
  • Waits 1–3 min to clamp
  • CCT with uterine guarding
  • Checks placenta and membranes
  • Inspects for tears, records loss

Episiotomy

Episiotomy = a surgical cut of the perineum in the 2nd stage to enlarge the vaginal opening. Not routine: cut only for a reason. Station: on a perineal model, "perform and explain an episiotomy", often followed by "now repair it", or a viva on a picture.

7. Indications
Assisted deliveryVentouse, forceps (most primigravidae) · breech · shoulder dystocia (room for the hand)
FetalFetal distress in the 2nd stage: shortens it · preterm or OP delivery: on judgement
PerineumRigid perineum holding up the head · a large tear about to happen (multiple tears, button-holing)
ScarringFemale genital cutting (anterior cut: de-infibulation) · old badly healed 3rd/4th degree tear
