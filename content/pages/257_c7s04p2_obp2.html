Viva questions
Why find the palpatory systolic first?
To avoid missing the auscultatory gap and under-reading systolic.
Which Korotkoff sound is diastolic in pregnancy?
K5 (disappearance); K4 only if sounds persist to 0.
1. Korotkoff sounds
READ THIS BP · First tapping at 142, silence at 90 → 142/90. In pregnancy the high cardiac output can keep sounds to 0: then use K4.
2. Errors that change the reading
ErrorReadingWhy
Cuff too small (obese arm)↑ falsely highNeeds more pressure to close the artery: the commonest error in clinic
Cuff too large↓ lowPressure spread over a wide area
Arm below heart level, hanging↑ highWeight of the blood column adds
Left lateral, cuff on the upper arm↓ lowUpper arm is above the heart: use the lower arm or sit her up
Supine, flat↓ lowAortocaval compression: supine hypotension
Deflating too fast↓ S, ↑ DYou miss the true first and last sound
Talking, full bladder, pain, just walked in↑ highSympathetic drive: rest 5 min
Cuff over a sleeve, loose cuff↑ highPressure not passed evenly to the arm
Stethoscope pressed hard on the artery↓ DSounds go on lower: false low diastolic
Rounding to 0 or 5wrongDigit preference: record to 2 mmHg
