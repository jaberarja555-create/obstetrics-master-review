2.11Thyroid Disease in Pregnancy

1. Physiology

Iodine need rises (urine loss + fetus): goitre appears in iodine-poor areas. Trimester TSH upper limit: 1st 2.5 · 2nd and 3rd 3.0 mU/L.

2. Aetiology of thyrotoxicosis

Graves' disease = about 95 %. Others: toxic multinodular goitre, toxic adenoma, subacute thyroiditis, drugs (amiodarone, lithium). Half have a family history of autoimmune thyroid disease.

3. Clinical features & investigations

Weight loss despite good appetite · tremor · persistent tachycardia > 100 (resting) · eye signs · goitre. Palpitations and heat intolerance also occur in normal pregnancy. TFTs: low TSH + high free T4. TRAb in Graves'. Ultrasound: size, nodules.
