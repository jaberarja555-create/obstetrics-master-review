Chapter1
Normal Pregnancy and Antenatal Care
1.1Physiology of pregnancy
Breathless at rest is not normal: mild breathlessness on effort is → Physiological hyperventilation (progesterone)
Faint when lying flat at 32 weeks → Supine hypotension: left lateral tilt
Diastolic murmur in a pregnant woman → Always abnormal: echocardiography
Cardiovascular
Plasma volume +45%, red cell mass +25% (dilutional anaemia) · cardiac output +40% · heart rate +10–15 · BP lowest at 22–24 wk · ejection systolic murmur normal
Respiratory
Minute ventilation +40–50% · PaCO₂ about 4 kPa (respiratory alkalosis) · FRC −20%
Renal
GFR +50% · creatinine < 75 µmol/L · proteinuria up to 300 mg/24 h · hydronephrosis (right) · glycosuria
Endocrine
hCG peaks 8–10 wk · placenta takes over progesterone at 8–10 wk · hPL → insulin resistance (OGTT 24–28 wk) · TSH low in 1st trimester (hCG) · prolactin ×10
Blood and liver
Fibrinogen 4–6 g/L · VTE risk ×4–6 · ALP ×2–4 (placenta) · albumin ↓
Key numbers
Weight gain 11.5–16 kg (normal BMI) · uterus 1000 g at term · uterine blood flow 500–750 mL/min
Exam traps
A raised ALP is normal, a raised ALT is not · creatinine 90 µmol/L is abnormal in pregnancy · low TSH in the first trimester is usually hCG, not thyrotoxicosis
1.2Diagnosis of pregnancy & dating
Missed period, positive urine test, empty uterus, hCG above 2000 → Ectopic until proved otherwise
hCG rises less than 63% in 48 hours → Non-viable or ectopic pregnancy
Scan and LMP differ by more than 5 days → Use the scan (CRL) date
Hormone tests
Serum hCG positive 8–11 d after conception · urine positive by the missed period · viable rise ≥ 63% in 48 h
Ultrasound
Sac at 4.5–5 wk (TVS) · heartbeat 6 wk · discriminatory zone hCG 2000 IU/L · CRL 7 mm without heartbeat = missed miscarriage
Clinical signs
Hegar (soft isthmus, 6–10 wk) · Chadwick (bluish vagina, 8 wk) · uterus above symphysis 12 wk · quickening 18–20 wk (primi), 16–18 wk (multi)
Fetal heart
Doppler from 12 wk · Pinard from 24 wk
Dating
Naegele: LMP + 9 months + 7 days (280 d) · long cycle: add the extra days · CRL at 11–14 wk best (± 5 d)
Term
37 to 41⁺⁶ wk · post-term ≥ 42 wk
Exam traps
Absent fetal heart on Pinard before 24 wk means nothing · a 35-day cycle moves the EDD 7 days later · quickening is a presumptive sign only
