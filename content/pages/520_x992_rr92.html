2Lochia
  • Rubra day 1–4: red
  • Serosa day 5–9: pink-brown
  • Alba day 10 to 3–6 wk: yellow-white
  • Total about 250 mL; mild smell, never foul
  • Foul: infection
  • Red again or persistent rubra: retained tissue, subinvolution
  • None + fever: lochiometra
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Red lochia again at day 12, bulky uterus → Retained tissue
3Physiological changes: other systems
  • Cardiac output rises straight after birth (autotransfusion): heart disease decompensates now
  • WBC up to 20–25 × 10⁹/L in the first days is normal
  • Hypercoagulable for 6 wk (VTE)
  • Diuresis day 2–5; void within 6 h (retention)
  • ≥ 38 °C is never normal
  • Blues in up to 80% on day 3–5
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Day 2, WBC 18 × 10⁹/L, afebrile and well → Reassure: normal leucocytosis
4Return of ovulation
  • Not breastfeeding: ovulation from day 25, menses at 6–8 wk; start contraception by day 21
  • Breastfeeding: prolactin blocks GnRH; but ovulation comes before the first period
5Lactation
  • Prolactin makes milk
  • Oxytocin: let-down and after-pains
  • Progesterone fall lets milk come in (day 3–4)
  • Colostrum to day 3: IgA, protein, laxative
  • Mature milk from 2 wk, about 750–800 mL/day
  • Cabergoline stops lactation
