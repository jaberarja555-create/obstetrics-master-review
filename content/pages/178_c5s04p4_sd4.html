9. Management: when HELPERR fails
ManoeuvreWhat and when
Clavicle fracturePress the anterior clavicle outward (away from the lung) to break it; shoulder width falls. Heals well
ZavanelliPush the head back into the vagina by reversing the mechanism (flex, turn back), with a tocolytic terbutaline 0.25 mg SC, then caesarean
SymphysiotomyCut the symphysis under local anaesthesia, urethra pushed aside with a catheter. Risks: urethral and bladder injury, incontinence, pain, walking problems
Dead babyCleidotomy (cut the clavicles) to spare the mother
