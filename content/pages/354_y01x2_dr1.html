Magnesium sulphateCerebral vasodilator, NMDA blocker, membrane stabiliser
Main use
Prevent / treat eclampsia · fetal neuroprotection < 32 wk
Dose · route · how long
Eclampsia: 4 g IV over 5–15 min → 1 g/h for 24 h after birth or last fit · refit: extra 2 g IV · IM option: 5 g IM 4-hourly · neuroprotection 4 g IV over 20 min → 1 g/h until birth
Side effects · monitor
Flushing · toxicity: loss of knee jerk → RR < 12 → arrest · check reflexes, RR, urine ≥ 30 mL/h hourly · neonatal hypotonia
When · cautions
Antidote calcium gluconate 10%, 10 mL IV slowly · any trimester, labour, 24 h postpartum

Dose · route · how long
200 mg PO TDS (max 2.4 g/day) · severe: 20 mg IV, then 40, 80 mg every 10 min (max 300 mg)

Bradycardia, bronchospasm · baby: low glucose, slow heart

Not in asthma · all trimesters, breastfeeding OK
