9. Fetal & neonatal complications (continued)
Three "most" answers

Commonest anomaly = cardiac. Most characteristic = caudal regression (sacral agenesis). Commonest neonatal problem = hypoglycaemia.

10. Management: pre-pregnancy care (type 1 and 2)
  • HbA1c < 6.5% before conceiving. Above 10%: advise against pregnancy for now; contraception until then.
  • Folic acid 5 mg daily from 3 months before conception to 12 wk.
  • Stop ACE inhibitors, ARBs, statins. Oral drugs except metformin → insulin. Check eyes and kidneys.
11. Management: antenatal care
  • Joint team clinic, frequent visits
  • Aspirin 75 mg from 12 wk
  • BP and urine protein each visit
  • Teach home glucose testing and hypo signs
