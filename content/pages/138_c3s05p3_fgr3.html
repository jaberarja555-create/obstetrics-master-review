Why hypoglycaemia? Low glycogen stores. Why polycythaemia? Chronic hypoxia drives erythropoietin. Why hypothermia? Little fat.

8Management: antenatal
  • Treat the cause: control BP and glucose, stop smoking, nutrition, treat malaria and anaemia
  • Surveillance: kick chart · CTG · growth scan every 3 wk · UA Doppler (weekly, more often if abnormal) · liquor
  • Steroids if delivery likely before 34 wk: dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12-hourly × 4
  • Deliver when risk inside the uterus > risk outside
