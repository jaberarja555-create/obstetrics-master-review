Viva questions
Which way do you pull for the anterior shoulder?
Gently downward (posteriorly), with a contraction.
What is restitution?
Head untwists to line up with the shoulders.
Common mistakes

Pulling the head sideways or hard (brachial plexus injury) · cutting a loose cord · forgetting to wait for restitution · not noting the time of birth · leaving the baby wet (hypothermia).
