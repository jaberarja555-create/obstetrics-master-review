10. Reading it aloud: DR C BRAVADO
FeatureWhat you say
DRDefine risk"A [age], G_P_ at [GA], in labour at [x] cm, high risk because of …"
CContractions"[n] in 10 minutes, lasting … s" · normal 3–5 · > 5 = tachysystole
BRaBaseline rate"Baseline 140 bpm" (mean over 10 min) · normal 110–160 (161–180 suspicious, > 180 pathological)
VVariability"Bandwidth about 15 bpm" · normal 5–25 (< 5 for > 50 min = pathological)
AAccelerations"Present: rises ≥ 15 bpm for ≥ 15 s" = healthy fetus
DDecelerations"None" / early / variable / late / prolonged (> 3 min)
OOverall"Normal / suspicious / pathological, so my plan is …"
Common mistakes

Reading before the story · forgetting contractions · maternal pulse taken for fetal bradycardia · "reactive" in labour (that is the antenatal NST) (reactive = 2 accelerations in 20 min) · no plan.

11. Example 1 · normal CTG
READ IT · Baseline 140, variability 10–15 bpm (V), 3 accelerations (A), no decelerations, 3 contractions in 10 min. Normal: continue.
12. Example 2 · early decelerations
READ IT · Baseline 135, variability normal, U-shaped dips that start, bottom out and recover with each contraction (E: the mirror image). Cause: head compression, late 1st or 2nd stage. Normal.
