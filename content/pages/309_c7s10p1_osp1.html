7.10Instruments, Drugs & Spotters

1. Examination: abdomen and speculum
Pinard fetal stethoscope
Pinard fetal stethoscopeسماعة بينارد
Trumpet-shaped tube. Fetal heart from about 24 wk; count a full minute.
Viva · Place over the fetal back; no hand on it while you listen.
Hand-held fetal Doppler
Hand-held fetal Dopplerجهاز دوبلر لسماع قلب الجنين
Ultrasound probe + gel. FHR from about 12 wk; intermittent listening in labour.
Viva · Feel the mother's pulse at the same time: do not count her heart.
SFH tape measure
SFH tape measureشريط قياس ارتفاع الرحم
Non-stretch tape, numbers face down. Fundus to top of the symphysis.
Viva · From 24 wk: cm = weeks ± 2. More than 3 cm off → scan.
Cusco speculum
Cusco speculumمنظار كوسكو
Bivalve, self-retaining. View the cervix, take swabs, see pooling of liquor.
Viva · Insert closed and oblique, turn, then open: cervix between the blades.
Sims speculum
Sims speculumمنظار سيمز
Duck-bill, not self-retaining; left lateral position. Vaginal walls, fistula, prolapse, repairs.
Viva · Needs an assistant to hold it; retracts the posterior wall.
Sponge-holding forceps
Sponge-holding forcepsجفت حمل الشاش
Long ring forceps with a lock. Hold swabs, clean, grasp the cervix, remove products at the os.
Viva · PPH: "walk" two forceps round the cervix to find a tear.
HVS and endocervical swabs
HVS & endocervical swabsمسحة مهبلية وعنقية
Sterile swab in transport medium. HVS: candida, BV, trichomonas, GBS. Endocervical: gonorrhoea.
Viva · Take through a speculum; label, send the same day.
Nitrazine (pH) paper
Nitrazine (pH) paperورق اختبار الحموضة
Yellow paper turns blue in alkaline liquor (pH 7–7.5). Helps confirm ROM.
Viva · False positive: blood, semen, urine, BV. Normal vagina pH ≈ 4.5.
2. Labour & delivery
Amniotomy hook
Amniotomy hookخطّاف تمزيق الأغشية
Plastic hook. ARM to induce or speed labour.
Viva · Head well applied first (cord prolapse); then check FHR and liquor colour.
Cord clamp
Cord clampمشبك الحبل السري
Disposable plastic clamp, 2–3 cm from the skin.
Viva · Clamp at 1–3 min if breathing; at once if the baby needs resuscitation.
Episiotomy scissors
Episiotomy scissorsمقص الشق العجاني
Angled, blunt-tipped scissors. Mediolateral cut at crowning.
Viva · 60° from the midline, after lidocaine, two fingers guarding the head.
Needle holder
Needle holderماسك الإبرة
Holder with a lock. Repair of episiotomy and tears.
Viva · Hold the needle ⅔ from its tip; count swabs and needles at the end.
