3. What to look at, in order
IntroitusFGM scar (type III narrows it): smallest speculum, slowly; if impossible, say so and do not force
Vaginal wallsDischarge: white, thick, cheese-like (candida) · thin grey, fishy (BV) (pH > 4.5; normal vaginal pH 4.5) · frothy green (trichomonas) · blood · tears
FornicesPool of clear fluid in the posterior fornix = liquor · meconium (green) · blood
OsClosed (nulliparous round, parous slit) or open (cm) · membranes bulging · cord or a fetal part seen
Fluid from the osLeak on coughing = ruptured membranes · blood from the os = uterine source
Cervix surfaceEctropion (red ring, bleeds on touch) · polyp · ulcer or mass (cancer) · IUCD threads
