16. Example 6 · tachycardia with reduced variability
READ IT · Baseline about 170 (T), variability < 5, no accelerations. Look at the mother: fever, long ROM, offensive liquor = chorioamnionitis; also dehydration, β-agonists, hypoxia. Two suspicious features = pathological.
17. Category → action
Bradycardia drill · whyماذا تقول
1Call for help, note the timeضربات قلب الجنين نزلت، وجبنا الفريق عشان نساعدك.
2Left lateral (or all fours) · stop oxytocin · IV fluid bolus · treat low BPلفّي على جنبك الشمال لو سمحتي.
3VE: exclude cord prolapse, check dilatation and stationمحتاج أكشف عليك من تحت بسرعة.
4Tachysystole: terbutaline 0.25 mg SC
5Not recovering by 9 min: deliver: 1st stage → category 1 CS · fully dilated, head low → instrumentalلازم نولّدك هسي عشان سلامة الجنين.
Presenting the findings · example 6
"Baseline 170, variability under 5, no accelerations or decelerations. The mother is 38.6 °C with membranes ruptured 30 hours. This is pathological, likely chorioamnionitis. I will give paracetamol, IV fluids and IV antibiotics, take blood cultures and HVS, and plan to deliver."
