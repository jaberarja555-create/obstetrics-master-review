Face and Brow Presentation

5Definition

Face: head fully extended, occiput on the back; denominator = mentum (chin). Brow: head midway between flexion and extension; denominator = frontal bone.

Incidence≈ 1 in 500≈ 1 in 2000 · the least common

6Aetiology
  • Anencephaly (face): scan it
  • Neck mass: goitre, cystic hygroma
  • Cord coils round the neck
  • Multiparity, lax uterus
  • Contracted pelvis · polyhydramnios
  • Prematurity · often no cause
