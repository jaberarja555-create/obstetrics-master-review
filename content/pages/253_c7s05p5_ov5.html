Vaginal Examination in Labour

10. Vaginal Examination in Labour: the Steps
Task

"This model is a primigravida at 40 weeks in labour. Perform a vaginal examination, explain as you go, and report your findings." Or: "…at 42 weeks booked for induction: assess her cervix (Bishop score)."

Stepماذا تقول
Before قبل الكشف
1Introduce, identify, explain, consent, chaperone, empty bladderحأكشف بالأصابع عشان أعرف فتحة الرحم قدر كم ونزول راس الجنا. موافقة؟
2Abdomen first: fundus, lie, fifths, contractions, FHR
3Position: on her back, heels together, knees apart; between contractionsحأنتظر لحدي ما الطلقة دي تخلص.
4Hand rub, sterile gloves; inspect the vulva: show, liquor colour, blood, oedema, FGM, warts, herpes
5Clean front to back with antiseptic (one swab per stroke)حتحسي بحاجة باردة.
Examine الكشف
6Part the labia (left hand); lubricated index + middle fingers of the right hand in, along the posterior wallخذي نفس عميق وارخي، حتحسي بضغط.
7Cervix: dilatation, length (effacement), consistency, position, applied to the head?
8Membranes intact or not · liquor colour · no cord felt
9Presenting part, station (vs spines), position (sutures, fontanelles), moulding, caput
10Withdraw; look at the glove: blood, liquor, meconium
After بعد الكشف
11Listen to the FHR again · cover her · explain the resultالفتحة أربعة سم، والولادة ماشية كويس.
12Gloves off, hand hygiene, record on the partogram
Distinguishing features · Before you put a finger in
Bleeding, no scan yetNo VE: scan first
ROM, FHR drops to 70VE now: cord prolapse?
Urge to push at 6 cm 1 h agoVE: full dilatation?
PPROM 30 wk, no painsSpeculum, not VE
Viva questions
Why examine the abdomen before the VE?
Fifths, lie and FHR first; the VE then makes sense of station.
First thing after membranes rupture?
FHR and VE to exclude cord prolapse.
