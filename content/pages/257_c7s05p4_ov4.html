4. Taking swabs
High vaginal swab (HVS)

Roll the swab in the posterior fornix for a few seconds, into its transport medium. For candida, BV, trichomonas, GBS.

Endocervical swab

Insert 1–2 cm into the os, rotate gently, withdraw without touching the walls. For gonorrhoea, chlamydia.

Viva questions
Name 3 causes of a false-positive nitrazine test.
Blood, semen, BV (also antiseptic, infected urine).
How is a fern test done?
Fluid from the fornix dried on a slide, viewed under the microscope.
6. Presenting the findings
Presenting to the examiner

“On sterile speculum examination: the vulva is healthy. There is a pool of clear fluid in the posterior fornix, more on coughing; nitrazine turned blue. The os is closed, no cord or fetal parts seen. HVS taken. These findings confirm ruptured membranes at 33 weeks: PPROM. I would not do a digital examination.”

What to say · to her
“The water around the baby has started to leak. You will stay in hospital; we will give you an injection to help the baby’s lungs and an antibiotic to prevent infection.”
