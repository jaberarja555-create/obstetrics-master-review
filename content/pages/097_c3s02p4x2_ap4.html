29. Complications

Maternal: massive PPH, DIC · hysterectomy · bladder, ureter injury · death. Fetal: preterm birth.

30. Management
  • Specialist centre, team: obstetrician, anaesthetist, urologist, blood bank
  • Consent for hysterectomy · cross-match 6–8 units · cell salvage if available
  • Planned caesarean at 36 wk; uterine incision away from the placenta
  • Do not pull the placenta: hysterectomy with placenta in place
31. Prevention

Avoid caesareans that are not needed: every scar raises the next risk.

Vasa Praevia

32. Definition

Fetal vessels run in the membranes, unprotected by cord or placenta, over or near the internal os. They tear at ROM: the blood lost is fetal, not maternal.
