7.8Operative delivery stations
Operative delivery stations: ventouse and forceps on a model, and caesarean section (consent, preparation, post-operative orders, viva).
1Indications
  • Prolonged 2nd stage
  • Maternal exhaustion
  • Fetal distress in the 2nd stage
  • Conditions where pushing is unsafe (heart disease, severe PE)
2Conditions before use (FORCEPS)
  • Fully dilated, membranes ruptured
  • Head ≤ 1/5 palpable, at or below the spines
  • Position known
  • Empty bladder
  • Analgesia
  • Consent
  • A place to go (theatre) if it fails
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Prolonged 2nd stage, head 0/5, station +2, OA → Low ventouse or forceps
3Ventouse
  • Cup on the flexion point (3 cm in front of the posterior fontanelle, on the sagittal suture)
  • Pull with contractions along the pelvic curve
  • Max 3 pulls, stop after 2 detachments
  • Not before 32 wk; care at 32–36 wk
  • Not for a face
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Delivery needed in the 2nd stage at 30 wk → Forceps, not ventouse
