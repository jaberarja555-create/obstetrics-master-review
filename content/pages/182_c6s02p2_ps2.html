7. Recognising sepsis early

Every postnatal woman has her observations on a MEOWS chart (modified early obstetric warning score). One red or two yellow triggers → urgent review by a doctor; suspect sepsis first if there is fever or a source.

SignYellow triggerRed trigger
Temperature35–36 °C< 35 or ≥ 38 °C
Systolic BP90–100 or 150–160< 90 or > 160
Heart rate100–120 or 40–50> 120 or < 40
Respiratory rate21–30> 30 or < 10
SpO₂—< 95%
Conscious level—Responds only to voice or pain, or confused
Obstetric qSOFA: ≥ 2 = high risk

Respiratory rate ≥ 25 · systolic BP < 90 · altered mental state. (Non-pregnant qSOFA: RR ≥ 22, SBP ≤ 100.)

Septic shock

Low BP despite fluids, needing vasopressors, or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L → ICU.
