Viva questions
Why examine the abdomen before the VE?
Fifths, lie and FHR first; the VE then makes sense of station.
First thing after membranes rupture?
FHR and VE to exclude cord prolapse.
11Indications
  • Confirm labour, then every 4 h (every 2 h on oxytocin)
  • Before ARM or induction (Bishop)
  • After the membranes rupture: exclude cord prolapse
  • FHR suddenly abnormal · urge to push
12Do not
  • APH before the scan excludes praevia
  • PPROM not in labour (speculum only) (PPROM = rupture before 37 wk; ROM > 24 h raises the infection risk)
  • Without consent, chaperone, gloves
  • During a contraction
