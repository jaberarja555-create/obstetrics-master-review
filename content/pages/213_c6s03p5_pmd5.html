21. Prevention
At booking: ask every woman
  • Past depression, psychosis, bipolar
  • Previous postnatal illness
  • Family history of bipolar or puerperal psychosis
  • Current psychiatric drugs
  • Support at home, wanted pregnancy
  • EPDS at booking and at 6 wk
  • Past PND: involve family, early review
Women with bipolar disorder
  • Pre-pregnancy counselling: switch valproate
  • Shared psychiatry plan in pregnancy
  • Hospital birth · protect sleep
  • Prophylaxis right after delivery: antipsychotic or lithium
  • Daily review for the first 2 weeks
