7. Applying it on the model
Step · whyماذا تقول
1FORCEPS checks; lithotomy, asepsis, catheter, test the pumpحنرفع رجليك على الحمّالات ونفضّي البول.
2Fold the cup, insert it sideways, place the centre on the flexion point
3Raise pressure to 0.2 kg/cm², run a finger round the rim: no vaginal skin or cervix caught; then up to 0.8 kg/cm² (green zone)
Step · whyماذا تقول
4Pull only with a contraction and her push, along the pelvic axis, perpendicular to the cup; other hand's fingers on the cup (finger-thumb) to feel slippingمع الطلقة: خدي نفس وازحري بكل قوتك… أحسنتي!
5Head should descend with every pull; episiotomy as the head distends the perineum, if needed
6Head born → release vacuum, deliver as normal · record pulls, detachments, time
When to stop: go to caesarean

No descent with a pull · head not born after 3 pulls · cup comes off twice · more than 15 min from application. Do not then try forceps "to finish" without clear descent (sequential instruments injure the baby).
