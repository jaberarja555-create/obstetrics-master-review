11. Episiotomy
Step · whyماذا تقول
1Explain the reason, consentمحتاجين نعمل فتحة صغيرة عشان الجنا يطلع بسهولة وما يحصل شق كبير. حنخدّر المكان أول.
2Clean; lidocaine 1% 10 mL: two fingers between head and perineum, needle from the fourchette along the line, aspirate before injecting, fan out. Wait 2–3 minحتحسي بوخزة صغيرة وحرقان شوية.
3Cut at crowning, at the height of a contraction (perineum thin, less bleeding); index and middle fingers protect the head
4One clean cut, straight blunt-ended scissors, mediolateral, 60°, 3–4 cmممكن تحسي بضغط، ما حيوجعك.
5Control the head (flexion + guard); delivery follows quickly. Pad on the cut if bleedingازحري هسي!
6After the placenta: look for extension, PR to feel the sphincter, then repair (OASI: best repaired at once, within 24 h)حأكشف من ورا بصباعي عشان أتأكد إنو العضلة سليمة.
12. Degrees of perineal tear

Each degree includes the layers above it. Always do a PR before calling a tear 2nd degree: a sphincter tear missed now becomes faecal incontinence or a rectovaginal fistula later.

Presenting the findings
"For delay with a rigid perineum I did a right mediolateral episiotomy under 10 mL of 1% lidocaine at crowning. The baby was born at the next contraction. On inspection the cut did not extend, and on PR the anal sphincter was intact. I will now repair it in layers."
«عملت فتحة جانبية صغيرة بعد التخدير، والجنا اتولد مع الطلقة الجاية. الجرح ما امتد، والعضلة سليمة. هسي حأخيّط الجرح.»
