4. Clinical features
Look forNote
SymptomsFever, rigors, sweats · headache, vomitingCan mimic labour pains
SignsPallor · splenomegaly · mild jaundiceSevere: coma, fits, shock
Best next step

Fever in pregnancy in an endemic area: test for malaria first (RDT or film). Many have no fever: anaemia or a small baby may be the only clue.

5. Investigations
  • Thick film: finds parasites, density
  • Thin film: species, % parasitaemia
  • RDT: quick, at the bedside
  • Negative but suspicious: repeat the film

Severity: RBG (and during quinine) · FBC · creatinine, bilirubin, urine output · urine for haemoglobinuria · fetal heart, growth scan.

6. Differential diagnosis
PresentationAlso consider
FeverUTI / pyelonephritis, typhoid, chorioamnionitis, brucellosis
Fits / comaEclampsia, meningitis, hypoglycaemia, epilepsy
JaundiceHepatitis (E is severe in pregnancy), HELLP, acute fatty liver

Cerebral malaria
