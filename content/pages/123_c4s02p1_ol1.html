4.2Monitoring in Labour

1. Definition

A graphic record of labour on one sheet: fetal condition, progress and maternal condition against time. It shows normal from abnormal labour early and cuts prolonged labour, obstruction, rupture and fistula. Open it in the active phase, from 4 cm.
