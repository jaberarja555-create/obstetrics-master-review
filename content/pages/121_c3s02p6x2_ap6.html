36Investigations

Antenatal: transvaginal colour Doppler if the 20 wk scan shows a risk factor. In a bleed: Apt test or Kleihauer, but never wait for it.

37Management
Known before labour
  • Admit at 30–32 wk, steroids
  • Caesarean at 34–36 wk, before ROM
  • No ARM, no sweep
Bleeding in labour
  • 1st stage: immediate caesarean (decision to delivery < 30 min)
  • Fully dilated, head low: instrumental (fastest)
  • Neonatal team, O-neg blood (10–20 mL/kg for the baby)
Clinical scenarios
Test to show that vaginal blood is fetal?
Apt test (fetal Hb resists alkali).
Bleed at ROM, FHR 80, fully dilated, head at +2. Best delivery?
Instrumental delivery now: fastest route.

Uterine Rupture

38Definition

Complete = all layers torn, endometrium to serosa · incomplete (dehiscence) = serosa intact, often silent, found at CS. Most happen in labour.

39Risk factors
  • Scar: VBAC, classical CS, myomectomy (rupture risk ≈ 0.5% after one lower-segment scar, 4–9% after a classical scar)
  • Obstructed labour in a multipara: malpresentation, macrosomia, hydrocephalus, contracted pelvis
  • Oxytocin or misoprostol overdose
  • Grand multiparity · trauma · internal version
