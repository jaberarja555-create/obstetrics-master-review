Chapter 3 · 3.2 Antepartum HaemorrhageUterine Rupture123
43Management

Call for help, resuscitate (as in abruption) → immediate laparotomy: deliver, then repair a clean tear (± tubal ligation, with consent) or hysterectomy if irregular, infected or unstable. Check the bladder.

Prevention: partograph, no oxytocin in obstructed labour, careful oxytocin with a scar; VBAC only in a hospital with theatre (and only after one lower-segment scar).

Distinguishing features
Obstructed labour, primigravidaFistula more likely
Obstructed labour, multiparaRupture more likely
Clean lower-segment tear, wants childrenRepair
Irregular infected tear, unstableHysterectomy
