4. How high is the head?
What to say · consent
"Your baby needs to be born soon because the heartbeat has slowed / you are very tired. I would like to help with a soft cup on the baby's head. With your next pains you push and I pull gently. It may leave a swelling that settles in days. If it does not work quickly, we go to theatre for a caesarean."
«الجنا لازم يتولد قريب لأنو دقات قلبه بطّأت / انتي تعبتي شديد. عايز أساعدك بكاسة ناعمة على راس الجنا: مع الطلقة انتي تزحري وأنا أشد بالراحة. ممكن تعمل ورم بسيط بيروح في أيام. لو ما نفعت بسرعة بنمشي العمليات ونعمل قيصرية. موافقة؟»
5. Ventouse: cups, flexion point and pulls

Ventouse (vacuum extractor): a cup held on the scalp by suction; the scalp sucked in forms an artificial caput, the chignon. Needs the mother to push. Not before 34 wk.
