11. Failed induction

Labour does not start after one full course (2 prostin doses or Foley, then ARM + oxytocin for 6–12 h). Reassess indication, fetus and cervix → rest and a second course, or caesarean. Fetal distress at any point → CS.

12. Induction after previous caesarean
One lower-segment CS
  • Induce only in a hospital with theatre
  • Foley balloon + ARM + oxytocin
  • Prostin: lowest dose, with care
  • No misoprostol with a live fetus
  • Continuous CTG; low oxytocin ceiling
Do not induce
  • Classical or T-shaped scar
  • Two or more previous CS
  • Previous rupture
  • Recurrent cause: CPD, praevia
  • → Elective caesarean
Exam trap

Scar rupture often shows first as fetal bradycardia, not pain. Then: scar pain, bleeding, contractions stop, head rises. Stop oxytocin → laparotomy.

Clinical scenarios
On oxytocin for 18 h with 3 L of 5% dextrose; now confused, fitting. Cause?
Water intoxication (hyponatraemia).
One previous CS, 41+3 wk, Bishop 3. Method of choice?
Foley balloon, then ARM + oxytocin.
13. Prevention

Induce only for a clear indication with sure dates · score the cervix before choosing the drug · one agent at a time · titrate oxytocin by pump or counted drops · CTG and partograph throughout · active management of the 3rd stage.
