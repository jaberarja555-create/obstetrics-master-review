Next pregnancy plan

Folic acid before conceiving · early booking · OGTT · aspirin if a placental cause · serial growth scans · delivery at term, not past dates. Treat the cause found (malaria, diabetes, APS).

What to say · closing
"I am very sorry for your loss. You are not alone: we will be with you through the birth and after. Here is when we meet again."
«أنا آسف جداً على فقدك. إنتي ما براك: حنكون معاك في الولادة وبعدها. وده ميعاد زيارتك الجاية.»
Common errors

"Don't worry, you are young" · promising a cause will be found · forgetting lactation and anti-D · no follow-up date.

Viva questions
Why avoid cabergoline in pre-eclampsia?
It constricts vessels: BP rises, stroke risk.
When is the follow-up visit, and what is its aim?
6 weeks: results, cause, grief, next pregnancy plan.
