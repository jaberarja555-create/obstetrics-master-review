5. How often in practice
Every antenatal visitBP + urine dipstick
Normal labourBP 4-hourly, pulse every 30 min
Severe pre-eclampsiaBP every 15 min until stable
After birth (PE)Keep measuring: eclampsia can follow birth
6. Viva questions
Why does BP fall in the 2nd trimester?Vessels relax: lowest at 20–24 wk
Drug for BP 170/115 at 36 wk?Labetalol IV or hydralazine 5 mg IV; nifedipine oral (target ≈ 130/85)
Which drugs never in pregnancy?ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics
Marks commonly lost

Wrong cuff · arm hanging · no palpatory estimate · deflating fast · K4 by habit · one high reading called "pre-eclampsia" · no repeat, no urine test.
