3.6Intrauterine Fetal Death

1. Definition

Stillbirth = a baby born with no signs of life after 24 wk or weighing ≥ 1 kg. IUFD = death of the fetus in the uterus after the cut-off of viability; WHO (and our exams) use ≥ 28 wk or ≥ 1000 g. Fetal loss before the cut-off is managed as a miscarriage.

2. Aetiology
GroupCauses
Unexplained≈ 50%: the commonest single group
MaternalPre-eclampsia, HTN · diabetes · infection: malaria, TORCH, syphilis, listeria · anaemia · thyroid disease · thrombophilia (APS, protein C/S) · Rh isoimmunisation
FetalCongenital anomaly (structural, chromosomal) · FGR · twins: TTTS · hydrops · feto-maternal haemorrhage
Placenta, cordAbruption and other APH · placental insufficiency, villous dysmaturity · cord accident: true knot, prolapse, tight loops
Distinguishing features · The likely cause

IUFD + macrosomia, polyhydramniosDiabetes: OGTT, HbA1c
Recurrent IUFD + DVT, miscarriagesAntiphospholipid syndrome
3. Clinical features

History: reduced or absent fetal movements · breasts soften, pregnancy symptoms fade · may present in labour or with bleeding. Ask: fever, BP, sugar, bleeding, previous IUFD, drugs.

Examination: uterus small for dates, SFH and weight stop rising · no fetal heart on Pinard or Doppler (suggestive, not diagnostic) · check pulse, BP, temp, pallor.
