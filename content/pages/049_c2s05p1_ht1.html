2.5Hypertensive Disorders of Pregnancy

1. Definition

Hypertension in pregnancy = BP ≥ 140/90 on two readings 4 h apart (seated, correct cuff, diastolic = Korotkoff V). One reading is enough if ≥ 160/110.

Pre-eclampsia: traditional definition = new HTN after 20 wk + proteinuria (≥ 300 mg/24 h or PCR ≥ 30 mg/mmol). Newer definition: proteinuria or maternal organ damage (↑ creatinine, ↑ liver enzymes, ↓ platelets, fits, visual loss, pulmonary oedema) or fetal growth restriction. In the exam, give the older definition first, then the current one.
