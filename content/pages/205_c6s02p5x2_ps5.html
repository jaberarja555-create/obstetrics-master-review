13. Complications
ComplicationClue and action
Septic shock, MODSLow BP despite fluids, lactate ≥ 4 · AKI, ARDS, liver failure → ICU, vasopressors
DICOozing from drips and wounds, low platelets and fibrinogen → treat sepsis, FFP, cryoprecipitate, platelets
Pelvic abscessSwinging fever, pelvic mass, diarrhoea, tenesmus (pouch of Douglas) → drain
Septic pelvic thrombophlebitisUsually ovarian vein, after CS · spiking fever despite antibiotics, patient looks well · CT/MRI; a diagnosis of exclusion → add therapeutic heparin
PeritonitisDistension, guarding, ileus · resuscitate, laparotomy
Necrotising fasciitisWound pain out of proportion, dusky skin, crepitus · urgent debridement
Secondary PPHInfected placental site or retained tissue (24 h – 6 wk)
LateTubal block → infertility, ectopic, chronic pelvic pain, wound hernia · death
BabyNeonatal GAS or GBS sepsis; feeding interrupted
Exam trap

Fever that persists on antibiotics is rarely "resistant bacteria" first: look for pus (abscess, wound), retained products, pelvic vein thrombosis, DVT and malaria.
