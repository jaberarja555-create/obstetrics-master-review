2.12HIV in Pregnancy

1. Definition

MTCT = HIV passes to the baby in utero, in labour or by breast milk.

2. Pathophysiology
Clinical picture
Lowest transmission
ART early+VL < 50+infant NVP=< 1 % MTCT
3. Risk factors for transmission
  • High viral load: the strongest factor
  • Low CD4, advanced disease, no ART
  • New infection in pregnancy
  • Prolonged ROM · chorioamnionitis
  • Preterm · early ARM · scalp electrode
  • Mixed feeding · cracked nipples
4. Complications

Maternal: repeated miscarriage, PROM, preterm labour, opportunistic infection, TB. Fetal: IUGR, prematurity, stillbirth, infected baby.

5. Investigations · screening
  • Offer HIV testing to every woman at booking (opt-out: tested unless she declines), with syphilis and hepatitis B.
  • Rapid test → positive: confirm with a second, different test (ELISA ± Western blot). Retest in late pregnancy if at risk.
  • Baseline: CD4, viral load, Hb, creatinine, LFT, HBV, HCV, TB screen; test the partner.
Distinguishing features

Test · Treat · Suppress · Protect. Test at booking, treat for life, suppress the viral load by 36 wk, protect the baby.
