8. Complications

Maternal (early): neurogenic and haemorrhagic shock · massive PPH, DIC · death.

Maternal (late): infection, endometritis · necrosis of a trapped fundus · re-inversion · recurrence next birth.

9. Management: the drill
THE ORDER · Replace first, everything else in parallel: every minute the cervical ring tightens and the uterus swells.
Exam trap

Do not remove the placenta before replacement: the raw bed bleeds torrentially and shock deepens. Replace the uterus with the placenta on, then remove it.
