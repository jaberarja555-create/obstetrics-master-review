6.4Maternal Mortality

1. Definition

Maternal death = death of a woman while pregnant or within 42 days of the end of pregnancy, whatever its duration or site, from any cause related to or aggravated by the pregnancy or its management, but not from accidental or incidental causes.

2. Classification
TypeMeaningExamples
DirectFrom an obstetric complication of pregnancy, labour or puerperium, or from interventions, omissions or wrong treatmentPPH, eclampsia, sepsis, VTE, ectopic, AFE, anaesthesia
IndirectPre-existing or new disease, not obstetric, made worse by the physiology of pregnancyHeart disease, anaemia, malaria, HIV, epilepsy
LateDirect or indirect cause, 42 days to 1 year after the end of pregnancyCardiomyopathy, suicide
CoincidentalWould have happened anyway; not counted in the MMRRoad accident, violence
Pregnancy-related deathDeath in pregnancy or within 42 days from any cause, accidents includedAny death to day 42
