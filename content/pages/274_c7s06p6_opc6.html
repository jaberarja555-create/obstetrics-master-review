13. Example 3 · variable decelerations
READ IT · Baseline 140, variability normal, abrupt V-shaped dips of 45–70 bpm, quick recovery, not tied to the same point of each contraction (V). Cause: cord compression (oligohydramnios, after ROM). Suspicious.
14. Example 4 · late decelerations
READ IT · Baseline 155, variability < 5, smooth dips with the lowest point after the peak of every contraction (L). Cause: placental insufficiency (PE, FGR, abruption, oxytocin). Pathological.
Presenting the findings · example 4
"A woman at 38 weeks with pre-eclampsia in labour. Three contractions in 10 minutes. Baseline 155, variability reduced below 5, no accelerations, repeated late decelerations. This is a pathological CTG. I would turn her to the left, stop oxytocin, give IV fluids, call the obstetric team and check for fetal acidosis or deliver."
