11. More drugs: steroids, anti-D, iron, tocolysis
Dexamethasone / betamethasone
Dexamethasone / betamethasoneالديكساميثازون / البيتاميثازون
Steroid ampoules for IM use. Fetal lung maturity < 34 wk.
Viva · Dexa 6 mg IM 12-hourly × 4; beta 12 mg IM × 2, 24 h apart.
Anti-D immunoglobulin
Anti-D immunoglobulinالأنتي دي
Prefilled syringe, fridge. RhD-negative mother after a sensitising event.
Viva · 500 IU IM within 72 h + Kleihauer; 250 IU before 20 wk.
Iron + folic acid
Iron + folic acidالحديد وحمض الفوليك
Ferrous sulphate 200 mg (65 mg iron) + folic acid. Prevention and treatment.
Viva · Black stools, constipation. Not with tea; go on 3 months after Hb is normal.
Terbutaline
Terbutalineالتربيوتالين
β₂-agonist ampoule. Acute tocolysis: cord prolapse, hyperstimulation.
Viva · 0.25 mg SC. Tachycardia; avoid in heart disease.
12. Ultrasound spotters: early pregnancy
Gestational sacYolk sac
Scan · sac with yolk sac
Scan: sac with yolk sacسونار: كيس الحمل داخل الرحم
Round sac inside the uterus with a small ring (yolk sac).
Viva · Confirms intrauterine pregnancy. TVS sees a sac when β-hCG ≥ 2000 IU/L.
Fetus: crown-rump length
Scan · CRL and heartbeat
Scan: CRL and heartbeatسونار: طول الجنين ونبضه
Fetal pole measured crown to rump; heart flicker.
Viva · Best dating at 7–13 wk. CRL ≥ 7 mm, no heart → missed miscarriage.
Snowstorm: many small cysts
Scan · snowstorm
Scan: "snowstorm"سونار: الحمل العنقودي
Uterus full of small cysts, no fetus: complete mole.
Viva · β-hCG very high. Suction evacuation, then follow hCG.
Empty uterusAdnexal mass
Scan · empty uterus + adnexal mass
Scan: empty uterus + adnexal massسونار: حمل خارج الرحم
No sac in the uterus, mass beside the ovary, fluid in the pouch.
Viva · Positive test + empty uterus = ectopic until proved otherwise.
13. Delivery-room extras
Lidocaine 1%
Lidocaine 1%الليدوكايين
Local anaesthetic vial. Infiltrate before episiotomy and repair.
Viva · About 10 mL; aspirate first; max 3 mg/kg (plain).
Absorbable suture 2-0
Absorbable suture 2-0خيط جراحي ممتص
Rapid polyglactin on a round-bodied needle. Vagina, muscle and skin of tears.
Viva · Continuous for vagina and muscle; subcuticular skin; then PR check.
Neonatal suction (bulb / catheter)
Neonatal suction (bulb / catheter)شفاط المولود
Bulb or soft catheter with a mucus trap.
Viva · Only if the airway is blocked; deep suction slows the heart.
Vitamin K (phytomenadione)
Vitamin K (phytomenadione)فيتامين ك
1 mg ampoule for the newborn, IM in the thigh, at birth.
Viva · Prevents haemorrhagic disease of the newborn.
