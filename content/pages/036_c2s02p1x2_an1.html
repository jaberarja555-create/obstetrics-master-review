4. Risk factors

Poor diet, pica, tea with meals · high parity, short birth interval · twins · teenage pregnancy · worms, malaria · sickle cell, thalassaemia · anti-epileptics.

5. Clinical features
History (often nothing)
  • Tired, dizzy, breathless
  • Pica · bleeding, worms
  • Family history: anaemia, jaundice
Examination
  • Pallor · tachycardia, flow murmur
  • Iron: smooth tongue, spoon nails
  • Jaundice, spleen · failure: S3
Clinical scenario
Hb cut-off for anaemia at 30 weeks?
Below 10.5 g/dL.
6. Investigations
First lineFBC + MCV · film · ferritin · malaria film/RDT · stool ova · urine
No responseElectrophoresis · B12, folate · G6PD · renal, liver, thyroid · HIV, TB
WHICH ANAEMIA? · Low MCV is iron until ferritin says otherwise. High MCV is folate in pregnancy. Normal MCV: look for haemolysis, malaria or chronic disease.
