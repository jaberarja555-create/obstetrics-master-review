6The three delays
  • Delay 1: deciding to seek care
  • Delay 2: reaching care
  • Delay 3: receiving care
  • Untreated, death in about 2 h with PPH, 1 day with rupture, 2 days with eclampsia, 6 days with sepsis
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Waits for her husband's permission → Delay 1
  • Maternity waiting homes → Delay 2
  • Reaches hospital, no blood for 3 h → Delay 3
7Prevention
  • Family planning, treat anaemia, girls' education
  • Antenatal care: 4–8 visits, IPTp and nets
  • Skilled birth attendant, partograph, AMTSL
  • EmONC: basic (antibiotics, oxytocics, MgSO₄, manual placenta, MVA, assisted birth, newborn resuscitation); comprehensive adds caesarean and blood
  • Postnatal checks
  • Post-abortion care
  • MDSR: every death reviewed with no blame
