Why is a primigravida more at risk?
No immunity yet to parasites that bind the placenta.
Drug for uncomplicated malaria in pregnancy (Sudan)?
Artemether-lumefantrine, any trimester, with fatty food.
18. Examiner marking points
  • Fever pattern, rigors
  • Severe features asked one by one
  • Can she swallow tablets?
  • UTI, chorioamnionitis excluded
  • Net, IPTp (first SP dose after quickening, 16–20 wk), drugs, allergy
  • Fetal movements, contractions
