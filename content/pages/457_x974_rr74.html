  • Unripe cervix, previous CS → Foley balloon
  • Unripe cervix, IUFD at 34 wk → Misoprostol
  • Prostin 2 h ago, no labour → Wait 6 h before oxytocin
5Oxytocin regimen
  • 5 IU in 500 mL saline = 10 mU/mL
  • Start 2 mU/min (4 drops)
  • Increase 2–4 mU/min every 30 min
  • Aim 3–4 contractions in 10 min, 40–60 s each
  • Maximum 32 mU/min
  • Saline, not large volumes of dextrose (water intoxication)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • 18 h of oxytocin with 3 L of 5% dextrose, now confused and fitting → Water intoxication (hyponatraemia)
6Complications and hyperstimulation
  • Drugs: hyperstimulation → fetal distress, uterine rupture; water intoxication
  • ARM: cord prolapse (high head), infection, abruption (polyhydramnios)
  • Any IOL: failed induction, prematurity, PPH, instrumental birth
  • Tachysystole: over 5 contractions in 10 min; hypertonus: one over 2 min; hyperstimulation = either + abnormal FHR
  • Stop oxytocin, left lateral, fluids, terbutaline 0.25 mg SC; restart lower after 30 min; persistent bradycardia → CS
🩺 Exam scenario
  • On oxytocin, 6 in 10 min, FHR 80 → Stop it, terbutaline
