17. Cervical cerclage
  • McDonald: purse-string at the cervico-vaginal junction, simple
  • Shirodkar: higher, bladder pushed up, harder
  • Contraindications: bleeding, contractions, infection, ROM, lethal anomaly
  • Remove at 37 wk, or at once with labour, ROM, APH or IUFD
  • Bulging membranes: reduce them first; success falls with wider dilatation
18. General measures
  • Treat bacterial vaginosis: metronidazole 500 mg BD × 7 d or clindamycin
  • Screen and treat bacteriuria, UTI, malaria
  • Stop smoking · improve nutrition, weight
  • Space pregnancies ≥ 1 yr
  • Control diabetes and hypertension
