4Antenatal management
  • Confirm dates; deliver earlier with PE, diabetes, FGR or APH
  • Membrane sweep at 40–41 wk (not with praevia or after ROM)
  • Offer induction from 41+0 wk (NICE 2021); local units often at 41+3
  • Fewer stillbirths, no rise in caesareans
  • Declines: CTG + deepest pool twice a week, kick count
  • Induce now with low liquor (deepest pool under 2 cm), reduced movements, poor growth, abnormal CTG
🩺 Exam scenario
  • 41+4 wk, declined induction, deepest pool 1.5 cm → Induce now
5Intrapartum care
  • Continuous CTG
  • Early ARM to see the liquor
  • Meconium + abnormal CTG → CS
  • Ready for shoulder dystocia
  • Neonatal team; active third stage
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Suctioning the mouth on the perineum to prevent meconium aspiration is not done
4.6Induction of labour
Induction of labour starts labour artificially before it begins on its own, aiming at vaginal birth. It differs from augmentation, which speeds up labour that has already started. The Bishop score decides whether the cervix must be ripened first.
1Indications
  • Prolonged pregnancy (commonest)
  • PROM at term
  • Pre-eclampsia, eclampsia
  • Unexplained APH at term
