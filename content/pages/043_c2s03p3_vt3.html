9. Management · intrapartum

Stop injecting at onset of labour or bleeding; time the last dose before planned IOL/CS. No regional block on IV heparin.

Distinguishing features
DVT at 10 wkLMWH, not warfarin
Last treatment dose 8 h ago, wants epiduralNo block: wait 24 h
PE, BP 75/40, gaspingUFH + thrombolysis
Previous unprovoked DVT, now 8 wkLMWH whole pregnancy
10. Management · postpartum

LMWH for ≥ 6 wk, or switch to warfarin: overlap until INR 2–3 on 2 days (≥ 5 days). Both safe in breastfeeding.

Clinical scenario
Breastfeeding mother on warfarin: stop feeding?
No: warfarin and heparin are both safe.
11. Massive PE

Hypotension + respiratory distress. ICU, O₂, fluids → IV UFH 5000 IU then 1000–2000 IU/h → thrombolysis or embolectomy. IVC filter if anticoagulation is contraindicated or PE recurs on it.

12. Prevention · thromboprophylaxis

Assess risk at booking, every admission, 36 wk, in labour and after birth. Count risk factors:

0–1 factorMobilise
2 factors10 d postpartum
3 factors28 wk → 6 wk PN
≥ 4 factorsAll pregnancy + 6 wk

Previous VTE: after major surgery → as 3 factors; unprovoked, oestrogen-related or with thrombophilia → whole pregnancy + 6 wk. Prophylactic enoxaparin: 40 mg once daily (50–90 kg) · 20 mg if < 50 kg · 60 mg if 91–130 kg.
