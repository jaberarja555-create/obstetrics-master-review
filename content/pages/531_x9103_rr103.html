OSCE Stations
7.1The OSCE station
An OSCE is a series of timed stations (about 5–8 min each) with a simulated patient, a model or data, marked on a fixed checklist. Every station has the same opening and closing, which carry easy marks.
1Station types
  • History
  • Examination on a model or patient
  • Procedure (delivery, VE, BP, dipstick)
  • Data (partogram, CTG)
