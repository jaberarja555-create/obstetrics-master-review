Presenting the case

"Fatima, 26, G3 P1+1 at 9 weeks, has heavy bleeding with clots and central colicky pain since this morning; she passed some tissue. No shoulder pain, no fever. Impression: incomplete miscarriage; ectopic must be excluded. I would check pulse and BP, do a speculum exam and a scan, FBC, group and Rh."

Viva questions
Pain stopped, bleeding settled, scan: empty uterus. Can you call it complete?
Not yet: ectopic possible; serial β-hCG.
Rh-negative, miscarriage at 11 wk. What does she need?
No anti-D before 12 weeks (NICE 2025); local practice often gives 250 IU IM within 72 h from 10 weeks.
