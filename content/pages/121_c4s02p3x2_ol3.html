10. Fetal distress

Diagnosis: pathological CTG + confirmation by fetal scalp blood pH where possible. Clues: thick meconium, FHR < 100 or > 160, late decelerations.

pH ≥ 7.25Normal · continue
pH 7.21–7.24Repeat in 30 min
pH ≤ 7.20Acidosis: deliver

Intrauterine resuscitation: left lateral · stop oxytocin · IV fluids · treat hypotension · terbutaline 0.25 mg SC for hyperstimulation · VE to exclude cord prolapse. Deliver: 1st stage → emergency CS · 2nd stage → instrumental. Complications: HIE, cerebral palsy, meconium aspiration, NICU admission.
