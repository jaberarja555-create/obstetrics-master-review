3Inspection
  • Size, shape (oval = longitudinal, broad = transverse)
  • Scars: Pfannenstiel (lower segment CS), midline (classical CS, laparotomy)
  • Linea nigra, striae
  • Fetal movements
4Fundal height
  • Centre the uterus, find the fundus with the side of the hand, then tape
  • 12 wk symphysis
  • 20 wk umbilicus
  • 36 wk xiphisternum
  • SFH = weeks ± 2 cm from 24 wk
  • Off dates by > 2 cm → growth scan
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Fundus at the umbilicus → About 20 weeks
5Leopold grips
  • Fundal: what is in the fundus
  • Lateral: back or limbs
  • Pawlik (1st pelvic): what presents
  • Pelvic (2nd, facing her feet): engagement
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Hard round ballotable mass in the fundus → Breech
  • Fundus and pelvis empty, broad uterus → Transverse lie
6Engagement and liquor
  • Head in fifths: ≤ 2/5 palpable = engaged
  • Hands converge below the head = not engaged
  • Tense uterus with a fluid thrill = polyhydramnios; parts felt too easily = oligohydramnios
