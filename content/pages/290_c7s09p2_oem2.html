5. The hands: show, do not only say
Presenting the case

"Para 5, primary PPH about 1200 mL from atony. Given O₂, 2 L warm saline, 2 units, oxytocin, ergometrine, TXA. Now uterus firm, placenta complete, no tears; pulse 100, BP 110/70. Plan: oxytocin 4 h, Hb, close watch."

Common errors

Waiting for drugs while nobody rubs the uterus.

Forgetting the catheter and the placenta check.

Ergometrine in a hypertensive woman.

Counting only visible blood: clots hide.

Viva questions
Drugs fail and theatre is 10 min away. What do your hands do?
Bimanual compression until theatre.
First operation if she wants more children?
B-Lynch brace suture.
6. Examiner marking points
  • Calls for help first, names roles
  • O₂, 2 large cannulae, cross-match
  • Rubs the uterus before waiting for drugs
  • Oxytocin dose and route right
  • Catheter, looks for the other Ts
  • Talks to the woman throughout
  • Gives each person a clear task
  • Correct drug order and doses
  • Knows the "not in" for each drug
  • Shows bimanual compression correctly
  • Presents loss, cause, state, plan
