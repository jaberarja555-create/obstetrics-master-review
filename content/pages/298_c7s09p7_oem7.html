Breech delivery station: the link

Same drill spirit: call help, paediatrician, hands off the breech until the umbilicus; push from above, never pull. Manoeuvres and their clues are on the breech pages (4.7): Pinard (extended legs), Løvset (arms up, rotate 180°), Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit (head, jaw flexion), Piper forceps if MSV fails. Aim ≈ 4 min umbilicus to head.

Viva questions
Why not push the cord back into the uterus?
Handling causes spasm; it rarely stays.
Why is knee-chest unsafe in a moving car?
She cannot be held safely: use left Sims.
20. Examiner marking points

  • Does not handle or replace the cord
  • Hand stays pushing the part up
  • Knee-chest or Sims shown
