🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Day 2, severe pain needing morphine, child with a sore throat → Group A strep sepsis: antibiotics now
  • Loin pain, rigors, catheter in labour → Pyelonephritis
6Recognising sepsis early
  • MEOWS chart: 1 red or 2 yellow triggers → urgent review
  • Red: temperature < 35 or ≥ 38 °C, systolic < 90, pulse > 120, RR > 30, SpO₂ < 95%
  • Obstetric qSOFA ≥ 2: RR ≥ 25, systolic < 90, altered mental state
  • Septic shock: low BP despite fluids or lactate ≥ 4 → ICU
⚠️ Exam trap
  • A normal temperature does not exclude sepsis: hypothermia and tachycardia are worse signs
7Investigations
  • Blood cultures before antibiotics
  • High vaginal swab, MSU, wound and throat swabs
  • FBC, lactate, CRP, U&E, LFT, clotting
  • Malaria film or RDT in every fever
  • Pelvic ultrasound: retained products, collection
  • CT if fever persists
8Differential diagnosis
  • Malaria
  • Typhoid
  • Pyelonephritis
  • Appendicitis
  • PE
  • Transfusion reaction
  • Drug fever
  • Meningitis after spinal
⚠️ Exam trap
  • Never wait for culture results or a "second fever" to treat a sick woman
