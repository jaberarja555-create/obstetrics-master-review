3.5Fetal Growth Restriction (IUGR)

1. Definition

FGR (IUGR) = fetus fails to reach its growth potential from a pathological cause. SGA = weight < 10th centile for gestation: a size, not a disease. FGR raises perinatal mortality 6–10 times and the risk of cerebral palsy about 4 times.

  • Of SGA babies, about ⅓ are truly growth restricted; ⅔ are constitutionally small (small parents, normal Doppler, healthy).
  • Low birth weight = < 2.5 kg at any gestation: ⅔ are preterm, ⅓ are SGA.
  • Size also depends on parents' height, maternal weight, parity, ethnicity, fetal sex.
