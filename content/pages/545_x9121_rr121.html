6Cord prolapse drill
  • Push the presenting part up, keep it up
  • Knee-chest or exaggerated left Sims
  • Fill the bladder with 500 mL
  • Terbutaline 0.25 mg SC
  • Category 1 CS (instrumental if fully dilated)
7Newborn resuscitation
  • Dry, warm, assess tone, breathing, heart rate
  • Airway in neutral position
  • 5 inflation breaths, then ventilate
  • HR < 60 after 30 s of good ventilation → compressions 3 : 1
  • Apgar at 1, 5 min (then every 5 min if < 7)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Floppy, apnoeic newborn → 5 inflation breaths
