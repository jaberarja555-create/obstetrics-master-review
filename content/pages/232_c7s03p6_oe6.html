12. Engagement in fifths

With the pelvic grip, count how many finger breadths of head are still above the brim. The head is ≈ 5 fingers deep, so each finger = one fifth. ≤ 2/5 palpable = engaged.

RULE OF FIFTHS · Abdominal fifths are not fooled by caput, so in labour record them beside the vaginal station.
