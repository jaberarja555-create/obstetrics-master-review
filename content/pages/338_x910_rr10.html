3.4Multiple pregnancy
Lambda (twin-peak) sign on early scan → Dichorionic twins
Monochorionic twins: one with polyhydramnios, one with oligohydramnios → TTTS: fetoscopic laser < 26 wk
Twin 1 is breech in labour → Caesarean section
Zygosity
Different sex = dizygotic, DCDA · split < day 3 DCDA · days 4–7 MCDA · 8–12 MCMA · > day 12 conjoined
Chorionicity scan
11–13⁺⁶ wk · lambda = DC · T sign = MC
MC complications
TTTS (10–15%, 80% fatal untreated) · TAPS (Hb gap, normal liquor) · TRAP (acardiac twin) · co-twin death → brain injury in survivor
Maternal risks
Hyperemesis, anaemia · pre-eclampsia ×5 · preterm labour · PPH · polyhydramnios
Surveillance
MC: scans every 2 wk from 16 wk · DC: every 4 wk · FGR in 25%
Delivery
DCDA 38 wk · MCDA 37 wk · MCMA 32 wk CS · triplets 35 wk · twin 2 within 20 min
Exam traps
Give oxytocin only after twin 2 is born · weight difference > 30% is severe · twin 1 non-cephalic → CS
3.5Fetal growth restriction
SFH 4 cm less than dates at 32 weeks → Growth scan
AC small, head spared, umbilical artery absent end-diastolic flow → Asymmetrical FGR: steroids, deliver by 34 wk
Early FGR with all measurements small and an anomaly → Symmetrical FGR: karyotype
Definitions
SGA: EFW < 10th centile · severe < 3rd · low birth weight < 2.5 kg
Causes
Uteroplacental insufficiency commonest (PE, smoking, renal disease) · chromosomal, infection (symmetrical) · twins
Diagnosis
Abdominal circumference best single measure · growth scans every 3 wk · low PAPP-A · uterine artery Doppler 20–24 wk
Doppler
UA raised PI → AEDF → REDF · MCA PI low = brain sparing · MCA peak velocity high = fetal anaemia · abnormal ductus venosus = deliver now
Delivery
SGA with normal Doppler 37–38 wk · AEDF by 34 wk · REDF about 32 wk · dexamethasone 6 mg × 4 before 34 wk
Newborn
Hypoglycaemia, hypothermia, polycythaemia · adult disease later (Barker)
Exam traps
Reduced movements (< 10 in 12 h) need CTG and scan · constitutionally small babies are two-thirds of SGA · dating must come from an early scan
