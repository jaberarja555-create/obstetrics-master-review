22. The sequence
Step · whyماذا تقول للأم
1Dry and stimulate, remove wet towel, wrap, hat: cold babies become acidoticالجنا محتاج مساعدة بسيطة في النفس، والفريق شغّال معاه.
2Assess tone, breathing, heart rate (stethoscope at the apex, or feel the cord)
3Head neutral (folded towel under the shoulders); jaw lift · suction only if the airway is blocked
45 inflation breaths, each 2–3 s, air, firm mask seal over mouth and nose; watch the chest rise
5Reassess HR: rising = lungs inflated → ventilate 30/min until breathing well; no chest rise → reposition, jaw thrust, repeat
6HR < 60 after 30 s of good breaths: chest compressions, two thumbs, lower sternum, 3 : 1, call senior help
7Breathing, HR > 100: to mother skin-to-skin, keep warm, observe · document timesالحمد لله الجنا بقى يتنفس كويس. حنخليه في حضنك ونراقبه.
