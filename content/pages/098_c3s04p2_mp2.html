9. Death of one twin
DCEarly: no harm. Late: preterm labour (90% deliver within 3 wk)
MCSurvivor bleeds into the dead twin: up to 25% die or brain damage, at the moment of death, so rushed delivery does not help. Weekly scan; aim for 36 wk

Mother: rare DIC or sepsis: check clotting.

10. Antenatal management

High risk: team care (obstetrician, sonographer, fetal medicine) and more visits.

  • Anaemia: iron + folic acid daily · CBC at booking and 28 wk
  • Pre-eclampsia: BP + urine protein every visit (moderate risk factor)
  • Preterm labour: teach symptoms · steroids if birth is likely
  • Scans: booking (viability, dating) · chorionicity 11–13+6 wk · NT · anomaly scan 18–20+6 wk
