7.4Blood Pressure & Urine Testing

Task

"Huda, 34 weeks, has a headache. Measure her blood pressure and tell the examiner the reading." · 5 min · a real woman or a colleague · a manual (aneroid or mercury) sphygmomanometer, cuffs of 2 sizes, a stethoscope.

Stepماذا تقول
Prepare التحضير
1Hand hygiene · greet · identify · explain · consentحأقيس ليك الضغط، الجهاز حيضغط على ضراعك شوية. موافقة؟
2Rest 5 min; no talking during the readingارتاحي خمس دقايق، وأثناء القياس ما تتكلمي.
3Position: sitting, back supported, feet flat, legs not crossed; or semi-recumbent 45°. Never flat on her backاقعدي مرتاحة وضهرك مسنود، ورجلينك على الأرض.
4Arm bare (no tight sleeve), supported at heart levelارفعي الكُم ده شوية، وخلي إيدك مرتاحة هنا.
5Choose the cuff: bladder covers ≥ 80% of the arm circumference; large cuff if the arm is > 33 cm
6Wrap firmly but not tight, lower edge 2–3 cm above the elbow crease, bladder centre (arrow) over the brachial artery
Measure القياس
7Palpatory systolic: feel the radial pulse, inflate until it goes, note the value. Add 30 mmHg: this is how high you pump. Deflate fullyحتحسي بشدة بسيطة وبعدين تفك.
8Diaphragm lightly on the brachial artery (medial cubital fossa), not under the cuff
9Inflate to palpatory + 30, deflate 2 mmHg/s
10K1 = systolic · K5 (sounds vanish) = diastolic; K4 only if sounds go on to 0
11Deflate fully, remove the cuff · record to the nearest 2 mmHg, arm, position, cuffخلصنا. ضغطك ١٣٠ على ٨٠، ده طبيعي.
12If raised: repeat after rest, check urine protein, ask about PE symptomsالضغط عالي شوية، حنعيده بعد شوية ونفحص البول.
Distinguishing features · Reading looks wrong
Obese arm, standard cuff, 150/100Repeat with a large cuff
Sounds heard down to 0Record K4 as diastolic
Lying flat, dizzy, BP 85/50Supine hypotension: sit or tilt
Silent gap 150 → 130, then soundsAuscultatory gap: palpate first
