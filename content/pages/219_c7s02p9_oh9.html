Station: Anaemia in Pregnancy

The station

"Khadija, 30, G6 P5 at 28 weeks. Her Hb today is 7.5 g/dL. Take a focused history." Then: likely type, first tests, how you treat.

Presenting the case

"Khadija, 30, G6 P5 at 28 weeks, Hb 7.5. She is tired and dizzy, not breathless at rest. Last birth 14 months ago, still breastfeeding; she drinks tea with meals and eats clay; stopped her iron because of nausea. No fever, no blood in urine. Impression: moderate iron deficiency anaemia. I would check FBC, MCV, film, ferritin, malaria film and stool, then give oral iron."

Common errors

No question on tablets she actually takes · forgetting malaria and worms (Sudan) · no heart failure screen · no previous PPH.
