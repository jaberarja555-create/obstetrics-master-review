7.11Counselling Stations

The station

A simulated patient (often with her husband) and a task: "Explain the result", "Discuss her options", "Advise her before pregnancy". No examination. Marks go to content (the right facts) and communication (7.1: SPIKES, BRAN, teach-back).

1. The counselling frame
Stepماذا تقول
1Agenda: say why you are hereأنا هنا عشان أشرح ليك نتيجة الفحص ونتكلم عن الخطوات الجاية.
2What she knows, what she fearsإنتي عارفة شنو عن حالتك؟ شنو أكتر حاجة شاغلة بالك؟
3Explain in 2–3 small facts, then stopخليني أشرح ليك بالراحة، ولو في حاجة ما واضحة وقّفيني.
4Draw it: a simple sketch helpsخليني أرسم ليك عشان الصورة تكون أوضح.
5Options: each with good and bad sidesفي أكتر من طريقة، وكل واحدة ليها فايدة وليها عيب.
6Her decision; time to think, talk with familyالقرار قرارك. لو دايرة تشاوري زوجك أو أهلك، خدي وقتك.
7Teach-backممكن تقولي لي بكلامك إنتي حنعمل شنو؟
8Safety-net, written note, next visitلو حصل أي حاجة من دي تعالي طوّالي. ودي ورقة فيها الكلام ده.
If she asks "What would you do?"

Do not hide behind "all options are equal". Say what most women choose and why, then respect her choice. Never pressure.

Common errors

A lecture with no pauses · jargon · talking only to the husband · false comfort ("don't worry") · blaming her ("you came late").

What to say · empathy without false promises
"I can see this is very hard for you. We will do everything we can, and I will be honest with you at every step."
«أنا شايف إنو الموضوع صعب عليك. حنعمل كل الممكن، وحأكون صريح معاك في كل خطوة.»
Hospital protocol

Ask whom she wants in the room; many women decide with the husband or mother: welcome them, but speak to her. If she draws comfort from faith, respect it, but still give the medical facts. Ask about cost and distance: a plan she cannot afford or reach will fail. Use a quiet corner, not the busy ward, for bad news.
