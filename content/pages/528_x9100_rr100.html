6Screening
  • EPDS: 10 questions, 0–3 each, at booking and 6 wk
  • ≥ 13 = probable depression
  • Any score on question 10 (self-harm) → same-day assessment
  • Check Hb and TSH
🩺 Exam scenario
  • EPDS 8 but question 10 scored 2 → Same-day assessment
7Management of depression
  • Mild: support, CBT, review 2–4 wk
  • Moderate–severe: sertraline 50 mg daily (to 200 mg) + CBT; continue 6 months after recovery
  • Suicidal or psychotic: admit
  • Keep breastfeeding; avoid fluoxetine (builds up in the baby)
  • Effect on the infant: poor bonding, growth, later behaviour
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Breastfeeding mother, 7 wk, moderate depression → Sertraline; continue breastfeeding
8Puerperal psychosis
  • Onset within 2–4 weeks, often after nights without sleep
  • Agitated, confused, rapidly changing mood, delusions about the baby, hallucinations; unaware she is ill
  • Risk factors: previous puerperal psychosis, bipolar disorder, family history, primigravida, stopping a mood stabiliser
9Management of psychosis: an emergency
  • Never leave her alone with the baby
  • Same-day psychiatry; admit, ideally a mother-and-baby unit
  • Exclude organic causes
  • Olanzapine or haloperidol; lithium for mania
  • ECT if life-threatening or not eating
  • Breastfeeding usually possible on antipsychotics; not on lithium
