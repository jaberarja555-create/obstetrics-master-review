6Labour and after birth
  • Stop injections when labour or bleeding starts
  • No regional block within 24 h of a treatment dose (12 h after a prophylactic dose)
  • Restart LMWH 8–12 h after birth, at least 4 h after the epidural catheter is removed
  • Postpartum: LMWH or warfarin (overlap until INR 2–3 on 2 days, at least 5 days)
  • Both are safe in breastfeeding
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Last treatment dose 8 h ago, wants an epidural → No block: wait 24 h
7Massive PE
  • Hypotension and respiratory distress
  • ICU, O₂, fluids
  • IV UFH 5000 IU then 1000–2000 IU/h
  • Thrombolysis or embolectomy
  • IVC filter if anticoagulation is contraindicated or PE recurs on it
8Thromboprophylaxis
  • Assess at booking, every admission, 36 wk, in labour and after birth
  • 0–1 risk factor: mobilise
  • 2 factors: LMWH 10 days postpartum
  • 3 factors: from 28 wk to 6 wk after birth
  • 4 or more: whole pregnancy + 6 wk
  • Previous VTE after major surgery: as 3 factors; unprovoked, oestrogen-related or with thrombophilia: whole pregnancy + 6 wk
  • Enoxaparin 40 mg daily (50–90 kg); 20 mg under 50 kg; 60 mg at 91–130 kg
