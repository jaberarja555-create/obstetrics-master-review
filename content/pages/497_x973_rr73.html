2Contraindications
  • Absolute: placenta or vasa praevia, transverse or oblique lie, cord presentation or prolapse, CPD, classical CS, 2 or more CS, previous rupture, active herpes, VVF repair
  • Relative: one previous lower-segment CS, breech, high free head, grand multipara, polyhydramnios, twins, heart disease, asthma (prostaglandins)
  • Before inducing: clear indication, sure dates, lie and pelvis checked, normal CTG
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • Induction asked, 2 previous CS → Elective caesarean
  • 41 wk, transverse lie → No induction: CS
3Modified Bishop score
  • Five items: consistency, position, effacement (cervical length), dilatation, station
  • Each 0–3 (consistency and position 0–2), maximum 13
  • 8 or more: favourable → ARM + oxytocin
  • 6 or less: unfavourable → ripen first
  • Success also depends on gestation and parity (multipara better)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Cervix 3 cm, 1.5 cm long, soft, mid, station −1 → Bishop 9: favourable
4Methods
  • Membrane sweep at 40–41 wk
  • Dinoprostone 3 mg PV, repeat after 6 h, max 2 doses: the live fetus
  • Misoprostol: live term fetus 25 µg PV 6-hourly or 25 µg oral 2-hourly (WHO 2022 prefers oral); IUFD dose by weeks; never in a scarred uterus
  • Foley balloon 30–50 mL, 12–24 h: no hyperstimulation, choice after a previous CS
