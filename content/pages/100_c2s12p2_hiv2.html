7Mode of delivery
Clinical picture
Elective caesarean
VL ≥ 1000 at 36 wk+not in labour=CS 38 wk + IV AZT
Clinical scenario
On ART, VL < 50 at 36 wk, wants a normal birth. Advice?
Vaginal birth; continue oral ART; no IV AZT.
The viral load decides everything: mode of birth, IV zidovudine and how long the baby takes prophylaxis.
8Intrapartum management
  1. Continue oral ART through labour and after. Do not stop it.
  2. IV zidovudine if VL ≥ 1000 or unknown: 2 mg/kg over 1 h, then 1 mg/kg/h until the cord is clamped.
  3. Avoid early ARM, prolonged ROM, scalp electrodes, fetal blood sampling, routine episiotomy.
  4. ROM at term: speed up delivery. Active 3rd stage. Universal precautions.
