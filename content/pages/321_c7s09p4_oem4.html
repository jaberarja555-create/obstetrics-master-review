11. Toxicity: order of signs
Presenting the case

"Primigravida, 35 wk, eclampsia: two fits, BP 170/112, protein ++. Left lateral, airway, O₂; MgSO₄ 4 g IV then 1 g/h; hydralazine 5 mg IV. No further fits, BP 145/95, reflexes present, RR 18, urine 40 mL/h, FHR 150. Plan: deliver once stable, MgSO₄ 24 h after birth."

Common errors

Holding her down or forcing a spoon into her mouth.

Running a fast drip: pulmonary oedema.

Forgetting glucose and malaria.

Ergometrine in the 3rd stage.

Rushing to caesarean while she is still fitting.

12. Examiner marking points
  • Help, left lateral, airway, O₂
  • Does not restrain or force the mouth
  • MgSO₄ loading and maintenance right
  • Treats BP ≥ 160/110
  • Hourly toxicity checks named
  • Plans delivery once stable
  • Right strength: 50% ampoule
  • 4 g = 8 mL, diluted to 20 mL
  • Names the 3 hourly checks
  • Toxicity order: knee → breath → heart
  • Antidote with dose and speed
