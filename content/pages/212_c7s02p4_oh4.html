10. Risk factors the history must find
  • Previous CS, myomectomy, uterine surgery
  • Previous PPH, retained placenta, abruption
  • Previous pre-eclampsia, stillbirth, preterm birth
  • Recurrent miscarriage, previous baby with anomaly
  • Medical disease: heart, diabetes, hypertension, epilepsy, sickle cell
  • Age < 18 or ≥ 35; grand multipara; short interval
  • Rh negative with antibodies; HIV, hepatitis B
  • Twins now; infertility treatment
