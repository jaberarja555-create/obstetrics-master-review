8. Applying forceps (OA, on the model)

Check before pulling: sagittal suture midline · posterior fontanelle 1 cm above the shanks · fenestrations admit ≤ a fingertip. Will not lock = wrong application: remove, re-check. Delivery in ≤ 3 pulls.

Viva questions
Which forceps blade goes in first?
The left blade, held in the left hand.
Blades will not lock. Next?
Remove, recheck position, reapply; else CS.
