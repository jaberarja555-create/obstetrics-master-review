Viva questions
Three parts of AMTSL?
Oxytocin 10 IU IM, CCT, uterine tone checks (rub up if soft).
Placenta not out at 30 min with AMTSL. Next?
Empty bladder, oxytocin, then manual removal.
Common mistakes

Oxytocin before excluding a twin · CCT without counter-pressure · clamping at once in a well baby · not looking at the membranes.

6Examiner marking points
  • Consent and a chaperone
  • Kit checked before crowning
  • Oxytocin drawn up, baby area warm
  • Asepsis: wash, gown, gloves, drape
  • Coaches pushing, then short quick breaths (panting)
  • FHR every 5 min in 2nd stage (normal length: primi up to 2 h, multi up to 1 h)
  • Flexion + perineal guard together
  • Head out slowly, between contractions
  • Checks cord round the neck
  • Down for anterior, up for posterior
  • Time of birth, baby on chest, dried
