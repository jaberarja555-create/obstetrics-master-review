Long Case

In the long case (clinical exam) you take the full history in the frame above, not a focused one. These tables show why each part is asked, so you can explain it to the examiner.

Hospital protocol

Introduce yourself, get consent and a female chaperone; sit at her level. Mothers-in-law and husbands often answer for her: ask sensitive questions (previous losses, FGC, violence, contraception) when she is alone. Ask for her antenatal card and old scan reports: they settle dates and results.
