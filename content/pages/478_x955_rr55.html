⚠️ Exam trap
  • Small baby + normal UA Doppler + normal liquor = usually constitutional SGA: do not rush delivery
6Complications
  • Before birth: IUFD, hypoxia, fetal distress in labour, meconium aspiration
  • Neonatal: hypoglycaemia (low glycogen), hypothermia (little fat), polycythaemia (chronic hypoxia), hypocalcaemia, RDS, NEC, IVH
  • Long term: cerebral palsy, learning delay; Barker hypothesis (adult HTN, diabetes, heart disease)
🩺 Exam scenario
  • FGR newborn, 2 h old, jittery → Blood glucose
7Management and timing of birth
  • Treat the cause; stop smoking; treat malaria and anaemia
  • Kick chart, CTG, growth every 3 wk, UA Doppler weekly
  • Dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12-hourly × 4 if birth before 34 wk
  • SGA, normal Doppler: 39 wk (RCOG 2024; local 37–38 wk)
  • Raised UA PI with EDF present: by 37 wk
  • Absent EDF: by 34 wk
  • Reversed EDF: about 32 wk
  • Abnormal DV, pathological CTG or fast falling liquor: now
  • Normal Doppler: induction with continuous CTG; absent or reversed EDF: caesarean
🩺 Exam scenarios
  • FGR 31 wk, DV a-wave reversed → Steroids + caesarean now
  • FGR 30 wk, absent EDF, CTG normal → Steroids, deliver by 34 wk
8Neonatal care and prevention
  • Paediatrician at birth, early feeding, glucose checks, warmth, check PCV
  • Prevention: early booking and dating, stop smoking, treat anaemia, malaria and HTN
  • Low-dose aspirin from 12 wk if high risk of placental disease
  • Interpregnancy gap over 6 months
