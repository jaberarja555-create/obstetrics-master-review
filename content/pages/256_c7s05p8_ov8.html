21. Presenting the findings
Presenting to the examiner

“On vaginal examination: the vulva is healthy, clear liquor draining. The cervix is 5 cm dilated, fully effaced, soft, mid-position, well applied to the head. Membranes are absent, liquor clear. Vertex at station −1, LOA, no moulding, no caput, no cord felt. She is in the active phase: I will plot it on the partogram.”

Present it · induction station

“Cervix 2 cm, 2 cm long, medium consistency, mid-position, station −2: Bishop score 5, unfavourable. I would ripen the cervix with prostaglandin before ARM.”

What to say · to her
“The neck of the womb is open 5 centimetres. Labour is going well and the baby’s heart is fine.”

Marks commonly lost

VE before asking about the placenta · during a contraction · fingers in and out many times · dilatation in "fingers" · station called from the caput · forgetting the cord check · no FHR after · not recording on the partogram.

Viva questions
Moulding +3 and head 3/5 at full dilatation. Ventouse?
No: obstructed labour, caesarean.
Commonest position at the start of labour?
Occiput transverse (LOT), then OA.
