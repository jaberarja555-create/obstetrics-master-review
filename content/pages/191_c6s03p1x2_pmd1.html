5. Aetiology

Sudden fall in oestrogen and progesterone after the placenta is delivered, plus sleep loss, pain and stress. Psychosis is mostly a bipolar episode triggered by delivery.

6. Baby blues
Clinical features
  • Up to 80% of mothers
  • Starts day 3–10, peaks day 4–5
  • Tearful, mood swings, irritable
  • Anxious, poor sleep, feels overwhelmed
  • Still copes and cares for the baby
Management
  • Explain: common, it passes in < 2 wk
  • Rest, sleep, family support
  • Help with feeding, listening visits
  • Review if it lasts > 2 weeks
  • A severe or long blues is a risk factor for depression
Exam trap

Tearful mother on day 4, looks after her baby well → baby blues: reassure. Do not start an antidepressant. The same mood still there at 3 weeks → consider depression and screen.
