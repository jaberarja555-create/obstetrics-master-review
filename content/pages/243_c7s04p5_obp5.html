Viva questions
Why may glycosuria be normal in pregnancy?
GFR rises and the renal glucose threshold falls.
Protein trace, BP normal, 28 wk. Act?
No: trace is not significant; repeat at next visit.
12. Quantifying protein
ScreenDipstick ≥ 1+
QuickPCR ≥ 30 mg/mmol
Gold standard24 h ≥ 0.3 g

Dipstick only measures concentration: a dilute sample hides protein, a concentrated one exaggerates it. That is why ≥ 1+ is confirmed by PCR or 24 h collection.

Hospital protocol

When PCR is not available, a 24 h urine collection is used. The dipstick at every antenatal visit with the BP stays the main screen for pre-eclampsia.

13. Presenting the findings
Presenting to the examiner

“Midstream urine, clear, yellow. Protein 2+, glucose negative, ketones negative, nitrite and leucocytes negative, blood negative. With her BP of 150/100 at 34 weeks this suggests pre-eclampsia. I would send a PCR, FBC with platelets, LFT, RFT and uric acid, and assess the baby.”

What to say · a urine infection
“The test shows signs of infection in your urine. We will send a sample to the lab and start a safe antibiotic for pregnancy. Drink plenty of water, and come back if you get fever, back pain or the pains of labour.”
