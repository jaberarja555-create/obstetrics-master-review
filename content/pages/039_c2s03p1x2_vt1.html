Exam trap

Leg swelling is common in normal pregnancy, but it is bilateral and painless. One painful swollen leg = DVT until proven otherwise: start LMWH, then scan.

Clinical picture
DVT in pregnancy
Left leg pain+calf > 2 cm wider+pregnant or postpartum=DVT
Distinguishing features
Both ankles swollen, painless, 36 wkPhysiological oedema
One calf hot, red, skin tender, fever 38.5Cellulitis

5. Investigations
Best next step

Clinical suspicion of DVT or PE: start treatment-dose LMWH first, then investigate (unless she is bleeding). Do not wait for results.

BaselineFBC (platelets), U&E, LFT, clotting profile: before anticoagulation
DVTDuplex (compression) ultrasound: shows flow and the vein wall. Positive → treat. Negative but suspicion high → keep LMWH, repeat on day 4 and day 7
Iliac DVTUltrasound may miss it: Doppler of the iliac vein or MRI venography
D-dimerNot useful: rises in normal pregnancy, so a positive test means nothing

PE · ECG: commonest = sinus tachycardia; S1Q3T3, right axis deviation, RBBB, T inversion V1–V4 (right heart strain; suggestive, not diagnostic). CXR: often normal; excludes pneumonia, pneumothorax, pulmonary oedema; may show atelectasis or effusion. ABG: ↓ PaO₂, ↓ PaCO₂. Echo if massive.
