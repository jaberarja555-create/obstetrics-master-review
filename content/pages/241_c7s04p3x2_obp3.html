Urine Dipstick Testing

8. Urine Dipstick: Sample & Steps
Task

"Huda's BP is 150/100. Test her urine and interpret the result." · a cup of urine (or water with a coloured strip given), a bottle of strips, gloves, a watch. Some stations first ask you to explain how to give a midstream sample.

9. Explaining a midstream (clean-catch) sample
What to say
“Wash your hands. Wash the private area with water from front to back. Pass the first part of the urine into the toilet, then fill this cup about half without stopping, and finish in the toilet. Close the lid and don’t touch the inside.”
«اغسلي إيدك، واغسلي المنطقة بالموية من قدّام لورا. نزّلي أول البول في الحمام، وبعدين املي الكاس ده لحدي النص من غير ما توقفي، وكمّلي الباقي في الحمام. اقفلي الغطا وما تلمسي جوّه الكاس.»
10. The steps
Stepماذا تقول
1Hand hygiene, gloves; explain the testحنفحص البول عشان نشوف الزلال والسكر والالتهاب.
2Sample: fresh (within 2 h), midstream; note colour, clarity, smell
3Check the bottle: expiry date, lid closed, strips not discoloured
4Take one strip; do not touch the pads; close the bottle at once (moisture spoils them)
5Dip all pads fully for ≈ 1 s; draw the edge on the cup rim to remove excess
6Hold the strip horizontal (colours do not run into each other); start the watch
7Compare each pad with the bottle chart at its own time, in good light
