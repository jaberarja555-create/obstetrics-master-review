Hospital protocol

Ask whom she wants in the room; many women decide with the husband or mother: welcome them, but speak to her. If she draws comfort from faith, respect it, but still give the medical facts. Ask about cost and distance: a plan she cannot afford or reach will fail. Use a quiet corner, not the busy ward, for bad news.
