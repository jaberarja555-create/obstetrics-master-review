OSCE Stations
7.1The OSCE station
Entering any station → Hand hygiene, introduce, check name and age, consent
Male student about to examine a woman → Named female chaperone
Breaking bad news → SPIKES with a warning shot
Opening (30 s)
Greet, introduce · confirm identity · explain and consent · privacy, chaperone
Communication
ICE (ideas, concerns, expectations) · open then closed questions · signposting · facts in 2–3 chunks · teach-back
Bad news (SPIKES)
Setting · perception · invitation · knowledge (warning shot) · emotions · strategy and summary
Consent (BRAN)
Benefits · risks · alternatives · nothing (doing nothing) · capacity: understand, retain, weigh, communicate
Presenting
< 1 min: who, why, positives, relevant negatives, impression, plan · one main differential
Closing
Summarise · safety-net (bleeding, leaking, fewer movements) · thank · wash hands
Exam traps
The first words in an emergency viva are "call for help" · never leave a medical word unexplained · let her speak first for about a minute
7.2Focused history stations
Headache, flashing lights and BP 150/100 at 34 weeks → Severe pre-eclampsia
Shoulder-tip pain and fainting at 7 weeks → Ruptured ectopic
Painless bleeding with a previous CS → Placenta praevia
Frame
Personal details · presenting complaint · history of pregnancy · past obstetric · gynaecological · medical, surgical · drugs, allergy · family · social
Dating
LMP, cycle, EDD (Naegele: +7 d −3 mo +1 yr) · early scan (CRL)
Obstetric terms
Gravida (pregnancies) · para (births ≥ 24 wk) · twins count as one · grand multipara ≥ 5
Past pregnancies
Mode, previous CS indication and type · PPH, PE, preterm birth, stillbirth, macrosomia (check glucose)
Clinical pictures
Vesicles + severe vomiting: mole · pain, hard uterus, dark blood: abruption · bleed at ROM, bradycardia: vasa praevia · pica: iron deficiency
Local pictures
Haematuria, canal water: schistosomiasis · fever, rigors, no bed net: malaria
Exam traps
High BP at 10 weeks = chronic hypertension · wet on coughing without pooling = stress incontinence · present a long history in 2–3 min
