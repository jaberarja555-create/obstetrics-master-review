4.2Monitoring in labour
Monitoring in labour follows the fetus, the progress and the mother. The partogram shows slow labour early, and CTG looks for fetal hypoxia in high-risk labour. Slow progress must be sorted into weak contractions, which are treated, and obstruction, which needs a caesarean.
1The partogram
  • Opened in the active phase at 4 cm, starting on the alert line
  • Alert line: 1 cm/h from 4 cm
  • Action line: 4 h to the right
  • Crossing the alert line in a health centre: refer
  • Crossing the action line: full review of the 3 Ps
  • Fetus: FHR every 30 min (110–160), liquor (I, C, M, B, A), moulding 0 to +3
  • Progress: cervix (X) every 4 h, descent in fifths, contractions every 30 min
  • Mother: pulse 30 min, BP 4 h, temperature 2 h, urine
🩺 Exam scenario
  • Liquor B with maternal tachycardia → Suspect abruption
