Delivery set
Delivery setطقم الولادة
Sterile tray: 2 artery forceps, scissors, cord clamp, gauze, kidney dish.
Viva · Open and check it before the 2nd stage; oxytocin drawn up beside it.
Partogram sheetالبارتوجرام
WHO chart from 4 cm: cervix, descent, contractions, fetus and mother.
Viva · Alert line 1 cm/h; action line 4 h to its right.
CTG machine
CTG machineجهاز تخطيط قلب الجنين
Two transducers on belts: ultrasound (FHR) and toco (contractions).
Viva · Paper 1 cm/min. Read it with DR C BRAVADO.
Newborn bag-valve-mask
Newborn bag-valve-maskجهاز الأمبو للمولود
Self-inflating bag, round masks size 0 and 1.
Viva · Mask over mouth and nose, not the eyes; 5 inflation breaths first.
4. Operative: instrumental birth & evacuation
Hand-held ventouse (Kiwi type)
Hand-held ventouse (Kiwi type)جهاز الشفط اليدوي
Disposable cup with hand pump and gauge. Outlet, low and rotational births.
Viva · Cup on the flexion point: 3 cm in front of the posterior fontanelle.
Metal ventouse cup
Metal ventouse cupكاسة الشفط المعدنية
Rigid cup with chain and vacuum pump. Marked caput, OP position.
Viva · Pull with contractions; max 3 pulls; stop after 2 cup detachments.
Wrigley forceps
Wrigley forcepsجفت ريجلي
Short, light forceps. Outlet delivery; the head at caesarean.
Viva · Outlet = scalp seen at the introitus without parting the labia.
Neville-Barnes forceps
Neville-Barnes forcepsجفت نيفل بارنز
Long, with pelvic curve. Low and mid-cavity, OA, non-rotational.
Viva · Also for the aftercoming head of the breech.
Kielland forceps
Kielland forcepsجفت كيلاند
Long, almost no pelvic curve, sliding lock. Rotation from OT or OP.
Viva · Sliding lock corrects asynclitism; skilled hands only.
MVA syringe and Karman cannulas
MVA syringe & Karman cannulasمحقنة الشفط اليدوي وقنيات كارمان
60 mL valved syringe + 4–12 mm cannulas. Evacuation of miscarriage < 12–14 wk.
Viva · Cannula size in mm ≈ weeks of gestation. Check the vacuum first.
Uterine curette
Uterine curetteمكشطة الرحم
Sharp or blunt loop. Evacuation, endometrial sampling.
Viva · Risks: perforation, Asherman syndrome; MVA is safer.
Uterine sound
Uterine soundمقياس عمق الرحم
Graduated, malleable blunt rod. Depth and direction before IUCD or D&C.
Viva · Normal cavity 6–9 cm. Perforation risk in a soft uterus.
