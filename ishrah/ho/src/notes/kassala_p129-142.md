# Kassala guideline — pages 129–142
## p129 Severe anaemia
- Anaemic or impending heart failure and/or Hb < 5 g/dL → transfusion (whole blood or packed cells) 10–20 mL/kg, usually wt × 20; malnourished 10 mL/kg (some 5–7); heart failure → packed cells if available.
- Example 2 y, 10 kg, Hb 4, distressed, very pale, haemic murmur, liver 2 cm: admit, O2 if distressed; cannula 24; fresh blood 200 mL (wt × 20) grouped & cross-matched; benzylpenicillin … MU 6-hourly (or ceftriaxone 12-hourly) if infection.
- Transfusion orders: furosemide 10 mg before and after (wt × 1 mg); run over 4 h; adrenaline, antihistamine, hydrocortisone at bedside; stop and call the doctor on any reaction.
## p130–132 Hepatitis
- Fever, yellow sclera, dark urine, pale stool (obstructive). Hx contact, FH jaundice/haemolysis, transfusion. Looks well (obstructive) or ill (hepatitis); jaundiced; pale/cyanosed → haemolysis (haemolytic anaemia, malaria); hepatomegaly ± tender.
- Inv: bilirubin (direct > 20%), hepatitis screen (HBsAg, HCV, anti-HAV), LFT + enzymes ± US; UG bile; BFFM (MAT); CBC + retic if haemolysis suspected; TORCH if indicated.
- Outpatient (stable, eating): ibuprofen (avoid paracetamol) + B-complex/multivitamin + lactulose ± encourage carbohydrate (restrict protein only in hepatic failure).
- Lactulose 0.5–1 mL/kg/day: < 1 y 2.5 mL, 1–5 y 5 mL, 5–10 y 10 mL 8-hourly. B-complex syrup 2.5–5 mL (tabs adolescents). Avoid constipation (fibre; lactulose); constipation and high protein aggravate encephalopathy.
- Admit: refusing feeds, hepatic encephalopathy, danger signs, vomiting everything. Plan: antibiotic (cefotaxime, Ampiclox or benzylpenicillin; avoid ceftriaxone in jaundice) + D10 maintenance + B-complex; advanced liver disease only: fat-soluble vitamins + lactulose (0.5–1 mL/kg/day ÷ TDS) + oral streptomycin 12–18 mg/kg OD.
- Fat-soluble vitamins: A < 10 kg 5000 IU, > 10 kg 10,000 IU OD; D 120–200 IU/kg OD; E 15–25 IU/kg/day or 100 mg daily (< 6 months 400 IU alternate days); K 2.5–5 mg daily orally. Ursodeoxycholic acid 10 mg/kg/dose BD (tab 250). Concavit syrup (5 mL: vit A 5000 IU, D 400 IU); Liverel tab OD (older).
- Jaundice ≠ always hepatitis: severe malaria (haemolysis), SCD hyperhaemolysis (retic), anaemia + jaundice in Hausa/Messiria → sickling test.
## p133–134 Pyogenic lymphadenitis
- Acute < 2 weeks, chronic > 6 weeks. Cervical/submandibular mass + fever. Hx URTI; night sweats/weight loss → TB or lymphoma; conjunctivitis → Kawasaki; cat contact → cat scratch. O/E inflammation, other nodes, hepatosplenomegaly.
- Inv CBC (neutrophilia), ESR, CRP; CXR (hilar nodes → neoplasm); excision biopsy if atypical mycobacteria or neoplasia suspected.
- ttt antipyretic + antibiotic + metronidazole. Stable: Amoclan or cefuroxime syrup (Zenon) + metronidazole syrup/tab. Ill/toxic/poor intake: admit: cefuroxime inj 30 mg/kg 8-hourly (50–100 mg/kg/day), or ceftriaxone + benzylpenicillin (ampicillin), or vancomycin 15 mg/kg 8-hourly in 30/50/100 mL NS; + metronidazole syrup wt/3 mL 8-hourly or infusion wt × 1.5 mL 8-hourly.
## p135 Mumps
- Parotid enlargement uni/bilateral; displaces ear lobe (lymph node does not). Swelling, mild fever ± poor feeding; looks well; non-tender (tender → parotitis). CBC not routine.
- Antipyretic/analgesic ± multivitamin; avoid acidic food (lemon, karkadi).
## p136 Torticollis
- SCM stiffness, head tilt; not meningism; usually afebrile unless lymphadenitis or retropharyngeal abscess. Causes: benign, skull tumour, posterior pharyngeal infection, C1–C2 subluxation, congenital/acquired, dystonic drugs (metoclopramide).
- Usually no Inv (CBC, CRP sometimes). Physiotherapy (stretching) + massage + analgesia (ibuprofen, mefenamic acid); antibiotic if infection.
## p137–138 Anaphylaxis / allergic reaction
- Itchy rash; severe: bronchospasm, rapid tongue swelling, facial/peripheral oedema (not all facial swelling is nephrotic). Exposure: food, drugs, insect or snake bite. Inv not routine.
- Mild (itch/rash only): antihistamine inj/oral ± antibiotic if skin infection. Syrup: 1–2 y 2.5 mL BD; 2–5 y 2.5 mL TDS; 6–12 y 5 mL every 4–6 h.
- Severe: adrenaline IM + hydrocortisone + antihistamine. Adrenaline 1:1000 0.01 mL/kg (wt × 1 unit insulin syringe); 1:10,000 (1 mL + 9 mL saline) 0.1 mL/kg. Hydrocortisone 4–5 mg/kg/dose; < 6 months 25 mg, 6 months–6 y 50 mg, 6–12 y 100 mg, > 12 y 200 mg. Antihistamine inj 10 mg/mL: < 2 y 1/4 mL, 2–5 y 1/2 mL, > 5 y 1 mL. Repeat IM adrenaline if no response; adrenaline neb if stridor; salbutamol neb if wheeze.
## p138 Drowning
- Submersion; unwell, hypothermia; chest clear or crackles. Inv Na (hyponatraemia → cerebral oedema), RBG, CBC.
- ABCs: suction, O2, remove wet clothes, NG to remove swallowed water, rewarm if < 32 °C (radiant heater ± warmed IV fluid), antibiotics (ceftriaxone + benzylpenicillin) ± hydrocortisone.
## p139 Electric shock
- Burn, respiratory distress, LOC, red urine (myoglobinuria). ABCs; IV NS or RL with urine output ≥ 2 mL/kg/h (significant burns, myoglobinuria); furosemide or mannitol.
## p140 Conjunctivitis
- Red eye, discharge. Neonatal: first 48 h chemical, self-limiting; day 2–7 gonococcal → systemic antibiotic (cefotaxime) + eye irrigation + fusidic acid or tetracycline ointment; after day 7 chlamydial → tetracycline ointment BD (2 months [printed]) ± erythromycin syrup if severe. Nursery: tobramycin drops 6-hourly ± fusidic ointment BD.
- Measles conjunctivitis: gentamicin drops 6-hourly or chloramphenicol drops 8-hourly. Others: Apiflox (ciprofloxacin) drops 6-hourly. Severe/complicated → ophthalmology. Malnourished → vitamin A plus drops.
## p141 Arthritis (joint pain ± swelling ± fever)
- Rheumatic fever (> 3 y): migratory arthritis, fever, chorea, erythema marginatum, nodules, recurrent tonsillitis. Inv ASO, CBC, CRP, ESR, ECG, echo. Aspirin 75 mg/kg/day + benzathine penicillin every 3 weeks.
- JIA (< 16 y, ≥ 6 weeks): pain, swelling ± fever, hepatosplenomegaly (systemic), small joints. CBC, ESR, CRP (exclude septic). Ibuprofen or diclofenac 1–3 mg/kg/dose ± low-dose prednisolone.
- Septic arthritis: fever (most important), hot swollen very painful joint. CBC, CRP, ESR, synovial aspiration & culture (most important). Ceftriaxone + benzylpenicillin for 3 weeks (adjust to culture) + analgesia.
- SLE (teenage, 15–35 y): joints, malaise, weight loss, myalgia, fever, butterfly rash; criteria. CBC, ESR, ANA, anti-dsDNA, RFT + electrolytes. Hydroxychloroquine + prednisolone ± NSAID ± cytotoxics.
## p142 Rate calculations
- Drops/min = amount (mL) × 20 / (hours × 60) = amount / (hours × 3) (20 drops/mL giving set; older sets 15).
- With pumps: mL/h = amount / hours.
- Glucose infusion rate (mg/kg/min) = % dextrose × hourly rate (mL/h) / (wt × 6), or % dextrose × mL/kg/day / 144. GIR > 12 mg/kg/min → investigate persistent hypoglycaemia.
