# Kassala guideline — pages 143–155
## p143 Urine output and GFR
- Urine output (mL/kg/h) = volume / wt / hours. e.g. 250 mL in 12 h, 10 kg → 2 mL/kg/h. Normal 0.5–2; oliguria < 0.5; polyuria > 4 mL/kg/h.
- GFR (Schwartz) = height (cm) × 0.41 / serum creatinine (mg/dL).
## p144 Cases for reassurance
- Umbilical hernia < 3 y (most close by 1 y; surgery if not closed by 3 y; unless complications: obstruction, inflammation).
- Vaginal bleeding in neonate (maternal hormone withdrawal, with breast enlargement).
- Erythema toxicum neonatorum (self-limiting; heavy covering).
- Neonatal anuria in hot weather (unless distended bladder or unwell): feed more; sometimes fluid challenge 20 mL/kg NS + furosemide 1 mg/kg.
- Constipation without distension, impaction or vomiting.
- Unconjugated jaundice below phototherapy level without risk factors (morning sun).
- Bulging fontanelle after vitamin A (hypervitaminosis): antipyretic if febrile, follow-up.
## p145 Emergency drugs with an insulin syringe (units = 0.01 mL)
- Adrenaline 1:1000 wt × 1 unit (0.01 mg/kg); antihistamine (chlorphenamine 10 mg/mL) wt × 3 units [= 0.3 mg/kg; as printed]; dexamethasone (4 mg/mL) wt × 4 units (0.15 mg/kg, meningitis), wt × 15 units single dose (0.6 mg/kg, croup); diazepam (5 mg/mL) wt × 6 IV (0.3 mg/kg), wt × 10 rectal (0.5 mg/kg); aminophylline (25 mg/mL) wt × 20 units (5 mg/kg).
- Rare drugs: cefpodoxime 5 mg/kg BD 7 days (50 → wt/2, 100 → wt/4 mL); ceftazidime (Fortum) 100–150 mg/kg/day ÷ BD–TDS; cefepime 50 mg/kg/dose BD–TDS; meropenem 20–40 mg/kg/dose BD or 8-hourly.
## p146 Infusions
- Acyclovir 10 mg/kg/dose 8-hourly in D5 (30/50/100 mL by age), ≤ 7 mg/mL; vial 250 mg.
- Vancomycin 40–60 mg/kg/day ÷ 8-hourly in NS / dextrose 1/5 or 1/2 saline / DNS, ≤ 5 mg/mL; vial 500 mg, 1 g.
- Dopamine renal 2–5, cardiac 5–15 mcg/kg/min: mg/24 h = wt × dose × 60 × 24 / 1000 in 100 mL NS over 24 h; ampoule 200 mg/5 mL.
- Calcium gluconate 2 mL/kg/dose diluted 1:4 in D5 over 1/2 h.

## p149 Napkin dermatitis
- Zinc oxide cream BD (mild); candida (itching) → antifungal (Candid) cream BD; ± antibiotic (Ampiclox) if secondary bacterial infection. Perianal excoriation in chronic diarrhoea (SAM, malabsorption): zinc oxide cream + zinc sulphate syrup.
## p150–151 Prescription
- Name, date, drug (with concentration), route and duration in Arabic, close the prescription (line), signature.
- Common mistakes: no concentration for drugs with several strengths (Amoclan 200 or 400); OD/BD abbreviations (write Arabic directions); vitamin K without route; mg instead of mL for parents (write mL / 3 mL syringe); "carbamazepine" written ambiguously → write "Carbamazepine tab 200 mg (Tegretol)" to avoid carbimazole.
## p152 Fatal mistakes
- Salbutamol nebuliser in anaemic heart failure (look for pallor → O2, IV NS 4 mL/kg/h until blood).
- CHD with SOB: salbutamol worsens tachycardia → O2 + NS nebuliser ± ipratropium.
- IV fluid in SAM except shock → ReSoMal & F75.
- IV bolus in scorpion sting (shock is anaphylactic/neurogenic) → adrenaline IM; adrenaline infusion may be needed.
- β-blocker (propranolol) in asthma.
## p153 Diabetic follow-up chart: date, time, RBG, urine sugar, acetone, insulin (regular/mixed), fluid, signature.
## p154–155 Preparing IV fluids
- Dextrose 5% 1/2 saline: 250 mL D10 + 250 mL NS.
- Dextrose 5% 1/5 saline: remove 200 mL from 500 mL D5W, add 100 mL D10 + 100 mL NS.
- DNS: remove 50 (60) mL from 500 mL NS, add 50 (60) mL D50.
- Dextrose 7.5% 1/2 saline (DKA, RBG < 100 with acidosis): remove 25 (30) mL from 500 mL D5 1/2 saline, add 25 (30) mL D50.
- Dextrose 10% 1/2 saline (DKA, quinine with hypoglycaemia): remove 50 (60) mL from 500 mL D5 1/2 saline, add 50 (60) mL D50.
- D10: remove 50 (60) mL from 500 mL D5, add 50 (60) mL D50. D12.5 (resistant hypoglycaemia): remove 25 (30) mL from 500 mL D10, add 25 (30) mL D50.
- References differ: WHO 25 mL, Sudan protocol 30 mL, others 32 mL.
