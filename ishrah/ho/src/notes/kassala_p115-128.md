# Kassala guideline — pages 115–128
## p115 SAM (cont.)
- F75 130 mL/kg/day: ÷ 12 (2-hourly) or ÷ 8 (3-hourly). When D/V stop and oedema subsides → F100.
- Folic acid on day 1 only (new SAM protocol). ReSoMal after motions: < 2 y 50–100 mL, > 2 y 100–200 mL (marasmus); 30 mL per motion in oedematous child. Profuse watery diarrhoea (cholera) → ORS instead of ReSoMal, same amounts.
- Order SAM + GE: admit; cannula 24; NG tube 5 or 8 (not routine if drinks well); ceftriaxone … mg 12-hourly (some give benzylpenicillin + gentamicin); ReSoMal wt × 20 over 2 h (or wt × 5 every 1/2 h for 2 h); then ReSoMal alternating with F75 hourly for 10 h (wt × 10); folic acid 1/2 tab today; zinc … mL 12-hourly; vitamin A … units (prophylactic/therapeutic); metronidazole syrup … mL 8-hourly; SG, UG, RBG ± BFFM, TWBC.
- No multivitamins/minerals (iron) in initial phase (bacterial overgrowth, free radicals).
## p116 SAM without dehydration (severe anaemia, BP, GE not dehydrated)
- Admit; cannula 24 ± NG tube 5 or 8; ceftriaxone 12-hourly; ReSoMal wt × 15–30 after each loss; F75 130 × wt / 12 2-hourly or / 8 3-hourly; folic acid 1/2 tab today; vitamin A; fresh blood wt × 10 mL if anaemic (grouped & cross-matched); SG, CBC, BFFM.
- No ReSoMal available: 2 L water + 2 ampoules KCl + 1 ORS packet + 5 spoons sugar (+ CMV ideally; ideally 3 ampoules KCl so 1 L ≈ 40 mmol K).
## p117 Failure to thrive
- Differs from PEM: commonest < 6 months; causes CHD, metabolic disorder, cleft lip/palate. Admission as PEM.
- Antibiotic (some benzylpenicillin + gentamicin) + diluted F100 + folic acid ± multivitamins + iron.
- Order: admit; cannula 24; NG tube 5; ceftriaxone 12-hourly; diluted F100 wt × 150 / 8 mL 3-hourly; zinc 2.5 mL BD; if stable ± multivitamin 3 mL daily, folic acid 1/2 tab daily, iron (Saferon) drops … daily; BFFM, RBG, SG, CBC.
## p118 Measles
- Coryza, cough, conjunctivitis; high fever, red eyes, rash. Admit if complications (pneumonia, GE) or danger signs.
- Outpatient: antipyretic (Fendol, ibuprofen) + eye drops (chloramphenicol or gentamicin 6-hourly) + vitamin A ± oral antibiotic if infection.
- Vitamin A: < 6 months 50,000 IU; 6–12 months 100,000 IU; > 1 y 200,000 IU — day 1, day 2 and day 14.
- Admitted: above + injectable antibiotic + IV fluid if diarrhoea/poor intake. Order: isolation ward; cannula 24; vitamin A therapeutic (day 1, 2, 14); paracetamol/diclofenac suppository or ibuprofen/Fendol syrup 6-hourly; ceftriaxone 12-hourly; CBC, BFFM, SG.
## p119 Dengue fever
- Aedes (day-biting). Like malaria; rash like measles. Fever, severe headache, maculopapular rash, arthralgia, myalgia → encephalopathy; or afebrile with bleeding and shock.
- Inv ideal PCR/culture; CBC: early leukopenia (≠ enteric), HCT ↑↑, platelets low; positive tourniquet test = haemorrhagic tendency.
- Supportive: paracetamol only (no Fendol/ibuprofen) + IV fluid (RL or NS) + H2 blocker/PPI (omeprazole, cimetidine) + antibiotic.
- Order: admit, cannula; paracetamol suppository/tab/infusion 6-hourly or PRN; RL … mL 8-hourly (or NS or dextrose saline); cimetidine (omeprazole) in last 100 mL of drip ± ceftriaxone (benzylpenicillin).
## p120–124 DKA
- Known or new DM; polyuria, polydipsia, vomiting, abdominal pain, acidotic breathing, coma. Ill, acidotic, dehydrated, hypotensive.
- Inv: RBG > 300 mg/dL; UG sugar +++, acetone +; ABG (not routine); BFFM (MAT), CBC (cause/infection).
- Plan: admit + IV line; NS + regular insulin ± KCl; RBG + urine sugar/acetone hourly. Fluid first, insulin after 1/2–1 h.
- NS until RBG < 300 then dextrose 1/2 saline. Volume: 1/2 deficit + maintenance in 24 h (deficit = wt × 100).
- Insulin: 0.3 U/kg SC, or 0.1 U/kg IV + 0.1 U/kg IM, then 0.1 U/kg hourly until acetone clears and acidosis resolves. Pump: 100 U in 100 mL NS (or 50 in 50) = 1 U/mL at 0.1 mL/kg/h.
- KCl to drip once passing urine (1 ampoule). Treat cause.
- Example 20 kg (pump): NS 2500 mL over 24 h (deficit 2000/2 + maintenance 1500); insulin 100 U in 100 mL NS at 2 mL/h until acetone clears, change solution 6-hourly; switch to dextrose 1/2 saline when RBG < 300; KCl ampoule when urine passed; RBG & urine hourly; monitor vitals (Dr Hatem). No pump: insulin 6 U SC now then 2 U SC hourly (or 2 U IV + 2 U IM now then 2 U SC hourly).
- Dr Al-Farouq (unusual): deficit + maintenance over 36 h ÷ 3 per 12 h, first hour 10 mL/kg: 20 kg → 3500 mL: 200 mL over 1 h, 900 mL over 11 h, then 1100 mL per 12 h.
- Notes: never stop hourly insulin until acidosis clears (not acidotic, not dehydrated, drinking) — not by glucose alone. Glucose < 100 with acidosis → raise dextrose to 7.5% (add 25 mL D50 per 500 mL [as printed]) or more.
- After clearance: known diabetic at meal time → usual mixed insulin; not meal time → regular insulin infusion 0.05 U/kg/h or SC regular 0.2–0.3 U/kg with snacks until meal. Newly diagnosed: mixed insulin 0.5–0.75 U/kg/day, 2/3 morning, 1/3 evening (15 kg → 7.5 U: 5 U before breakfast, 2.5 before supper). Some give regular 0.25 U/kg 6-hourly with 6-hourly RBG/urine first (better).
## p125 Drug poisoning; kerosene
- Identify drug. Within 1–2 h → gastric lavage (NG, NS in and out, ideal 10 mL/kg, until clear). Contraindicated: kerosene/petroleum, corrosives (NaOH).
- Kerosene: no lavage; admit, O2 + benzylpenicillin + hydrocortisone; CXR on day 2.
## p126 Hair dye (PPD) poisoning
- Admit + IV line: adrenaline IM (if anaphylaxis/neck or tongue swelling; wt × 1 unit insulin syringe = 0.01 mL/kg 1:1000) + antihistamine IM/IV (< 2 y 2.5 mg 1/4 mL; 2–5 y 5 mg 1/2 mL; 6–12 y 10 mg 1 mL) + hydrocortisone 5 mg/kg 6-hourly ± antibiotic (benzylpenicillin or cefuroxime if infection) — avoid gastric lavage. ENT urgently if facial oedema increases; ICU if respiratory distress, reduced consciousness, irregular pulse.
## p127–128 Iron deficiency anaemia
- Easy fatigue, pica, anorexia, palpitations, SOB (severe/anaemic HF). Pale, no jaundice/cyanosis; haemic murmur ± tachycardia; mild splenomegaly if chronic (Plummer–Vinson rare).
- CBC: low Hb, MCV, MCH; ↑RDW.
- Well and Hb > 5: oral iron ± folic acid. Saferon syrup 50 mg/5 mL: 3–6 mg/kg/day = wt × 0.6 mL/day (6 mg/kg); e.g. 10 kg 6 mL daily or 3 mL BD (best) for 3 months.
- Folic acid 0.2 mg/kg/day: < 1–2 y 1/2 tab, > 1–2 y 1 tab daily.
- Saferon drops: premature 1–2 drops/kg/day; infant up to 1 y 10–20 drops; 1–12 y 20–40 drops daily. Feroglobin syrup (iron + folate + vitamin C): 1–2 y 2.5 mL BD, 3–12 y 5 mL BD (expensive). Others Hematin, Ferbin.
- Diet advice; Hb + retic after 1 month (2 weeks ideal); retic ↑ = response; poor response → thalassaemia, malabsorption (coeliac).
- Transfuse: symptomatic anaemia (anaemic or impending HF), or Hb < 5 g/dL and clinically pale. Low Hb without pallor → oral iron, home.
- Pica: treat worms (hookworm) — mebendazole with iron.
