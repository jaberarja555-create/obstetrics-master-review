# Kassala guideline (Guideline of common pediatric cases 2024, Kuwaiti Hospital Kassala) — transcription
Guideline page numbers (PDF page = guideline page + 4). Shortcuts "wt/4" = mL per dose = weight(kg) ÷ 4.

## p1 URTI: Sinusitis
- C/O fever, headache, nasal block or runny nose. O/E NAD. Inv: BFFM, CBC (TWBCs).
- ttt: antibiotic (if needed) + antipyretic + nasal drop. Choose simple → potent by severity (clinical or TWBCs).
- Ampiclox syrup 250 mg/5mL (no 125): 25 mg/kg/dose 6-hourly (range 25–200 mg/kg/day). Simply wt/4 mL 6-hourly (=50 mg/kg/day*). [*as printed]
- Cephalexin syrup 125 or 250 mg/5mL: 25–100 mg/kg/day ÷ 4 (6-hourly). 125 → wt/2 mL; 250 → wt/4 mL 6-hourly.
- Older children/adolescents: cephalexin caps 250 mg 6-hourly; adult and ≥40 kg: 500 mg.
## p2
- Erythromycin syrup 125/250: 30–60 mg/kg/day; 125 → wt/2, 250 → wt/4 mL 6-hourly.
- Azithromycin syrup 200 mg/5mL (one form): 10 mg/kg/dose once daily 3 days; wt/4 mL. Not under 6 months (pyloric stenosis). Brands Zomax, Zocin, Zithrokan. Tabs 250 mg older children, 500 mg adults, daily 3 days.
- Cefixime syrup 100 mg/5mL (one form): 8 mg/kg/dose once daily 6 days; wt × 0.4 mL. Bottle 30 mL only: if dose > 6 mL prescribe 2 bottles or caps. Brands Betixim, Amixime, Suprax, Magnacef. Caps 200/400 mg daily 6 days (adult & older child).
- NB Ampiclox and erythromycin (prokinetic) cause diarrhoea → in diarrhoea give azithromycin or cefixime.
## p3 Antipyretics
- Paracetamol syrup 120 mg/5mL: 10 mg/kg/dose; wt/2 mL (or wt × 0.42) PRN, can be 6-hourly for 24 h then PRN if running fever.
- Mefnac syrup (mefenamic acid 50 mg/5mL; Fendol, Pangesic): 6.5 mg/kg/dose; wt/2 + 1 mL PRN, can be 8-hourly 24 h then PRN. e.g. 10 kg → 6 mL. Tab 250 mg children/adolescents, 500 mg adults & >12 y.
- Ibuprofen syrup (100 mg/5mL as standard; printed "100 mg/mL"): 20 mg/kg/day; 40 mg/kg/day in severe pain (SCD painful crisis, rheumatoid arthritis). Not < 6 months. wt/2 or wt/3 mL by severity, PRN or 8-hourly 24 h then PRN.
- Nasal drops: normal saline drops (Apisal, Sinus, Baby Nadif) 6-hourly (can 4-hourly); Otrivin drop 8-hourly not > 7 days; Physiomer spray 6-hourly; Oxymet drop morning & evening.
## p4 Common cold
- Runny nose ± fever; O/E NAD; BFFM −ve; CBC normal or TWBC with neutrophils or lymph.
- ttt: antipyretic + decongestant ± antibiotic if needed (yellowish/foul discharge → as sinusitis). Advice warm drinks (karkadi, lemon, honey).
- Unifed DM syrup: 2–5 y 2.5 mL, 6–12 y 5 mL 8-hourly; not under 2 years.
- Cetirizine syrup (Cetrine): 2–6 y 2.5 mL (2.5 mg), 6–12 y 5 mL, >12 & adult 10 mL or 5 mL BD. Allergic rhinitis.
## p5–7 Tonsillitis
- Fever, sore throat/dysphagia ± abdominal pain (mesenteric adenitis). O/E tonsils enlarged & hyperaemic ± follicles. Every febrile child: examine throat and ears.
- Inv BFFM, CBC. ttt antipyretic (most important, convulsion risk) + antibiotic ± metronidazole (follicular).
- Ideal: penicillin V 3 times/day 10 days or benzathine penicillin. Alternatives: macrolides (erythromycin, azithromycin wt/4, clarithromycin) or Amoclan.
- Clarithromycin syrup 125/250 or tab 250: 15 mg/kg/day BID; 125 → wt/3, 250 → wt/6 mL morning & evening.
- Amoclan (co-amoxiclav) syrup: 20–50 mg/kg/day. 200 → wt/2, 400 → wt/4 BD; 156 → wt/2 8-hourly, 312 → wt/4 8-hourly; 228 → wt/2 BD, 457 → wt/4 BD. 156 causes diarrhoea. Brands Clamox, Clavox, Megamox, Augmentin. Tab 375 for adolescents/older children (BD or 8-hourly by weight); 625 / 1 g adults.
- Amoclan IV 600 mg / 1.2 g: neonate 30 mg/kg BD; 1–2 months 30 mg/kg 12-hourly; 3 months–17 y 30 mg/kg 8-hourly (max 1.2 g TID).
- Admission: refuses feeding, vomits everything, febrile convulsion. After admission: benzylpenicillin + IV fluid + antipyretic ± metronidazole infusion.
- Metronidazole (Flagyl) 7.5 mg/kg/dose 8-hourly: syrup 200 mg/5mL wt/3 mL TDS; infusion (5 mg/mL) wt × 1.5 mL 8-hourly (10 kg → 15 mL TDS); tab 250 older children.
- Recurrent tonsillitis > 4/year → advise tonsillectomy (avoid rheumatic fever, PSGN).
## p7 Scarlet fever
- Fever + tonsillitis + rash + strawberry tongue. Antipyretic + procaine penicillin 50,000 IU/kg/day once 10 days; or erythromycin if allergic.
## p8–9 Otitis media
- Acute: fever, ear pain/irritability, pulls ear. Chronic: discharge, no pain, mastoiditis, deafness. Irritable child: think otitis media and colicky abdominal pain.
- Inv BFFM, CBC. Antibiotic + analgesic ± gentamicin/Gentadex ear drops 6-hourly 3 days. Antibiotics: Ampiclox, azithromycin, erythromycin, clarithromycin, Amoclan, cefaclor.
- Cefaclor (Midocef/Cloracef) 20–40 mg/kg/day 8-hourly: 125 → wt/2, 250 → wt/4 mL 8-hourly; tabs 250 older children, 500 adults.
- Admission: general danger signs; suspected meningitis. Plan: cefuroxime (Zinoxime, Maxil) inj 50–100 mg/kg/day, usually 30 mg/kg/dose 8-hourly (wt × 30 mg), max 750 mg/dose. Vial 750 mg dissolved in 7.5 mL water = 100 mg/mL. Complications → ENT.
## p9 Mastoiditis
- Untreated OM; high fever, swelling behind ear, irritability, brown discharge ± deafness; mastoid tenderness. IV antibiotics (Maxil or ceftriaxone) + metronidazole infusion + analgesia ± dressing; surgery may be required.
## p10 Adenoid hypertrophy
- Snoring, mouth breathing, apnoea, recurrent infection, cor pulmonale if late. Neck X-ray may be needed. Most resolve; adenoidectomy if obstructive sleep apnoea.
## p10–12 Croup
- Viral (laryngotracheobronchitis) or spasmodic. Barking cough ± SOB ± hoarseness; stridor audible. CBC, neck X-ray not routine.
- ttt: adrenaline nebuliser or normal-saline nebuliser + dexamethasone IM 0.6 mg/kg single dose (max 8 mg = 2 ampoules; ampoule 4 mg/1 mL). e.g. 10 kg → 6 mg (1.5 mL); 20 kg → 8 mg (2 mL).
- Adrenaline neb: 0.5 mL in 2 mL saline or 1 mL in 3 mL saline every 20 min, by age/severity; rebound after 2–3 h → saline neb first, adrenaline if not improving. Saline neb 3 mL every 20 min for an hour. Budesonide (Pulmicort) neb may replace dexamethasone.
- Further dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg/dose 6-hourly if needed.
- If OK: observe 2–3 h then discharge on Orazone (dexamethasone syrup 10 mg/100 mL): age 1 y 1 mL, 2 y 2 mL, 3 y 3 mL — 6-hourly day 1, 8-hourly day 2, BD day 3, once day 4 then stop. ± antibiotic if needed (not routine).
- Admit if not improving/distressed: O2; adrenaline or saline neb 2-hourly; ± benzylpenicillin 6-hourly; ± IV fluid 2/3 maintenance; dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg 6-hourly.
- Other stridor: epiglottitis (toxic, drooling, muffled), retropharyngeal abscess, foreign body (sudden choking, unilateral ↓ breath sounds), angioneurotic oedema (hair dye), diphtheria (bull neck, nasal voice). Spasmodic croup: no coryza, recurrent, night, atopy; same management.
## p13–14 Pneumonia
- Fever, cough ± SOB; crackles and/or wheeze. Inv BFFM, CBC (↑ neutrophils), ± CXR.
- ttt antibiotics (as sinusitis: Ampiclox, azithromycin, Amoclan, clarithromycin, cefaclor) + antipyretic ± cough syrup.
- Cough syrups not < 6 months except natural Mini Guava: <1 y 5 drops, 1–3 y 10 drops, >3 y 20 drops 8-hourly. Mucolytics (Solvexin, Ambolar, Mucoram, Muconab): 2.5–5 mL <5 y, 5–10 mL >5 y. Prospan best (secretolytic, spasmolytic) — good for asthmatics.
- Admit: severe pneumonia (distress), danger signs, slow resolution on oral antibiotics, toxic, SpO2 < 90%, diagnostic work-up (TB: ESR, CXR, sputum AFB). High TWBC alone is not an indication if stable → potent oral antibiotic.
- Cefuroxime syrup (Zenon) 10–15 mg/kg/dose BID; 125 → wt/2, 250 → wt/4 mL BD; max 500 mg/day; not < 3 months. (125 bottle 100 mL, 250 bottle 50 mL.)
## p15–16 Severe bronchopneumonia
- Fever, cough, SOB; ill, distressed, febrile; crackles ± wheeze. Inv BFFM, CBC ± CXR.
- ttt: O2 if hypoxic + antibiotic + antipyretic ± 2/3 maintenance IV fluid.
- Antibiotics: benzylpenicillin; Maxil (cefuroxime); ceftriaxone (Oncef/Samixon); cefotaxime (Ceftax); Amoclan inj.
- Benzylpenicillin 100,000 IU/kg/day 6-hourly; by age: <2 months 1/4 MU, 2–6 months 1/2 MU, >6 months–1 y 1/2–1 MU, >1 y 1 MU, 6-hourly. mg: neonate <7 d 25 mg/kg 12-hourly (8-hourly severe); >7 d 25 mg/kg 8-hourly (50 severe); child 25 mg/kg 6-hourly, 50 mg/kg 6-hourly (even 4-hourly) severe (BNF). 600 mg = 1 MU.
- Cefuroxime inj 30 mg/kg TDS (max 750/dose) if high TWBC or not improving.
- Ceftriaxone 30–80 mg/kg/day, usually 50 mg/kg/day once (wt × 50 daily) or wt × 30 12-hourly; NOT 50 mg/kg BD (meningitic dose); severe up to 80 mg/kg/day (wt × 80 OD or wt × 40 BD).
- Sudan protocol: neonate & infant < 1 y combined: ampicillin (or benzylpenicillin) + gentamicin, or cefotaxime + ampicillin/benzyl in severe. >1 y: benzylpenicillin, or cefotaxime/ceftriaxone, or Amoclan IV.
- Ampicillin inj: 50 mg/kg/dose BID first week of life; 25 mg/kg/dose 6-hourly usual; vial 500 mg.
- Gentamicin 80 mg/2 mL: 2.5 mg/kg 8-hourly; BID in neonate (p39).
- Cefotaxime 100–150 mg/kg/day TDS, usually wt × 50 mg 8-hourly; first week 50 mg/kg BID.
