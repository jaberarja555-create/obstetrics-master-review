# Kassala guideline — pages 59–72
## p59 Maintenance (cont.)
- 25 kg: 1000 + 500 + 100 = 1600 mL/24 h → 533 mL 8-hourly. Maximum IV fluid 2.5 L/day male, 2 L/day female.
- Fluid type by age: neonate first 2 days D10; day 3–6 months dextrose 1/5 saline; > 6 months–5 y dextrose 1/2 saline; > 5 y DNS (dextrose normal saline). (Dr Al-Farouq, Kassala: 6–12 months dextrose 1/2 saline; > 1 y DNS.)
## p60 GE + some DH
- Plan B: ORS 75 mL/kg over 4 h orally or NG. Modified plan B (now used, good outcome in some situations): Ringer's lactate 50–60 mL/kg within 4 h IV.
- Plan: ceftriaxone (not routine) + Ringer's lactate then maintenance + zinc.
- Order sheet: admit emergency ward; cannula 24; ceftriaxone … mg 12-hourly; Ringer's lactate … mL over 4 h then dextrose with … saline … mL 8-hourly; zinc … mL BD; BFFM, TWBC, SG.
## p60–62 GE + severe DH (plan C)
- Ceftriaxone + Ringer's lactate (plan C) + zinc. RL wt × 100 mL: < 1 y 1/3 in 1 h then 2/3 in 5 h; > 1 y 1/3 in 1/2 h then 2/3 in 2.5 h.
- e.g. 6 months 6 kg: 600 mL → 200 mL over 1 h then 400 mL over 5 h. 13 months 9 kg: 900 mL → 300 mL over 1/2 h then 600 mL over 2.5 h.
- No IV access: ORS 20 mL/kg/h for 6 h via NG (wt × 20 × 6 over 6 h), watch abdominal distension. e.g. 5 kg → 100 mL/h, 600 mL over 6 h.
- Order sheet: admit; cannula 24; ceftriaxone … mg 12-hourly; RL … mL over 1/2 h (1 h if < 1 y) then … mL over 2.5 h (5 h if < 1 y); zinc 2.5 or 5 mL BD; BFFM, CBC, SG.
- Zinc sulphate (10 mg/5 mL or 20 mg/5 mL): 1–2 mg/kg/day; 5 mg/kg/day in growth retardation / zinc deficiency (acrodermatitis). By age: < 6 months 2.5 mL BD, > 6 months 5 mL BD; commonly < 1 y 2.5 mL BD, > 1 y 5 mL BD for 10–15 days.
## p63–64 GE investigations and stool general
- BFFM positive → dysenteric malaria. Low TWBC → typhoid; high → infection. Low Hb (+ low platelets) → HUS; low platelets → DIC.
- SG: pus cells + RBC → dysentery; uncountable pus + E. histolytica trophozoites → amoebic; uncountable pus/RBC only → bacillary; G. lamblia trophozoites → giardiasis; ova (H. nana, schistosomiasis).
- ttt by SG: G. lamblia trophozoite → metronidazole syrup or tab 250; cyst → no treatment; pus ± RBC or mucus ± RBC → cefixime, co-trimoxazole (Septrin), nalidixic acid, ceftriaxone; pus + E. histolytica trophozoites → metronidazole; ova H. nana / T. saginata / T. solium → praziquantel 25 mg/kg single dose; schistosomiasis → praziquantel 40 mg/kg single dose (tab 600 mg).
- Any diarrhoea treated as shigellosis (cefixime, Septrin) unless G. lamblia, E. histolytica or ova found. Metronidazole only for giardia or amoeba in diarrhoea.
- Other metronidazole indications: severe malnutrition; anaerobes: wounds, NEC, dental caries (+ amoxicillin + analgesic), aphthous ulcer, follicular tonsillitis.
- Metronidazole 7.5 mg/kg/dose 8-hourly: syrup 200 mg/5 mL wt/3 mL; infusion wt × 1.5 mL; tab 250 older. Giardiasis 5–7 days; amoebiasis double dose 15 mg/kg/dose 8-hourly for 10 days.
- Co-trimoxazole (Septrin) 20 mg/kg/dose BD (sulfamethoxazole) = wt/2 mL BD (syrup 240 mg/5 mL); by age < 6 months 2.5 mL, 6 months–2 y 5 mL, 2–7 y 7 mL [printed], > 12 y tab BD. Not < 2 months [printed; standard < 6 weeks]. Tab 480 mg.
- Cefixime 8 mg/kg/dose (wt × 0.4 mL) daily 6 days.
## p65 Nalidixic acid; outpatient GE; ORS
- Nalidixic acid tab 500 mg only: 50 mg/kg/day ÷ 4 (6-hourly) → wt × 50 / 4 mg; 10 kg → 125 mg (1/4 tab 6-hourly).
- Outpatient GE (less frequent D&V, looks well, oral intake good): BFFM, CBC, SG; antibiotic (Septrin, cefixime, nalidixic acid) + zinc (< 1 y 2.5 mL, > 1 y 5 mL) + ORS ± antipyretic (paracetamol, Fendol, ibuprofen).
- ORS: 1 sachet in 1 L water (8.5 tea glasses of 120 mL); 7–10 mL/kg after each motion; < 2 y 50–100 mL, > 2 y 100–200 mL after each motion.
- No antiemetics or antimotility in children: rehydration stops vomiting.
## p66 Antiemetics
- Avoid domperidone/metoclopramide (extrapyramidal: oculogyric crisis, lockjaw, torticollis).
- Domperidone (Motilat, Vomilux) only in GERD when first steps fail: 0.2–0.4 mg/kg/dose [printed "0.2-0.04"] 8-hourly; 2.5 mL per 10 kg = wt/4 mL 8-hourly, 1/2 h before feeds.
- Ondansetron safest: 0.05–0.2 mg/kg/dose (2–4 mg/dose); amp 4 mg & 8 mg/2 mL. B6 inj 1/2–1 ampoule IM also used.
- Metoclopramide inj 10 mg/2 mL: 0.1–0.15 mg/kg/dose IV/IM.
## p67 Acute watery diarrhoea (cholera)
- Profuse rice-water diarrhoea ± vomiting; dehydration or shock. Inv CBC, BFFM, SG.
- Aggressive Ringer's lactate (plan C if severe; maintenance or 1.5 × maintenance if no DH) + ORS when tolerated + zinc + antibiotic: erythromycin < 8 y; doxycycline 100 mg > 8 y; tetracycline alternative.
## p67–68 Hyponatraemic dehydration
- Na < 135 (normal 135–145); dehydration over-estimated clinically; iatrogenic with hypotonic fluids. Correct when Na < 125, slowly (central pontine myelinolysis).
- Na deficit (mmol) = wt × (130 − patient Na) × 0.6. Give as 3% saline (0.5 mmol/mL), or NS (0.15 mmol/mL; 1 L = 154 mmol) if 3% unavailable.
## p68–69 Hypernatraemic dehydration
- Na > 145. Under-estimated: pulse good volume and skin pinch normal despite losses (fluid shifts to ECF). D&V ± convulsions. Risk: formula feeding, hot weather.
- Correct if symptomatic / moderate–severe (usually Na > 150 with symptoms) over 24–48 h (rapid → cerebral oedema). Aim fall of 1/2 mmol/h (12 mmol in 24 h).
- Rehydration: 1/2 deficit + maintenance in 24 h, or deficit + maintenance in 48 h, as dextrose 1/2 saline or dextrose 1/5 saline. Deficit = wt × 100; maintenance Holliday–Segar.
- e.g. 12 kg, Na 155: deficit 1200, maintenance 1100 → 600 + 1100 = 1700 mL over 24 h; Na 6-hourly.
## p70 Oral candidiasis / aphthous / herpetic gingivostomatitis
- White coated tongue (thrush); ulcers of tongue and lips (aphthous); excess salivation, ulcers, bad odour, swollen bleeding gums (herpetic gingivostomatitis). May refuse feeds.
- Miconazole oral gel (Mycoheal) 8-hourly — best for thrush. Nystatin oral drops 8-hourly (or 6-hourly). Gentian violet paint 8-hourly ± metronidazole syrup — best in aphthous ulcer and herpes; with nystatin in thrush. Clean and treat mother's nipple and areola before feeds.
## p71 Typhoid fever (enteric)
- Fever, headache, diarrhoea or constipation (triad); relative bradycardia. Inv: CBC leukopenia (most important; exclude dengue); Widal not accurate; culture ideal (blood week 1, urine week 2, stool week 3).
- ttt: cefixime syrup/tab for 10 days + antipyretic; or amoxicillin syrup 125 wt/2 or 250 wt/4 mL 8-hourly 7–10 days; > 12 y ciprofloxacin 500 mg BD 2 weeks (as adult). Admitted: ceftriaxone.
## p72 Abdominal pain
- Exclude surgical causes first (appendicitis, obstruction, intussusception). Then CBC (low TWBC → typhoid), SG (giardiasis → pain, distension), epigastric pain → H. pylori ICT or stool antigen.
- Giardiasis: metronidazole + antispasmodic. Prifinium bromide (Riabal) syrup: < 3 months 1 mL; 3–6 months 1–2 mL; 6–12 months 2 mL; 1–2 y 5 mL; 2–6 y 5–10 mL, every 6–8 h. Cid water or gripe water 1/2 spoon 8-hourly. Hyoscine > 6 y 0.01 mg/kg/dose IM/IV (20 mg/mL), 1/2–1 mL by age and severity.
