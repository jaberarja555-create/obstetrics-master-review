# Kassala guideline — pages 17–30
## p17 Severe bronchopneumonia notes
- Refusing feeds / diarrhoea / vomiting → IV fluid 2/3 maintenance, never full (risk of SIADH in the distressed child).
## p17–18 Severe bronchopneumonia + bronchospasm (SBP + wheeze)
- As SBP + nebuliser: O2 + antibiotic + antipyretic (paracetamol suppository or infusion) + nebuliser salbutamol or saline.
- Salbutamol (Ventolin) neb 0.1–0.5 mg/kg/dose; practice: < 5 y 2.5 mg (= 0.5 mL of concentrate 5 mg/mL, or 1¼ mL of the plastic diluted nebule 2.5 mg/2.5 mL); > 5 y 5 mg (= 1 mL concentrate, or a whole plastic nebule 2.5 mL).
  Concentrate: 0.5 mL in 2 mL NS (<5 y) or 1 mL in 3 mL NS (>5 y) every 20 min for 1 hour. Small nebules (2.5 mL = 1 mL Ventolin + 1.5 mL water): <5 y half nebule (1¼ mL) in 2 mL NS; >5 y whole nebule, can be used without saline. Beware: small nebules are diluted.
## p19–20 Bronchiolitis
- Infants < 2 y (mostly 6 months–2 y). Cough, SOB, no or mild fever; looks well but distressed ("happy wheezer"); rhonchi ± crackles. Inv BFFM, CBC (normal or lymphocytes), ± CXR hyperinflation.
- Not severely distressed: 3 nebuliser sessions then recheck → if OK home on mucolytic syrup (Solvexin, Prospan) ± oral antibiotic. Severely distressed or no improvement after 3 sessions → admit: O2 + saline (3% or NS) or Ventolin neb ± antibiotics (benzylpenicillin not routine).
- Start with 3% saline neb (3 mL every 20 min for 1 h then 2-hourly), not Ventolin (wheeze from mucus, not bronchospasm); Ventolin trial if NS fails, especially FHx asthma. Adrenaline neb 0.5 mL in 2 mL NS every 20 min for 1 h then 2-hourly if no response to saline. Solvexin neb 3 mL every 20 min 1 h then 2-hourly. Ipratropium (Atrovent) 0.5 mL in 2 mL NS or 1 mL in 2 mL NS every 20 min 1 h then 2-hourly.
- Options order: 3% saline, adrenaline, ipratropium, Solvexin, Ventolin (FHx asthma). Antibiotics only if bacterial infection suspected.
## p21–22 Bronchial asthma
- Usually > 2 y. Cough/SOB at night and early morning; past similar episodes/nebulisation; FHx asthma; atopy. O/E distressed, rhonchi (wheeze audible without stethoscope). Inv not routine.
- Not distressed: 3 neb sessions → reassess → OK: discharge on oral bronchodilator ± prednisolone ± antibiotics.
- Salbutamol syrup (2 mg/5 mL): 0.1 mg/kg/dose, wt/4 mL 8-hourly for 3–5 days.
- Aironyl (terbutaline) syrup 0.3 mg/mL*: 0.2 mg/kg/dose 8-hourly, wt/3 mL. [*printed "0.3 mg/5mL"; wt/3 mL × 0.3 mg/mL ≈ 0.1 mg/kg; flag for review] Tab 2.5 mg.
- Bronclyn (terbutaline 1.5 mg/5 mL): <3 y 2.5 mL; 3–6 y 2.5–5 mL; 7–11 y 5–10 mL; 12–15 y 10 mL; adult 10–15 mL, 8-hourly. Bronchovent also possible.
- Prevention: ketotifen (Ketofen 1 mg/5 mL): 6 months–3 y 2.5 mL BID; ≥3 y 5 mL BID; adults 5–10 mL BID.
- Prednisolone tab 5 mg: 1–2 mg/kg/day; e.g. 10 kg → 2 tabs (1 BD), 13 kg → 15 mg (1 tab 8-hourly); adolescents 6 tabs/day (max 60 mg/day).
## p23–26 Acute severe asthma
- Not responding to 3 Ventolin sessions; distressed, tachycardic → admit: O2 + Ventolin neb + hydrocortisone ± antibiotics ± ipratropium.
- Hydrocortisone 100 mg vial (dissolve in 2 mL water): 4–5 mg/kg/dose 6-hourly (can 4-hourly; 5–10 mg/kg severe). By age: <2 y max 25 mg, 2–5 y 50 mg, 5–18 y 100 mg 6-hourly. Prednisolone 1–2 mg/kg/day orally is superior if can take orally.
- Antibiotics only for bacterial infection: NOT benzylpenicillin in asthmatics (may trigger allergy); ampicillin (Ampiclox), cefuroxime, ceftriaxone.
- No NSAIDs (Mefnac, ibuprofen, diclofenac) in asthma (leukotriene → bronchospasm); paracetamol is the safe antipyretic.
- Ventolin side effects: tachycardia, hypokalaemia; avoid in cardiac patients.
- Not improving: SC adrenaline 1:1000 0.01 mg/kg (0.01 mL/kg = wt × 1 unit on insulin syringe), max 0.5 mg, every 30 min up to 3 doses; or dilute 1:10,000 and give 0.5 mL regardless of weight. Not if HR > 180 (infant) / > 160 (older child).
- Then MgSO4 50–100 mg/kg (BNF 40 mg/kg), max 2 g, = 0.1–0.2 mL/kg (50% solution) diluted in NS over 20 min; contraindicated if hypotensive; NS bolus before.
- Aminophylline (250 mg/10 mL = 25 mg/mL) 5 mg/kg/dose = wt × 20 units on insulin syringe, in NS over 20–30 min (rapid → arrhythmia, hypotension, death). 10 kg → 50 mg (2 mL) in 30 mL NS over 1/2 h. Not recommended in new guidelines.
- Ipratropium neb 15 mcg/kg in 3 mL NS 4–6-hourly: <20 kg 0.5 mL in 2 mL NS; >20 kg 1 mL in 3 mL NS (polyneb 125 or 250 mcg/mL); can mix with Ventolin.
- Steps: 1 O2 keep SpO2 > 95%; 2 Ventolin neb; 3 hydrocortisone (or prednisolone); 4 ipratropium; 5 SC adrenaline; 6 MgSO4 (aminophylline alternative); 7 CPAP / intubation. Hydration: 2/3 maintenance IV.
## p27–28 Whooping cough
- Cough with vomiting, paroxysmal cough, apnoea/cyanosis in infants; contact; unvaccinated. O/E may be normal, subconjunctival haemorrhage. CBC leukocytosis with absolute lymphocytosis (characteristic); CXR if complicated.
- Erythromycin 40 mg/kg/day for 14 days (neonate 12.5 mg/kg/dose 6-hourly): 125 → wt/2, 250 → wt/4 mL 6-hourly for 2 weeks. Effective in catarrhal phase.
- Alternatives: azithromycin 200 mg/5mL wt/4 mL for 5 days (not < 6 months); clarithromycin 125 → wt/3, 250 → wt/6 BD for 1 week.
- Distressed/danger signs: admit O2 + IV benzylpenicillin + erythromycin ± nebuliser ± 2/3 maintenance.
## p29–30 Cardiac problems
- SOB ± cyanosis, dyspnoea on feeding. Hyperoxia test: 100% O2 10 min — resolves → respiratory; not → cardiac (except pneumothorax); ABG PO2 rise > 20 kPa → respiratory.
- Heart failure: SOB, dyspnoea on feeding; tachycardia, tachypnoea, tender hepatomegaly; older child ↑JVP, basal crepitations, gallop. Inv CBC, CXR, echo. ttt: continuous O2, cardiac bed (older), furosemide 2 mg/kg/dose now then 1 mg/kg/dose BID, spironolactone 2–3 mg/kg/day.
- CHF + bronchopneumonia: fever, cough ± SOB, ill, crackles. Inv BFFM, CBC ± CXR. O2, antibiotics (ceftriaxone, ceftazidime) as bronchopneumonia, antipyretic, continue anti-failure.
- Infective endocarditis: fever prominent, haematuria; clubbing, splenomegaly, new/changing murmur. Inv BFFM, CBC, CRP, blood culture, echo. O2 if needed; benzylpenicillin + gentamicin OR vancomycin + ceftriaxone or ceftazidime (then by culture); antipyretic.
- Furosemide inj 20 mg/2 mL: 2 mg/kg first (not without measuring BP; if hypotensive 1 mg/kg + inotropes dopamine ± dobutamine), then 1 mg/kg/dose BID. Spironolactone tab 25 mg 2–3 mg/kg/day ÷ BID. Refractory: captopril 0.2 mg/kg/dose BID–TDS increasing to 4 mg/kg/day (tabs 25, 50 mg).
