# Kassala guideline — pages 45–58
## p45–47 Neonatal jaundice
- Yellow skin/sclera. Hx: onset, urine & stool colour, associated symptoms. Jaundice on day 1 = pathological (Rh/ABO).
- Inv: serum bilirubin (total, direct, indirect), baby & mother blood groups (most important), CBC; CRP, RFT, Coombs.
- Conjugated if direct/total × 100 > 20% or direct > 2 mg/dL → hepatitis screen, fasting & post-prandial US (biliary atresia), urine reducing substances (galactosaemia), TORCH, ± metabolic screen.
- ttt: phototherapy by chart (single, double, exchange). Mild below phototherapy level and well → reassure, home (early morning sun). Admit if ill, deep jaundice or TSB > 5 mg/dL day 1, > 9 day 2, > 15 mg/dL.
- 1 mg/dL = 17 µmol/L. No phototherapy in conjugated jaundice (bronze baby).
- Clinical estimate (Kramer): face ~6 mg/dL; chest ~9 (8–10); abdomen ~12 (12–15); feet ~15 (15–20).
- Exchange transfusion if above double-phototherapy level or kernicterus risk. Kernicterus: hypotonia, opisthotonus, lethargy, seizures, death. IVIG 500 mg–1 g/kg reduces exchange need in Rh/ABO (expensive). Phenobarbitone (day 1) induces glucuronyl transferase.
- Plan = neonatal sepsis plan + phototherapy. Very ill: cefotaxime + ampicillin/benzyl + vitamin K ± IV/NG + phototherapy or double. Stable: gentamicin + benzylpenicillin + vitamin K ± IV/NG + phototherapy.
## p48–50 Neonatal seizures
- Abnormal movement/posture. Causes sepsis, electrolytes, B6 deficiency, birth asphyxia, ICH. Subtle seizures (preterm): blinking, nystagmus, eye rolling, sucking, yawning, apnoea. Apnoea + tachycardia = subtle seizure (phenobarbitone); apnoea + bradycardia = other cause (aminophylline, esp. preterm).
- Plan: sepsis plan + phenobarbitone ± phenytoin: phenobarbitone, cefotaxime, ampicillin/benzylpenicillin, vitamin K, NG feeding or IV 2/3 maintenance. No diazepam in neonates except tetanus neonatorum.
- Phenobarbitone tab 30 mg; inj 200 mg/1 mL (often unavailable): LD 15–20 mg/kg; MD 3–5 mg/kg/day ÷ 12-hourly. Seizures continue → 10 mg/kg, then 10 mg/kg more (total 40 mg/kg). Then phenytoin.
- Phenytoin 250 mg/5 mL: LD 15–18 mg/kg; MD 5–6 mg/kg/day ÷ BID; further 10 mg/kg if needed. Then pyridoxine, then midazolam.
- Pyridoxine 100–200 mg IV (under EEG ideally); MD 50–100 mg daily.
- Midazolam 15 mg/3 mL: LD 0.15–0.2 mg/kg; MD 0.4–0.6 mcg/kg/min [as printed; Sudanese emergency protocol; "max 6 mg/kg/min in 50 mL NS" printed — flag for review]. Still not controlled → antiepileptics.
- Stopping: seizure-free 48 h → taper phenytoin while continuing phenobarbitone; then if well and neuro normal taper phenobarbitone (not > 1/3 per day). Neuro abnormal → phenobarbitone maintenance 1 month, then stop if well; not controlled after 1 month → EEG.
## p51 Haemorrhagic disease of newborn (VKDB)
- Umbilical bleeding (commonest), cephalhaematoma, epistaxis, haematuria, bulging fontanelle (ICH). Inv CBC, PT/PTT/INR (prolonged PT, APTT, CT).
- ttt: sepsis plan except vitamin K 5 mg daily for 3–5 days (regardless of weight); FFP if active bleeding (10 mL/kg; printed "10 mg/kg"); transfuse if Hb < 10 g/dL.
## p52 NEC
- Preterm, LBW, formula, UVC, CHD. Bilious vomiting, distension, blood & mucus in stool; ill, lethargic ± acidotic; distended (erythematous) tender abdomen.
- Inv CBC (↓Hb, ↓platelets, ↑WBC), abdominal X-ray erect & supine (pneumatosis intestinalis), RFT + electrolytes, RBG.
- ttt: NPO 7–14 days; NG tube open; IV maintenance with K, Ca, Na; cefotaxime + ampicillin/benzyl, or vancomycin + meropenem; metronidazole 7.5 mg/kg/dose (1.5 × wt mL) 8-hourly. Surgery if not responding.
## p53 Tetanus neonatorum
- Home delivery, contaminated cord. Repeated convulsions, refusal of feeds with lockjaw, opisthotonus; ill, spastic.
- ttt: dark quiet room; diazepam 0.1–0.3 mg/kg every 3–6 h; ± chlorpromazine 50 mg/2 mL 0.5–1 mg/kg/dose 4–6-hourly; tetanus IG IM 500 IU (3000–6000 IU recommended) or antitetanic serum 50,000–100,000 U IM; benzylpenicillin + gentamicin + metronidazole; IV maintenance with electrolytes.
## p54 Respiratory distress in neonate
- RDS: prematurity, diabetic mother; tachypnoea + signs of prematurity; CXR homogeneous infiltrate (ground glass); ABCs + surfactant via ETT + sepsis plan.
- TTN: caesarean, macrosomia, male, diabetic or asthmatic mother; tachypnoea usually without hypoxia; CXR fluid in fissures; supportive + sepsis plan.
- MAS: term/post-term; tachypnoea ± hypoxia, meconium-stained cord/skin; CXR consolidation, patchy atelectasis; ABCs + broad-spectrum antibiotic + sepsis plan.
- Also congenital pneumonia.
## p55 Intracranial haemorrhage (IVH)
- Bulging fontanelle ± seizure; ill, lethargic, pale (most important) ± shock; ↑BP ↓HR (Cushing). Inv CBC, bleeding profile, transcranial US and/or CT.
- ABCs; blood group; vitamin K 5 mg IV daily 5 days; ± FFP 10 mL/kg; ± PPI; transfuse if Hb < 10 g/dL; head circumference BID.
## p56 Birth asphyxia (HIE)
- Did not cry at birth; SOB, grunting, cyanosis, convulsions; distressed/cyanosed. Inv ± RBG, CBC, RFT.
- ABCs: suction + O2 first (airway before IV line), then IV line; antibiotics cefotaxime + ampicillin/benzyl or gentamicin + benzyl; cooling + vitamin K. Cooling if within 6 h of birth and GA > 35 weeks: 33–35 °C whole body or head for up to 72 h then rewarm.
## p57 Gastroenteritis
- Summer/rainy; diarrhoea & vomiting; mostly rotavirus (vaccine 6 & 10 weeks). Ask frequency and colour (rice-water = cholera → vigorous rehydration).
- Classification: no / some / severe dehydration; isotonic / hyponatraemic / hypernatraemic.
- No DH (may have excessive ongoing loss): Inv BFFM, CBC, stool general; ttt zinc sulphate, ORS ± IV maintenance, ceftriaxone (Oncef/Samixon) [local].
- Some DH (2 of: sunken eyes, skin pinch goes back slowly, irritable, drinks eagerly): BFFM, CBC, SG; ORS or Ringer's lactate, zinc ± ceftriaxone.
- Severe DH (2 of: deeply sunken eyes, skin pinch very slowly, lethargic, drinks poorly/unable): BFFM, CBC, RFT & electrolytes; Ringer's lactate, antibiotic (ceftriaxone), zinc.
## p58 GE + excessive loss (ongoing loss)
- No signs of DH but frequent (> 5 stools or > 4 vomits). Ideal: IV fluid + zinc; antibiotics only if stool general shows significant pus cells or shock/severe DH. Local hospital guideline: ceftriaxone + IV maintenance + zinc.
- Maintenance (Holliday–Segar): first 10 kg × 100, second 10 kg × 50, remainder × 20 mL/day. 6 kg → 600 mL/24 h → 200 mL 8-hourly (dextrose-saline); 14 kg → 1200 mL → 400 mL 8-hourly "glucose with 1/2 saline".
